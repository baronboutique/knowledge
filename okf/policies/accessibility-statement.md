Baron Intl' Pvt Ltd is committed to providing a fully accessible website experience on the Baron Boutique website for users of assistive technologies.

# Accessibility Statement

## Our Commitment to Accessibility

Baron Boutique is committed to providing a website that is accessible to all users, including those who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, magnification software, and keyboard navigation.

We aim to make the Baron Boutique website, [https://baronboutique.com/](https://baronboutique.com/), usable and inclusive for as many people as possible.

## Accessibility Standards

Our goal is to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines help make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of abilities.

## Ongoing Improvements

We are continuously reviewing and improving our website to enhance accessibility. While we strive to ensure all areas meet accessibility standards, some parts of the site may still be in the process of improvement.

## Need Assistance?

If you experience any difficulty using our website or accessing any content, please contact us and we will do our best to assist you.

Email: [contact@baronboutique.com](mailto:contact@baronboutique.com)

We aim to respond to accessibility-related requests within a reasonable timeframe.
