# Shirt Fit Guide

Find your perfect shirt fit—Slim, Standard, Relaxed, or Athletic. Baron Boutique's guide helps you choose the best custom garment for you.

## Women’s Shirt Fit Guide

A shirt’s fit defines how it flatters your frame, moves with your body, and layers under tailored pieces. Your test garment will reflect how close or relaxed the fit feels, helping you select the silhouette that best suits your shape and personal style.

---

## Slim Fit

Tailored and contoured through the torso and sleeves. The shirt is slim through the shoulders, chest, and waist. Narrower sleeves with higher armholes.

**Best for:** A lean frame or those who prefer a sharply tailored, body-contouring look.

---

## Standard Fit

Gently shaped at the waist for a flattering silhouette. Offers a comfortable, natural fit through the shoulders and chest. Sleeves are moderately tapered with standard armhole height for ease of movement.

**Best for:** An average build or those who prefer a classic, comfortable silhouette with room for natural movement and versatile layering.

---

## Relaxed Fit

A more generous cut throughout the body and sleeves for added comfort. The shirt provides extra room in the shoulders, chest, and waist. Sleeves are fuller with lower armholes for all-day comfort and unrestricted movement.

**Best for:** Those who prefer a looser silhouette or require extra room through the chest, waist, or arms. Ideal for casual styling or easy movement throughout the day.

Women’s Shirt Fit Guide

## The Shirt That Finally Fits, With No Guesswork

If you have ever stood in a fitting room watching a shirt gape between the buttons, or bought one that fit your shoulders but strained across the bust, you already know why women’s shirts are so hard to get right off the rack. They are cut to an average body, and almost no one is average. A custom-made shirt removes the gamble completely: there are no standard sizes, we cut it to your measurements, and before we cut your final cloth we send you a free test garment so the fit is proven on your body first. This page is here to answer the worries that come with ordering a shirt you cannot try on in a store yet.

---

How It Should Sit

## The fit checkpoints, so you know what “right” looks like

Bust

The most common worry, and the one we fix. The shirt closes smoothly across the bust with no gap or strain between the buttons, because it is shaped and cut to your bust measurement rather than an average.

Shoulders & collar

The shoulder seam sits at the edge of your shoulder, and the collar closes comfortably with room for a finger, so it never chokes or gapes.

Waist

Gently shaped through the waist for a tailored line that tucks in cleanly, or left easier if you prefer to wear it loose, your choice.

Sleeve & length

The sleeve ends at your wrist bone and the hem is cut to tuck in or wear out, whichever you tell us, so it is never too long or too short.

Every shirt is made to your measurements with no standard sizing, and a free test garment confirms the bust, shoulders, and sleeve on your body before your final cloth is cut. The test garment is yours to keep.

Your Worries, Answered

## The doubts most women have, and why you can relax

### My shirts always gape at the bust, it is embarrassing. Will this actually be different?

Yes, and it is the single most common reason women come to us. Bust gape happens because ready-to-wear is cut for an average bust-to-shoulder ratio that probably is not yours. We shape the shirt with darts and cut it to your real bust measurement, so it closes smoothly with no gap and no strain. If the free test garment shows even a hint of pull, we correct it before the final shirt is made, so you never have to send back a shirt that gapes again.

### I have never ordered a shirt I could not try on first. How do I know it will fit?

Because you do try it on first. After you send your measurements, we make a free test shirt in a stand-in cotton to those numbers and post it to you. You put it on, check the bust, shoulders, and sleeves, and send us your notes and a couple of photos. Only once you are happy do we cut your final cloth, so the finished shirt is not a leap of faith, it is a fit you have already approved.

### I am nervous I will measure myself wrong.

You do not have to be perfect. Our guide walks you through each measurement, a friend or a local tailor can help with the tricky ones, and if you are unsure you can simply send your best numbers. The whole point of the free test garment is to catch any small error before it matters, we see the fit on your body and adjust, so an imperfect first measurement is completely normal and nothing to worry about.

### I have always struggled to find shirts that fit my shape. Is custom-made really for someone like me?

It is made for exactly you. The women who struggle most with off-the-rack, a fuller bust, broad or narrow shoulders, a long or short torso, a small waist with a fuller bust, are the ones who benefit most, because we cut to your proportions instead of forcing you into a size. There is no shape that is wrong here; there are only your measurements.

### What if the shirt arrives and something still is not quite right?

That is what the test garment stage is designed to prevent, because the final shirt is cut to the exact fit you already approved. In the rare case something needs a tweak, the finished shirt is made with seam allowances so a local tailor can fine-tune it, and you can always contact us and we will help make it right.

### A silk blouse worries me, I do not want it clinging or gaping.

A well-cut silk blouse should drape and skim, not cling, and that comes down to the fit through the bust and shoulders, which we tailor to you. Because silk is unforgiving of a poor fit, a custom-made shirt actually matters more here, not less, and the test garment lets you confirm the drape before we cut into the silk.

### It feels like a lot to spend on a single shirt made just for me. What is my safety net?

Your safety net is that nothing final, and nothing expensive, is cut until you have approved the fit on the free test garment. You are not paying for a gamble; you are paying for a shirt whose fit you have already seen and signed off on your own body. And because a custom-made shirt in good cloth lasts for years and always fits, the cost per wear is usually far lower than the ill-fitting shirts that end up unworn.

### What if my size changes over time?

Your finished shirt is made with seam allowances, so a local tailor can take it in or let it out at the sides within reason if your shape shifts. And whenever you want another, we already have your fit on record, so reordering is simple.
