# Pants Fit Guide

Find your perfect pants fit—Slim, Standard, Relaxed, or Athletic. Baron Boutique's guide helps you choose the best custom garment for you.

## Pants Fit Guide

Your pant fit sets the foundation for both style and comfort. This choice determines the cut and ease of movement in your test garment. Use this guide to select the fit that best matches your body type and style preference.

---

## Slim Fit

A sleek, tapered shape from thigh to ankle.

**Best for:** Those with a lean build or who prefer a more tailored, modern look. The cut hugs the body closely without restricting movement.

---

## Standard Fit

A straight-leg classic offering all-day comfort.

**Best for:** Most body types. This is the most versatile pant fit for everyday wear, offering balance between structure and ease.

---

## Relaxed Fit

Generous room from hip to hem.

**Best for:** Those prioritizing ease of movement or requiring more space in the seat and thigh. Ideal for casual or comfort-driven tailoring.

---

## Athletic Fit

Room in the thighs with a modern taper.

**Best for:** Muscular builds with broader thighs and glutes. Provides space without bagginess, tapering neatly at the lower leg.

Men’s Trouser Fit Guide

## How Men’s Trousers Should Fit

Trousers are where ready-made forces the biggest compromise. A waist that fits means a seat and thigh that pull, or a seat that fits means a waistband you have to cinch. Trousers made to your measurements remove that trade-off entirely. You choose the leg shape and the fit, we cut the waist, seat, thigh, rise, and length independently to your body, and a free test garment confirms it on you before your final cloth is cut.

A pair that truly fits is one you wear on repeat and keep reaching for long after, not something you tolerate for a season. This guide covers the four fits we offer and where trousers should sit so yours feel right the day they arrive and years down the line.

---

Step One

## Choose your fit

Slim

Trim through the seat, thigh, and leg, tapering to the ankle for a sleek, modern line that works cropped or full length.

Standard

A clean, even line with a little room through the seat and thigh. The most classic and versatile fit, the easiest to dress up or down.

Relaxed

More room through the seat, thigh, and leg for comfort and movement, with an easy, contemporary drape that still looks intentional.

Athletic

Cut for a built lower half, with extra room through the seat and thigh and a trimmer waist, so muscular legs fit cleanly with no pulling or strain.

---

Step Two

## The fit checkpoints

Waist

The waistband sits comfortably where you like to wear it, snug without digging in, and stays put without a belt straining to hold it. Because it is cut to your waist, you avoid the usual cinch or gap.

Seat & thigh

The trousers skim your seat and thigh cleanly, with no pulling and no horizontal strain lines. This is exactly where off-the-rack fails and custom cutting wins, because the seat is cut independently of the waist.

Rise

The rise, how high the trouser sits from crotch to waistband, is cut to you, so it sits at the height you prefer without pulling low or riding high. Tell us where you like your trousers to sit.

Length & break

You choose the length: a full break resting on the shoe, a light break with a single soft fold, or a clean cropped ankle. Every hem is cut to your leg and your shoe height.

Every pair is made to your measurements, in the leg shape and fit you choose, with the waist, seat, and rise cut independently for a true fit. A free test garment confirms it before your final cloth is cut, and it is yours to keep.

Your Worries, Answered

## The doubts most men have, and why you can relax

### Trousers never fit my waist and my seat at the same time. Am I just an awkward shape?

You are not, ready-made is the problem, not your body. Off-the-rack trousers are cut to one fixed waist-to-seat ratio, so if yours is different, either the waist gaps or the seat and thigh pull, whatever size you buy. We cut your waist and your seat independently to your own measurements, so both fit at once, and the free test garment proves it on your body before the final pair is made.

### I have never bought trousers I could not try on first. How do I know they will fit?

Because you do try them on first. We make a free test pair in a stand-in cloth to your measurements and post it to you. You put them on, sit, walk, check the waist, rise, seat, and length, and send us notes and photos. Only once you are happy do we cut your final fabric, so the finished trousers are a fit you have already approved.

### I am nervous I will measure the waist, seat, or rise wrong.

You do not have to be exact. Our guide walks you through each measurement, a friend or a local tailor can help, and if you are unsure you can send your best numbers. The free test garment is there to catch anything slightly off, we see the fit on your body and adjust, so a first-try measurement is completely normal.

### I always end up paying a tailor to alter every pair. Will this be different?

Completely, because there is no size to fall between. You send your measurements, we cut to them exactly, and the free test garment confirms the fit, so you receive trousers that are already right rather than a compromise you have to pay to fix afterwards.

### It feels like a lot for one pair of trousers. Is it worth it?

Trousers that finally fit your waist and seat get worn constantly, while the ill-fitting pairs sit unused, so a proper pair usually costs far less per wear over its life. Nothing final is cut until you have approved the fit, so you are not gambling, and a pair cut to you and made well stays comfortable and sharp for years, not one season.

### Will I still be reaching for these in a few years, or is it a one-off?

These are made to become a staple. We cut a timeless shape in quality cloth with real seam allowances, so they keep their line and can be adjusted as you change. Most men find a well-fitting pair turns into the trousers they wear more than any other, and years on they still feel right. That is what owning them, rather than just buying them, looks like.

### What if my weight changes later on?

Your finished trousers are made with seam allowances, so a local tailor can take them in or let them out at the waist and seat within reason if your shape shifts. And we keep your measurements on record, so ordering another pair that fits is simple whenever you want one.
