# Skirt Fit Guide

Find your perfect skirt fit—Slim, Standard, or Relaxed. Baron Boutique's women's guide helps you choose the best custom garment for your style.

## Skirt Fit Guide

A skirt’s fit shapes your comfort, movement, and overall silhouette. Your test garment will reflect how closely the skirt follows your body or how freely it drapes. Use this guide to find the best fit for your build and style.

---

## Slim Fit

Fitted closely through the hips and thighs with a narrow hemline. Designed to contour the lower body for a sleek, polished silhouette.

**Best for:** A lean build or those who prefer a tailored, form-skimming shape that complements structured or professional looks.

---

## Standard Fit

Softly shaped through the waist and hips with a classic straight or slightly flared cut. Offers balanced proportions and everyday versatility.

**Best for:** An average build or anyone seeking a timeless silhouette that blends comfort with structure.

---

## Relaxed Fit

Loosely cut with extra room through the waist and hips for ease of movement. Designed for comfort and a fluid, unrestrictive drape.

**Best for:** Those who prioritize comfort or prefer a more relaxed, flowing silhouette around the midsection and thighs.

Women’s Skirt Fit Guide

## How a Tailored Skirt Should Fit

A pencil skirt is only as good as three measurements, the waist, the hip, and the length, and off-the-rack rarely gets all three right on one body. A custom-made skirt does. You choose the length and the fit, we cut the waist and hip to your measurements, and a free test garment confirms it on you before your final cloth is cut. This guide covers the fits and where a tailored skirt should sit.

---

Step One

## Choose your fit

Slim / Pencil

Closely follows the waist, hip, and thigh for a sharp, tailored line. The classic work-skirt silhouette, cut with a vent or split so you can still walk freely.

Standard (Classic) Fit

Skims the hip with a little more ease than a true pencil, comfortable for all-day wear while keeping a clean, tailored shape.

Relaxed / A-line

Fitted at the waist and easing gently over the hip, for more movement and a softer line that flatters most figures.

---

Step Two

## The fit checkpoints

Waist

The waistband sits at your chosen height, snug and level, with no gap at the back when you sit. Cut to your waist, it stays put without rolling or pinching.

Hip & seat

The skirt skims the widest part of your hips and seat cleanly, with no horizontal pull lines and no riding up when you walk. This is where cutting to your hip measurement matters most.

Length

You choose it: on the knee for a classic look, just above for a sharper line, or midi for elegance. Every hem is cut to your height and proportions.

Walking room

A fitted skirt is cut with a back vent, split, or kick pleat so you can walk and climb stairs comfortably without the skirt feeling restrictive.

Every skirt is made to your measurements, in the fit and length you choose, with the waist and hip cut to you and a vent for easy walking. A free test garment confirms it before your final cloth is cut, and it is yours to keep.

Your Worries, Answered

## The doubts most women have, and why you can relax

### Pencil skirts always ride up or gape at my waist. Am I just hard to fit?

You are not, ready-to-wear is. Skirts are cut to one fixed waist-to-hip ratio, so if yours is different, the waist gapes or the hips pull and the skirt rides up. We cut your waist and your hip independently to your own measurements, so the skirt sits level with no gap and skims the hip without riding, and you confirm exactly that on the free test garment before the final skirt is made.

### I can never walk comfortably in a fitted skirt. Will this be different?

Yes. A tailored pencil skirt is cut with a back vent, split, or kick pleat that gives you real stride room for walking and stairs, and with enough ease through the hip to sit down comfortably. When you try the free test garment, take a few steps and sit, and if you want more walking room we simply add it before the final skirt is cut.

### I have never bought a skirt I could not try on first. How do I know it will fit?

Because you do try it on first. We make a free test skirt in a stand-in cloth to your measurements and post it to you. You put it on, walk, sit, and check the waist, hips, and length, then send us notes and photos. Only once you are happy do we cut your final cloth, so the finished skirt is a fit you have already approved.

### I am nervous I will measure my waist or hips wrong.

You do not have to be exact. Our guide walks you through each measurement, a friend or a local tailor can help, and if you are unsure you can send your best numbers. The free test garment catches anything slightly off, we see the skirt on your body and adjust, so a first-try measurement is completely normal.

### I struggle to find any skirt that flatters my shape. Is custom-made really for me?

It is made for exactly you. A small waist with fuller hips, or the reverse, a longer or shorter body, these are the shapes off-the-rack fails and custom-made tailoring fits best, because we cut to your proportions rather than a size. You also choose the length that flatters you, cut to your height, so nothing is left to chance.

### What if it arrives and something is not quite right?

The final skirt is cut to the exact fit you approved on the test garment, so surprises are rare by design. If a small tweak is ever needed, the finished skirt carries seam allowances at the waist and seat so a local tailor can fine-tune it, and you can always contact us and we will help make it right.

### What if my size changes later?

Your finished skirt is made with seam allowances, so a local tailor can take it in or let it out at the waist and seat within reason. And we keep your measurements on record, so ordering another skirt that fits is simple whenever you want one.
