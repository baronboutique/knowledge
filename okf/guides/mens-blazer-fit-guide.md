## Blazer Fit Guide

The fit of your custom blazer defines its look and comfort. Your test garment will reflect the room and structure you choose here. Use this guide to select the fit that best matches your body type and style preference.

---

## Slim Fit

Modern and tapered for a close-to-the-body silhouette.

**Best for:** A lean build and those who prefer a sharp, modern look that closely follows the body’s lines.

---

## Standard Fit

A timeless, comfortable fit with moderate room for movement.

**Best for:** An average build or for a classic, all-purpose business style. This is our most popular, versatile choice.

---

## Relaxed Fit

Our most generous fit, with extra room throughout the chest and waist for maximum comfort.

**Best for:** Those who prioritize comfort over a tapered look, or for body types that require more room in the midsection.

---

## Athletic Fit

Specifically designed for a V-shaped torso.

**Best for:** Broader shoulders and a larger chest, combined with a distinctly narrower waist. This cut prevents the jacket from being too tight in the shoulders or too loose at the waist.

Men’s Blazer Fit Guide

## How a Men’s Blazer Should Fit

A blazer is the piece that does the most work in a wardrobe. It dresses up jeans, sharpens chinos, and carries a shirt through anything short of black tie. That is exactly why the fit has to be right: a blazer that sits well becomes the jacket you reach for without thinking, and one that does not just hangs in the closet. Made to your measurements, the fit is not left to chance. You choose the cut, we shape it to your body, and a free test garment confirms it on you before your final cloth is cut.

This is a piece to own for years, not for one night. Cut to your shoulders and chest and built to last, a good blazer only gets more useful with time. This guide covers the four fits we offer and the points to check so yours sits exactly right.

---

Step One

## Choose your fit

Slim

Trim through the chest and waist with a shorter, sharper line. Best over a shirt or fine knit for a clean, modern look with less layering room.

Standard

Shaped but easy, with enough room to layer a shirt or light knit comfortably. The most versatile fit and the one that flatters the widest range of builds.

Relaxed

A softer, roomier line with a longer, easy drape. Ideal if you carry a broader frame, like to layer over knitwear, or prefer an unstructured feel.

Athletic

Cut for a built frame, with extra room through the chest and shoulders and a trimmer waist, so a muscular or V-shaped build sits clean with no pulling across the back.

---

Step Two

## The fit checkpoints

Shoulders

The seam ends right at the edge of your shoulder, flat and clean, with no divot below it and no overhang past it. Get the shoulder right and everything below falls into place.

Chest & lapel

The lapels lie flat against your chest with no gap and no bowing, and the jacket closes without strain at the button. This is where a blazer looks either effortless or forced.

Waist & length

A gentle shape through the waist keeps it from looking boxy, and the hem falls around the middle of your hand, long enough to cover the seat, short enough to keep the line clean.

Sleeve

The sleeve ends at the wrist bone with a little shirt cuff showing, so the jacket reads as tailored rather than borrowed. You tell us your preference and we set it to you.

Every blazer is made to your measurements with no standard sizing, in the fit you choose. A free test garment confirms the shoulders, chest, waist, and length on your body before your final cloth is cut, and it is yours to keep.

Your Worries, Answered

## The doubts most men have, and why you can relax

### I have never bought a blazer I could not try on first. How do I know it will fit?

Because you do try it on first. After you send your measurements, we make a free test garment in a stand-in cloth to those numbers and post it to you. You put it on at home, check the shoulders, chest, and length, and send us your notes and a couple of photos. Only once you are happy do we cut your final cloth, so the finished blazer is a fit you have already seen and approved on your own body.

### Off-the-rack blazers never sit right on my shoulders. Will this actually fix that?

Yes, directly. The shoulder is the one line that ruins most ready-made jackets and the hardest to alter afterwards, which is why we cut it precisely to you and confirm it on the test garment first. A blazer that sits clean on the shoulder is the whole difference between a jacket you wear constantly and one you avoid.

### I am nervous I will measure myself wrong.

You do not have to be exact. Our guide walks you through each measurement, a friend or a local tailor can help, and if you are unsure you can send your best numbers. The free test garment is there to catch anything slightly off, we see the fit on your body and adjust, so a first-try measurement is completely normal.

### Will I actually get a lot of use out of it, or is this an occasional piece?

A well-fitting blazer is the most-worn tailored thing most men own, because it goes with almost everything. Over jeans, over chinos, with or without a tie, it carries you from work to dinner to a weekend event. Because it is cut to you and built properly, it stays comfortable and sharp through all of it, so it earns its place many times over.

### It is a real investment. Will I still love it in a few years?

That is exactly what it is made for. We cut a timeless line rather than a trend, in quality cloth with generous seam allowances, so it holds its shape and can be adjusted as you change. Most men find the blazer becomes their default jacket, the one they trust for anything, and five years on it still feels like theirs. You are meant to own it and enjoy it, not replace it.

### What if it arrives and something is still not right?

The final blazer is cut to the exact fit you approved on the test garment, so surprises are rare by design. If a small tweak is ever needed, the finished jacket carries seam allowances so a local tailor can fine-tune it, and you can always contact us and we will help make it right.

### What if my weight changes later on?

Your finished blazer is made with seam allowances, so a local tailor can take it in or let it out within reason if your shape shifts. And we keep your measurements on record, so ordering another piece that fits is simple whenever you want one.
