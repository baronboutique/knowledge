# Outwear Fit Guide

Find your perfect outwear fit—Slim, Standard, or Relaxed. Baron Boutique's guide helps you choose the best custom garment for you.

## Women’s Outerwear Fit Guide

Outerwear fit affects layering and overall shape. Your test garment will show how your custom coat fits through the shoulders, chest, and body. Use this guide to select the fit that best matches your body type and style preference.

---

## Slim Fit

Sleek and close-fitting for a modern silhouette. Slim through the shoulders, chest, and waist with higher armholes and a tailored sleeve. Designed to layer minimally.

**Best for:** A lean frame or those who prefer a structured, body-contouring outerwear look.

---

## Standard Fit

Clean and classic with gentle shaping at the waist. Offers comfortable space through the shoulders and chest for light layering, with standard armholes and sleeve width.

**Best for:** An average build or those who want balanced structure and everyday versatility.

---

## Relaxed Fit

Loosely tailored for ease and layering comfort. Provides generous room through the shoulders, chest, and waist with fuller sleeves and lower armholes. Ideal over thicker garments.

**Best for:** Those who prioritize comfort, layering, or prefer an oversized, relaxed silhouette.

## A Coat Made to Fit You, and Everything You Wear Under It

A coat is a bigger commitment than most garments, you wear it over everything, everyone sees it, and it usually costs more, so it is natural to feel more nervous ordering one made to your measurements. The most common fear is simple: will it be roomy enough over a jumper, but still shaped and not a sack? With a made-to-measure coat you do not have to choose. There are no standard sizes, we cut it to your measurements with the right ease built in for layering, and a free test garment confirms the fit, over your usual layers, before your final cloth is cut. This page answers the worries that come with buying a coat you cannot try on yet.

How It Should Sit

## The fit checkpoints, so you know what “right” looks like

Room for layers

A coat is cut with more ease than a jacket so it closes comfortably over a jumper or blazer without straining, while still keeping a shaped, tailored line rather than hanging like a box.

Shoulders

The shoulder seam sits at or just past the edge of your shoulder to allow for layers underneath, with no pulling. This is cut precisely to you.

Length

Your choice, from a cropped or mid-thigh cut to a full-length coat, drafted to your height so the proportion is right for your frame.

Sleeves

Cut a touch longer than a jacket so they cover your wrist and a shirt or jumper cuff, and sit correctly when your arms are down.

Every coat is made to your measurements with the ease built in for layering, in the length and fit you choose. A free test garment lets you confirm the fit over your usual layers before your final cloth is cut, and it is yours to keep.

Your Worries, Answered

## The doubts most women have, and why you can relax

### I am worried a custom-made coat will be too tight over my jumpers.

This is the most common coat worry, and it is exactly what we plan for. A coat is cut with more ease than a jacket precisely so it closes comfortably over your layers, and when you try the free test garment we ask you to put it on over the jumper or blazer you would normally wear underneath. If it feels at all snug, we add room before the final cloth is cut, so the finished coat is never tight over your usual layers.

### A coat is a big spend. What if it arrives and it is wrong?

Nothing final, and nothing costly, is cut until you have approved the fit on the free test garment. You are not gambling a large sum on a coat you have never seen on your body; you are confirming the fit on a test version first, over your layers, and only then do we cut the real cloth. That is the whole point of the process, to take the risk out of the biggest, most visible piece in your wardrobe.

### I have never been able to find a coat that fits my shoulders and my shape at once.

That is precisely who custom-made is for. Off-the-rack coats are cut to an average frame, so if your shoulders, bust, or height are not average, something is always wrong. We cut the shoulders, body, and length to your own measurements, so the coat fits your frame rather than a size, and the test garment confirms it before we commit to the final cloth.

### I am nervous about measuring myself for something this important.

You do not need to get it perfect. Our guide takes you through each measurement, a friend or a local tailor can help, and you measure over light clothing so we can allow for layers ourselves. The free test garment is there to catch anything that is slightly off, we see the coat on your body and adjust, so a first-try measurement is completely fine.

### How do I choose the right length without seeing it on?

Tell us the look you want, cropped, mid-thigh, knee, or full-length, and we draft it to your height so the proportion suits your frame. If you are unsure, a length that ends around the mid-thigh or knee flatters most people. The test garment then lets you see the length on your body and change it before the final coat is cut, so you are never guessing.

### Will a fitted coat still look shaped, or will it end up boxy to fit the layers?

It stays shaped. We build in enough room for layers while still cutting a waist and a clean line, so the coat reads as tailored, not as a box. You choose how close or relaxed you want it, and the test garment lets you confirm that balance of room and shape on your own body.

### What if my size changes, or I want to wear it over heavier layers in future?

Coats carry generous seam allowances, so a local tailor can adjust the body within reason if your shape changes. And because we cut with layering in mind and keep your measurements on record, you can tell us up front if you plan to wear it over heavy knits, and we allow for it.

### The whole custom process feels intimidating. What actually happens?

It is simpler than it sounds. You choose the coat, cloth, and length and send your measurements; we make a free test garment and post it to you; you try it on over your layers and send notes and photos; we adjust and then cut and hand-tailor your final coat; and it ships to you with tracking. At no point are you committing to a finished coat you have not already seen fit on your body.
