## Questions About This Women’s Business Suit

### How long does a Super 160s wool business suit last if I wear it to work every week?

Worn two or three days a week with a day of rest between wears, a Super 160s wool suit typically gives five to eight years of regular office wear. The limits are usually the trouser seat and the jacket elbows, where fine fibers wear first, and a fused jacket front that bubbles after repeated dry cleaning. This suit has a canvas front, which does not delaminate, and ample seam allowances so it can be altered as your shape changes.

### Which measurement matters most for a women’s business suit?

The shoulder width and the bust matter most for the jacket, and the rise matters most for the trousers. A jacket that is too wide at the shoulder cannot be properly corrected later, and a wrong rise makes trousers pull or sag however well the waist fits. You enter every measurement on this page, the women’s body measuring guide shows where to take each one, and the free test suit catches anything that is slightly off before the wool is cut.

### Is this a made to measure suit, or does it come in standard sizes?

Neither: every suit is cut from a new pattern drawn from your own measurements, with no standard sizes and no stock pattern underneath. Made to measure usually means adjusting an existing size pattern, while this pattern starts from your numbers. That matters most in a fine wool like Super 160s, which shows any leftover fitting error that an adjusted stock pattern tends to leave behind.

### What is a tailor made women’s business suit?

A tailor made women’s business suit is a matching jacket and trousers cut individually to one woman’s measurements, in a business cloth and color, for professional settings. Unlike a ready-made suit, the jacket and trousers are not tied to one size, so each piece fits the part of the body it covers. This one is made in Super 160s Italian wool in seven business colors, with a free test suit to confirm the fit first.

### Can I get a custom fitted women’s suit without visiting a tailor?

Yes, you can order a custom fitted suit entirely from home by entering your measurements online and fitting a test suit in your own mirror. The test suit is a complete cotton version of your suit, cut on the same pattern as the final one. Your notes go into the pattern before the wool is cut, so the fitting happens without an appointment, wherever you live.

### What is the best color for a women’s business suit?

Charcoal or dark gray and navy are the most versatile colors for a women’s business suit, because they read as authoritative in any industry and pair with almost any shirt. Black is the most formal and doubles as evening wear, while steel gray and light gray feel lighter and work well as separates. All seven colors of this suit are the same price, so choose by where you will wear it most.

### What makes a women’s suit look expensive?

Fit makes a suit look expensive far more than price or label does: shoulders that end where yours end, a jacket that closes flat over the bust, and trousers that break cleanly on the shoe. After fit, the cloth and the construction show: fine wool drapes instead of creasing, and a canvas front keeps the chest smooth where a glued front ripples. Working sleeve buttons and hand-finished buttonholes are the details people notice up close.

### Is a finer wool always better for a business suit?

No, a finer wool such as Super 160s is softer and drapes better, but it is less hard-wearing than a coarser wool like Super 110s or 120s if worn every day. Fine wool is at its best in a rotation, with a day of rest between wears so the fibers recover their shape. If you need one suit for every working day, tell us before you order and we will be honest about whether this cloth suits your week.

Baron Boutique is an independent tailoring house based in Nepal, operating since 2000, with an office in Dubai, and suits are made in both Kathmandu and Dubai.
