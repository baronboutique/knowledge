## Custom, Bespoke and Tailored Suits for Women: What the Words Mean

| Off the rack | Sold in sizes; any tailored suit for women bought this way is altered after purchase, within the limits of the seams. |
| --- | --- |
| Custom made suits for women | Cut from a pattern adjusted to your measurements; how far the pattern changes depends on the maker. |
| Bespoke suit for women | A pattern drafted for one body, refined on a fitting garment before the cloth is cut, which is how this suit is made, with the fitting done at home on the cotton test suit. |
| Tailored fit | A description of shape, not of how the suit is made: a tailored suit can be ready-made. |

Whatever you call it, custom tailored women’s suits, bespoke women’s suits, ladies tailored suits or a tailor made suit for women, the test is the same: does the suit fit your body, or a size chart? Women’s custom suits made here are cut from your own measurements, refined on a cotton suit, and finished with a canvas front. Handmade details include the hand-basted canvas, hand-finished buttonholes and buttons sewn by hand.

In our experience, cheaper women’s suits fail in two ways: the cloth gives out in the weather, and the construction lets the front and shoulders slip out of shape. That is why women’s suits and tailoring are worth the fitting.

Women’s bespoke tailoring is not only about measurements. A tailored fit for women means the waist is shaped, the bust is placed and the shoulders follow your own line, so a modern women’s tailored suit looks feminine without decoration. Fitted tailored women’s suits like this one, fine tailored suits in general, and every handmade suit for women share that starting point. In womenswear, suits and tailoring have long borrowed from men: women in men’s suits made the shape famous, and here it is cut for a woman instead. ## Questions About the Women’s Business Suit

### What is Super 160s wool?

A very fine worsted wool: the higher the Super number, the finer the fiber. Super 160s feels smooth and soft and has a light sheen, and it is more delicate than an everyday Super 120s.

### Is a finer wool always better?

No. Finer wool feels softer and drapes more fluidly, but it wears faster at the elbows and seat. For a suit worn every day, a Super 120s to 130s is tougher; Super 160s is the choice for a suit worn in rotation and on important days.

### How long does a Super 160s suit last?

Many years, if it is rested. Wear it no more than two or three days a week, hang it on a shaped hanger after each wear, brush it instead of cleaning it, and dry clean only when it really needs it.

### Is this suit custom, bespoke or tailor made?

It is cut from your own measurements and refined on a cotton test suit before the cloth is cut, which is the principle of bespoke, with the fitting done at home. Tailor made and custom describe the same thing here: no stock sizes are used.

### Which measurements matter most for a business suit?

Shoulder width, bust and the waist-to-hip distance decide how the jacket sits, and the seat and thigh decide how the trousers hang. Take those carefully; small errors elsewhere are corrected on the test suit.

### Do I need a fitting near me?

No. The fitting happens at home on the cotton test suit: you try it on, send photos and notes, and we correct the pattern. Clients order this way from many countries.

### How do I style a tailored business suit?

Keep the top simple, a silk shell or a fine knit, and let the lapels and the cut lead. Black with a camisole works in the evening; navy or gray with a white shirt suits the boardroom.

### Can I change the lapel, vents or pockets?

Yes. Enter the change in the comment box of the measurement form, or email us with your order number. A notch lapel, a center vent or no ticket pocket are all possible.

### Can I order a skirt instead of trousers?

Ask through the Ask a question about this piece form below Add to Cart. We will confirm whether a skirt can be made in the cloth you choose.

### Can I see the Super 160s cloth before ordering?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples of about 2 by 4 inches. They usually arrive within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 2600013 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### Can I design my own suit?

Yes, within the cut of this suit: to design your own suit, women choose the cloth, lining and fit in the selector, then describe any change in the comment box of the measurement form. The test suit then shows how those choices look on you before the final cloth is cut.

### How long will the business suit take?

The cotton test suit takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. The business suit then takes 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after it ships.

### How do I care for it?

Brush it after wearing and rest it for a day on a shaped hanger. Dry clean only when it needs it.
