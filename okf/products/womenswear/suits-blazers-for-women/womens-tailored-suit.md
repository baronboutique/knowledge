### An Investment in Legendary Fabric: VBC Wool

A suit of this caliber begins with a world-class fabric. We use a premium wool that signals quality and is built to perform.

- **From the Legendary Vitale Barberis Canonico Mill:** We use a Super 160s’ worsted wool from VBC, one of the oldest and most respected mills in Italy (est. 1663). This fabric is renowned for its incredibly smooth finish, beautiful drape, and four-season comfort.
- **A Palette of 7 Power Neutrals:** Choose from a curated range of 7 sophisticated, versatile shades, including classic black, steel grey, and midnight blue.

### The Architecture of Authority

Every detail of this suit is engineered to create a silhouette of pure, unapologetic power.

- **The Power Jacket:** A single-breasted jacket featuring wide, elegant peak lapels and a full canvas construction—the hallmark of true bespoke tailoring. It is designed to mold to your body over time.
- **The Perfect Tapered Trouser:** Clean, flat-front trousers with a gentle taper create a modern, leg-lengthening line that is both professional and incredibly chic.
- **A Suit of Details:** From the functional “kissing” sleeve buttons to the traditional ticket pocket, every element is a nod to the art of high-end tailoring.

### Our Bespoke Fit Guarantee: The Free Test Suit

The difference between a suit and *your* suit is the fit. We eliminate all risk and sizing anxiety by sending you a **free test suit**—a complete cotton version of both the jacket and trousers—made to your initial measurements. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect every aspect of the silhouette, from the shoulder fit to the trouser break. It is the definitive solution to the ill-fitting women’s suit.
