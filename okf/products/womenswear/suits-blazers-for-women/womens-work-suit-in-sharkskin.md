## One Button Has to Do All the Work

A single-button jacket is held closed at one point, so any mismatch between the bust and the waist shows straight away: the front either strains at the button or opens above it. On a test suit we often move that button or adjust the bust shaping, and we keep the button where the front lies flat. The other correction we see often on women’s suits is in the trousers, where a hip that is fuller at the back than at the front needs room added behind and taken away in front.

When the right fix would change the look, such as an added dart in a clean front, we explain it and you choose: the fit that follows your body exactly, or the line in the photos exactly.

What Is Sharkskin

## What Is Sharkskin in Wool?

Sharkskin in wool is a weave, not a material. A sharkskin suit is made of worsted wool woven with alternating light and dark yarns, which builds a tiny stepped pattern on the face of the cloth. That pattern gives sharkskin wool its two-tone luster: the suit looks lighter or darker depending on the angle of the light.

| Made of | Here, 100% worsted wool in Super 120s: smooth, firm yarns spun from long fibers. |
| --- | --- |
| The look | Solid from a distance, textured close up, with a soft sheen rather than a shine. |
| Wear | A firm, tightly woven cloth that holds a crease in the trousers and resists bagging at the knee. |
| Season | A breathable worsted that wears comfortably year-round in heated and air-conditioned offices. |

Sharkskin suits were a fixture of 1960s menswear, which is why most pictures you will find are of men. The cloth itself has nothing masculine about it, and in a women’s cut it gives a plain work suit more depth than a flat worsted.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Women’s Sharkskin Suit

### What is a sharkskin suit, and what is it made of?

Sharkskin is the name of a weave, not an animal skin. Two contrasting yarns are woven together, so the cloth looks solid from a distance and shows a tiny two-tone pattern and a soft sheen up close. This suit is 100% worsted wool in Super 120s.

### Is sharkskin a good material for a work suit?

Yes. The tight worsted weave is hard-wearing, resists creasing through a day at a desk and recovers overnight on a hanger. That is why sharkskin has been a business cloth for decades.

### Are sharkskin suits still in style?

Yes. Because it reads as a solid color, sharkskin never dates the way a bold pattern does. A slim single-button cut keeps it modern.

### Is it shiny?

No. It has a subtle two-tone sheen that changes with the light, never a synthetic shine.

### Do women wear sharkskin suits?

Yes. The cloth suits any cut, and this suit is made for a woman’s body from your own measurements, not adapted from a men’s pattern.

### Is it right for a conservative office?

Yes. From across the room it reads as solid gray, blue or black; the texture only shows up close.

### Why does the jacket have only one button?

A single button at the natural waist marks the narrowest point and keeps the front long and clean. It needs an accurate bust and waist fit, which the test suit checks.

### Can I have a slim fit?

Yes. Choose slim in the selector for a close line, standard for a classic business fit or relaxed for more room. The test suit shows the result before the sharkskin is cut.

### Can I add back pockets to the trousers?

Yes. Mention it in the comment box of the measurement form, or email us with your order number. Back pockets, a ticket pocket or a different cuff can all be added this way.

### I have no tailor nearby. How do I measure?

Ask someone to help with the tape if you can. On your own, measure bust, natural waist, hips, trouser waist, thigh and ankle, then a jacket and trousers that fit you well; we can guide you by video call or WhatsApp.

### Can I see the sharkskin before I order?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples of about 2 by 4 inches. They usually arrive within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 2600008 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How long will the sharkskin suit take?

The cotton test suit is made in 3 to 4 weeks and reaches you 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Then the sharkskin suit is made in 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after it ships.

### How do I care for it?

Hang it on a shaped hanger after each wear and give it a day’s rest. Dry clean only when it needs it.
