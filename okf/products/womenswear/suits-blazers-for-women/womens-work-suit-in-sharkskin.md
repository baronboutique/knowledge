## Your New Secret Weapon for the Office

This is the suit for the woman who understands that true style is in the details. It’s the answer to the generic, forgettable work uniform. We’ve taken the classic, single-breasted power suit and crafted it from Sharkskin—a legendary fabric that signals a sophisticated, intelligent approach to professional dressing.

### The Subtle Power of Sharkskin Wool

This suit’s unique character comes from its exceptional fabric, chosen for its subtle visual texture and all-day performance.

- **A Unique Two-Tone Luster:** We use a Super 120s’ worsted wool woven in a “sharkskin” pattern. This technique uses two different colored yarns to create a fabric with a slight, two-tone sheen that has more depth and character than a standard flat wool.
- **Lightweight & Breathable:** This fabric is lightweight and breathable, making it comfortable for year-round wear and long days at the office.
- **A Palette of 7 Modern Colors:** Choose from a curated selection of 7 professional shades, including a sharp petrol blue and a classic slate grey.

### Engineered for a Commanding Silhouette

This suit is architected with clean, modern lines to create a look of pure, understated power.

- **The Lean, Modern Jacket:** A sharp, single-button jacket with a classic notch lapel creates a clean, elongating line, while a full canvas construction ensures it molds to your body over time.
- **The Perfect Tapered Trouser:** Clean, flat-front trousers with a gentle taper create a modern, leg-lengthening silhouette that is both professional and incredibly chic.

### Our Bespoke Fit Promise: The Final Calibration

A suit with this level of fabric sophistication demands a perfect fit. We guarantee a flawless silhouette by sending you a **free test suit**—a complete cotton version of both the jacket and trousers—made to your initial measurements. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect every aspect of the silhouette. It’s the final, crucial step in creating a garment that is truly built for you.
