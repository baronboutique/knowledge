### The Genius of Fresco Wool: A Natural Air Conditioner

A blazer this bold requires a fabric that can perform under pressure. Fresco is a legendary textile, engineered in England specifically for its resilience and comfort.

- **Unmatched Breathability:** Fresco is crafted from multiple strands of high-twist wool, woven into a plain, open weave. This creates a crisp, porous fabric that allows air to circulate freely, keeping you cool and comfortable.
- **Incredible Wrinkle Resistance:** The high-twist nature of the yarn makes Fresco naturally resilient to creasing, ensuring you look sharp and unrumpled from morning to night.
- **A Palette of 10 Power Colors:** While classic red is the ultimate statement, this blazer is available in 10 versatile shades, including timeless navy and charcoal.

### A Silhouette of Sharp Authority

This blazer is architected with clean, modern lines to create a look of pure, understated power.

- **The Lean, Modern Cut:** A sharp, single-button jacket with elegant peak lapels creates a clean, elongating line, while a full canvas construction ensures it molds to your body over time.
- **Bespoke Details:** From the hand-carved horn buttons to the functional “kissing” sleeve cuffs, every element is a nod to the art of high-end tailoring.

### Our Bespoke Fit Promise: The Final Calibration

A statement blazer demands a perfect fit to look powerful, not costume-like. We guarantee a flawless silhouette by sending you a **free test blazer** made to your initial measurements before we craft your final garment. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect every aspect of the silhouette. It’s the final, crucial step in creating a garment that is truly built for you.
