## A Statement Color Shows Every Fitting Error

The fitting points we check most on a women’s blazer are the shoulders, the back and the bust position. Shoulders are rarely the same front and back, or left and right, and a bust that does not follow the rest of the measurements pulls a one-button front out of line. What Is Fresco

## What Is a Fresco Wool Blazer?

Fresco is a plain, open weave of tightly twisted yarns. The twist makes the yarns springy and the open weave lets air through, so a fresco wool blazer feels crisp, wears cool for its weight and recovers well from creases after sitting or travel.

| 70% wool | Gives the cloth its natural drape, breathability and the depth of color in the red. |
| --- | --- |
| 30% polyester | Adds strength and crease recovery. The trade-off is honest: it breathes a little less than 100% wool. |
| Super 130s | A fine yarn grade, so the surface is smooth rather than coarse despite the open weave. |
| Seasons | Light enough for spring and summer offices, and over a knit it carries into autumn. |

We say red wool blend blazer rather than red wool blazer because that is what it is. In our experience, a cheaper blazer fails first in the cloth, which loses its color or its surface in the weather, and then in the construction, when the front and shoulders slip out of shape. A canvas front and a firm fresco are what keep this one in shape for years.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Women’s Red Blazer

### What do I wear with a red blazer?

Navy, black, gray or white let the red lead. For the office, wear it over a white or cream top with dark trousers; at the weekend, over a striped top and jeans; in the evening, over a black dress. One strong color is enough, so keep shoes and bag neutral.

### Is a red blazer appropriate for work?

Yes, in most offices. A clear red with a structured cut and dark trousers reads confident rather than loud. In a very conservative setting, keep it for presentations and days when you want to be remembered.

### Is this a pure wool blazer?

No. It is a Super 130s fresco of 70% wool and 30% polyester. The wool gives drape and breathability; the polyester adds strength and helps it recover from creases.

### What is fresco?

An open plain weave of high-twist yarns. It breathes well, feels crisp and springs back from creases, which suits a blazer worn on long days.

### Which red is it?

A clear, strong red, shown in the photos and in the close-up of the cloth. Screens vary, so if the exact shade matters, ask for a sample first.

### Can I change the buttons, lapels or pockets?

Yes. Describe the change in the comment box of the measurement form, or email us with the order number. Black buttons instead of brown horn, notch lapels or a second button are all possible.

### Can I order matching trousers or a skirt?

Ask through the Ask a question about this piece form below Add to Cart, and we will confirm what can be made to match. For a complete suit, see our **[women’s fresco wool suit](https://baronboutique.com/womens-fresco-wool-suit/)**.

### Is it warm enough for winter?

It is a light, breathable cloth. In winter, wear it over a fine knit, or choose a heavier wool blazer.

### My bust and shoulders do not match standard sizes. Will it fit?

That is common, and it is why a red blazer is unforgiving off the rack: a strong color shows every pull. This one is cut to your measurements and checked on a cotton test blazer first.

### I have no tailor nearby. How do I measure?

A helper makes it easier. On your own, measure bust, natural waist and hips, then a blazer that already fits you well; we can guide you by video call or WhatsApp.

### Can I see the red before I order?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples of about 2 by 4 inches. They usually arrive within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 2800002 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How long will the red blazer take?

We make the cotton test blazer in 3 to 4 weeks, and it arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. After you approve the fit, the red blazer takes 3 to 4 weeks more and arrives 5 to 10 days after it ships.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean rarely and hang it on a shaped hanger between wears. Keep a strong red out of long direct sunlight, which fades any dyed cloth.
