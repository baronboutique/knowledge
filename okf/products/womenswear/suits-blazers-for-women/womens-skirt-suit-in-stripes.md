## The Power Suit, Redefined

This is the modern answer to formal and professional dressing. A powerful silhouette that bridges the gap between the traditional suit and the classic dress. We’ve taken the iconic skirt suit and sharpened it for today, combining a commanding pinstripe with the precision of custom tailoring to create a look of pure, unapologetic authority.

### A Foundation of All-Season Wool

The heart of a true investment suit is its fabric. This suit is crafted from a versatile, medium-weight wool chosen for its beautiful drape and year-round wearability.

- **Super 120s’ Worsted Wool:** This premium wool is a perfect “all-season” weight, providing enough structure to look sharp while remaining comfortable in most climates.
- **The Authority of the Pinstripe:** Choose from 5 classic pinstripe combinations, including timeless charcoal with chalk stripes and sophisticated navy with dotted stripes, to make a powerful statement.

### A Study in Sharp, Feminine Tailoring

This suit is architected with strong lines and thoughtful details to create a silhouette that is both powerful and flattering.

- **The Commanding Jacket:** A single-breasted jacket featuring sharp peak lapels and a full canvas construction—the hallmark of high-end tailoring—designed to mold to your body over time.
- **The Perfect Pencil Skirt:** A clean, straight-cut pencil skirt with a tall, 2-inch waistband creates a powerful, elongating line, while a deep center-back vent ensures complete freedom of movement.
- **A Suit of Versatility:** While powerful together, both the jacket and skirt are designed to be worn as separate, versatile statement pieces, dramatically increasing the value of your investment.

### Our Bespoke Fit Promise: The Free Test Suit

The power of a suit lies entirely in its fit. We eliminate all risk and sizing anxiety by sending you a **free test suit**—a complete cotton version of both the jacket and skirt—made to your initial measurements. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect every aspect of the silhouette. It is the definitive solution to the ill-fitting skirt suit.
