### Our Bespoke Fit Promise

The power of a blazer lies entirely in its fit. We eliminate the risk of a boxy waist or a tight bust by sending you a **free test blazer** first.

1. **Receive Your Test Garment:** We mail you a test blazer made in cotton to your exact initial measurements.
2. **Confirm the Sculpted Fit:** This is your chance to perfect the fit through the shoulders, bust, and waist, ensuring the final garment provides a powerful, feminine silhouette.
3. **We Craft Your Masterpiece:** We apply your feedback to tailor the final merino wool blazer for a silhouette that is flawlessly, intentionally yours.

The test blazer is yours to keep.
