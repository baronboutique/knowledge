## A Fitted Blazer Is Only as Good as Its Button Point

A fitted blazer that is a size too small pulls into creases across the front; one that is a size too big hangs like a borrowed jacket. Between the two is a narrow band, and on a woman it depends on bust, waist and the distance between them, which is why we make this tailored fitted blazer for women in cotton first.

In more than 25 years of fittings, the length of a blazer is rarely the problem; the photos of the test blazer show that at a glance. What we adjust most on a fitted blazer is what a tape cannot fully describe: one shoulder sitting lower than the other, a back that needs more room than the chest, a bust point higher or lower than the pattern expects. Woven, Not Knit

## Woven Merino Blazer or Knit Sweater Blazer?

Both are merino. The difference is how the yarn is made into cloth, and it decides how the blazer behaves after a year.

| Knit sweater blazer | Soft and stretchy, often machine washable, easy to pack. The shoulders and lapels relax with wear, and the front can bag at the pockets. |
| --- | --- |
| Woven merino blazer (this one) | A smooth worsted cloth with a crisp hand. It holds a pressed lapel and a clean shoulder line, recovers from creasing overnight on a hanger, and is dry cleaned rather than washed. |
| Wool blend blazers for women | Usually wool mixed with polyester to cut cost. A women’s wool blend blazer is sold as the durable wool blend blazer for daily wear, yet the blend is what shines first at the elbows. This one is 100% wool and breathes better. |

If you want a soft unstructured wool blazer, women’s knitwear or an oversized wool blazer is the better buy. If you want a fitted navy blue wool blazer that women’s wardrobes can lean on for a decade, a tailored, woven cloth is the one that lasts.

## Why One Button, Peak Lapels and No Vent

Blazer side view

Front darts and a single button at the waist draw it in without pulling across the bust.

Blazer back

No vent, so the back falls in one clean line over trousers or a skirt.

| One button at the natural waist | It marks the narrowest point of the body, so the blazer reads as fitted even when worn open. The trade-off: a single button carries all the strain, which is why its placement is checked on the test blazer. |
| --- | --- |
| Peak lapels | The points lift the eye toward the shoulders and give a tailored blazer a feminine silhouette with some authority. A notch lapel is softer; ask for it in the comment box if you prefer. |
| No vent | A ventless back gives the cleanest line over the hips. A vent makes sitting and reaching easier; if you drive a lot or carry a bag on the shoulder, you can ask for a center vent. |
| Canvas front | A layer of canvas behind the front lets the chest and lapel roll keep their shape. Fused blazers, where the front is glued to a stiff interlining, tend to bubble or go limp after repeated cleaning. |

The ladies blazer back side view and the side profile above show the result: a shaped blazer, fitted through the waist, not a tight blazer, and long enough to cover the top of the hips. Feminine blazers often get their silhouette from stretch; this fitted blazer gets its feminine silhouette from the cut, so it looks tailored with any outfit. ## Questions About the Women’s Merino Wool Blazer

### Is this a knit merino blazer?

No. It is woven from Super 120s merino, so it keeps a tailored shape. Knit merino blazers are softer and stretchier but lose their structure sooner.

### Is it 100% wool or a wool blend?

It is 100% wool. Many wool blend blazers mix in polyester; this one does not, which keeps it breathable and less prone to shine.

### How should a fitted blazer fit?

The shoulder seam should end at your shoulder bone, the sleeves at the wrist bone, and the front should button flat without pulling into an X. The hem should cover the hip bone. Shoulders are the hardest part to alter later, so they are set first on the test blazer.

### Will it suit a curvy figure?

Yes, because it is cut to your measurements and checked on a test blazer. Bust and waist are set separately, so the front lies flat instead of pulling.

### Which color should I choose?

Navy and charcoal are the most versatile; black suits evening; cobalt, olive or chocolate brown make the blazer the statement over jeans or neutral trousers. If you are torn between two, order samples and compare them in daylight.

### What does it look like after years of wear?

Woven merino with a canvas front ages slowly. Brush it after wearing, rest it on a shaped hanger between days, and the lapels and shoulders keep their line; the usual first sign of age is a soft polish at the elbows.

### How does it travel?

Fold it inside out along the shoulders and hang it up when you arrive. Fine worsted merino lets most creases drop out overnight; steam the rest.

### Can I wear it with jeans?

Yes. Navy, chocolate brown or cobalt over dark denim is one of the easiest ways to wear it, as in the photos.

### Can I order it as a gift?

Yes, but we need her measurements, so the measuring guide on the product page is the place to start. The free test blazer then confirms the fit before the merino is cut.

### Can I ask for a notch lapel, a vent or a different length?

Yes. Enter the length on the measurement form and write any style change in its comment box, or email us with your order number.

### Can I see the merino colors first?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples of about 2 by 4 inches. They usually arrive within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 2800006 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How long will the merino blazer take?

Three to four weeks for the cotton test blazer, which arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Then three to four weeks more for the merino blazer, with the same delivery time.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean only, and sparingly. Brush the cloth after each wear and let it rest a day before wearing it again.
