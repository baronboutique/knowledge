## The Blazer, Upgraded with a Designer’s Edge

This is the antidote to the boring office uniform. A blazer designed for the woman who appreciates classic tailoring but demands a touch of modern, high-fashion detail. We’ve taken the essential work blazer and elevated it with the subtle luxury of a leather collar and the unique texture of sharkskin wool, creating a piece that is both powerful and full of personality.

### A Fabric with Intelligence and Texture

This blazer’s unique character comes from its exceptional fabric, chosen for its subtle visual interest and all-day performance.

- **The Sharkskin Difference:** We use a Super 120s’ worsted wool woven in a “sharkskin” pattern. This technique uses two different colored yarns to create a fabric with a slight, two-tone luster that has more depth and character than a standard flat wool.
- **A Palette of 7 Modern Colors:** Choose from a curated selection of 7 professional shades, including a sharp petrol blue and a classic slate grey.

### Defined by a Luxurious, Unexpected Detail

This blazer is engineered with clean lines, but its true genius lies in a single, powerful detail.

- **The Leather-Trimmed Collar & Pockets:** The star of the show. The classic notch lapel collar and the flaps of the hip pockets are trimmed in genuine leather, adding a touch of edgy, luxurious texture that sets this blazer apart.
- **A Lean, Modern Silhouette:** A sharp, single-button jacket with a low stance closure and a full canvas construction creates a clean, elongating line that is both modern and incredibly chic.

### Our Bespoke Fit Promise: The Final Calibration

A blazer with such unique details demands a perfect fit. We guarantee a flawless silhouette by sending you a **free test blazer** made to your initial measurements before we craft your final garment. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect the fit through the shoulders and waist, ensuring the final jacket is a true reflection of both your style and your shape.
