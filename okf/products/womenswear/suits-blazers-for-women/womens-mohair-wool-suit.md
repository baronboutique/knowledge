### The Mohair Advantage: A Smarter Fabric

The true innovation of this suit lies in its intelligent fabric, chosen for its unique combination of resilience and beauty.

- **Natural Wrinkle Resistance:** We use a Super 140s’ mohair and wool blend. Mohair fibers are famously resilient, allowing the fabric to naturally shed wrinkles and resist creasing, making it the perfect choice for travel and long workdays.
- **A Subtle, Professional Luster:** Unlike flat, standard wools, mohair has a natural, subtle sheen that catches the light beautifully, giving the fabric a rich, dynamic appearance that signals quality.
- **A Palette of 6 Essential Colors:** Choose from a curated selection of 6 versatile shades, including classic light grey, navy, and a sharp teal.

### Timeless, Uncompromising Tailoring

This suit is architected with the classic details of bespoke tailoring, ensuring a look that is both powerful and eternally stylish.

- **The Classic Single-Breasted Jacket:** Features a clean, two-button front, classic notch lapels, and a full canvas construction—the hallmark of high-end tailoring—designed to mold to your body over time.
- **The Perfect Straight-Leg Trouser:** Clean, flat-front trousers with a classic straight-leg cut create a timeless, leg-lengthening line that is always office-appropriate.

### Our Bespoke Fit Promise: The Final Calibration

A high-performance suit requires a perfect fit to perform optimally. We guarantee a flawless silhouette by sending you a **free test suit**—a complete cotton version of both the jacket and trousers—made to your initial measurements. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect every aspect of the silhouette. It’s the final, crucial step in creating a garment that is truly built for you.
