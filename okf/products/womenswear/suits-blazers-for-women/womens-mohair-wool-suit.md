## A Crisp Cloth Needs an Accurate Fit

Mohair holds its shape, which is its strength and also its demand: a crisp cloth does not settle into a body the way a soft flannel does. Any tension across the shoulders or back stays visible, so the shoulders, back and bust position are the points we look at hardest on the test suit, along with buttons placed so the front never gapes.

## Questions About the Women’s Mohair Suit

### What is a mohair suit?

A suit woven from mohair, the hair of the Angora goat, blended with wool. Pure mohair would be too stiff to tailor, so it is always blended. The result is crisp, cool to wear, resists creases and has a soft natural luster.

### Is mohair better than wool for a suit?

Different, rather than better. A mohair blend is crisper, cooler and more crease resistant than pure wool, and it has a sheen; pure merino is softer and more forgiving. For warm weather and evenings, mohair is the stronger choice.

### Is mohair itchy?

A mohair and wool suiting is smooth, but it feels crisper than pure merino. The jacket is fully lined, and if you are sensitive, ask for the trouser lining to run to the hem instead of the knee.

### Are mohair suits expensive?

Mohair is a scarcer fiber than wool, so mohair cloth usually costs more. This suit is $699 for jacket and trousers cut to your measurements, including the free cotton test suit.

### Does it wrinkle?

Very little. The springy fiber recovers after sitting or travel; hang it overnight and most creases drop out.

### Is it a summer suit?

It wears cooler than most wool suits and works from spring to fall. In winter, layer a fine knit underneath.

### Does it shine?

It has a soft luster, never a synthetic shine. The luster is stronger in darker colors and under evening light.

### Do you make mohair suits for women?

Yes. This one is cut for a woman’s body from your own measurements, and the bust, waist and hips are fitted on the test suit.

### Can I change the trouser leg or length?

Yes. Enter the length on the measurement form and write changes such as a wider or tapered leg in its comment box, or email us with your order number.

### Can I order only the jacket?

Ask through the Ask a question about this piece form below Add to Cart. We will tell you what is possible for the color you want.

### Can I see the mohair cloth before ordering?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples of about 2 by 4 inches. They usually arrive within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 2600012 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How long will the mohair suit take?

The cotton test suit is made in 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. After your approval, the mohair suit takes 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after it ships.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean the jacket rarely and brush it after wearing. Let it rest on a shaped hanger between wears.
