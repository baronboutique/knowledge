Fitting a Sheath

## Why a Sheath Dress Is the Hardest Piece to Buy Ready-Made

A sheath dress shows body structure more honestly than any jacket. When the bust does not follow the waist and hip measurements, or the hip is fuller at the back than at the front, a ready-made dress either pulls across the front or bunches below the seat. These are the corrections we make most often on a test dress, usually with an extra dart or a little more room where the body needs it. If the style cannot take another dart, we explain the trade-off and you choose between keeping the exact look and getting the exact fit.

If no tailor is near you, measure the bust, natural waist and hips yourself, then measure a dress that already fits you well, laid flat; we guide you through it by video call or WhatsApp.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Women’s Wool Flannel Suit Dress

### What is a women’s suit dress?

A suit dress is a matching jacket and dress cut from the same cloth and worn together like a suit. The dress is usually a knee-length sheath with a hidden back zip, and the jacket a tailored blazer. Here both are custom made in wool flannel, the dress a sleeveless sheath and the jacket a two-button blazer.

### Is a suit dress as formal as a pant suit?

Yes. With the jacket closed it carries the same weight at an interview or a board meeting, and it often looks more polished in conservative offices. The advantage is that the dress also works alone in the evening.

### Why wool flannel rather than stretch crepe?

Flannel is soft, matte and warm, and it drapes over the body without clinging. Stretch crepe and ponte suit dresses are easier to sell in sizes, but they pull at the hip and lose their shape after a season of wear.

### Is it warm enough for winter and cool enough for summer?

It is made for the cooler months and for air-conditioned offices. For summer heat, a tropical wool suit is the cooler choice.

### Can I wear the jacket and the dress separately?

Yes. Both are finished as standalone pieces: the dress with a cardigan or a knit, and the jacket over trousers or a skirt. With no outside pockets the jacket stays slim and flat when worn alone.

### Is it suitable for work and interviews?

Yes. Gray, charcoal and black are the formal choices for work, interviews and meetings; burgundy reads warmer for ceremonies and dinners.

### What if my bust and hips are different sizes?

That is the normal case, and it is exactly where ready-made suit dresses fail, because the jacket and the dress are cut from one size. Here both pieces are cut to your measurements and checked together on the cotton test suit.

### Can I change the dress length or the neckline?

Yes. Enter the length on the measurement form and write other changes, such as sleeves or a higher neckline, in its comment box, or email us with your order number.

### Can I have it in a different cloth?

The flannel colors are in the selector. For another cloth, ask through the Ask a question about this piece form below Add to Cart.

### Can I see the flannel before ordering?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples of about 2 by 4 inches. They usually arrive within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 2600005 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How long will the suit dress take?

Your cotton test suit is cut and sewn in three to four weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after it ships. Once you approve the fit, the flannel suit dress takes another three to four weeks, again arriving 5 to 10 days after dispatch.

### How do I care for wool flannel?

Dry clean only, and not often. Brush the flannel after wearing to lift the nap, and hang the dress and the jacket separately so neither creases.
