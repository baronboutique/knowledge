## The Modern Woman’s Power Uniform

This is the strategic solution to the “what to wear” dilemma. It’s a single, cohesive statement of power, designed for the woman who needs to look impeccably put-together with zero effort. We’ve taken the authority of a suit and blended it with the elegance of a dress, creating the ultimate modern uniform for success.

### The Softness of Flannel, The Structure of Wool

A power suit should feel as good as it looks. The secret to this ensemble’s unique character is its unexpected, luxurious fabric.

- **Super 120s’ Wool Flannel:** Unlike stiffer wools, this high-quality flannel is renowned for its incredibly soft hand-feel and beautiful, fluid drape, providing warmth and comfort without sacrificing a sharp, professional line.
- **A Palette of 10 Essential Colors:** Choose from a curated selection of 10 versatile shades, including timeless grey and a rich, deep red, to build your power wardrobe.

### Two Pieces, One Powerful Statement

This suit is architected to be greater than the sum of its parts, while offering incredible versatility.

- **The Sculpted Jacket:** A clean, single-breasted jacket with a two-button closure and classic notch lapels is tailored with precise darts to create a flattering, feminine shape.
- **The Perfect Sheath Dress:** The sleeveless, V-neck sheath dress is a powerful piece on its own, designed with its own darting to skim the body beautifully.
- **Versatility, Built-In:** While designed as a cohesive suit, both the jacket and dress are powerful, standalone pieces that dramatically expand your wardrobe options.

### The Guarantee of a Flawless Silhouette

A suit this precise must fit perfectly to convey true authority. We guarantee a flawless result by sending you a **free test suit**—a complete cotton version of both the jacket and the dress—made to your initial measurements. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect the fit of the dress and ensure the jacket layers over it seamlessly. It is our commitment to a truly bespoke experience.
