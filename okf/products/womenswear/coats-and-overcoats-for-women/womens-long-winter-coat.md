## A Women’s Fitted Wool Coat That Is Fitted on You, Not the Hanger

Many coats sold as fitted are fitted on the hanger. On a body, one waist dart can only take in so much, so the cloth either pulls across the hips or hangs straight past the waist. This women’s fitted wool coat uses two parallel darts at each side of the waist instead of one, so the shaping is spread out and the coat follows you without strain.

It is a single-breasted, mid-thigh coat in soft wool flannel with notch lapels, a chest pocket and a long center vent, cut to your measurements with a canvas front and checked first in a free test coat, where you choose slim, standard or relaxed.

Two Darts

## Why a Fitted Wool Coat for Women Needs Two Darts

| One dart | All the shaping in one seam, so the cloth puckers at the point or pulls across the hip. |
| --- | --- |
| Two parallel darts | The shaping is shared between two seams, so the waist comes in smoothly and the hip stays flat. |
| Canvas front | Holds the chest and lapels in shape, so the fitted line lasts. |
| Minimal shoulder pads | Enough structure for a clean shoulder, without a boxy look. |

That is the difference in a tailored coat for women: the shape comes from the cut, not from a belt. If you searched for a fitted coat, a womens fitted coat or a tailor winter coat, this is the coat built around the waist.
