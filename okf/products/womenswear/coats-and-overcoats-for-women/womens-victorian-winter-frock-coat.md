## Where Timeless Elegance Meets Modern Adventure

This is the antidote to the ordinary winter coat. It is a statement of grace, a nod to a world of adventure, and a masterpiece of feminine tailoring. Inspired by one of history’s most imaginative heroes, we have crafted a frock coat that blends the sharp, flattering lines of Victorian design with a touch of modern, whimsical flair.

### A Study in Rich Burgundy and Black

The soul of this coat is its beautiful interplay of color and texture, designed for both warmth and visual impact.

- **Rich Burgundy Flannel Wool:** The body is crafted from a substantial, high-quality 100% flannel wool in a deep, complex burgundy that shifts beautifully in the light, projecting pure sophistication.
- **The Plush Faux Fur Collar:** A soft, contrasting black faux fur collar and lapels provide a luxurious, tactile element and frame the face beautifully, ensuring you stay warm in the most elegant way possible.

### The Power of a Flattering Silhouette

The secret to this frock coat’s breathtaking look is in its architecture, drawing from the best of historical tailoring to create a shape that is undeniably feminine and powerful.

- **The Victorian Cut:** We use elegant princess seams and a defined waist to create a sharp, sculpted fit through the torso, which then flows into a beautifully pleated skirt at the back, allowing for graceful movement.
- **The Braided Accent:** The coat’s most unique feature. Three horizontal rows of intricate black braiding provide a stunning, sartorial detail that is both decorative and authoritative, transforming the coat from a simple garment into a piece of wearable art.

### Our Commitment to a Perfect Silhouette

A coat with such a defined shape must fit flawlessly to achieve its intended elegance. We eliminate all sizing anxiety by sending you a **free test garment** made to your initial measurements before we craft your final masterpiece. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect the cinched waist and the elegant lines of the Victorian cut, ensuring your final coat looks as if it were sculpted just for you.

#### Caring For Your Coat

To preserve the beauty of the wool and faux fur, we recommend professional dry-cleaning only.
