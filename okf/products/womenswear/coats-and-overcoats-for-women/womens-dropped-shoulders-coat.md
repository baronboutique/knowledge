## A Women’s Oversized Coat That Stops Fighting Your Sweater

Tailored coats fight what you wear under them. A chunky knit gets crushed at the shoulders, a full sleeve bunches inside a narrow one, and bracelets and gloves disappear under the cuff. This coat stops the fight: dropped shoulders, a wide body, an open front with no buttons and 3/4 bell sleeves that stop above the wrist.

It is a women’s oversized wool cashmere coat in 95% wool and 5% cashmere of about 14 oz, unstructured and unlined so it drapes softly, with wide notch lapels and a back yoke. It is cut to your measurements and checked first in a free test coat, where you decide how oversized it should be.

## Oversized on Purpose, Not by Accident

An oversized coat that is simply too big looks borrowed. The difference is in the shoulder drop, the sleeve length and how much room the body has, and those are set in the cotton test coat, made to your measurements. Try it over your thickest knit and tell us whether you want more volume or less.

1

**Choose your cloth, then enter your measurements**  
 Pick your cloth color in the selector, then enter your measurements on the product page, with the length you want. Any style change goes in the comment box on the measurement form.

2

**We make your free test coat**  
 A test coat in cotton, cut to your measurements, takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch by trackable courier.

3

**You try it on at home**  
 Check the shoulders, the sleeve length, the amount of room and the length over the clothes you will wear underneath, and tell us any change, however small.

4

**We make the final coat**  
 We apply every change, then make the final coat in wool and cashmere in 3 to 4 weeks; it arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. The test coat is yours to keep.

## About Baron Boutique

Founded in Kathmandu, Nepal in 2000, Baron Boutique has spent over 25 years creating bespoke garments with a focus on artisanal craftsmanship, dramatic design, and collector-quality tailoring. From cinematic-inspired coats and formalwear to handcrafted cashmere pieces, every garment is made with precision, individuality, and attention to detail.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Women’s Oversized Wool Cashmere Coat

### What is a dropped shoulder coat?

A coat whose shoulder seam sits below the shoulder point, giving a relaxed line and room for knitwear underneath.

### How oversized is it?

As oversized as you want. The amount of room, the shoulder drop and the sleeve length are set in the test coat.

### Does it close?

It has an open front with no buttons, worn open or held closed. A button or tie can be requested in the comment box on the measurement form.

### Is it warm without a lining?

Yes for cool weather, because the wool and cashmere is about 14 oz. To ask about adding a lining, write it in the comment box on the measurement form.

### Why 3/4 sleeves?

They leave room for a full sleeve underneath and show a watch, bracelets or long gloves. Full-length sleeves can be requested.

### What colors does it come in?

Camel as photographed, and shades such as black, navy and coffee brown; the selector shows the full range.

### How does the free test coat work?

We make the coat in cotton to your measurements, you try it over your thickest knit and tell us any change, and then we cut the wool and cashmere. The test coat is yours to keep.

### How long does it take?

The test coat takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. The final coat takes another 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch, so order as early as you can before you need it.

### Can I change details, such as the length?

Yes, enter the length you want on the measurement form and write any other change in its comment box, such as sleeve length or pocket style. For anything else, use the Ask a question about this piece form below Add to Cart.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean only, and not often. Hang it on a wide hanger so the dropped shoulders keep their shape.
