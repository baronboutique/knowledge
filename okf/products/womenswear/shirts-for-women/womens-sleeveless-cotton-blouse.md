The Shape

## Princess Seams, a Waist Seam and a Tailed Hem

Princess seams are long shaped seams that run from the armhole over the bust and down to the hem, front and back. Unlike darts, which pinch out cloth at one point, princess seams shape the whole panel, so the blouse follows the curve of the body in one smooth line. That is what makes a tailored sleeveless blouse look sculpted rather than loose.

| Princess seams | Front and back; shape the bust and waist in one line |
| --- | --- |
| Waist seam | Flat-felled across the waist, for a defined, slightly vintage silhouette |
| Tailed hem | Long and curved, longer at the back; worn out, it covers the hips |
| Collar | Classic business collar on a French front |

The side view shows the shape most clearly: the collar stands neatly at the neck, the waist seam marks the narrowest point, and the tailed hem drops lower at the back. A collared sleeveless blouse with this line looks deliberate worn out over jeans, which is how it is shown here.

From behind, the back princess seams and the curve of the tail hem do the work; there is no yoke, so the back is one clean shaped panel.

Not when they are cut to you. The armhole is set from your measurements and checked on the free test blouse, with your arms raised, before the final blouse is made.

### What are princess seams?

Long shaped seams running from the armhole over the bust to the hem. They shape the whole front and back panel, so a blouse follows the body without darts.

### Can I add sleeves?

Yes. Add short or long sleeves with the free customizer on the product page.

In most offices, yes, with a jacket or cardigan to hand. The business collar keeps it looking like a shirt.
