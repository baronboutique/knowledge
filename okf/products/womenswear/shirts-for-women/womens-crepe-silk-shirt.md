Why Crepe Silk

## Why a Silk Crepe Blouse, and Not Satin

Silk is woven many ways, and the weave decides how a silk women’s blouse looks and lives.

| Crepe silk | Twisted yarns, matte grainy surface, soft drape; hides small creases; this blouse |
| --- | --- |
| Charmeuse or satin | High shine on the face, slippery and fluid; shows creases and water marks |
| Crepe de chine | A lighter crepe, finer and more sheer |
| Polyester crepe | Looks similar, but does not breathe like silk |

For a pure silk blouse to wear to work, crepe is the practical choice. It breathes in heat and keeps you warm in air conditioning, the matte face reads as tailored rather than evening, and the natural give means a fitted blouse moves without straining at the seams. As a white silk blouse women can wear under a dark suit, it looks crisp without the glare of satin.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Crepe Silk Blouse

### What is crepe silk?

Silk woven from tightly twisted yarns, which gives it a fine, grainy, matte surface and a soft drape. It creases less and shines less than satin silk.

### Is a crepe silk blouse good for work?

Yes. The matte surface looks tailored rather than evening, the cloth breathes, and it hides small creases through a long day.

### Is it see-through?

Crepe silk is more opaque than crepe de chine or chiffon. In white, a skin-tone camisole or bra underneath is the safest choice, as with any light silk.

### Can I have long sleeves?

Yes, long sleeves are available on request; note it in the comment box on the measurement form.

### How do I care for silk?

Dry cleaning is recommended. If you need to press it, use a low setting on the reverse side; crepe silk irons easily.

### Why does it cost more than your cotton shirts?

Because the cloth is pure silk, which costs more to buy and takes more care to cut and sew; the fit process, with a free test blouse, is the same.

### What do the pockets and pleat add?

The flap pockets give it the look of a utility shirt in a soft cloth, and the center back pleat adds room to reach and move.

### Is the test blouse free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test blouse is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final blouse takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
