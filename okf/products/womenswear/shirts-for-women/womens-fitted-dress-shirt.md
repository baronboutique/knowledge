## What Is a Dress Shirt for Women?

What is a dress shirt for women? It is a shirt built like a man’s dress shirt, with a structured collar, a full button front, a yoke across the shoulders and buttoned cuffs, but cut for a woman’s body. That last part is where most fail. A men’s dress shirt for women, simply sized down, fits the shoulders and gapes at the bust; a blouse drapes softly but has no collar stand to hold a tie or sit under a lapel.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Women’s Dress Shirts

### What is a dress shirt for women?

A shirt with a structured collar, full button front, yoke and buttoned cuffs, cut for a woman’s body. It holds its shape under a jacket, which a softer blouse does not.

### What is the difference between a dress shirt and a blouse?

A dress shirt has a collar stand, a button front and cuffs, and keeps its shape; a blouse is softer, often without a collar stand, and drapes. For suits and formal offices, a dress shirt looks sharper.

### Which colors should I start with?

White and light blue go under every suit; black works for evenings and creative offices. Pink and lavender soften charcoal and navy.

### Can I get a straighter, less fitted cut?

Yes. Most fitted dress shirts, women’s included, are shaped with darts; tell us on the measurement form how close you like the fit; the darts can be made shallower for a straighter cut, and the free test shirt shows the result.

### Is Egyptian cotton worth it for a work shirt?

Yes, if you wear it often. Long fibers make a smooth, soft cloth that stays that way through repeated washing, and this one has a natural stretch and irons easily.

### Can I have French cuffs or a different collar?

French and other cuffs are available on request. Describe any other change, such as a different collar or a pocket, in the comment box on the measurement form.

### Can I have it longer so it stays tucked?

Yes. Long dress shirts for women stay tucked better when you reach or sit; give the length you want on the measurement form and check it on the test shirt.

### How do I care for it?

Machine wash it cool with similar colors and hang it to dry; dry clean is recommended for a longer life. Iron while slightly damp.

### Is the test shirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test shirt is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final shirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
