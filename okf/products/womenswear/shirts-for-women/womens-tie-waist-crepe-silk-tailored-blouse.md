Crepe Silk

## Why Crepe Silk for a Shirt Dress

Crepe silk is woven from twisted yarns, so it has a matte, finely grained surface and falls in soft folds. That drape is what makes a silk crepe dress move well, and the matte face keeps it day-appropriate where satin would look like evening wear.

| Crepe silk | Matte, grainy, soft drape; this dress |
| --- | --- |
| Dupioni silk | Crisp and slubbed with a sheen; holds a stiff shape, used for suits more than shirt dresses |
| Satin silk | High shine and slippery; more formal, shows creases |

Worn as a tunic, it is a crepe silk shirt women can wear over trousers. If you searched for a dupioni shirt dress, crepe is the softer alternative: dupioni stands away from the body, crepe follows it. Silk crepe clothing like this also has a natural give, so a belted dress is comfortable seated.

## Questions About the Silk Shirt Dress

### Is it a dress or a tunic?

Both. It is a shirt dress; cut to knee length it wears as a dress, and over trousers it wears as a long tunic, as in our photos. Tell us the length you want.

### What is a crepe silk dress like to wear?

Soft and fluid, with a matte surface that does not cling. Crepe silk breathes, has a natural give, and hides small creases, so it stays neat through a day.

### How do I style a silk shirt dress?

Belt it and add loafers for day, wear it open over trousers for a relaxed look, or belt it tighter with heels for evening. One-color outfits look longest.

### Is this dupioni silk?

No, it is crepe silk, which is soft and matte. Dupioni is crisp and slubbed with a sheen, better suited to structured jackets.

### Can I wear it without the belt?

Yes. Because the dress is cut to your measurements, it hangs neatly unbelted; the belt is there to mark the waist when you want it.

### How do I care for it?

Dry cleaning is recommended for silk. If it needs a press, use a low heat on the reverse side; crepe silk irons easily.

### Can I have short sleeves or a different collar?

Describe the change in the comment box on the measurement form, and we will confirm what we can do before the test dress is made.

### Is the test dress free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test dress is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final dress takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
