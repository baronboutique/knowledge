The Ruffle

## The Ruffle Collar, From Ruff to Modern Shirt

Ruffled necklines have a long history in tailoring: the stiff ruff of the sixteenth century, the lace jabot worn at the throat for the next two hundred years, and the ruffled collars of the late Victorian era. What they share is the job of drawing the eye to the face. A modern ruffle collar does the same thing with far less fabric.

| At the back | The ruffle stands up around the neck: a high ruffled collar, like a soft stand collar |
| --- | --- |
| At the front | A ruffle neck runs down either side of the opening, so this white shirt with ruffles in front frames a V rather than closing at the throat |
| At the cuffs | A matching ruffle at the wrist, making it a white shirt with ruffled cuffs, closed with a single button through a notched cuff |
| Changes | A smaller or larger ruffle, or plain cuffs, on request |

Ruffle collar or frill collar? The words describe the same thing: a strip of cloth gathered so it ripples. Some call this a white frilly shirt, a frilly collar shirt, a frilled shirt or a frilly dress shirt; the older ruff collar shirt wore a separate, starched ruff. On this ruffled collared shirt the ruffle is cut from the same Egyptian cotton as the body, so it holds its shape and washes with the shirt. A white ruffle neck shirt draws the eye up to the face, which is the whole point.

Is it a ruffle button down shirt or a blouse? It buttons all the way down, so it is a ruffle collar button down shirt, and the buttons sit under a hidden placket: a ruffle neck button down shirt with nothing to break the line. On many a white ruffle button up shirt the buttons are on show; a button up shirt with ruffles reads calmer when they are hidden. For women, ruffle collar single-breasted shirt styles like this one sit between the blouse and the classic shirt: one row of buttons, edge to edge, and the ruffle does the decorating.

Types of shirt collars for ladies fall into a few families, and knowing them helps when you compare women collared shirts:

| Point collar | The classic office collar; the most formal |
| --- | --- |
| Button-down | Collar points buttoned to the shirt; relaxed, preppy |
| Band or stand | A collar that stands up with no fold; clean and modern |
| Ruffle collar | Gathered cloth at the neck; the most decorative, as here |

A white collar shirt women wear to work is usually a point collar. The ruffle collar is the softer choice among shirts with collars for women, and still a professional shirt women can wear under a suit. Unlike a high ruffle collar blouse that closes at the throat, this one opens into a V: a ruffle collar blouse women can wear with a suit without looking buttoned up.

From behind you see why it is a high collar shirt as much as a ruffle shirt: the ruffle sits close at the nape, the back darts curve the waist, and the curved hem falls evenly.

## Questions About the Ruffle Collar Shirt

### How do you wear a ruffle collar shirt without looking costumed?

Keep everything else plain. Wear it under a dark tailored jacket or with simple trousers, keep jewelry small, and let the ruffle be the only detail.

### Can I have a smaller ruffle, or no ruffle on the cuffs?

Yes. The size of the ruffle and the cuff ruffles can be changed on request; note it on the measurement form, and check the proportion on your free test shirt.

### Is this a white ruffled blouse women can wear to work, or a shirt?

It is a shirt, tailored with darts and a hidden placket, so it reads polished under a suit jacket. It sits between a white collared shirt, women’s tailoring at its most formal, and a white women’s blouse with collar ties, which reads softer.

### Will a white shirt with ruffles on sleeves fit under a jacket?

Yes. On a white long sleeve shirt with ruffles, ask for the shirt sleeve a little longer than your jacket sleeve so the ruffle shows just past the jacket cuff without bunching.

### What is the difference between a ruffle and a frill collar?

There is no real difference; both are a strip of cloth gathered so it ripples. Ruffle is the more common word in the US, frill in the UK.

### Why Egyptian cotton for a ruffle shirt?

Its long fibers make a smooth, crisp cloth that holds a ruffle’s shape. A thin, soft cotton lets a ruffle go limp; a stiff one makes it look fussy. This one also has a natural stretch and irons easily.

### How do I iron the ruffles?

Iron the ruffle while it is slightly damp, working from the stitched edge out to the free edge with the tip of the iron, then the body of the shirt.

### Does it have a chest pocket?

No, to keep the front clean; you can add one with the free customizer.

### How do I care for it?

It is machine washable; dry clean is recommended for a longer life. Wash white with white so it stays bright.

### Is the test shirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test shirt is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final shirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
