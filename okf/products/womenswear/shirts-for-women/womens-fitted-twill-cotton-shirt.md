## A Free Test Shirt to Set the Fit

Before we cut the twill, you get the shirt in cotton: made from your measurements, sent free, and yours to keep.

A fitted shirt has less room for error than a relaxed one, so the test shirt is where we find the right amount of ease. Do the pinch test: with the shirt buttoned and tucked, pinch the cloth at your side at the waist. How Fitted

## Fitted Shirts for Women, and How Fitted Is Fitted

Fitted, slim and classic are three degrees of the same shirt. The difference is ease: the extra room between your body and the cloth.

| Fitted | Follows the waist closely with front and back darts; tucks without billowing; this shirt |
| --- | --- |
| Slim | Narrow overall but straighter, with less shaping at the waist |
| Classic | Straight from bust to hem; comfortable, but blouses out when tucked |

A tailored button down shirt women wear fitted needs one more thing a men’s shirt does not: room at the bust before the waist narrows. That is what the front darts do. Without them a fitted dress shirt women buy off the rack is either tight at the chest or baggy below it. With them, it joins the fitted collared shirts women actually want, tailored from every side: a fitted business shirt women can wear all day, and a fitted long sleeve shirt, women’s cut, that does not ride up when you move.

The fitted shirt back is where the work shows: two back darts curve the waist and the one-piece yoke keeps the shoulders smooth. Seen from behind, a fitted shirt should have no horizontal pull lines and no extra cloth collecting above the waistband.

The Cutaway Collar

## The Cutaway Collar

A cutaway collar has points that spread wide, cut away from the center, so the collar frames the neck rather than pointing down the front. It is the more modern of the classic collars, and it suits a fitted shirt because both lines are clean and close. On a fitted collar shirt women want the collar as neat as the body, which is why the cutaway is used here.

| Worn open | The collar lies flat and wide, opening the neckline as in our photo |
| --- | --- |
| Buttoned | Sits neatly, without the points pushing out from under a jacket |
| Faces | A wide spread balances a narrow face or a long neck |

The Cloths

## Twill Cotton in Eight Colors

Twill is woven so the threads step over one another on a diagonal, giving a fine slanted rib. That weave is why the cloth has a natural stretch on the bias, which a fitted shirt needs, and why it irons easily and holds a crisp line. It is denser than a plain poplin, so light colors such as light blue are less sheer.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Fitted Shirt

### How fitted should a women’s shirt be?

Close enough that about an inch of cloth pinches at the side of the waist, with no pulling across the bust or back. Less than that is too tight; two inches or more is a classic, not fitted, cut. That applies to any fitted button shirt women wear daily.

### Will a women’s fitted button down gape at the bust?

Not when it is cut to your bust measurement and shaped with front darts. The free test shirt lets us check the front while you sit and reach before the twill is cut, whether you call it a women’s fitted button up or button down.

### What is a cutaway collar?

A collar with widely spread points that frame the neck. It looks modern, lies flat when worn open, and sits neatly under a jacket.

### Is light blue twill see-through?

Less than poplin. Twill is a denser weave, so pale colors like light blue stay opaque under normal light.

### Can I make it less fitted, or change the cuff?

Yes. Say on the measurement form how close you want the fit, and ask for French or other cuffs; both can be set before the final shirt is made. Long fitted shirts for women stay tucked better, so give the length you want too.

### Does twill cotton stretch?

It has a natural stretch along its diagonal weave, enough to move with you when you reach; it is not an elastic stretch.

### How do I care for it?

Machine wash cool and hang to dry, or dry clean for a longer life. Twill irons easily; press it while slightly damp.

### Is the test shirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test shirt is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final shirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
