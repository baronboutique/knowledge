A fitted shirt must fit perfectly to be flattering. We eliminate the risk of gaping at the bust or a boxy waist by sending you a **free test shirt** first.

2. **Confirm the Fit:** This is your chance to perfect the fit through the bust and waist, ensuring the darts provide the perfect sculpted look without being restrictive.
