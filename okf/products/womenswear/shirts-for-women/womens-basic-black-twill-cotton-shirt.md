Why Twill

## Why a Twill Black Shirt Looks Better Than Poplin

Many black dress shirts for women are cut from poplin, a plain weave that is smooth and light. This shirt is twill: the yarns cross in a diagonal rib, which packs them more closely. You can see the difference in four ways.

| Opacity | Denser than poplin, so a black cotton shirt in twill is less likely to show through under office lights |
| --- | --- |
| Give | The diagonal weave has a natural stretch, so the shirt moves with you when you reach or sit |
| Creasing | Twill hides small creases better and is easy to iron |
| Surface | A soft, slightly matte sheen that keeps black looking deep rather than flat |

That is why a twill shirt reads as a black professional shirt women can wear to any meeting, rather than a basic black shirt women replace every year: the same color, but a cloth with depth. It is a tailored cotton shirt women can wear daily, and a black cotton button up shirt women can iron easily.

Keeping Black Black

## How to Keep a Black Shirt From Fading

Black cotton fades for three reasons: heat, friction and sunlight. You can slow all three.

| Wash | Inside out, cold, on a gentle cycle, with dark colors only |
| --- | --- |
| Dry | Hang to dry out of direct sun; avoid a hot dryer |
| Iron | On the reverse side, while slightly damp; twill irons easily |
| Longest life | Dry clean is recommended when you want the black to stay deepest |

Machine washing is fine; dry cleaning simply keeps black twill darker for longer.

## Questions About the Black Collar Shirt

### How do I stop a black button shirt women wear from gaping at the bust?

Buy it cut to your bust measurement, not one size up. This shirt is made from your measurements and checked on a free test shirt, and darts shape the waist so the buttons sit flat without strain.

### Is a black twill shirt see-through?

Much less than a poplin shirt. Twill is woven more densely, so a black cotton shirt in twill stays opaque under normal office light.

### Does a 100% cotton shirt stretch?

Twill has a natural stretch along its diagonal weave, so it gives a little when you reach or sit. It is not an elastic stretch; the give comes from the weave itself.

### How do I keep a black shirt from fading?

Wash it inside out in cold water with dark colors, hang it to dry out of the sun, and dry clean it when you want the deepest black. Heat and sunlight fade black cotton fastest.

### Should a black button down women wear to work be tucked in or worn out?

Both work because the hem is curved. Tuck it in for the office or under a jacket; wear it out over trousers or jeans for a relaxed look, as in our photos.

### Can I change the collar, cuff or add a pocket?

Yes. French or other cuffs are available on request, and you can describe other changes, such as a pocket or a different collar, in the comment box on the measurement form.

### What is a French front?

A French front has no raised placket strip; the button edge is folded under, so the front of the shirt is flat and clean. It reads more formal than a placket front.

### Is the test shirt free, and how long does it take?
