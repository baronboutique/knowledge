## The Icon, Perfectly Reinterpreted for You

The Oxford shirt is a legend for a reason. It’s the symbol of effortless, intellectual style. But for too long, women have had to settle for boxy, ill-fitting versions. This is the end of that compromise. We’ve taken the soul of the classic Oxford—its iconic fabric and timeless details—and re-engineered it with the precision of custom tailoring for a flawless, feminine fit.

### The Soul of the Matter: True Oxford Cotton

This shirt’s character comes from its unique and durable fabric. It’s a choice that values tradition and quality.

- **Authentic Oxford Weave:** Unlike standard shirting, Oxford cotton uses a basket-weave construction that gives it a distinctive, substantial texture. It’s renowned for being incredibly durable yet softening beautifully with every wash, creating a shirt that gets better with age.
- **A Modern, Versatile Palette:** While this chic pink is a standout, the shirt is available in 11 classic solids and stripes, giving you a foundation for countless looks.

### A Masterclass in Understated Detail

The beauty of an Oxford lies in its simple, perfectly executed details. It’s about getting the classics exactly right.

- **The Perfect Button-Down Collar:** The soul of the Oxford. Our collar is designed with the perfect “roll,” staying neatly in place and providing that timeless, polished look whether buttoned up or left open.
- **Sculpted with Darts:** We use subtle, precisely placed darts on the back to gently pull the fabric in, creating a beautiful shape at the waist that eliminates the boxiness of traditional Oxfords.

### Our Promise: A Flawless, Feminine Fit

A menswear-inspired classic must be perfectly tailored to flatter a woman’s form. We guarantee this by sending you a **free test shirt** made to your initial measurements before we craft your final Oxford. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect the fit through the bust and waist, ensuring the final shirt is sculpted perfectly for your body. It is the definitive solution to the ill-fitting “borrowed-from-the-boys” shirt.
