## Three Ways to Close the Gap for Good

Cutting the shirt to your measurements removes most of the strain. For extra security, choose one of these and write it in the comment box on the measurement form. Each one is made at the same price.

A Button at Your Bust Line

We space the buttons so one sits at the fullest point of your bust, where the pull is greatest. It is the quietest fix and keeps the classic look of an oxford button-down.

A Hidden Snap

A small snap is sewn inside the placket between two buttons at the bust. It holds the front closed when you move and cannot be seen from outside.

A Concealed Placket

A fold of cloth covers the buttons entirely, so even if the front moves, nothing opens to view. It gives the shirt a cleaner, more formal front.

## Oxford Cloth vs. Poplin: Which Shirt Fabric Is Right for You?

Oxford cloth is woven in a basket weave, with pairs of yarns crossing each other, which gives it a soft, visible texture and more body than smooth shirting. Poplin is woven flat and tight, so it looks crisper and dressier. The difference matters in how the shirt lives with you.

| | Poplin | Oxford Cloth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surface | Smooth, slight sheen | Soft basket-weave texture |
| Coverage | Thin, can show what is underneath | Thicker, rarely see-through |
| Creases | Every crease shows | Texture hides small creases |
| Over time | Stays crisp, can wear thin | Softens with each wash and lasts for years |
| Best for | Formal suits and evening | Office, weekends and travel |

Oxford cloth needs less pressing than poplin, but for a sharp look, iron it warm while still slightly damp, collar and cuffs first.

### How do I stop a button-down shirt from gaping at the bust?

The lasting fix is a shirt cut to your bust measurement, so the front is not pulled tight, with a button placed at the fullest point of your bust. For extra security, add a hidden snap between the buttons or choose a concealed placket. Write your choice in the comment box on the measurement form and we make it at no extra cost.

### Why do women’s shirts gape between the buttons?

Ready-made shirts are cut for a standard bust, so on many women the front is stretched across the fullest point. The buttons are spaced evenly from the collar, so the nearest one often sits above or below the bust, where it cannot hold the placket closed. Cutting to your bust and placing a button at your bust line solves both causes.

### What is the difference between an oxford shirt and a poplin shirt?

Oxford cloth has a basket weave that gives it a soft texture, more body and better coverage, and it hides small creases. Poplin is woven flat and tight, so it looks smoother and dressier but shows every crease. Oxford suits the office, weekends and travel; poplin suits formal suits and evening.

### How should a women’s oxford shirt fit?

The front should lie flat and closed when you reach forward, the shoulder seam should sit at the edge of your shoulder, and the cuff should reach the base of your thumb with your arm relaxed. The body should follow your waist without pulling at the buttons. Our women’s shirt fit guide shows each point.

### Does an oxford shirt need ironing?

Less than a poplin shirt, because the textured weave hides small creases, so many people wear it straight from the line for a relaxed look. For a sharp office look, iron it warm while slightly damp. The shirt is machine washable, and oxford cotton softens with each wash.
