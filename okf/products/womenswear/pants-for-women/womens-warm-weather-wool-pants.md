Why Most Skip Them

## Why Most Women’s Dress Pants Skip Back Pockets

It is not an accident. A back pocket only lies flat when the trouser fits the seat well. On ready-made pants cut for one standard body, a real pocket on a tighter seat pulls open and shows, so many brands leave it out, or sew it shut so it keeps its shape on the rail. It also saves sewing time on every pair.

When the trousers are cut to your seat and hip measurements, that problem goes away. The welt sits flat because the cloth around it is not under strain, and the pocket can be real. It is one of the quieter advantages of trousers made for one person rather than for a size.

## Questions About These Dress Pants with Back Pockets

### Do women’s dress pants have back pockets?

Many women’s dress pants have no back pockets, and many of those that appear to have them are stitched shut. These pants have two real single-welt back pockets that open fully, plus two slanted front pockets, so you have four usable pockets.

### Are the back pockets on these pants real?

Yes, both back pockets are real and open all the way. They hold a card, a key or a slim phone. Because the trousers are cut to your seat measurement, the pockets lie flat instead of pulling open.

### Can I have flap pockets on the back instead of welt pockets?

Yes, write “flap back pockets” in the comment box on the measurement form and we will make them with flaps, at the same price. A flap covers the opening and hides the outline of whatever is inside.

### Can I have buttoned back pockets?

Yes, write “buttoned back pockets” in the comment box on the measurement form and we will add a button closure to each back pocket, at the same price. It is the most secure option if you carry a card or key in a back pocket, because nothing slips out when you sit or travel.

### Why do so many women’s pants have fake pockets?

Ready-made pants are cut for one standard body, and a real pocket on a trouser that is tight at the seat pulls open and shows. Brands avoid that by leaving pockets out or stitching them shut, which also saves sewing time. When the trouser is cut to your own measurements, the pocket can be real and still lie flat.

### Do back pockets make dress pants look bulky?

Not when they are welt pockets on trousers that fit. A welt pocket is a slim, flat opening with no patch of extra cloth, so the back of the trouser stays smooth. Keep what you carry flat and light, such as a card or a key, and the pocket will not show.

### Are back pockets appropriate for formal or office trousers?

Yes, welt back pockets are a standard detail on tailored trousers and look entirely appropriate for the office, meetings and formal events. For the most formal look, choose the standard welt pocket; for more security, choose buttoned.

### Is mohair wool good for hot weather?

Yes, a lightweight mohair wool blend is one of the most comfortable cloths for warm weather. The open weave lets air through, and mohair resists creasing, so the trousers stay sharp through a long, warm day and after sitting.

A fine Super 140s mohair wool blend feels smooth rather than scratchy, and the front of these trousers is lined to the knee. If your skin is sensitive to wool, ask for the lining to be extended to the hem and it will be lined all the way down.
