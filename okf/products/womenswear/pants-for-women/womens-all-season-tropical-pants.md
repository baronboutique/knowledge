## What Tropical Wool Is, and Why It Suits a Relaxed Trouser

Tropical wool is not a special fiber but a lightweight worsted wool woven in an open plain weave. The small gaps in the weave let air pass through, which is why tailors have long used it for warm climates. At 9 oz it is still substantial enough to hang well, so these lightweight tropical wool trousers work in summer and in heated offices through the winter.

Breathable

The open weave and the easy cut work together: air moves around the leg instead of the cloth clinging to it.

Recovers from sitting

Wool has a natural spring, so creases from a day at a desk mostly drop out when the trousers hang overnight.

Super 140s, 100% wool

A fine, smooth worsted, lined at the front to just below the knee in the lining color you choose.

## Questions About These Relaxed Fit Trousers

### What is tropical wool?

A lightweight worsted wool woven in an open plain weave, so air passes through it easily. These trousers use 100% Super 140s tropical wool at 9 oz, light enough for warm days and heavy enough to hang cleanly.

### Are tropical wool trousers good for hot weather?

Yes. The open weave breathes, and wool handles moisture well, so they often feel cooler than a dense cotton or polyester work trouser. The relaxed cut helps too, because the cloth does not cling to the leg.

### Can I wear them all year?

Yes. 9 oz tropical wool is comfortable in summer and in heated offices in winter. In a cold climate, pair them with warmer layers above, or ask for a full lining for a little more warmth.

### What does relaxed fit mean for these trousers?

The waist is cut to your exact measurement, the seat, hip and thigh have extra room, and the leg falls straight from the knee. They are relaxed, not loose fit pants: nothing gapes at the waist or bunches at the ankle.

### How do I keep relaxed trousers from looking baggy?

Three things: a waist that fits, a length that just touches the shoe, and a pressed front crease. All three are set to you here, and you check them on the free test pair before your wool is cut.

### Can I choose how much room they have?

Yes. Tell us in the comment box how easy you like your trousers, then try the test pair and ask for more or less room through the seat and thigh. If you would rather have a closer, straighter fit, ask for that instead.

### Are these trousers lined?

The front of each leg is lined to just below the knee in the lining color you choose. If wool makes you itch, ask for a full lining to the hem at no extra cost, or ask for them unlined.

### Can I machine wash these wool trousers?

Yes. Wash them cold on a gentle wool cycle and hang them to dry, or dry clean them if you prefer. Between wears, hang them by the hem so any creases drop out.

### Which colors look most formal for work?

Navy, Charcoal Grey, Black and Dark Grey are the most formal. Light Grey, Medium Grey and Melange are softer gray work pants that suit warmer months, and Tan and Beige pair well with white or cream tops.

### How much do these relaxed fit trousers cost?

They are $239 in any of the 14 tropical wool colors, with any lining color. The price includes a free cotton test pair that is yours to keep, so the fit is confirmed before your wool is cut.

### How long does it take to receive my trousers, and do you ship internationally?

Your test pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Both ship worldwide with tracking, and if you need them by a date, order as early as you can.
