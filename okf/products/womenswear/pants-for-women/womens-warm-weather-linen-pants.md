## What Makes Linen Trousers Look Tailored

Linen has almost no stretch and no spring, so it shows every fitting mistake. Too tight and it pulls into creases across the lap; too loose and it collapses into folds. Three things keep these trousers looking intentional.

A straight leg

The leg keeps the same width from the knee to the hem, so the linen hangs in a clean column. It is narrower than a wide leg, so there is less cloth to crumple, and it takes a pressed crease well.

Real trouser details

A 1.5-inch waistband with five belt loops, a flat front, slanted front pockets and two single-welt back pockets. The inside of the waistband has a grip lining that keeps a shirt or blouse tucked in.

Cut to your measurements

We cut the waist, seat, thigh and length to your body, with enough ease to sit comfortably. Where linen is not strained, it creases softly instead of in hard lines.

Linen Wrinkles

## Will Linen Trousers Wrinkle? Yes, and Here Is How to Live With It

Every 100% linen wrinkles. It is the same property that makes it cool: the flax fiber is stiff and does not spring back. We will not promise wrinkle-free linen, but you can keep straight leg linen trousers looking neat through the day.

- A good fit matters most. Creases are softest where the cloth is not pulled tight, which is why the free test pair checks the seat and thigh when you sit.
- Textured shades hide creases more. Grey, Charcoal, Blue and Herringbone Natural have a mixed yarn that breaks up the lines.
- Hang the trousers by the waistband or hem as soon as you take them off; most sitting creases drop out overnight.
- Steam, or iron while the linen is still slightly damp, on the linen setting. A pressed front crease holds the straight line longer.

## Are White Linen Trousers See-Through?

Linen is woven with a little air between the yarns, which is why it is cool. In the lighter colors, that can mean some show-through in bright sun. You have two simple options.

| Dark and textured linens | Black, Charcoal, Dark Blue, Blue, Brown, Burgundy, Grey and Herringbone Natural. Wear them unlined, as standard. |
| --- | --- |
| Mid tones | Khaki, Natural, Soft Tan, Turquoise and Cornflower. Fine unlined for most people; choose skin-tone underwear. |
| Light linens | White, Off White, Light Blue, Lavender and Pink. If show-through concerns you, ask for the trousers lined to the knee or fully lined. |

To add lining, write “lined to the knee” or “fully lined” in the comment box on the measurement form. A lining makes the trousers slightly warmer, so for very hot weather, unlined with skin-tone underwear is the cooler choice.

### What are these straight leg linen trousers made of?

### How do I stop linen trousers from wrinkling?

You cannot stop 100% linen from creasing, but you can keep it neat. A fit that is not tight through the seat and thigh creases softly, textured shades like Grey and Charcoal hide creases, and hanging the trousers straight after wearing lets most creases drop out. Steam or iron while slightly damp.

### Are white linen trousers see-through?

Light linens such as White, Off White, Light Blue, Lavender and Pink can show through in bright sun. Ask for them lined to the knee or fully lined in the comment box, or choose skin-tone underwear if you prefer them unlined.

### Do you make brown linen trousers?

Yes. Brown is a warm tobacco brown, and Soft Tan, Khaki and Natural give you lighter brown and beige options. All are $239 in the same straight leg cut.

### What is the difference between straight leg and wide leg linen trousers?

A straight leg keeps the same width from the knee to the hem, so it looks more tailored and has less cloth to crease. A wide leg is fuller and moves more in a breeze. If you want a wider leg in this cut, write it in the comment box.

### Can I machine wash linen trousers?

Yes. Wash cold on a gentle cycle and hang to dry, or dry clean if you prefer. Avoid the tumble dryer, which sets creases and can shrink linen.

### Can I choose the rise and length?

Yes. The rise and length come from your measurements, and you check both on the free test pair. Tell us the shoes you plan to wear, flats or heels, so we can set the hem length.

### Do these linen trousers have pockets?
