## Questions About These Formal Trousers

### What makes these formal pants rather than everyday trousers?

The cut and the cloth: a straight leg with a pressed crease, a buttoned waistband with belt loops, and a fine Super 130s merino and cashmere suiting. In Black or Charcoal they are formal enough for evening events.

### What does menswear-inspired mean?

The details come from a man’s suit trouser: a straight leg, a crease, a buttoned waistband and a suiting cloth. The pattern itself is drawn for a woman, with the waist, seat and hip cut to your measurements.

### What fabric are these trousers made of?

A Super 130s cloth of 98% fine merino wool and 2% cashmere. It drapes cleanly, holds a crease and feels softer than plain suiting.

### Does 2% cashmere make a difference?

A small one. The merino gives the trousers their drape and durability; the cashmere softens the hand and adds a faint luster. These are wool trousers with a cashmere touch, not cashmere trousers.

### What is the difference between formal pants and dress pants?

The terms overlap. Dress pants usually means any tailored trouser for the office; formal pants are the dressier end, in a darker, finer cloth with a sharper line, suited to ceremonies and evening events as well as work.

### What should I wear with formal pants for an evening event?

Black trousers with a silk blouse or a fine knit and heels are a classic choice. Tell us your heel height so the hem sits just right on the shoe.

### Which colors are available?

Six: Black, Charcoal, Gray, Gray Micro Check, Petrol Blue and Brown. The trousers in the photos are Black.

### Are these trousers lined?

The front of each leg is lined to just below the knee in the lining color you choose. If wool makes you itch, ask for a full lining to the hem at no extra cost, or ask for them unlined.

### Can I machine wash these trousers?

Yes. Wash them cold on a gentle wool cycle and hang them to dry, or dry clean them if you prefer. Between wears, hang them by the hem so any creases drop out.

### What does the back of these formal trousers look like?

From the back side, the pant falls straight from the seat to the hem, with single-welt back pockets and a buttoned waistband, the same clean line as a man’s formal trouser.

### How much do these formal trousers cost?

They are $239 in any of the 6 colors, with any lining color. The price includes a free cotton test pair that is yours to keep, so the fit is confirmed before your cloth is cut.

### How long does it take to receive my trousers, and do you ship internationally?

Your test pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Both ship worldwide with tracking, and if you need them by a date, order as early as you can.
