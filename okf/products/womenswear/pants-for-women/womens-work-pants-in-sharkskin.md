## The Power of a Perfect Flare

This is how you master a notoriously tricky silhouette. We’ve taken the timeless power of the flare and perfected it with the precision of custom tailoring, creating a pair of trousers that is guaranteed to flatter, elongate, and command attention.

### The Luxury of Sharkskin Wool

The foundation of these trousers is a unique, high-performance fabric, chosen for its subtle luster and beautiful drape.

- **Super 120s’ Worsted Wool:** Tailored from a high-quality wool in a unique “sharkskin” weave, which gives the fabric a subtle two-tone luster and a smooth, elegant feel perfect for a sharp silhouette.
- **A Palette of Modern Neutrals:** Choose from 7 versatile, sophisticated shades including classic navy, slate blue, and charcoal.

### Engineered for a Flattering Silhouette

These aren’t just flare pants; they are a study in proportion and drape, designed to create the ultimate leg-lengthening effect.

- **The Perfect Flare Cut:** The design is fitted through the hip and thigh before breaking at the knee into a graceful, elegant flare, creating a powerful, balanced silhouette.
- **Classic Trouser Details:** Features a zip fly with a durable horn button closure, practical slanted front pockets, and two clean single-welt back pockets.
- **A Smarter Waistband:** The interior of the waistband is enforced with a grip, a high-end detail that helps keep your blouses and shirts neatly tucked in.

### The Secret to a Flawless Flare: Your Free Test Pants

The elegance of a flare is decided at the knee. A poor fit can ruin the entire look. We eliminate this risk completely by sending you a **free pair of test pants** first.

1. **Receive Your Test Garment:** We mail you a test pair made in cotton to your exact initial measurements.
2. **Confirm the Fit and Break:** This is your chance to perfect the fit through the thigh and, most importantly, the precise point where the flare begins, ensuring a supremely flattering, leg-lengthening effect.
3. **We Craft Your Masterpiece:** We apply your feedback to tailor the final wool flare pants for a silhouette that is perfectly, intentionally yours.

The test pants are yours to keep.
