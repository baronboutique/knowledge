Sharkskin Wool

## What Sharkskin Wool Is, and Why It Suits a Flare

Sharkskin is not a material but a weave. Yarns of two tones, one lighter and one darker, are woven together so the surface looks like a fine, even texture that shifts slightly as the light moves. Up close it has a quiet two-tone sheen; from across a room it reads as a solid color.

Crisp enough to hold the line

The tight weave has body, so the flare falls in a clean shape from the knee instead of collapsing around the ankle.

Forgiving at work

The two-tone surface hides small creases from sitting and light lint better than a flat, dark cloth.

Super 120s wool

100% worsted wool with a smooth hand, lined at the front to just below the knee in the lining color you choose.

## Questions About These Tailored Flare Trousers

### Can I wear flare trousers to work?

Yes, when they are cut like these: a flat front, belt loops, welt back pockets and a crisp suiting wool. In Navy, Black or Dark Grey with a tucked-in blouse and a fitted jacket, a tailored flare looks as professional as a straight trouser.

### What is sharkskin wool?

A wool cloth woven from yarns of two tones, which gives it a fine texture and a subtle sheen that shifts in the light. These trousers are in 100% Super 120s worsted wool with a sharkskin weave.

### Is sharkskin shiny?

Only slightly. It has a soft luster up close, not a shine, and reads as a solid color from a few steps away. The darker colors, Navy and Black, show the least sheen.

### What is the difference between flare and bootcut trousers?

Both are fitted through the thigh. A flare opens more widely from the knee; a bootcut opens only slightly, so the hem is a little wider than the knee. If you want a gentler line, see our tailored bootcut trousers, or ask for a narrower flare in the comment box.

### What shoes go with flare trousers?

Heels or block heels suit a flare, because the hem is meant to cover most of the shoe. Tell us your heel height so we can set the length, and check it on the free test pair wearing those shoes.

### Do you make navy tailored trousers in other cuts?

Yes. These are cut with a flare, but you can ask for a bootcut or a straight leg in the same sharkskin in the comment box, and you will see the result on the test pair.

### Are these trousers lined?

The front of each leg is lined to just below the knee in the lining color you choose. If wool makes you itch, ask for a full lining to the hem at no extra cost, or ask for them unlined.

### Can I machine wash these wool trousers?

Yes. Wash them cold on a gentle wool cycle and hang them to dry, or dry clean them if you prefer. Between wears, hang them by the hem so any creases drop out.

### How wide is the flare?

It opens from the knee to a hem that is clearly wider than a bootcut but not as full as a wide leg or bell bottom. The exact width is set to your proportions on the test pair, and you can ask for it wider or narrower in the comment box.

### How much do these flare trousers cost?

They are $239 in any of the 8 sharkskin colors, with any lining color. The price includes a free cotton test pair that is yours to keep, so the fit is confirmed before your wool is cut.

### How long does it take to receive my trousers, and do you ship internationally?

Your test pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Both ship worldwide with tracking, and if you need them by a date, order as early as you can.
