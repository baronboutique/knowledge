## Why Velvet Pants Bag at the Knee, and How We Stop It

Velvet is a woven cloth with a short, dense pile standing up from its surface. Where the trouser pulls tight, usually across the knees, thighs and seat, the pile is pressed flat and the cloth stretches a little each time you sit. Over a day, that shows as a bag at the knee and shiny patches where the light catches the flattened pile.

Cut to your measurements

We cut the thigh, knee and calf to your own measurements, with just enough ease to sit and walk without the velvet being pulled tight.

A slight stretch

The velvet has a little stretch, so it moves with you and recovers, instead of holding the shape of your knee.

Lined to the knee

The front of each leg is lined to just below the knee, which takes the strain when you sit. Lining can be removed, or extended to the hem, on request.

The free test pair is where this is set. You sit, stand and walk in it, and we adjust the ease at the knee and seat before your velvet is cut.

Velvet Care

## Keeping the Pile Up

- Wash inside out, cold, on a gentle cycle, and hang to dry; or dry clean if you prefer.
- Never iron the velvet face. Steam from a short distance instead: it lifts the pile and removes crush marks from sitting.
- Brush lightly with a soft brush in the direction of the pile after wearing.
- Hang the trousers by the hem or waistband rather than folding them, so no crease is pressed into the pile.

## Questions About These Velvet Skinny Trousers

### What are these velvet skinny trousers made of?

A poly-cotton velvet with a slight stretch and a short pile. The cotton gives the soft, deep color, the polyester helps it keep its shape and color, and the stretch lets a skinny leg move with you.

### Why do skinny velvet pants bag at the knees?

When a skinny leg is tight at the knee, the velvet stretches each time you sit and the pile is pressed flat. These are cut to your own knee and thigh measurements with a little ease, have a slight stretch that recovers, and are lined to the knee, so they keep their shape.

### How do I remove crush marks from velvet trousers?

Steam them from a short distance, then brush lightly in the direction of the pile. Never press an iron on the velvet face. Hanging the trousers by the hem overnight also lets the pile recover.

### Can I machine wash velvet trousers?

Yes. Turn them inside out, wash cold on a gentle cycle and hang them to dry, or dry clean them if you prefer. Do not tumble dry or iron the pile.

### Are these like velvet skinny jeans?

They have the slim leg, belt loops and patch back pockets people like about velvet jeans, but they are tailored trousers: a flat front, a clean waistband and a hand-stitched hem, cut to your measurements.

### Can I have a straight leg instead of a skinny leg?

Yes. Write “straight leg” in the comment box on the measurement form and we will cut the leg straight from the knee. You can also ask for a higher rise.

### Are these velvet trousers lined?

The front of each leg is lined to just below the knee as standard. You can ask for them unlined, or fully lined to the hem, in the comment box on the measurement form.

### What do you wear with red velvet pants?

Keep the rest plain and smooth: a black silk blouse or fine black knit for evenings and holidays, a cream sweater and a navy blazer for the office, a loose white shirt for weekends. Black ankle boots, loafers or simple heels all work.

### Can I wear velvet trousers to work?

Yes, especially in black, navy, burgundy or maroon velvet with a fine knit or a blazer. The short pile looks sleek rather than plush, so it reads as smart rather than party wear.
