## The Ultimate Figure-Flattering Silhouette

This is how you unlock the legendary power of the bootcut. We’ve taken this famously flattering silhouette and perfected it with the precision of custom tailoring, creating a pair of trousers that is guaranteed to elongate the leg and create a beautifully balanced shape.

### The Luxury of Four-Season Wool

The foundation of these trousers is an exceptional, high-performance fabric chosen for its drape and subtle luster.

- **Super 140s’ Worsted Wool:** An incredibly smooth, **lightweight wool** that provides a beautiful, fluid drape, which is essential for a perfect bootcut. It features a slight, elegant sheen for a polished finish.
- **A Palette of 10 Colors:** Choose from a curated range of 10 vibrant shades, including classic off-white, bold cobalt blue, and rich deep purple.

### Engineered for a Flawless Fit

These aren’t just bootcut pants; they are a study in proportion and drape, designed to create the ultimate leg-lengthening effect.

- **The Perfect Slim-Fit Bootcut:** The design is slim-fitting through the hip and thigh before flaring gently from the knee, creating the ultimate figure-flattering, balanced silhouette.
- **Classic Trouser Details:** Features a clean, flat front, a zip fly with a durable hook closure, and practical slanted front pockets.
- **Partially Lined for Comfort:** The front of the trousers is lined to the knee in breathable acetate, ensuring a smooth drape and added comfort.

### The Secret to the Perfect Bootcut: Your Free Test Pants

The magic of a bootcut is decided at the knee. A poor fit can ruin the entire silhouette. We eliminate this risk completely by sending you a **free pair of test pants** first.

1. **Receive Your Test Garment:** We mail you a test pair made in cotton to your exact initial measurements.
2. **Confirm the Fit and Flare:** This is your chance to perfect the slim fit through the thigh and, most importantly, the precise point where the bootcut flare begins, ensuring a supremely flattering, leg-lengthening effect.
3. **We Craft Your Masterpiece:** We apply your feedback to tailor the final wool bootcut trousers for a silhouette that is perfectly, intentionally yours.

The test pants are yours to keep.
