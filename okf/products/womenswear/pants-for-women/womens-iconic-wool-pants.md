The Bootcut Line

## What Makes a Bootcut a Bootcut

A bootcut is slim from the hip to the knee, then opens slightly so the hem is a little wider than the knee. It was made to sit over a boot or a heel, and on a tailored trouser it balances the hips and makes the leg look longer. It is quieter than a flare.

| Bootcut (this pair) | Slim through the thigh, a gentle opening from the knee, hem a little wider than the knee. Works for the office and with heels or boots. |
| --- | --- |
| Flare | A wider, more dramatic opening, often starting higher. A bolder, more fashion-led line. |
| Straight leg | The same width from the knee to the hem. Cleaner with flats and loafers. |

Want the opening a little wider or narrower than ours? Write it in the comment box, and you will see the result on the test pair.

Heels and Length

## Bootcut Trousers and Heels: Getting the Length Right

A bootcut is meant to be long. The hem should cover most of the heel and sit about half an inch off the floor at the back, so the leg reads as one long line. That only works for one heel height, so tell us which shoes you will wear most.

- High heels or heeled boots: the classic bootcut length, with the hem resting over the top of the foot.
- Low heels or block heels: a little shorter, so the hem does not drag.
- Flats: possible, but a bootcut on flats can pool at the ankle. If you wear flats most days, ask for a straight leg instead.

## Questions About These Tailored Bootcut Trousers

### How should tailored bootcut trousers fit?

Close through the hip and thigh without pulling, then opening gently from the knee so the hem is a little wider than the knee. The hem should cover most of your heel and sit about half an inch off the floor at the back. You check all of this on the free test pair.

### What is the difference between bootcut and flare trousers?

A bootcut opens gently from the knee, so the hem is only a little wider than the knee. A flare opens wider and more dramatically. A bootcut is the quieter of the two and sits easily in an office.

### What shoes do you wear with bootcut trousers?

Heels, heeled boots and block heels suit a bootcut most naturally, because the opening was made to sit over a heel. Tell us your heel height so we can set the length. If you mostly wear flats, a straight leg is usually the better choice.

### Are bootcut trousers flattering?

A slim bootcut balances the hips with a slightly wider hem and, worn long over a heel, makes the leg look longer. Whether it suits you depends on where the leg opens and where the hem lands, which is why both are set on your test pair.

### What are these bootcut trousers made of?

A 9 oz Super 140s worsted wool, 100% wool, with the front of each leg lined in acetate to just below the knee.

### Are these trousers lined?

Yes, the front of each leg is lined to the knee, and you choose the lining color. If wool makes you itch, ask for a full lining to the hem at no extra cost, or ask for them unlined.

### Can I wear bootcut trousers to work?

Yes. In Black, Charcoal Grey, Dark Grey or Off White with a blouse and a jacket, these are tailored work trousers with a flat front, belt loops and welt back pockets. Cobalt, Deep Purple or Red make a stronger statement.

### Can I machine wash these wool trousers?

Yes. Wash them cold on a gentle wool cycle and hang them to dry, or dry clean them if you prefer. Between wears, hang them by the hem so the creases drop out.

### Can I change the leg to straight or a wider flare?

Yes. Write “straight leg”, “narrower bootcut” or “wider bootcut” in the comment box on the measurement form, and you will see the result on the test pair before your wool is cut.

### How long does it take to receive my trousers, and do you ship internationally?
