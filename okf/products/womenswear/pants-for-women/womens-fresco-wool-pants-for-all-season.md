Fresco Wool

## What Fresco Wool Is, and Why It Suits a Tapered Leg

Fresco is a wool cloth woven from high-twist yarns in an open plain weave. The tight twist gives each yarn a spring, and the open weave lets air through. The result feels dry and slightly crisp in the hand rather than soft and smooth, which is exactly what a narrow leg needs.

Shakes off creases

The springy yarns recover well. Creases from a long meeting or a flight mostly fall out when the trousers hang overnight.

Breathes in the heat

The open weave lets air move through the cloth, so fresco is a long-standing choice for warm weather and travel.

Holds the line

The crisp hand keeps the taper clean from knee to ankle instead of folding into the soft wrinkles a lighter cloth would show.

## Questions About These Tapered Dress Pants

### What is fresco wool?

A wool cloth woven from high-twist yarns in an open plain weave. It feels dry and crisp, breathes well and recovers from creasing. These trousers are in Super 120s fresco wool.

### Does fresco wool wrinkle?

It creases when you sit, like any wool, but the springy high-twist yarns let those creases fall out when the trousers hang overnight.

### Are fresco trousers good for hot weather?

Yes. The open weave lets air through, so fresco is one of the classic warm-weather trouser cloths. With the front lined only to the knee, the lower leg stays light.

### Are these good trousers for travel?

Yes. Fresco recovers well from being packed and worn on a long journey. Hang them when you arrive and most creases drop out by the next morning.

### What are tapered dress pants?

Tailored trousers whose leg narrows from the knee to the hem, so they are slimmer at the ankle than a straight leg. They look neat with heels and flats and suit a tucked-in top.

### How long are these trousers?

Ankle length, ending just above the ankle bone. You check the exact length on the free test pair with your shoes, and you can ask for full length in the comment box.

### Do these trousers have belt loops?

No. They have a clean 1.5-inch waistband, which keeps tucked-in tops smooth. If you wear a belt, ask for belt loops in the comment box.

### Are these trousers lined?

The front of each leg is lined to just below the knee in the lining color you choose. If wool makes you itch, ask for a full lining to the hem at no extra cost, or ask for them unlined.

### Can I machine wash these wool trousers?

Yes. Wash them cold on a gentle wool cycle and hang them to dry, or dry clean them if you prefer. Between wears, hang them by the hem so any creases drop out.

They are $239 in any of the 13 fresco colors, including the two striped cloths, with any lining color. The price includes a free cotton test pair that is yours to keep, so the fit is confirmed before your wool is cut.

### How long does it take to receive my trousers, and do you ship internationally?

Your test pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Both ship worldwide with tracking, and if you need them by a date, order as early as you can.
