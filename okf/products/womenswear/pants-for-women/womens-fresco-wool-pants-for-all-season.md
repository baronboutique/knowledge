## The Modern Wardrobe, Perfected

This is the silhouette that defines modern, effortless style. We’ve taken the chic tapered leg and elevated it with a high-performance, travel-ready fabric and the precision of a custom fit, creating the single most versatile trouser in your collection.

### The Genius of Fresco Wool

The foundation of a truly versatile trouser is its fabric. These are crafted from a modern, breathable wool chosen for its unique performance properties.

- **High-Twist Fresco Wool:** We use a Super 120s’ Fresco wool, a high-twist, open-weave cloth renowned for its exceptional **natural wrinkle resistance and breathability**. It’s the ultimate fabric for looking polished and fresh, even after a long day of travel or back-to-back meetings.
- **A Palette of 10 Modern Colors:** Choose from a curated selection of 10 sophisticated shades, including classic tan, navy, and a range of versatile greys.

### Engineered for a Sharp, Modern Fit

These aren’t just tapered pants; they are a study in proportion, designed to create a clean, elongating, and stylishly modern silhouette.

- **The Perfect Tapered Leg:** A clean, flat front and a classic mid-rise flow into a leg that is tailored to gently taper from the knee to the ankle, creating a sharp, clean line.
- **Classic Trouser Details:** Features a zip fly with a durable hook closure, practical slanted front pockets, and two clean, button-through single-welt back pockets.
- **A Smarter Waistband:** The interior of the waistband is enforced with a grip, a high-end detail that keeps your blouses and shirts neatly tucked in for a polished look that lasts all day.

### The Secret to a Perfect Taper: Your Free Test Pants

The elegance of a tapered leg depends entirely on how it fits your specific proportions. We eliminate the risk of a poor fit completely by sending you a **free pair of test pants** first.

1. **Receive Your Test Garment:** We mail you a test pair made in cotton to your exact initial measurements.
2. **Confirm the Fit and Taper:** This is your chance to perfect the mid-rise placement, the fit through the thigh, and the precise degree of taper to the ankle, ensuring a supremely flattering, modern silhouette.
3. **We Craft Your Masterpiece:** We apply your feedback to tailor the final Fresco wool trousers for a silhouette that is perfectly, intentionally yours.

The test pants are yours to keep.
