The magic of a true tuxedo jacket lies in the interplay of textures. We use classic, luxurious materials to honor this tradition.

### The Architecture of After-Dark Style

This jacket is engineered with the authentic details that define a true tuxedo, sculpted to create a powerful, feminine silhouette.

- **The Perfect Silhouette:** A single-button closure at your natural waist, combined with precise darting, creates a beautiful hourglass shape that is powerful, not boxy.
- **Bespoke-Level Construction:** A hand-basted full canvas front—the hallmark of high-end tailoring—ensures the jacket molds to your body over time, providing a fit that only gets better with wear.
- **A Clean, Sharp Finish:** A vent-less back provides a clean, uninterrupted line, ensuring the jacket drapes perfectly over evening gowns or tailored trousers.

### Our Bespoke Fit Promise

An iconic jacket demands a perfect fit. We eliminate the risk of a boxy waist or a tight bust by sending you a **free test jacket** first.
