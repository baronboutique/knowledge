## Strong Shoulders and Wide Lapels Leave No Room for Guesswork

A tuxedo with roped shoulders and four to five inch lapels shows its fit at the top. Few people have the same shape at the front and back of the shoulder, and the wider the lapel, the more any pull across the bust bends it out of line. These are the points we correct most often on a women’s test suit, along with the waist suppression, and the buttons are kept close enough to stop the front gaping when you sit.

Some corrections would change the look, such as an extra dart beside the satin lapel. When that happens we explain the trade-off and you decide whether the tuxedo follows your body exactly or the style in the photos exactly.

## Questions About the Women’s Bespoke Tuxedo

### What makes this a tuxedo and not a black suit?

The satin. The lapels are faced in black silk satin, the pocket flaps and buttons are covered in it, and the trousers close with side adjusters instead of belt loops. A black suit has none of these, which is why it never reads as evening dress.

### How much does a custom tuxedo cost?

Custom tuxedos commonly range from about $500 to $2,500, depending on the cloth, how much of the work is done by hand and how many fittings are included. This one is $699 for the jacket and trousers, and the price includes a free cotton test suit, so the fitting stage is not an extra cost.

### Should I rent or buy a women’s tuxedo?

Rentals are cut for an average figure, rarely in women’s patterns, and go back the next morning. If you will wear a tuxedo two or three times, buying one cut to your measurements usually costs less per wearing, and it fits from the first evening.

### Is it a men’s tuxedo resized?

No. It keeps the menswear details, the roped shoulders, the wide peak lapels and the ticket pocket, but the pattern is drawn for a woman’s body from your own measurements. Bust, waist and hips are set separately and checked on the test suit.

### Are the lapels real silk?

Yes. The lapels, the pocket flaps and the buttons are covered in black silk satin. Polyester satin has a hard, glassy shine under flash; silk has a softer glow that photographs well.

### Can I wear it to my own wedding?

Yes, and many brides now do. Black suits an evening ceremony; gray or charcoal suits a daytime one, and petrol blue is a quieter choice for a civil wedding. Wear it with a silk camisole and the jacket closed for photos.

### Will a tuxedo with wide lapels suit a petite woman?

Yes, if the proportions are scaled. The 4 to 5 inch peak lapels in the photos suit an average or tall frame; on a petite figure we can narrow them, and the jacket length and button point are set from your measurements so the tuxedo does not swamp you.

### Can I add a satin stripe to the trousers or change the lapel?

Yes. Note it in the comment box of the measurement form, or email us with your order number. A satin trouser stripe, a shawl collar or a single button are all changes we make on this tuxedo.

### Is it warm enough for winter, or too warm in summer?

Merino with a little cashmere is a year-round cloth. It is comfortable indoors at a summer reception and warm enough for winter evenings with a coat over it.

### How do I measure if there is no tailor near me?

Ask someone to help with the tape if you can. Alone, take your bust, natural waist, hips, trouser waist, thigh and ankle, then measure a jacket and trousers you already like; we can talk you through it by video call or WhatsApp.

### Can I see the merino and cashmere before I order?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples of about 2 by 4 inches. They usually arrive within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 2600001 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How long does the tuxedo take?

The cotton test suit is made in 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. After you approve the fit, the tuxedo is made in another 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after it ships, so order well before the event.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean rarely. Keep perfume and deodorant away from the satin, and hang the jacket on a broad hanger that supports the roped shoulders.
