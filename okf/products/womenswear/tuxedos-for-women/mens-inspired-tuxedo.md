This is not a “female version” of a tuxedo. This is the tuxedo, in all its sartorial glory, meticulously re-engineered to honor the female form. It’s a statement of power, an homage to classic menswear, and a masterpiece of bespoke tailoring, built for the woman who isn’t afraid to own the room.

### A Foundation of Merino and Cashmere

A true tuxedo demands a fabric that drapes beautifully and feels luxurious. We use a premium blend chosen for its beauty and performance.

- **A Luxurious Blend:** We use a Super 130s’ fabric that is **98% fine merino wool and 2% cashmere**. This blend provides the beautiful drape and structure of wool with the exceptional softness and subtle luster of cashmere.
- **A Palette of 6 Classic Colors:** Choose from 6 timeless, elegant shades perfect for any formal occasion.

### The Art of True Menswear Tailoring

This suit is defined by its authentic, high-end details—features rarely seen outside of a traditional men’s bespoke house.

- **The “Wing Peak” Lapel:** The jacket’s commanding feature. A dramatic, wide peak lapel faced in pure satin silk, with an extended “wing” shape that signals a deep appreciation for classic, powerful tailoring.
- **Bespoke Construction:** With **roped shoulders** for a strong shoulder line, **hand-pick-stitching** on the lapels, and a full canvas front, this jacket is built like a true bespoke garment.
- **The Perfect Tuxedo Trouser:** Clean, flat-front trousers with bespoke buckle side-adjusters provide a clean, belt-free waistline, completing the authentic tuxedo look.

### Our Bespoke Fit Promise: The Final Word

A tuxedo this detailed must fit flawlessly. We eliminate all risk and sizing anxiety by sending you a **free test suit**—a complete cotton version of both the jacket and trousers—made to your initial measurements. This personal fitting allows us to perfect every aspect of this powerful silhouette. It is our commitment to delivering a true bespoke experience.
