## A Satin Stripe Shows Every Fault in the Trousers

The stripe down the outseam is a straight line on a moving body, so it reveals what a plain trouser hides: a hip that is fuller at the back than the front twists the stripe forward, and a small seat leaves folds that bend it. On the jacket, roped shoulders and a shawl lapel need an accurate bust and shoulder fit. These are the points we correct on the test suit, keeping the buttons close enough that the front stays closed when you dance or sit.

If a correction would alter the look, such as an extra dart beside the shawl lapel, we explain the trade-off and you choose between an exact fit and the exact style in the photos.

## What Makes a Female Tuxedo Different From a Suit

| Satin facings | The lapel is faced in silk satin; a suit lapel is the same cloth as the jacket. |
| --- | --- |
| Satin stripe | A half-inch satin stripe runs down the outer seam of each trouser leg. |
| Covered buttons | Buttons are covered in fabric rather than horn or plastic. |
| No belt loops | Buckle side adjusters set the waist, so nothing breaks the line under the jacket. |
| Shawl lapel | One continuous curve from collar to button, the softest and most formal of the tuxedo lapels. |

Whether you search for a female tux or a tuxedo set in female sizing, the details above are what make it formal. Cheap tuxedo sets usually give out in two ways, in our experience: the cloth and satin lose their color and sheen, and the front loses its shape because nothing is built into it. A canvas front and a fine worsted are what keep this one looking right for years of formal events.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Women’s Tuxedo Suit

### Is a women’s tuxedo a good choice for prom?

Yes. A tailored tuxedo is a sharp alternative to a gown, easier to move and dance in, and it can be worn again to weddings and formal dinners. Ready-made prom tuxedos are cut from men’s or generic patterns; this one is cut to your measurements.

### How far ahead should I order for prom?

Count back from the date. The cotton test suit takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you approve it, the tuxedo takes another 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after it ships. Leave a few extra days for trying the test suit and sending your notes.

### What is a shawl lapel?

A lapel cut in one smooth curve from the back of the collar to the button, with no notch or peak. It is the most formal tuxedo lapel and the softest looking, which suits a woman’s neckline well.

### Is the lapel real silk satin?

Yes. The shawl lapel is faced in silk satin, and the trouser stripe is satin too.

### What color is the satin stripe, and can I change it?

Black, as in the photos. For another color, add a note in the comment box of the measurement form, or send us an email quoting your order number.

### Can I change the lapel, the buttons or the trousers?

Yes, in the same way. A peak lapel, a single button, a skirt instead of trousers, or trousers without the stripe are all possible.

### Can I wear it to a wedding?

Yes. As a guest, wear it in navy, charcoal or black with a silk camisole; as a bride, white tropical wool with the satin shawl lapel makes a striking alternative to a gown.

### Is a white tuxedo practical?

White suits summer, daytime events and photographs beautifully. Black, navy and charcoal are easier to keep clean if you will wear it often.

### Rent or buy?

A rented tuxedo is cut for an average body and returned the next morning. One cut to your measurements fits from the first wearing, and after two or three events it has cost less than repeated rentals.

### How do I measure without a tailor nearby?

A friend with the tape makes it easier. On your own, measure bust, natural waist, hips, trouser waist, thigh and ankle, then a jacket and trousers that fit you; we can help over video call or WhatsApp.

### Can I have plum tuxedo trousers?

The cloths for this women’s tuxedo are the ones in the selector, so plum tuxedo trousers are not an option. A plum or purple satin lining is available in the lining selector, which shows when the jacket is open.

### Can I see the wool before ordering?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples of about 2 by 4 inches. They usually arrive within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 2600003 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean only and rarely, and keep perfume away from the satin. Store a white tuxedo in a breathable cotton bag, never plastic.
