## Own the Room

This is for the woman who understands that true power is a choice. It’s the decision to reject the expected dress in favor of an icon of unapologetic glamour. We have taken the legendary tuxedo and masterfully re-engineered it for a woman’s body, creating a silhouette that is not just worn, but wielded.

### The Statement Jacket: A Study in Contrast

The heart of the tuxedo is its jacket, a masterpiece of texture and light.

- **A Foundation of Fine Wool:** The body is crafted from a pristine, Super 140s’ worsted wool, providing a sharp, structured silhouette.
- **The Luster of Satin:** In classic tuxedo tradition, the wide, round shawl lapels are faced with pure satin silk, creating a stunning contrast that catches the evening light.
- **Architected for Form:** We use high-end tailoring techniques like **roped shoulders** for a strong, defined shoulder line and **parallel double darts** to sculpt a beautiful, feminine waist. This is the antithesis of a “boxy” fit.

### The Perfect Trouser: A Line of Pure Elegance

The trousers are designed to be the perfect, sleek counterpoint to the statement jacket.

- **The Iconic Satin Stripe:** A single, sharp stripe of satin runs down the outseam of each leg, a non-negotiable detail of a true tuxedo that creates a beautiful, elongating line.
- **Bespoke Detailing:** The trousers feature buckle side-adjusters instead of belt loops, providing a clean, custom-tailored look at the waist.

### Our Red-Carpet Fit Guarantee

A garment this iconic demands a flawless silhouette. We eliminate all sizing anxiety by sending you a **free test suit**—a complete cotton version of both the jacket and trousers—made to your initial measurements. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect the sculpted waist, the strong shoulder line, and the trouser break. It is our commitment to ensuring that on your big night, the fit is as memorable as the occasion.

#### Caring For Your Tuxedo

To preserve the beauty of your tuxedo for a lifetime of events, we recommend professional dry-cleaning only.

*A complimentary allergy-protection service is available upon request.*
