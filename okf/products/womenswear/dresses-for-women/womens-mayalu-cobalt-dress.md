## The Length and the Sleeves, on You

A mini dress has less fabric to hide a fitting error, so the test dress matters more than usual. We check that the high waist seam sits just under the bust rather than across it, that the skirt has enough room at the seat to stay put when you sit, and that the sleeves end at the elbow without pulling when you reach forward.

We also look at the balance of the whole dress: on a shorter figure, the hem and the sleeve length work together, and moving one by an inch often fixes the proportion of the other.

1

**Choose the color and lining**  
 Pick cobalt or another color and a satin lining in the selector, then enter your measurements and the length you want.

2

**A cotton version comes first**  
 Your test dress is cut in cotton to your measurements, with the sleeves set in; it takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch.

3

**Sit down and reach forward**  
 Sit on a chair and check the hem: a mini that fits stays where you set it. Reach forward as if driving; the sleeves should not pull across the back, and they should end at the elbow.

4

**The wool is cut**  
 We adjust the length, the sleeves and the fit, then cut the wool; the final dress takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. The test dress is yours to keep.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Tailored Mini Dress

### What shade of blue is cobalt?

Cobalt is a bright, clear blue, lighter than navy and more vivid than royal blue, with a faint green rather than violet undertone. It is the color in the photos. Screens vary, so if the exact shade matters, ask for a sample first.

### Is cobalt blue the same as royal blue?

No. Royal blue is deeper and leans slightly toward violet; cobalt is brighter and cleaner. We offer both in the selector, so you can compare them side by side through our swatch service.

### What goes with a cobalt blue dress?

Silver jewelry sharpens it and gold warms it. Nude or black shoes keep the color in charge; a white jacket gives a crisp contrast. Keep accessories to one color so the blue stays the statement.

### Is this a cobalt blue sheath dress?

It is closer to a shift: it skims the body, with a seam across the high waist and a skirt that eases out slightly to the hem. If you prefer a closer sheath through the waist and hips, ask in the comment box and we cut it that way.

### How short is this mini dress?

In the photos it ends a few inches above the knee, which is the polished end of mini. A fashion mini usually stops at mid-thigh. You enter the length you want, and we check it on the test dress with the shoes you will wear.

### What length suits me if I am petite?

On a shorter frame, a hem two to four inches above the knee usually lengthens the leg more than a hem at the knee, which can cut the leg at its widest point. We look at the proportion on the test dress and move the hem until it balances with the sleeve length.

### Can I wear a mini dress to work?

With elbow-length sleeves and a hem just above the knee, it reads as tailored rather than casual and suits many offices. For a conservative workplace, enter a knee-length hem; the dress keeps its shape at any length.

### What is Super 140s wool like to wear?

Super 140s is a fine, smooth worsted wool. It is light enough for most of the year, breathes better than polyester, and resists creasing, so the dress looks the same at the end of the day as at the start.

### Can I change the sleeve length?

Yes. Write it in the comment box of the measurement form, or email us with your order number. Short sleeves, three-quarter sleeves, long sleeves or no sleeves can all be cut on this pattern.

### Can I see the cobalt before I order?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples about 2 by 4 inches, usually within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 3200003 and the colors, for example cobalt and royal blue; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How long will my dress take?

The cotton test dress takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you approve the fit, your final dress takes another 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch, so plan around the date you need it.
