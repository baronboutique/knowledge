## An Asymmetrical Hem Dress That Keeps Its Angle

Most asymmetrical hem dresses are made in chiffon or thin polyester, because those fabrics are inexpensive and drape easily. The problem is that the angle, the whole point of the dress, droops by the end of the day, and the dress needs layers underneath to be opaque.

This asymmetrical hem zipper dress takes the opposite approach. It is cut to your measurements in Super 120s worsted wool woven as sharkskin, fully lined in satin, with a round neck and an overlapping front panel that is sewn in place and falls to an angled hem above the knee. The shape is a shift with a slight A-line flare, it closes with a hidden zip at the center back, and a free cotton test dress comes first so the angle sits where it should on you.

## Setting the Angle on Your Body

An asymmetric hem has a short side and a long side, and both have to suit you. On the test dress we set the length of the short side for sitting, then the drop of the diagonal, so the line looks intentional from the front and the side. We also check that the overlapping panel lies flat across the stomach; if it lifts, the bust or hip needs more room, which we add before the wool is cut.

If your fit would change the look in the photos, such as a longer short side, we explain the trade-off and you decide.

Sharkskin Wool

## What Sharkskin Is

Sharkskin is a worsted wool woven with alternating light and dark yarns. Up close it shows a tiny stepped pattern; from a distance it looks like a solid color with a soft two-tone sheen that shifts in the light. It is smooth, resists creasing and holds sharp lines, which is why it is a suiting cloth.

| Super 120s | A fine, smooth worsted that is still sturdy enough for regular wear. |
| --- | --- |
| Season | Comfortable most of the year indoors; too warm for a hot outdoor summer day. |
| Care | Dry clean rarely; hang it on a shaped hanger and brush it between wears. |

## Questions About the Asymmetrical Hem Zipper Dress

### Where is the zipper on this asymmetrical hem dress?

It is a hidden zip down the center back, so nothing interrupts the angled line at the front. The overlapping front panel is sewn in place and never needs adjusting.

### Are asymmetrical dresses in style?

An asymmetric hem has been part of tailored dressing for decades and is worn at offices and weddings alike. What keeps it from looking like a trend piece is cloth that holds the angle crisply; in a limp fabric the line droops and looks accidental.

### Is an asymmetrical dress slimming?

A diagonal line moves the eye across the body rather than around it, so it breaks up width at the hip. That helps, but fit does more than any line: a dress that is too tight at the hip or too loose at the bust adds visual width. We fix both on the test dress.

### Which dress shape flatters most figures?

A shift that skims the body with a slightly flared hem suits more figures than a tight cut, because it does not depend on a defined waist. This dress is that shape. If you want the waist marked, a fitted sheath such as our classic black sheath dress is the alternative.

### What is sharkskin wool, and is it made from shark?

No shark is involved. Sharkskin is a worsted wool woven with alternating light and dark yarns, which gives a fine two-tone surface and a soft sheen like the skin it is named after. The name is also used for some shiny synthetic fabrics; ours is 100% wool.

### Is sharkskin a good choice for a dress?

Yes. It is smooth, holds sharp lines and resists creasing, which is why it is used for suits. The slight sheen makes a plain dress look finished without any decoration.

### How is a wool asymmetrical dress different from a chiffon one?

Chiffon floats and needs layers to be opaque; it suits evening and summer parties. Wool sharkskin hangs with weight, so the angled panel keeps its shape, and the full lining makes it opaque and smooth over tights. It works at an office, a wedding or a dinner.

### Is this a shift or a sheath dress?

A shift: it falls from the shoulders with light shaping and flares gently to the hem, rather than following the waist closely. That makes it comfortable for long days of sitting and walking.
