## Fitted Before the Day, Not on It

Bridal shops usually fit a gown over several appointments; we do it at home, in cotton, before the wool is cut. On a sheath, the corrections that matter most are the U-neck, which must lie flat when you lean toward someone, the seam under the bust, which must sit exactly under it, and the back skirt, which needs enough room for the hem to stay level when you sit through a ceremony.

If a correction would change the look, such as a higher neckline, we explain it and you decide.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Minimalist Sheath Wedding Dress

### What is a sheath wedding dress?

A wedding dress with a slim, straight line that follows the body from the neckline to the hem, without a full skirt, train or crinoline. It is the modern, quiet end of bridal. This one is tailored in fine wool to the knee, which suits civil ceremonies, courthouse weddings and second weddings better than a floor-length gown.

### Can I have it in white or ivory?

Yes, on request. The selector shows the colors we keep for this dress; for white or ivory, email us before you order and we will confirm the cloth and send a sample through our swatch service if you want to see it first.

### Can a bride wear a colored wedding dress?

Yes. White became the bridal custom only after Queen Victoria wore it in 1840, and many brides now choose blue, gray or black, especially for a civil ceremony or a second wedding. A midnight blue or light gray sheath looks bridal in photos without pretending to be a gown.

### What should a bride wear to a courthouse wedding?

A dress that is clearly special but practical for a short ceremony, stairs and photos outside. A tailored sheath at the knee is a classic choice because it looks elegant standing beside a desk or on courthouse steps, and you can wear it again afterward.

### Is a sheath wedding dress flattering?

Yes, when it is cut for you. The single vertical line lengthens the figure, which helps petite brides, and it follows an hourglass shape without exaggerating it. The risk is a ready-made sheath that pulls at the hip or gapes at the neckline, which is exactly what the test dress corrects.

### Can my bridesmaids wear it?

Yes. Each bridesmaid orders her own dress with her own measurements, in the same color, so they match in cloth while each one fits. Order early enough for every test dress to be tried, since bridesmaids rarely live in the same place.

### What makes a minimalist sheath dress look refined rather than plain?

The cloth and the fit, because there is nothing else to look at. Super 160s wool has a soft sheen that photographs well, and without trim every seam must lie flat and every line run straight. That is why we settle the fit in cotton before the wool is cut.

### Is wool too warm for a summer wedding?

Not this wool. Super 160s is fine and light and breathes better than polyester satin, so it is comfortable indoors and on mild summer days. For an outdoor ceremony in real heat, a linen dress is cooler.

### Can I change the neckline or add sleeves?

Yes. Write it in the comment box of the measurement form, or email us with your order number. A V-neck, a higher neck, cap sleeves or a longer length can all be made on this pattern.

### When should I order for my wedding?

The cotton test dress takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; after you approve the fit, the wool dress takes another 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order with room to spare for any small change after the test dress.

### Can I see the cloth before I order?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples, each about 2 by 4 inches, usually within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 3200002 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.
