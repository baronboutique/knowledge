## Choosing a Stripe for a Wedding

From a few steps away, a fine or dotted stripe reads as solid navy with a slight texture; up close, it shows the pinstripe. A bold chalk stripe stays visible at a distance and reads closer to business wear. For the ceremony and photographs, the quieter stripes are the safer choice; the bolder ones suit a city wedding or a rehearsal dinner.

## Questions About the Mother of the Bride Sheath Dress

### How do I avoid looking matronly as mother of the bride?

Fit matters more than style. What reads as matronly is usually a dress cut loose and boxy to cover the figure, or a jacket-and-dress set in a stiff fabric. A sheath that follows your shape, in a crisp wool and a clean V-neck, looks current at any age.

### What color should the mother of the bride wear?

Navy is one of the most worn choices because it is formal, photographs well and sits comfortably next to almost any wedding palette. Jewel tones and soft neutrals are also common. Ask the couple about their colors before you choose.

### What colors should the mother of the bride avoid?

White, ivory and cream belong to the bride. Many families also avoid matching the bridesmaids exactly, and some traditions avoid black at a wedding, so it is worth checking. A navy pinstripe avoids all of these.

### Who chooses a dress first, the mother of the bride or the mother of the groom?

By tradition the mother of the bride chooses first and shares the color and length with the mother of the groom, so the two complement each other. Many families now simply talk it through together.

### What length should a mother of the bride dress be?

Follow the formality of the wedding. Knee or just above suits daytime and most ceremonies; a black-tie evening calls for a longer dress. This dress is shown above the knee, and you can enter any length on the measurement form.

### What is the etiquette for a mother of the bride dress?

Dress slightly more formally than the guests, avoid the bride’s colors, and match the formality of the wedding party. Beyond that, choose a dress you can stand, sit and dance in for a whole day, which is why we fit it on a test dress first.

### Is a pinstripe dress suitable for a wedding?

Yes, in the right stripe. Fine and dotted stripes read as solid navy from a few steps away and look dressy in photos; a bold chalk stripe reads more as business wear. Choose a quieter stripe for the ceremony, or ask for swatches to compare.

### Is it suitable for a fall wedding?

Yes. Super 130s worsted wool is comfortable from spring through fall and in cool winter churches, and it resists creasing through a seated ceremony. For an outdoor wedding in real summer heat, a lighter cloth such as linen is cooler.

### Can I add sleeves for more coverage?

Yes. Note it in the comment box of the measurement form, or email us quoting your order number. Cap, short or three-quarter sleeves can be added; a matching jacket is another option, as in our pinstripe skirt suit.

### Can I see the pinstripes before I order?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples about 2 by 4 inches, usually within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 3200004 and the stripes you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How long will my dress take?

The cotton test dress takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you approve the fit, your wool dress takes another 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch, so order well ahead of the date you need it.
