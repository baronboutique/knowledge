The ones we make most often are a bust that is fuller than the other measurements suggest, which moves the princess seams outward, and a seat that is fuller at the back than at the front, which needs more length and room in the back skirt so the hem hangs level instead of lifting at the back.

Why Hems Ride Up

## Why a Sheath Dress Rides Up, and How We Stop It

| Too tight at the hip or thigh | As you walk, the cloth climbs to a narrower part of the body. The cure is room at the hip, not a looser waist. |
| --- | --- |
| Waist-to-hip length | If the distance from your waist to the fullest part of your hip differs from the pattern, the dress pulls upward. We measure it and cut the waist shaping to match. |
| A rounder back hip | The back skirt needs extra length and room, or the hem tilts up behind; this is the most common change on a sheath. |
| Static and cling | A full satin lining lets the wool slide over tights instead of clinging and crawling up. |

Ready-made sheath dresses solve this with stretch cloth, which pulls back down until it wears out. A sheath dress cut to your hips and seat stays at the knee because it has no reason to move. The cut matters too: a straight sheath dress silhouette, falling from the hip to the knee without tapering, climbs less than a narrow pencil line, which is why this classic sheath dress silhouette tapers only slightly and keeps a vent at the back.

## Why Super 160s Wool for a Black Dress

| What Super 160s means | The wool fibers are very fine, about 15 microns, so the cloth is smoother and lighter than an everyday Super 110s or 120s. |
| --- | --- |
| The slight sheen | Fine fibers woven close reflect a little light, so black looks deep and finished rather than flat, which is why this dress moves from day to evening. |
| Wrinkles | The high-twist yarn springs back, so creases from sitting fall out after an hour on a hanger. |
| The trade-off | Fine wool is softer and less tough than a coarser worsted; it rewards a hanger and a brush, and it should rest between wears. |

## Questions About the Classic Black Sheath Dress

### Do sheath dresses go out of style?

The classic sheath has stayed in use since the 1950s because it has no detail that dates it: no trend sleeve, no fashionable neckline. What dates a sheath is fit and length; a knee-length sheath that follows your body looks current in any decade.

### How do I stop a sheath dress from riding up?

A sheath rides up when it is tight at the hip or thigh, or when the waist-to-hip distance does not match the pattern, so walking pushes the cloth to a narrower part of the body. Slips and anti-static sprays only treat the symptom. We cut room into the hips and back skirt from your measurements and check it on the test dress, and the satin lining stops the wool clinging to tights.

### Is Super 160s wool better than Super 120s?

Finer, not simply better. Super 160s fibers are finer, so the cloth is smoother, lighter and has a soft sheen; Super 120s is a little tougher. For a dress worn to evenings and important days, the finer wool looks richer; for daily hard wear, a lower number lasts longer.

### Is Super 160s wool good for summer?

Yes, for most summer days indoors. Fine worsted wool is light and breathes, and it handles air-conditioned offices well. In humid heat outdoors, linen or cotton is cooler.

### What is the difference between a sheath dress and a slip dress?

A slip dress hangs from thin straps in a soft, bias-cut fabric and is mostly an evening piece. A sheath is a tailored dress with a structured neckline and wider shoulders, so it works at a desk as well as at dinner.

### Is a black sheath dress appropriate for work?

Yes. At knee length, with a round neck and a jacket or cardigan over it, it is one of the most accepted work dresses there is. In conservative offices, choose a lining that does not show at the vent and keep the hem at the knee.

### Will a sheath dress suit me if my hips are fuller than my bust?

Yes, if it is cut for your hips rather than for your bust size. A size-chart sheath fitted to the bust pulls across fuller hips; ours takes the waist, hip and seat measurements separately, so the skirt has the room it needs and the top still fits.

### What do I wear with a black sheath dress?

Low heels and a blazer for work; heels, bare arms and one strong piece of jewelry for evening; opaque tights and a fitted coat in winter. Keep the rest simple, because the dress is the frame.

### Can I add sleeves or change the neckline?

Yes. Write it in the comment box on the measurement form when you order, or email us with your order number. Cap sleeves, a boat neck or a V-neck can all be made on this pattern.
