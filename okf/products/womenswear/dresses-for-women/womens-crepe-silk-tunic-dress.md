## Drape Is Decided at the Bust and Shoulders

A loose dress still has to fit somewhere, and on a tunic that place is the shoulders and the bust. When the bust measurement is large compared with the rest of the frame, a dress cut to the bust hangs out like a tent below it; cut to the shoulders, it pulls across the chest. On a test dress we usually move fullness between the front and back, adjust the shoulder slope and set the sleeve length, so the silk falls straight in front instead of swinging out.

If a correction would change the look, such as a dart at the bust that the photos do not show, we explain it, and you decide between the closer fit and the line in the photos.

Why Crepe Silk

## Crepe Silk, Not Charmeuse

| Matte, pebbled surface | Silk crepe is woven from tightly twisted yarns, so it has a fine grain and a soft glow rather than the mirror shine of charmeuse. |
| --- | --- |
| Creases less | The twisted yarn springs back, so crepe silk shows fewer creases after sitting or travel than smooth silks. |
| More opaque | The grain scatters light, so crepe is less sheer than georgette; pale colors can still show a dark bra. |
| Falls instead of clinging | Crepe has weight for its thinness, which is what lets an unlined silk tunic hang straight. |

In our experience, inexpensive silk tunics fail in the cloth first: thin habotai or polyester satin shows every seam, clings in dry weather and creases into a map by lunchtime. A heavier crepe silk, cut to the wearer, is what still looks right the hundredth time it is worn.

## Questions About the Women’s Silk Tunic Dress

### What is the difference between a tunic and a dress?

A tunic is a straight, loose top that falls to the hip or thigh; a tunic dress is the same shape cut long enough to wear on its own. It has no waist seam, so it hangs from the shoulders and bust rather than being shaped at the waist. This one is cut above the knee, and you can order it longer to wear as a dress or shorter to wear over slim trousers.

### Are tunic dresses flattering?

They are when the shoulders and bust fit, and unflattering when they do not. A tunic bought by size is usually cut for a bust in proportion with the rest of the body, so on a fuller bust it tents out below it and on a smaller bust it hangs loose at the chest. Cut to your measurements, the fullness is placed where your body needs it, so the dress falls straight instead of adding width.

### Is crepe silk real silk, and is this 100% silk?

Yes. Crepe silk, also called silk crepe, is pure silk woven from tightly twisted yarns, which gives the matte, slightly grainy surface. This dress is 100% crepe silk; crepe made from polyester or viscose looks similar in photos but does not breathe or drape the same way.

### Is crepe silk a good fabric for a dress?

For a dress that should move and travel, yes. The twisted yarns make it crease less than smooth silks, it is more opaque than georgette, and it has enough weight to hang straight without a lining. Its weakness is water: it can shrink and lose its texture if washed, so it is a dry-clean fabric.

### Can I order it knee length, midi or maxi?

Yes. Enter the length you want on the measurement form; the photos show it above the knee. Longer lengths keep the same straight line, and for a maxi we suggest a side slit so you can walk freely, which you can request in the comment box.

### Does it come in prints?

It is made in solid crepe silk colors, shown in the selector, from black and navy to beetroot and sage. Solid crepe shows the cut and the gathered back yoke, which a busy print would hide.

### Can I change the collar, sleeves or add a slit?

Yes. Write the change in the comment box on the measurement form when you order, or email us with your order number. A round neck without the collar, three-quarter sleeves, plain cuffs or side slits are all possible.

### Why does a silk tunic dress cost $399?

Ready-made silk tunics range widely in price, and most are sold in sizes or as one oversized size. This one is cut from your measurements after you order, in 100% crepe silk, and a cotton test dress is made first so the drape is settled before the silk is cut. You pay for a dress that fits rather than one that is adjusted afterward.

### How do I care for a crepe silk dress?

Dry clean it and steam out creases rather than ironing. Hang it on a padded hanger, and keep perfume and deodorant off the silk, since both can mark it.

### How long does it take?

The cotton test dress takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; the silk dress then takes 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after it ships.
