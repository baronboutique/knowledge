## Where a Sheath Dress Usually Needs Correcting

A close dress fails at the hips before anywhere else. When the distance from the waist to the fullest part of the hip is longer or shorter than the pattern assumes, or the back hip is rounder than the front, the skirt climbs as you walk and creases sideways when you sit. On a test sheath we most often adjust the back hip, move the waist shaping up or down to your waist, and correct the bust seams so they pass over the bust point instead of beside it.

If a correction would change the line in the photos, such as a deeper dart, we explain the trade-off and you choose between the closer fit and the photographed shape.

## Questions About the V-Neck Wool Flannel Sheath Dress

### What body type looks good in a sheath dress?

Every body type, once the sheath is cut for it. A ready-made sheath suits an hourglass figure because that is the shape it is patterned for; on a pear shape it pulls at the hips, and on a straight figure it wrinkles at the waist. Cut to your measurements, the shaping in the princess seams and darts is placed where your body needs it, so the same dress works on any figure.

### What is the difference between a sheath dress and a bodycon dress?

A bodycon dress is knitted with a lot of stretch and clings to every curve. A sheath is woven, tailored with seams and darts, and skims the body with a little ease, so it reads as polished rather than tight. This one has no stretch at all.

### Is a sheath dress the same as a shift dress?

No. A shift falls straight from the shoulders with little shaping at the waist; a sheath is shaped to the waist and hips. A sheath looks more formal, which is why it is the usual choice for offices and ceremonies.

### Is a wool flannel dress itchy?

Rarely. Itch comes from coarse fibers pressing into the skin; Super 120s wool is fine, and flannel is brushed, which softens the surface further. The dress is also fully lined, so the wool touches the skin only at the edges of the neckline and armholes.

### What are the downsides of a wool dress?

It needs dry cleaning rather than the washing machine, it should be stored away from moths, and flannel is too warm for a hot summer. In return it keeps its shape for years, breathes, resists creasing and can be altered as your body changes.

### What does Super 120s mean, and is a higher number better?

The Super number describes how fine the wool fiber is; Super 120s is a fine fiber that still wears well every day. Higher numbers feel smoother but are more delicate, so for a dress you will wear to work week after week, Super 120s flannel is a sensible balance of softness and durability.

### Can I see and feel the flannel before I order?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples, each about 2 by 4 inches, usually within 4 to 6 business days. Quote the product number 3200001 and the colors you want; the courier fee is credited toward your first order when you go ahead.

### Can I wear it in summer?

Not comfortably in real heat. Flannel is brushed and holds warmth, so it is a dress for fall, winter and cool spring days. For summer, a lighter worsted such as the tropical wool of our collared crossover sheath dress is the better choice.

### Can I change the neckline or add sleeves?

Yes. Note it in the comment box on the measurement form when you order, or email us with your order number. A round neck, a higher V, cap sleeves or three-quarter sleeves are all possible.

### How long will my dress take?

The cotton test dress takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you approve the fit, the flannel dress takes another 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch.
