## Finding the Right Ease

A shift is measured as much by the room it leaves as by the size. Too little ease and it becomes a tight sheath that pulls across the hips; too much and the belt gathers it into folds. The usual changes on a test shift are at the bust and hips: taking out fullness at the side seams where the hip is smaller than the bust, or adding room at the back hip so the hem stays level.

Where a correction would change the straight line in the photos, we explain the trade-off, and you choose between the closer fit and the photo’s shape.

1

**Choose the color and length**  
 Pick the twill color in the selector, then enter your measurements and the hem length you want: above the knee as shown, at the knee, or longer.

2

**Try the shape before the twill**  
 A test dress is cut in cotton to your measurements; it takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch.

3

**Belt it, then unbelt it**  
 Wear it both ways. Without the belt the dress should hang straight, not cling at the hips; with the belt tied, the cloth should gather softly at the waist, not bunch. Put your hands in the pockets and check that they do not pull open.

**Your dress is cut**  
 We adjust your pattern and cut the chino twill; the dress takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. The test dress is yours to keep.

Shift vs Sheath

## Shift Dress vs Sheath Dress, and Where a Wrap Dress Fits

| Shift dress | Hangs straight from the shoulders and bust with few or no waist darts, so it stands slightly away from the waist and hips: the shift dress straight silhouette. |
| --- | --- |
| Sheath dress | Shaped closely to the waist and hips with darts or seams, so it follows the body. |
| Wrap dress | Wraps across the front and ties, so the waist is defined by the wrap itself. |
| This dress | A tailored shift dress with a belt: the ease of a shift, with the waist shown when you want it. |

Many shoppers search for a waist defining wrap dress because a wrap dress with a V neck gives waist definition without a fitted bodice. The catch, in our experience, is that a wrap dress can gape at the V and loosen during the day, which is why wrap dress office style often needs a camisole and a pin. A belted shift gives the same wrap dress waist defining style with a fixed V-neck and a zip, so nothing moves. If you prefer the wrap look itself, see our **[women’s crossover sheath dress](https://baronboutique.com/womens-wrap-dress/)**.

## Questions About the Belted Shift Dress

### What is the difference between a shift dress and a sheath dress?

A shift hangs straight from the shoulders and bust with little or no waist shaping; a sheath is shaped close to the waist and hips with darts or seams. A shift is easier to wear all day, a sheath looks more formal. This dress is a shift with a belt, so you can wear it either way.

### What body type looks good in a shift dress?

A shift suits straight and athletic figures without a belt, and curvier figures once the belt marks the waist. Shift dresses look unflattering when they are too big, because the spare cloth adds width; cut to your measurements, the ease is just enough to hang straight.

### How do I belt a shift dress?

Tie the belt at your natural waist, the narrowest point, not at the hips, and pull it just enough to gather the cloth softly. Our loops are placed at your waist from your measurements, so the belt cannot slide up, and the dress is cut with little enough ease that it does not bunch under the belt.

### What is another name for a shift dress?

It is sometimes called a chemise or a sack dress, and in the 1960s simply a shift. All describe a straight dress without a waist seam.

### What shoes go with a shift dress for work?

Low block heels, loafers or pointed flats keep a knee-length shift polished. With the hem above the knee, as in the photos, a slim heel balances the straight line; with a longer hem, flats work better.

### Can I have it at knee length or longer?

Yes. Enter the length you want on the measurement form; it is shown above the knee. At knee length it reads more formal and works better for the office.

### Can I change the pockets or add sleeves?

Yes. Write it in the comment box on the measurement form when you order, or email us with your order number. Patch pockets without flaps, no pockets, or cap sleeves are all possible, and the belt can be left off.

### Why does a cotton dress cost $399?

Because it is cut and sewn for you after you order, from your own measurements, rather than taken from a size run. The price includes a cotton test dress made first, so the waist, hips and length are settled before the final dress is cut. Over years of wear, the difference is a dress you reach for instead of one that needs alterations.

### Does chino twill wrinkle, and can I wash it?

Chino twill creases less than linen and holds a crisp line, though it softens and creases a little at the belt after a day’s wear. It is unlined and machine washable on a gentle cycle; iron it while still slightly damp.

### How do I measure without a tailor?

Measure bust, natural waist and hips, ideally with a helper, and enter the length from the shoulder to where you want the hem; we can guide you by video call or WhatsApp.

### How long does it take?

The cotton test dress takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; the final dress then takes 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after it ships.
