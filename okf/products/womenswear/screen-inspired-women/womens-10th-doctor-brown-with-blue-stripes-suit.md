## A Free Test Suit Before the Stripes Are Cut

Stripes are unforgiving. On a pinstripe suit every seam has to meet the stripe beside it, and a jacket that pulls across the bust bends the stripes into curves you notice from across a room. That is why the fit is settled on a test suit first, so the final pattern is right before the striped cloth is cut.

1

**Choose the stripe and the lining, then enter your measurements**  
 Pick brown with blue or blue with red stripes and a satin lining in the selector, then enter your measurements on the product page. Any change goes in the comment box of the measurement form.

2

**We make your test suit in cotton**  
 Your test suit takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch by trackable courier.

3

**Try it the way you will wear it**  
 With the shirt, tie and shoes you plan to wear, and the long coat over it if you have one. Check the buttoning point over the bust, the jacket length, the room through the hips and the trouser break, then tell us every change.

4

**Then we cut the pinstripe**  
 We correct the pattern and make the final suit in 3 to 4 weeks; it arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. The test suit is yours to keep.

## Questions About the Women’s 10th Doctor Suit

### Is this a good suit for a female 10th Doctor cosplay?

Yes, it is cut for exactly that: a women’s pattern, made to your measurements, with the four-button front, notch lapels and blue-on-brown stripes of the look. Unlike most 10th Doctor suits sold as costumes, it is lined, has a canvas front and is finished like a suit you can wear again.

### Is it the suit David Tennant wore?

No. It is our own women’s version inspired by the Tenth Doctor’s brown pinstripe suit, cut for a woman’s frame. It is not the screen garment and not a copy of it.

### What is the fabric, and are the stripes woven?

It is a woven cotton and polyester blend, pre-shrunk, with the stripes digitally printed rather than woven. Printing lets the stripe color and spacing follow the screen reference, and the blend holds its press well.

### What do I need to complete a Tenth Doctor cosplay?

The Tenth Doctor suit is the center of the Tenth Doctor outfit: add a white or pale blue shirt, a patterned tie, sneakers and a long brown coat. In brown with blue stripes, the Tenth Doctor brown suit is the one most people picture.

### Is there a blue version?

Yes, choose blue with red stripes in the selector for the Tenth Doctor’s blue suit. The cut and details are the same.

### Can I order only the jacket?

Please ask before ordering, with the Ask a question about this piece form below Add to Cart or by email, and we will tell you what is possible for your order.

### Can I change details, such as a skirt instead of trousers?

Yes, write it in the comment box of the measurement form or email us with your order number. Clients can ask for trousers or a skirt, a different leg shape or other changes, and the test suit follows what you ask for.

### How do I measure for the suit?

Use our **[women’s jacket measuring guide](https://baronboutique.com/womens-jacket-measuring-guide/)** and see the **[women’s suit fit guide](https://baronboutique.com/womens-suit-fit-guide/)** for how much room each fit leaves. If no one can help, measure a jacket and trousers that already fit you well and we can guide you by video call or WhatsApp.

### When will my suit arrive?

The test suit takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you approve the fit, the final suit takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch, so order as early as you can before a convention.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean only. Hang the jacket on a shaped hanger and the trousers by the hem between wears, and press with a cloth over the print rather than an iron directly on it.
