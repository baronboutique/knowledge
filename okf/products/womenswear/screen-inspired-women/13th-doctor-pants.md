Questions & Answers

## Questions About These Thirteenth Doctor Pants

### Which Doctor Who look are these pants based on?

The Thirteenth Doctor’s cropped, high-waisted trousers, as worn by Jodie Whittaker. These are our own screen-inspired recreation, made from screen references, not an official costume.

### What color are the Thirteenth Doctor’s trousers?

A deep blue green teal. Our Teal mohair wool is the color in the photos; Navy, Black and three grays are also available if you want a quieter version for everyday wear.

### How long are these pants?

They are cropped to mid-calf and finished with a 2-inch turn-up. You check the exact length on the free test pair and can ask for it longer or shorter.

### Can I wear 13th Doctor suspenders with them?

Yes. There are suspender buttons inside the 2-inch waistband, so button-on suspenders attach without clips.

### Are these good for cosplay and for everyday wear?

Yes. In Teal with suspenders they complete a Thirteenth Doctor cosplay; in Navy or a gray with a simple top they are high-waisted cropped trousers you can wear any day.

### What fabric are they made of?

A Super 140s mohair and wool blend. The mohair gives the cloth body and a soft sheen and helps the turn-ups hold their shape.

### Do you make the men’s version?

Yes. We make Thirteenth Doctor pants for men as a separate piece, cut to a man’s measurements with the same high waist and cropped leg.

### Are these trousers lined?

The front of each leg is lined to just below the knee in the lining color you choose. If wool makes you itch, ask for a full lining at no extra cost, or ask for them unlined.

### Can I machine wash these trousers?

Yes. Wash them cold on a gentle wool cycle and hang them to dry, or dry clean them if you prefer. Press the turn-ups after washing to keep them crisp.

### What does the price include?

The price shown above covers any of the 6 colors and any lining color, plus a free cotton test pair that is yours to keep, so the fit is confirmed before your cloth is cut.

### How long does it take to receive my trousers, and do you ship internationally?

Your test pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Both ship worldwide with tracking, and if you need them for an event, order as early as you can.
