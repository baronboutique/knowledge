## Questions & Answers

**Q:** Why are there so few good women's versions of this coat?  
**A:** Because the market treats the women's buyer as a Halloween shopper. Most major retailers sell a polyester blazer or printed jacket and call it a villain coat. Nobody has built the women's version as actual outerwear in real leather with proper construction. That is the gap this coat occupies. This is a serious garment, and the women's version should be too.

**Q:** What do the princess seams do that a standard back construction does not?  
**A:** Princess seams run vertically through the back from shoulder to hem, allowing the coat to be shaped at the waist and flared through the skirt. On a women's coat this creates a figure-defining silhouette, structured at the shoulders, fitted through the torso, dramatic through the lower half. A flat-back construction with side seams only hangs straight from the shoulders and adds bulk rather than shape. The princess seams are what make this coat look like it was made for the person wearing it.

**Q:** Why does the test coat matter so much for leather?  
**A:** Because leather cannot be corrected after cutting. A sleeve that lands at the wrong point on the wrist is permanent. A shoulder that sits too wide is permanent. With wool you can press and adjust. With leather, what is cut is what you wear. The test coat in cotton lets you check every element of the fit, including how the princess seams shape through the back, before any leather is touched. It is not optional for this garment.

**Q:** Can this be worn as everyday outerwear?  
**A:** Yes, and many customers order it specifically for that. In black cow Nappa with the princess-seam back and wide peak lapels, it reads as dramatic luxury outerwear. In Dark Brown Lambskin it reads as pure fashion outerwear. Several customers order it purely as a statement leather coat. The silhouette is the design.

**Q:** What is the difference between Cow Nappa and Lambskin for this coat?  
**A:** Cow Nappa is firmer and heavier, it holds the wide peak lapels and flared cuffs in shape without internal support, and gives the coat significant presence. Lambskin is softer and lighter, the coat drapes more and feels more relaxed. Both are full-grain genuine leather, not bonded or synthetic. Cow Nappa Black is the stronger choice for a bold statement; lambskin works better for a softer, more wearable everyday coat.

**Q:** Can I specify a coat length shorter or longer than mid-calf?  
**A:** Yes. Note your preferred coat length at checkout. The test coat process also gives you the opportunity to check the length on your actual frame before any leather is cut, and if it needs adjusting, we correct it before final production.
