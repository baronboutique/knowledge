Super 140s describes how fine the wool fiber is; tropical weight describes how light the cloth is woven. Together they give a smooth, matte black that drapes in long straight folds, without the weight of a winter coat. ## Questions About the Women’s Percival Graves Robe

### Is this the Percival Graves robe from Fantastic Beasts?

No, it is our own screen-inspired women’s version, with inspiration taken from Percival Graves in Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them. It is not official or licensed. It is a replica made in wool tailoring, cut to your measurements.

### Is it a costume or a real coat?

A real coat. Most wizard robes are polyester costumes that shine under light and crease into the same lines; this one is cut in Super 140s worsted wool with a canvas front and hand-finished buttonholes. It reads as tailoring at a convention and holds up for years.

### Will I be too hot in a long wool robe?

Much less than in a heavy coat. The cloth is Super 140s worsted wool at tropical weight, light and breathable, so it can be worn indoors through a full day.

### Is it a robe or a coat?

Both. It is cut and lined like a coat, with a canvas front, but it is open, light and long like a robe. People search for it as a wizard coat or a wizard trench coat; both describe it.

### Is it a black wizard cloak?

No. A cloak has no sleeves; this is a sleeved robe with a long shawl collar, white piping and wide vented cuffs.

### Is it cut for a woman?

Yes. It is cut on princess seams that follow the bust and waist, with a half belt at the back and a flared skirt. It is then fitted to you in the free test robe.

### Can I change the length or the lining?

Yes. Enter the length you want on the measurement form and choose another satin lining in the selector. Any other change goes in the comment box, or by email with your order number.

### How do I get the ankle length right?

Wear the shoes you will wear when you try the test robe. An ankle-length hem looks right only at one shoe height, so we set it on you before the wool is cut.

### Can I see the wool before ordering?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples of about 2 by 4 inches. They usually arrive within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 7003014 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How long does the robe take?

The test robe takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you approve it, the wool robe takes another 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after it ships, so order well before a convention.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean only, and not often. Hang it on a wide hanger with room around the hem, and keep the white piping away from makeup.
