## Questions About the Women’s 7th Doctor Utility Jacket

### Is this the 7th Doctor’s actual jacket?

No. It is our own women’s version inspired by the Seventh Doctor’s pale jacket, cut on a women’s pattern and made to your measurements. It is a tailored jacket of our own design, not a costume copy of the screen garment.

### Which color is closest to the 7th Doctor jacket?

Stone, the shade in our photos. Khaki is a little warmer and darker if you prefer a deeper tan.

### Can I wear it as an everyday utility jacket?

Yes. In stone or khaki it is a tan utility jacket for weekends; in navy blue it works as a utility blazer for the office.

### What is wool gabardine, and is it warm?

Gabardine is a tight twill weave; ours is Super 130s worsted wool. It is a mid-weight cloth for spring, autumn and mild winters, warm over a knit but light enough indoors, and the tight weave sheds light rain.

### What do the belt and double vents do?

The half belt across the back shapes the waist from behind. The double vents let the hem open when you sit or use the hip pockets, so the jacket does not ride up or pull.

### How many pockets does it have?

Six: two chest pockets and two large hip pockets, all with flaps, and two interior pockets inside the lining.

### Can I change the pockets or buttons?

Yes. Write it in the comment box of the measurement form, or email us with your order number; a second front button, pockets without flaps or brown instead of matching buttons are all possible.

### How do I measure for the jacket?

Use our **[women’s jacket measuring guide](https://baronboutique.com/womens-jacket-measuring-guide/)**, ideally with someone helping, and see the **[women’s jacket fit guide](https://baronboutique.com/womens-jacket-fit-guide/)** for how much room each fit leaves. On your own, measure a jacket that already fits you well and we can guide you by video call or WhatsApp.

### When will my jacket arrive?

The test jacket takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you approve the fit, the final jacket takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch, so order as early as you can before a convention or event.

### Is there a men’s version?

Yes, our **[men’s 7th Doctor jacket](https://baronboutique.com/7th-doctor-who-jacket/)** is cut on a men’s pattern with the same free test jacket.
