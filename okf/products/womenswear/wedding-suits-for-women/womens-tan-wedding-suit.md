## Fitted Before the Day, Not in the Week Before

Bridal suits for wedding days are photographed from every side, so small faults show, and for a bride a wedding suit for women has to look right at every angle. The corrections we make most on women’s suits are the bust position and tension across the back, and, in the trousers, folds below a smaller seat or pulling over a fuller back hip. A single-button jacket also needs its button placed close enough to stop the front gaping when you move.

If the cleanest fix would change the style, such as a dart through the front, we explain it, and you choose between the fit that follows your body exactly and the look in the photo exactly.

| Daylight | Tan glows in sun and garden light, where black looks heavy and white can glare in photos. |
| --- | --- |
| Season | An 8 ounce tropical wool breathes, so a tan suit for women works for beach, garden and city hall weddings from spring to early autumn. |
| After the wedding | Unlike a gown, the jacket and trousers can be worn again separately, with a white shirt or with jeans. |
| Who wears it | Brides, and also bridesmaids, mothers of the couple and guests who want a wedding suit for women instead of a dress. |

Searching for khaki suits for women, many brides mean the same family of colors; khaki is greener, tan is warmer. If a khaki suit is what you had in mind, women usually find tan the more flattering of the two in wedding photos. In our experience, cheap tan suits fail where light colors are least forgiving: the cloth marks and fades, and loose construction lets the front lose its shape by the end of a long day.

## Questions About the Women’s Tan Wedding Suit

### Can a bride wear a suit?

Yes. Bridal suits and pantsuits have become a popular alternative to a gown, especially for civil, outdoor and destination weddings. A suit gives you freedom to move and pockets, and it can be worn again afterward.

### When should I order a wedding suit?

As early as you can. The cotton test suit takes 3 to 4 weeks to make plus 5 to 10 days for delivery, the wedding suit takes another 3 to 4 weeks plus delivery, and you need a few days in between to try the test suit.

### Is tan a good color for a bride?

Yes, especially for daytime, outdoor and beach weddings, where it photographs warmly in natural light. If you want a traditional bridal color, choose white in the selector.

### What color suit should a wedding guest avoid?

As a guest, avoid white and ivory, which belong to the bride, and check before wearing black to a daytime ceremony. Tan, navy and gray are safe choices for guests.

### Is the cloth too warm for a summer wedding?

No. It is an 8 oz tropical wool that breathes, which is why it suits warm-weather weddings.

### Can I change the details for my wedding?

Yes. Put it in the comment box of the measurement form, or email us with your order number. Satin lapels, a second button, a skirt instead of trousers or a wider leg are all possible.

### Will the suit look good in photos?

Tan photographs warmly in daylight. The ventless back keeps a clean line from behind at the altar, and the test suit lets you check the fit in a mirror before the day.

### Can bridesmaids order matching suits?

Yes. Each person orders with her own measurements and the same cloth; for a group, ask through the Ask a question about this piece form below Add to Cart.

### Can I wear it after the wedding?

Yes. It is finished as a wedding suit, but the jacket and trousers work separately for summer events and work afterward.

### Can I wear it with a veil or flowers?

Yes. A short veil, a flower on the lapel or a silk scarf, as in the photo, all suit the tan.

### Can I see the tan before I order?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples of about 2 by 4 inches. They usually arrive within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 2600007 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How do I care for it after the wedding?

Have it dry cleaned before storing it. Keep it on a shaped hanger in a breathable bag and away from direct light, which fades light colors.
