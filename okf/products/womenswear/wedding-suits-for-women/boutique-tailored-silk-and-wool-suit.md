## A High Three-Button Jacket Has to Sit Exactly Right

A three-button jacket with a high stance shows every small error in fit: a top button a little too high presses on the chest, a little too low and the jacket loses its long, straight line. So before any of the wool and silk is cut, you try the whole suit in cotton.

A suit is two garments meeting one body, and few bodies follow a size chart. The test suits we see most often need work at the shoulders, across the back and at the seat: a fuller back hip with a flatter front, or a bust that does not follow the waist and hip measurements. Where the three-button style leaves no room for an extra dart, we ask you which matters more, the exact look in the photos or the exact fit, and correct the pattern your way.

2

**We cut a cotton version of the whole suit**  
 Jacket and trousers, made to your measurements, take 3 to 4 weeks and arrive 5 to 10 days after dispatch.

3

**Wear it the way you will wear the real one**  
 Sit in it, reach forward, walk in the shoes you plan to wear. On this suit the points to check are where the top button lands on the chest, how the high lapel lies over your collarbone, and the trouser length over your heel.

4

**The wool and silk is cut to match**  
 With your changes applied, the final suit takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and is with you 5 to 10 days after it ships. The cotton suit stays with you.

Day to Evening

## How a Wool and Silk Suit Changes With the Light

Wool gives the cloth its body: it springs back from creases, holds a pressed trouser line and lets the suit breathe through a long day. Silk adds a fine, soft luster and a cooler, smoother hand. At 30% silk the effect is subtle; in daylight this reads as an elegant women’s tailored suit, and under warm evening light the cloth shows a soft glow that a pure wool suit never has.

| At the office | Gray, navy or charcoal look like a classic women’s wool suit at a distance; only up close does the silk show. |
| --- | --- |
| At a wedding | Light gray, petrol blue or dark khaki with a silk blouse and tailored trousers is an elegant woman’s alternative to a dress, and many women’s silk suits for weddings are far shinier than this. |
| At dinner | Black or charcoal with the jacket closed works as evening wear without looking like a tuxedo. |

The trade-off: a silk blend suit is a little less hard-wearing than pure worsted wool. Wear it in rotation rather than every day and it will last for many years; worn daily, the seat and elbows will show polish sooner.

## Questions About the Women’s Wool and Silk Blend Suit

### Are wool and silk blend suits good?

Yes, if you want a suit with a soft sheen and a smoother drape than pure wool. The trade-offs are honest ones: silk is less springy than wool, so the suit needs a hanger and a rest day, and it shows water marks more easily. For a hard-working daily suit, pure worsted wool is tougher.

### What exactly is the fabric?

A Super 130s cloth of 70% worsted wool and 30% silk, so it carries more wool than a 50% wool women’s suit. Every fiber is natural; it is not a part-wool cloth padded out with polyester. The wool gives structure and crease recovery, which is why it works as a tailor made women’s business suit.

### Is it a silk suit or a wool suit?

Both, in one cloth. It behaves like a wool suit for structure and crease recovery, and the silk adds a soft sheen and a smoother feel against the skin.

### Does it shine?

Only a little. In daylight it reads as matte; under warm evening light the silk gives a soft glow, which is what makes it work from the office to dinner.

### Can I wear it to a wedding?

Yes. Lighter shades with a silk blouse make an elegant daytime wedding outfit, and darker shades suit evening ceremonies. The silk content gives it more occasion than a plain business suit.

### Why a high three-button jacket?

A high button stance and high-gorge lapels give a long, narrow line through the torso and frame a blouse neatly. It only works if the top button sits exactly right on your bust, which is why we check it on the test suit.

### Can I order it as a business suit?

Yes. Navy, charcoal and gray are the business choices, and the free test suit lets you check the fit before anything is cut.

### Is it warm?

It is an all-season weight that breathes well. In real heat a tropical wool suit is cooler; in winter, add a fine knit underneath.

### Can the style be changed?

Yes. Two buttons instead of three, a skirt instead of trousers, or a different trouser leg can be requested in the comment box of the measurement form, or by email with your order number.

### Can I see the wool and silk cloth first?

Yes. Our **[swatch service](https://baronboutique.com/swatch-service/)** sends up to four samples of about 2 by 4 inches. They usually arrive within 4 to 6 business days. Quote product number 2600004 and the colors you want to compare; the courier fee is credited toward your first order.

### How long does the wool and silk suit take?

The cotton test suit takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after it ships; the final suit then takes 3 to 4 weeks plus the same delivery time. Order as early as you can if you have a date.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean the jacket rarely and brush it after wearing. Rest it on a shaped hanger so the silk does not crease at the shoulders, and keep it away from rain.
