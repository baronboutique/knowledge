This is for the woman who understands that true power isn’t just projected; it’s felt. ### A Luxurious Alliance: Wool & Silk

The true character of this suit comes from its extraordinary fabric, a blend that delivers what no single material can.

- **The Best of Both Worlds:** We use a Super 130s’ fabric that is **70% fine worsted wool and 30% pure silk**. The wool provides structure, durability, and all-season breathability, while the silk lends a beautiful, fluid drape and a subtle, elegant luster that catches the light.
- **A Palette of 8 Sophisticated Colors:** Choose from a curated selection of 8 shades, including timeless grey and a rich, deep red, to build your power wardrobe.

### A Silhouette of Quiet Authority

This suit is architected with unique, sartorial details that signal a deep appreciation for classic tailoring.

- **The High-Stance Jacket:** A sharp, three-button jacket with a high stance closure creates a longer, leaner, and more commanding line than a standard blazer.
- **A Suit of Details:** From the traditional ticket pocket to the extreme high gorge notch lapels, every element is a nod to the art of high-end, bespoke tailoring.
- **Full Canvas Construction:** This suit is built with a full canvas front, the hallmark of true bespoke craftsmanship, ensuring it will mold to your body and only get better with time.

### Our Bespoke Promise: The Final Touch

A suit made from such a beautiful fabric must fit perfectly to realize its full potential. We guarantee a flawless silhouette by sending you a **free test suit**—a complete cotton version of both the jacket and trousers—made to your initial measurements. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect every aspect of the silhouette. It’s our commitment to delivering a true bespoke experience.

#### Caring For Your Investment

To preserve the beauty and unique character of your wool-silk blend, we recommend professional dry-cleaning only.
