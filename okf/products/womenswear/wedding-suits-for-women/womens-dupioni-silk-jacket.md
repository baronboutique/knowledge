## A Women’s Silk Blazer That Holds Its Shape

Most silk blazers and silk jackets for women are made from charmeuse or crepe: soft, fluid cloths that drape beautifully on a hanger and lose their shape on the body, especially at the shoulders and lapels. Dupioni is different. It is a crisp silk woven from uneven yarns, so it has a fine slubbed texture, a natural sheen and enough body to hold a tailored line.

Why Dupioni

## Dupioni Silk Jacket or a Soft Silk Blazer?

| Dupioni silk | Crisp, with a slubbed texture and a two-tone sheen from different colored warp and weft. Holds a lapel and a sculpted waist; the right silk for a tailored jacket. |
| --- | --- |
| Charmeuse or satin | Liquid and glossy. Lovely for a blouse or the unstructured silky jacket ladies often buy for summer, but it creases easily and collapses at the shoulders when tailored. |
| Silk crepe | Matte and drapey, closer to a dress fabric than a jacket cloth. |

The trade-offs are worth knowing. Dupioni creases at the elbows and the back after sitting; the creases soften on a hanger and a cool iron through a cloth removes them. Water can mark silk, so avoid wearing it in heavy rain, and keep perfume off the cloth.

## Questions About the Women’s Dupioni Silk Blazer

### What is dupioni silk?

A plain-weave silk made from uneven yarns, which gives it small slubs, a crisp hand and a natural sheen. It is firmer than most silks, which is what makes it suitable for a tailored jacket.

### Is dupioni silk good or bad?

Good for structure, poor for drape. Its crisp body holds lapels and a sculpted waist far better than charmeuse or crepe, which is why it suits a blazer; the same stiffness makes it wrong for a soft blouse. It also creases and marks with water, so it rewards a little care.

### Can I wear a dupioni blazer in summer?

Yes, for evenings and events. Silk breathes and the blazer is light, but dupioni is a woven, lined jacket rather than a beach layer, so it suits summer weddings and dinners more than hot afternoons outdoors.

### Does the color change in different light?

Yes. Many dupioni colors are woven with one color lengthwise and another across, so the silk shifts slightly as you move.

### Does it crease?

It creases at the elbows and back after sitting, as all dupioni does. Hang it after wearing and the creases soften; press with a cool iron through a cloth if needed.

### When would I wear it?

Weddings, celebrations, evenings and dinners. In a dark shade over a black top, it also works at the office.

### How do I care for it?
