The Cloth

## Super 160s Wool From Vitale Barberis Canonico

Super 160s describes how fine the wool fiber is: the higher the number, the finer the yarn and the smoother the cloth. At 160s, the worsted has a soft, almost silky hand and a drape that keeps a pencil skirt silhouette without stiffness. It is woven in a plain weave as a four-season cloth, so the same skirt works in an air-conditioned office in July and under a coat in January.

We name the mill on this skirt because the cloth is the point of it. A fine worsted is what separates a professional skirt from the pencil office skirt styles sold in stretch blends: it does not shine at the seat with wear, and it presses back to a clean line.

Which Gray

## Light, Medium, Steel or Dark Gray: How to Choose

The question clients ask us most about this skirt is how the grays differ. On a screen they can look alike; in a room they do not. From lightest to darkest:

Light Grey

The palest, near dove gray. Fresh with white and pastels.

Medium Grey

A true mid gray, neither warm nor cool; works with every jacket.

Steel Grey

Darker than medium, with a cool, slightly blue cast.

Dark Grey

Near charcoal; the most formal gray and the easiest to wear daily.

Black

The color in our photos.

Navy Blue

A clear navy, brighter than midnight.

Medium and dark gray are the two most often confused: medium is the gray of a classic suit trouser, dark sits close to charcoal and reads almost black in low light. Steel gray falls between them with a cool cast. Midnight blue, also in the selector, is darker than navy and can pass for black under office lighting. If you are unsure, ask us with the form below Add to Cart and we will describe the shades against your jacket or blouse.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Office Pencil Skirt

### What is the difference between medium gray, steel gray and dark gray?

Medium gray is a true mid gray, steel gray is a little darker with a cool, blue cast, and dark gray sits close to charcoal. Light gray is the palest. If you are choosing between two, tell us what you will wear it with through the question form below Add to Cart.

### What does Super 160s mean on a skirt?

It is a measure of fiber fineness: Super 160s wool is spun from very fine fibers, which gives a smooth, soft cloth with a clean drape. For a skirt it means a polished surface that holds its line; it should be hung and rested between wears rather than worn every day.

### Is this a good professional pencil skirt for interviews?

Yes. A knee-length pencil skirt in dark gray, navy or black wool is one of the safest choices for interviews and formal offices, and it pairs with a jacket in the same color for a skirt suit.

### Who makes the cloth?

Vitale Barberis Canonico, a wool mill with a very long history, known for fine worsteds. The skirt itself is cut and sewn by our tailors to your measurements.

### Can I order it as an office pencil mini skirt or a midi?

Yes. Enter the length you want on the measurement form. For a shorter skirt we keep enough ease at the hip so it stays down when you sit; for a longer one we lengthen the vent.

### Does the waistband keep a shirt tucked in?

Yes, there is a grip inside the 1.5 inch waistband that holds a blouse or shirt in place. The waistband closes with a button, hand-stitched and reinforced, above the hidden zipper.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean is recommended for a longer life. It can also be machine washed on a gentle cool cycle and hung to dry. Brush it after wearing and give it a day’s rest on a clip hanger.

### Can I add pockets or change the waistband?

Yes. Note what you want in the comment box when you fill in your measurements, or send us an email quoting your order number, and it goes into your pattern before the test skirt is cut.

### Is the test skirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, it is free, in cotton and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; your wool skirt then takes 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
