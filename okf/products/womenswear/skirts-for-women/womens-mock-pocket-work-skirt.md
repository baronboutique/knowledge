### An Investment in Superior Fabric: VBC Wool

A skirt this smart deserves an exceptional fabric. We use a world-class wool that outperforms and outlasts standard textiles.

- **From the Legendary Vitale Barberis Canonico Mill:** We use a Super 160s’ worsted wool from VBC, one of the oldest and most respected mills in Italy (est. 1663). This fabric is renowned for its incredibly smooth finish, beautiful drape, and four-season comfort.
- **A Palette of 7 Essential Colors:** Choose from a curated range of 7 sophisticated, versatile shades, including classic black, steel grey, and midnight blue—the essential colors for a power wardrobe.

### The Genius of a Clean Silhouette

The magic of this design is in its seamless integration of form and function.

- **Perfectly Placed Pockets:** Two slanted front pockets with clean, single-welt openings provide space for your essentials.
- **The Clean, Waistband-Free Design:** This skirt features a modern, streamlined look with a hidden back zipper and no waistband, creating a smooth, uninterrupted line from waist to hip—perfect for wearing with tucked-in blouses.
- **Designed for Confident Movement:** A deep center-back vent ensures you can walk, sit, and move with graceful ease.

### Our Bespoke Fit Promise

The success of a pocketed pencil skirt lies in ensuring it never pulls or bulges. We guarantee a flawless fit by sending you a **free test skirt** made to your initial measurements before we craft your final garment. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect the fit through your hips and waist, ensuring the pockets lay flat and the silhouette remains sharp. It is the definitive solution to the bulky pocket problem.

#### Caring For Your Skirt

To preserve the beauty and structure of your VBC wool, we recommend professional dry-cleaning only.
