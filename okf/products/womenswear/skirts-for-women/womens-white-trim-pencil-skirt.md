## The Wardrobe Staple, Reimagined

This is the power of a single, perfect detail. We’ve taken the most essential piece in a professional wardrobe—the black pencil skirt—and given it a sharp, graphic update. ### A High-Performance Fabric for a High-Performance Career

A true wardrobe cornerstone needs a fabric that can keep up. We use a modern, breathable wool chosen for its unique, all-day performance.

- **The Genius of Fresco Wool:** This Super 130s’ high-twist wool is an open-weave cloth renowned for its exceptional **natural wrinkle resistance and breathability**. It’s the ultimate fabric for looking polished and fresh, even after a long day of travel or back-to-back meetings.
- **A Palette of 10 Professional Colors:** While classic black with white trim is the hero, this skirt is available in 10 versatile shades to build out your professional wardrobe.

### Defined by a Single, Powerful Detail

The genius of this skirt is how a simple detail transforms the entire design, creating a look that is both unique and timeless.

- **The Piped-Trim Masterstroke:** Crisp, contrasting white piping meticulously outlines the high waistband, adding a sharp, graphic element that is both subtle and incredibly chic.
- **A Smarter Waistband:** The interior of the waistband is enforced with a grip—a high-end detail that keeps your blouses and shirts neatly tucked for a polished look that lasts all day.
- **Designed for Confident Movement:** A deep center-back vent ensures you can walk, sit, and move with graceful ease, defeating the restrictive nature of a typical pencil skirt.

### Our Bespoke Fit Promise

The power of a pencil skirt lies entirely in its fit. We eliminate the risk of pulling or pinching by sending you a **free test skirt** made to your initial measurements before we craft your final garment. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect the fit through your hips and waist. It is the definitive solution to the ill-fitting work skirt, ensuring your investment looks and feels truly custom-made.
