Free Test Skirt

## Placing the Lines on a Test Skirt

We make your skirt first in cotton, from your measurements, and send it to you free to keep. It is unlined; your satin lining goes only into the fresco skirt.

On a piped skirt the test garment matters more than usual, because the white lines show the fit to everyone. If the skirt is tight at the hip, the side lines bow outward; if the back is too long, the lines wander as they reach the vent. On the test skirt we look at where the lines would fall on your body, and move them in or out so they sit symmetrically and follow your shape.

| Effect | Frames the pencil skirt silhouette and draws the eye up and down rather than across |

Piping is a thin cord wrapped in cloth and sewn into a seam, so the line sits slightly raised and stays crisp through wear and cleaning. The contrast of white on black is what makes the shape: viewed from a distance, a black skirt with lines placed this way looks narrower at the waist than a plain one of the same size.

From behind, the pencil skirt back shows the two lines either side of the hidden zipper. They should sit at the same distance from the center all the way down; this is the first thing we check on your test skirt photos.

How to Wear It

## Styling a Black Pencil Skirt With White Trim

Let the skirt be the pattern. A plain white or black top keeps the lines clean; a fitted black knit makes the white piping the only detail, and a white blouse picks up the trim. For evenings, a silk top in a deep color and a slim heel turn the same skirt from office to dinner without changing anything else.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Piped Pencil Skirt

### Does the white piping make a skirt look slimmer?

Placed like this, yes. Two lines that curve in at the waist and run straight down draw the eye vertically and frame the narrowest point, so the skirt reads narrower than a plain black skirt of the same size.

### Will the white trim stay white?

Keep it clean the way you would a white shirt collar: dry clean is recommended, and if you machine wash it, use a cool gentle cycle with like colors so the black does not dull the piping.

### Can I have the piping in another color?

Ask us before ordering; write your request in the comment box on the measurement form or in the question form below Add to Cart, and we will tell you what we can do.

### Can the lines be placed wider or narrower?

Yes. The test skirt shows where the lines fall on your body, and we can move them in or out on the final skirt so they suit your shape.

### What should I wear with a black skirt with white trim?

A plain white or black top, a fitted knit or a black jacket that stops at the waist. Keep other patterns away so the piping stays the only detail.

### Is fresco wool good for warm weather?

Yes. High-twist fresco is woven open and lets air through, which makes it one of the cooler wools, and it sheds creases quickly after sitting.

### Can I change the length or add pockets?

Yes. Enter the length on the measurement form and write other changes, such as pockets, in the comment box there or by email with your order number.

### Is the test skirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, it is free, in cotton and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and reaches you 5 to 10 days after dispatch; your piped fresco skirt then takes 3 to 4 weeks plus 5 to 10 days for delivery. Order as early as you can.
