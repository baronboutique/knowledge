## A Classic, With a Twist

This is the answer for the woman who appreciates timeless style but desires a touch of the unexpected. We’ve taken the classic pencil skirt and given it a smart, functional, and stylish update: a sharp front kick pleat. It’s a design that is both professional and full of personality.

### An Investment in Superior Fabric: VBC Wool

A skirt this smart deserves an exceptional fabric. We use a world-class wool that outperforms and outlasts standard textiles.

- **From the Legendary Vitale Barberis Canonico Mill:** We use a Super 160s’ worsted wool from VBC, one of the oldest and most respected mills in Italy (est. 1663). This fabric is renowned for its incredibly smooth finish, beautiful drape, and four-season comfort.
- **A Palette of 7 Essential Colors:** Choose from a curated range of 7 sophisticated, versatile shades, each featuring a classic, contrasting waistband.

### The Genius of the Front Kick Pleat

This skirt is architected, not just sewn. Every detail serves to enhance its unique blend of style and function.

- **The Defining Detail:** A crisp, deep kick pleat at the front-center of the skirt adds a touch of menswear-inspired flair and provides complete freedom of movement—a stylish solution to the restrictive nature of a typical pencil skirt.
- **The Contrasting Waistband:** A sharp, contrasting waistband creates a clean, deliberate line and adds another layer of unique, sartorial detail.
- **A Smarter Interior:** The inside of the waistband is enforced with a grip, a high-end detail that keeps your blouses and shirts neatly tucked for a polished look that lasts all day.

### Our Bespoke Fit Promise

The success of a skirt with such unique details lies in a perfect fit. We guarantee a flawless result by sending you a **free test skirt** made to your initial measurements before we craft your final garment. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect the fit through your hips and waist, ensuring the kick pleat hangs straight and the silhouette is sharp. It’s our commitment to a perfect, personalized result.

#### Caring For Your Skirt

To preserve the beauty and structure of your VBC wool, we recommend professional dry-cleaning only.
