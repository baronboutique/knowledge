## Below the Knee Skirt Lengths, and Which One Suits You

Below the knee skirts cover a wide band, from just under the kneecap to mid-calf, and the same word means different things on different heights. In our photos the hem sits just below the knee. These are the three lengths clients ask us for most, and what each does:

| Just below the knee | The classic office length. Just below the knee skirts show the narrowing of the leg and read polished with any heel. |
| --- | --- |
| Midi skirt below knee, to mid-calf | More covered and more formal. Worth it with a heel or a boot; with a flat, the leg can look shorter. |
| At the knee | If you are petite, the knee itself can be the most flattering point; the test skirt tells you. |

Clients who need a modest length for work or faith often want the hem lower than a store skirt allows; give the length on the form and the vent is lengthened with it, so you can still walk at a normal pace. Among below knee skirts for women, length is the reason to have one made rather than bought.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Below the Knee Pencil Skirt

### Where should a below the knee skirt end?

For most women, just below the knee, where the leg starts to narrow, is the most flattering point, and it is the length in our photos. Mid-calf works too but asks for a heel or boot. Give your length on the measurement form and check it on the free test skirt with your own shoes.

### Is a below knee pencil skirt hard to walk in?

Not if the vent is right. The longer the skirt, the more the vent matters, so we lengthen it with the hem and check your stride on the test skirt. A pencil skirt below knee length with too short a vent is what makes people shuffle.

### What is the difference between a midi and a below the knee skirt?

Below the knee usually means just under the kneecap; midi usually means mid-calf. Brands use the words loosely, which is why we ask for a measurement instead of a size.

### Can I have it longer for a modest or religious dress code?

Yes. Enter the length you need on the measurement form; the vent is lengthened to match. If you prefer the vent closed or replaced with a pleat, write it in the comment box on the measurement form, or email us with your order number.

### What does the grip in the waistband do?

It is a strip inside the waistband that holds a tucked blouse or shirt in place, so it does not creep out over the day. The waistband is 2 inches tall and lined in cotton.

### Is Super 160s wool delicate?

It is fine and smooth, so treat it gently: hang it on a clip hanger, brush it, and rest it a day between wears. For a skirt it wears well, because a skirt sees far less rubbing than trouser knees or jacket elbows. Dry clean only.

### Is the test skirt really free?

Yes. It is cut in cotton from your measurements, sent at no charge, and yours to keep. It has no lining; your satin lining goes into the final skirt.

### How long will it take?

The test skirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you approve the fit, your wool skirt takes 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can if you have a date in mind.

### Can I change details such as the waistband height or add pockets?

Yes. Write the change in the comment box on the measurement form, or email us with your order number. The test skirt shows the change before the wool is cut.
