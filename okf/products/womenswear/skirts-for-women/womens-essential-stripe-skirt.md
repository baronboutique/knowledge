## A Test Skirt to Keep the Stripes Straight

We first make your skirt in cotton from your measurements and post it to you at no charge; you keep it. It is unlined, and your satin lining goes only into the striped skirt.

For a striped skirt the test garment answers one question above all: does the skirt hang straight on you? If it is tight across the seat or one hip sits higher, a striped cloth would show it as bent lines, so we look for pulling at the hip and a hem that rises at the back. The test skirt is plain cotton, yet the same tension shows as drag lines, and your photos tell us where to give ease.

Straight Stripes

## What a Good Striped Pencil Skirt Looks Like

Three things decide whether a striped pencil skirt looks tailored or cheap:

| Stripes plumb | Each stripe hangs vertical from waist to hem. Bowing at the hips means the skirt is too tight there |
| --- | --- |
| Matching at the back | Stripes meet at the center back seam and run on past the zipper and into the vent |
| Level hem | A hem that rises at the back tilts the stripes; extra length at the seat corrects it |

Pinstripe or chalk stripe? A pinstripe is a fine, sharp line, as here; a chalk stripe is softer and wider, like a line drawn in chalk. The fine line is the more formal and reads as a blue pinstripe skirt from across a room, plain navy up close only in very low light.

How to Wear It

## Styling a Navy Pinstripe Skirt

A navy blue striped skirt is easier to wear than it looks. Keep the top plain and let the stripe do the work: white, pale blue, pink or cream blouses, or a navy fine knit for a quieter look. As a navy blue skirt with white stripes, it pairs with a solid navy jacket for a suit look without a matching suit, and with a cream or camel coat in winter.

Avoid a second stripe near it unless it is far finer or far bolder; two pinstripes of a similar width compete. Shoes in navy, black or nude keep the leg line long under a knee-length hem.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Pinstripe Pencil Skirt

### Why do the stripes on some striped pencil skirts look curved?

Because the skirt is pulling. When a pencil skirt is tight over the hip or seat, the cloth stretches across and the stripes bow outward. A skirt cut to your measurements, with the fit checked on a test skirt, lets the stripes hang straight.

### Is this a blue pinstripe skirt or navy?

Navy: a deep blue with fine white pinstripes. In daylight it reads clearly blue; under office lighting it looks darker. If you want a lighter blue pin stripe skirt, ask us; the stripes on offer are in the selector.

### What is the difference between a pinstripe and a chalk stripe?

A pinstripe is a very fine, crisp line, as on this skirt. A chalk stripe is wider and softer-edged. Pinstripes read more formal and slimmer.

### What do I wear with a navy and white striped skirt?

Plain tops in white, pale blue, pink or cream, or a navy knit. A solid navy jacket makes it a suit look. Keep other patterns far finer or far bolder than the stripe.

### Is a blue and white striped skirt too bold for a conservative office?

Not with a pinstripe this fine. From a few steps away it reads as a quiet navy texture, and it is the stripe banks and law firms have worn for generations. Keep the top plain and it stays formal.

### Is merino and cashmere warm?

It is a year-round cloth rather than a winter one. Super 130s merino is fine and breathable, and the 2% cashmere adds softness more than warmth.

### Can I get it longer or with a slit instead of a vent?

Yes. Enter the length on the measurement form, and write any style change, such as a slit or pockets, in the comment box there or by email with your order number.

### How should I care for it?

Dry clean is recommended for a longer life; a gentle cool machine wash is possible. Hang it by the waistband and let it rest a day between wears.

### Is the test skirt really free, and how long does it take?

Yes. It is made in cotton, free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; your striped skirt then takes 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
