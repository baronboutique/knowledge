## The Authority of the Pinstripe, Masterfully Tailored

There are few patterns as powerful or as timeless as the pinstripe. It’s the uniform of confidence. But it has one unforgiving rule: the lines must be perfect. We have engineered the definitive striped pencil skirt, combining a luxurious wool-cashmere blend with a bespoke process that ensures every line is flawless and every fit is commanding.

### A Luxurious Blend of Merino and Cashmere

The foundation of a true investment piece is its fabric. This skirt is crafted from a premium blend chosen for its beauty and performance.

- **Merino Wool & Cashmere Blend:** We use a Super 130s’ fabric that is **98% fine merino wool and 2% cashmere**. This blend provides the beautiful drape and structure of wool with the exceptional softness and subtle luster of cashmere.
- **A Palette of Classic Stripes:** Choose from 4 sophisticated pinstripe combinations, including classic navy with white and charcoal with white.

### A Study in Clean, Powerful Lines

This skirt is engineered with thoughtful details that enhance both its look and its all-day wearability.

- **The Perfect Pencil Silhouette:** The classic, knee-length pencil cut creates a timeless, powerful silhouette that is always appropriate and effortlessly chic.
- **A Smarter Waistband:** The interior of the waistband is enforced with a grip—a high-end detail that keeps your blouses and shirts neatly tucked for a polished look that lasts all day.
- **Designed for Confident Movement:** A deep center-back vent ensures you can walk, sit, and move with graceful ease, defeating the restrictive nature of a typical pencil skirt.

### Our Guarantee: Perfect, Unbroken Lines

The power of a pinstripe is only unlocked by a perfect fit. We eliminate the risk of warped or curved stripes by sending you a **free test skirt** made to your initial measurements before we craft your final garment. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect the fit through your hips and waist, ensuring the stripes hang perfectly straight and true. It’s our commitment to a flawless result.

#### Caring For Your Skirt

To preserve its beauty and structure, we recommend professional dry-cleaning only.
