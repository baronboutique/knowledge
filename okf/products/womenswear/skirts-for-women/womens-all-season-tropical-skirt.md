## The Power Skirt, Redefined for Reality

The high-waisted pencil skirt is the uniform of corporate power. But too often, that power comes at the cost of comfort. This is the end of that compromise. We have re-engineered this iconic silhouette with a high-performance fabric and a bespoke fit, creating a skirt that feels as commanding as it looks.

### The Intelligence of Tropical Wool

A true wardrobe workhorse needs a fabric that can perform in any environment. We use a modern, breathable wool chosen for its unique properties.

- **A Four-Season Fabric:** We use a Super 140s’ **8oz tropical wool**, an open-weave cloth renowned for its exceptional breathability that keeps you cool in the summer, while the natural insulation of wool provides warmth in the winter.
- **Wrinkle-Resistant by Nature:** The high-twist nature of tropical wool makes it naturally resistant to wrinkling, ensuring you look sharp and polished from your first meeting to your last.
- **A Palette of 13 Professional Colors:** Choose from a wide range of 13 sophisticated shades, including classic black, oxford blue, and a variety of versatile greys.

### Architected for a Commanding Presence

This skirt is engineered with thoughtful details that enhance both its powerful look and its all-day wearability.

- **The Power of a High Waist:** A clean, 1.5-inch tall waistband sits at your natural waist, creating a strong, defined line and a beautiful, elongating effect.
- **A Smarter Interior:** The inside of the waistband is enforced with a grip—a high-end detail that keeps your blouses and shirts neatly tucked for a polished look that lasts all day.
- **Designed for Confident Movement:** A deep center-back vent ensures you can walk, sit, and move with graceful ease, defeating the restrictive nature of a typical pencil skirt.

### Our Commitment to a Perfect Fit

A power silhouette demands a perfect fit. We eliminate the risk of pulling or pinching by sending you a **free test skirt** made to your initial measurements before we craft your final garment. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect the fit through your hips and waist, ensuring the final skirt is sculpted perfectly for your body.

#### Caring For Your Wool

To maintain its beauty and structure, we recommend professional dry-cleaning only.
