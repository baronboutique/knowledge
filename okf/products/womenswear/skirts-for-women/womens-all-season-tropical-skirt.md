The High Waist

## Why a High Waisted Knee Length Skirt Works

High waisted means the top of the skirt sits at the natural waist, the narrowest part of the torso, usually an inch or two above the navel. Most ready-made skirts sit lower, at the hip bone, because a lower rise forgives more sizes. A high waist knee length skirt only looks right when it is cut to that exact point, which is why we take the waist measurement at its narrowest and check it on the test skirt.

Worn with a shirt tucked in, as in our photo, the high waist makes the legs look longer and gives a clean line to a blazer that stops at the waist. It is a strong shape for a knee length business skirt, and it is the shape clients ask for when they want a skirt to carry a whole outfit.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the High Waisted Pencil Skirt

### Where should a high waisted skirt sit?

At the natural waist, the narrowest part of your torso, usually an inch or two above the navel. If it sits lower it is a mid-rise skirt; if it sits higher it will ride up when you sit.

### Is a high waist knee length pencil skirt flattering on short women?

Usually yes. The high waist lengthens the line from the waist down, and a hem at or just above the knee keeps the proportion. Petite clients often take the hem slightly shorter; the test skirt shows the right point.

### What are the usual pencil skirt lengths?

Above the knee, at the knee and just below the knee are the three office lengths; midi to mid-calf is the longer option. This skirt is made to the length you enter on the measurement form.

### Is tropical wool too thin for winter?

It is light, about 8 oz, but worn with tights and a jacket it works through a mild winter and in heated offices. For the coldest months a heavier wool is warmer.

### Will the waistband keep my shirt tucked in?

Yes. There is a grip inside the 1.5 inch waistband that holds a shirt or blouse in place, and the inside face is lined in cotton.

### Can I add pockets, change the waistband or the vent?

Yes. Put the change in the comment box on the measurement form, or email us with your order number, and we cut it into your pattern.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean is recommended for a longer life; a gentle cool machine wash is possible. Hang it overnight and light creases relax out of the open weave.

### Is the test skirt free, and how long does the whole process take?

Yes, the cotton test skirt is free and yours to keep. It is made in 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; after you approve the fit, the wool skirt is made in 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
