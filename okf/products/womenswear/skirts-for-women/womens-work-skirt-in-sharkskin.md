The Back

## Pencil Skirt Back View: What a Good Fit Looks Like

In a well-fitted pencil skirt back view, the zipper runs straight down the center, the cloth falls cleanly from the fullest point of the seat to the hem, and the vent stays closed while you stand still. There are no lines pulling across the hips and no fold of loose cloth hanging below the seat.

| Too large at the seat | Cloth sags in a soft fold below the seat and the zipper ripples, as in our client’s first test photo. The fix is to take in the center back, more at the fullest point and less toward the hem. |
| --- | --- |
| Too tight at the seat | Horizontal pulls across the hips, the vent pulls open when you stand, and the skirt rides up every time you sit. The fix is more room through the seat, not a bigger waist. |
| Waist too high or too low | The waistband rolls or bites when you sit. The waist position is set on the test skirt, and a taller waistband can be cut if you prefer one. |

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the High-Waisted Pencil Skirt

### What should the back of a pencil skirt look like?

The zipper should run straight, the cloth should fall cleanly from the seat to the hem, and the vent should stay closed while you stand. A fold of loose cloth below the seat means it is too large there; lines across the hips or a vent that pulls open mean it is too tight.

### Why is my pencil skirt loose at the back below the seat?

Ready-made skirts are cut for an average curve between waist and hip, so if your seat is less full or sits higher, the extra cloth collects below it. The fix is to take in the center back, more at the fullest point and less toward the hem, which is exactly the correction our client marked on her test skirt.

### Is the test skirt really free, and what does it check?

Yes, it is included in the price and yours to keep. It is cut from your pattern in cotton so you can check the waist position, the seat, the vent and the length at home before we cut the sharkskin wool.

### Can I have a taller waistband?

Yes. The standard waistband is 1.5 inches tall; write the height you want in the comment box of the measurement form, or email us with your order number. Our client in the photos chose 1.75 inches.

### Does sharkskin wool stretch?

No, it is 100% worsted wool with no elastane, which is why it keeps a sharp line and does not bag at the seat after a day of sitting. The ease comes from the cut and the center back vent, so the fit at the seat matters more than in a stretch skirt.

### How long should a pencil skirt for work be?

Ours is cut to the knee, which is the easiest length to sit, walk and climb stairs in at the office. You can enter any length on the measurement form and check it on the test skirt with the shoes you will wear.

### Will my blouse stay tucked in?

Yes, that is what the grip inside the waistband is for. It holds a blouse or shirt in place so you do not have to keep tucking it back in.

### Can I add pockets?

Yes, ask for them in the comment box of the measurement form or by email with your order number. We leave them off by default because they add bulk at the hip in a fitted skirt.

### How do I care for it?

It is machine washable as well as dry cleanable, but we recommend dry cleaning to keep the press and the line of the wool. Between wears, hang it by the waistband and let it rest a day.

### How do I measure for a pencil skirt?

Follow our **[women’s skirt measuring guide](https://baronboutique.com/womens-skirt-measuring-guide/)**, ideally with someone helping, and see the **[women’s skirt fit guide](https://baronboutique.com/womens-skirt-fit-guide/)** for how much room each fit leaves. On your own, take your waist and hips and measure a skirt that already fits you well; we can guide you by video call or WhatsApp.

### When will my skirt arrive?

The test skirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you approve the fit, the final skirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch, so order as early as you can before you need it.
