## The Power Silhouette, Engineered for Comfort

The high-waisted pencil skirt is a weapon of confidence, but too often it’s a choice that sacrifices comfort for style. This is the end of that compromise. We have taken this iconic power silhouette and engineered it for real life, combining a luxurious, breathable fabric with a truly bespoke process that guarantees a flawless fit you can actually wear all day.

### A Foundation of Sharkskin Wool

The secret to a comfortable yet structured skirt is its fabric. We use a premium worsted wool with a unique weave, chosen for its beauty and its performance.

- **The Sharkskin Difference:** This Super 120s’ worsted wool is woven in a “sharkskin” pattern, giving it a subtle two-tone luster and a remarkably smooth finish. It’s lightweight, breathable, and drapes beautifully to create a sharp, clean line.
- **A Palette of 7 Modern Neutrals:** Choose from a sophisticated range of 7 versatile shades, including classic black, slate blue, and a sharp petrol blue.

### Details That Define All-Day Wearability

This skirt is architected, not just sewn. Every detail serves to enhance its comfort and practicality without sacrificing its powerful shape.

- **The High-Waisted Cut:** The silhouette begins at your natural waist, creating a beautiful, elongating effect that is both classic and incredibly flattering.
- **A Smarter Waistband:** The interior of the waistband is enforced with a grip—a high-end detail that keeps your blouses and shirts neatly tucked in for a polished look that lasts from your first meeting to your last.
- **Designed for Movement:** A deep center-back vent ensures you can walk, sit, and move with graceful ease, defeating the restrictive nature of a typical pencil skirt.

### Our Bespoke Fit Promise

The success of a pencil skirt lies entirely in how it fits your unique curves. We eliminate the risk of pulling or pinching by sending you a **free test skirt** made to your initial measurements before we craft your final garment. This personal fitting, done from the comfort of your home, allows us to perfect the fit through the hips and waist, ensuring the final skirt is sculpted perfectly for your body. It’s the definitive solution to the ill-fitting power skirt.

#### Caring For Your Skirt

To maintain its beauty and structure, we recommend professional dry-cleaning only.
