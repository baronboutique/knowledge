## Test the Pockets Before the Wool Is Cut

Before we cut your wool, we send you the skirt in cotton, made from your measurements, free and yours to keep. It is unlined; the satin you choose goes into the wool skirt.

The Pockets

## A Pencil Skirt With Pockets That Lie Flat

A pencil skirt with pockets works only when three things line up: the slant of the opening, the welt that holds the edge, and enough ease over the hip that the pocket is not stretched open. Here the pocket bags sit inside the lining, so nothing shows through the wool.

| What they hold | A phone, keys, a card or a lipstick; not a full wallet, which would spoil the line |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | Slanted, finished with a single welt so the edge stays crisp |
| Flat when standing | Yes, if the hip measurement is right; the test skirt confirms it |
| Remove them? | Yes. Ask for a plain front in the comment box on the measurement form |

If you have been looking for work skirts with pockets that do not look like casual wear, this is the difference between a welt pocket and a patch pocket: a welt is cut into the skirt and finished from inside, the way pockets are made on a suit jacket, so it reads as tailoring rather than utility.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Skirt With Pockets

### Do skirts have pockets?

Many fitted skirts do not, because a pocket on a close cut tends to gape or bulge. This one does: two real slanted welt pockets, built so they lie flat when the hip measurement is right.

### Will the pockets make my hips look wider?

Not when they are cut on a slant and the skirt has enough ease over the hip. Pockets look bulky when the skirt is tight and pulls them open, which is exactly what the free test skirt checks before the wool is cut.

### What can I carry in them?

A phone, keys, cards or a lipstick. Heavy or thick items such as a full wallet will spoil the line of any pencil skirt.

### What are some outfit ideas for styling a knee-length black skirt with pockets for both casual and professional occasions?

For work, a silk blouse or fine knit tucked in, with a blazer and heels; the no-waistband finish keeps the tuck smooth. For casual days, a striped tee or a crew-neck sweater, loose over the waist, with loafers or clean sneakers, hands in the pockets.

### What is the right work skirt length?

At the knee or just above or below it suits nearly every office. Our default is knee length; you can enter any length on the measurement form, from a pencil skirt above the knee to just below it.

### Can I have the pockets removed, or a waistband added?

Yes. Write it in the comment box on the measurement form, or email us with your order number, and the change goes into your pattern before the test skirt.

### Which knee length office skirt styles suit a formal workplace?

Yes. A knee-length pencil skirt in black, navy or dark gray wool, with welt pockets that sit flat, reads as tailored rather than casual and works with a matching jacket.

### How do I care for it?

Dry clean is recommended for a longer life, and a gentle cool machine wash is possible. Empty the pockets before hanging, and hang it from the waist on a clip hanger.

### Is the test skirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, it is free, in cotton and yours to keep. Making it takes 3 to 4 weeks, and it reaches you 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you approve the fit, the wool skirt takes another 3 to 4 weeks and 5 to 10 days to arrive, so order as early as you can.
