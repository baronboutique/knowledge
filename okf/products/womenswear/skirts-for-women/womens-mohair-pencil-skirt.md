Not a Fuzzy Mohair

## A Mohair Skirt Woven Like Suiting, Not Knitted

Mohair comes from the Angora goat. Spun loosely and brushed, it makes the fluffy knits most people picture. Spun tightly and woven with wool, as here, it makes a crisp, slightly lustrous cloth that resists creasing and holds a sharp edge. That is why the same blend appears in tailoring rather than knitwear.

| Brushed mohair skirt | Knitted, fuzzy, warm, usually A-line or pull-on with an elastic waist; can shed and pill |
| --- | --- |
| This mohair skirt | Woven mohair and wool, smooth face, cool to wear, holds a pencil skirt silhouette |
| Plain wool pencil skirt | Softer drape; creases set more easily after sitting |

If you want a gray wool skirt that looks pressed at six in the evening, or a gray skirt for women who travel for work, the mohair is the reason to choose this one over a plain wool pencil skirt. It feels a little crisper to the hand at first and softens with wear.

From Behind

## The Pencil Skirt Back: What a Good Fit Looks Like

Look at the pencil skirt from behind before you buy one. On a good pencil skirt back, the waistband runs level across the back, the zipper hangs straight, and the cloth falls clean from the seat to the hem without a fold. In a skirt back view like ours, the back seam should run plumb; if it swings to one side, the hip is uneven and the pattern needs adjusting.

The pencil skirt silhouette depends on that line. A gray straight skirt that pulls across the seat or bags beneath it reads as cheap from across a room, however fine the cloth. That is the part of the fit the test skirt photos check first.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Mohair Pencil Skirt

### Is a mohair skirt itchy or fuzzy?

Not this one. Fuzzy mohair skirts are knitted from brushed yarn; this is woven from tightly spun mohair and wool, so the surface is smooth, and it is fully lined in satin, so the cloth does not sit against your skin.

### What is the difference between mohair and wool in a skirt?

Mohair adds spring and a light sheen. A mohair and wool cloth recovers from creases faster than plain wool and keeps a crisper edge, while the wool gives it body and drape. The result looks pressed for longer.

### Is mohair good for summer?

Woven mohair is one of the cooler suiting cloths, which is why it has long been used for summer suits. The open structure of the yarn lets air through, and in a light gray the skirt reads cool too.

### What should I wear with a gray pencil skirt?

It is the gray pencil skirt women reach for because it goes with almost anything. White, pale blue, pink and black tops all work with gray, and so do navy and burgundy knits. For a suit look, wear it with a jacket in a darker gray or navy; for a long gray pencil skirt to mid-calf, choose a heel or a slim boot.

### Can I have a long gray pencil skirt instead of knee length?

Yes. Enter the length you want on the measurement form; the vent is lengthened with the hem so you can still walk freely. The free test skirt shows the length on you before the mohair is cut.

### How should a pencil skirt fit from behind?

Level at the waist, smooth over the seat, and falling straight to the hem without a fold under the seat or pulling across the hips. The back zipper and seam should hang plumb.

### Does the waistband keep a blouse tucked in?

Yes. The 2 inch waistband has a grip inside that holds a shirt or blouse in place, and the waistband is lined in cotton.

### How do I care for a mohair pencil skirt?

Dry clean is recommended because it keeps the cloth and lining in shape for longer, but the skirt can also be machine washed on a gentle cool cycle and hung to dry. Brush it between wears and let creases hang out overnight.

### Is the test skirt really free, and how long does it all take?

Yes, the cotton test skirt is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; the mohair skirt then takes 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.

### Can I change the waistband, add pockets or a slit?

Yes. Write the change in the comment box on the measurement form, or email us with your order number, and we build it into your pattern.
