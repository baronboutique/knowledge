No Waistband

## Why a Black Office Skirt Women Tuck Into Has No Waistband

A waistband adds a band of cloth, a seam and often a button at the waist. Here there is none: the skirt ends at the waist with a facing turned inside, so the top edge lies flat. That is the reason clients choose it to tuck into, where most ladies’ skirts in shops add a band. A fine knit or a silk blouse goes in without a ridge, and a belt, if you want one, sits on a clean surface.

The trade-off is honest. A skirt with no waistband has nothing to grip a blouse, and there is no grip inside; if you want a tall waistband with a grip, our midi pencil skirt has one, and a waistband can be added here on request. Fit at the waist matters more without a band, which is exactly what the test skirt checks.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About This Black Work Skirt

### What is the appropriate skirt length for work?

For most offices, a pencil skirt that reaches the knee or sits just above it is safe everywhere, and just below the knee is the more formal choice. Very short and floor length both draw attention. On this skirt you enter the length you want on the measurement form, and the free test skirt lets you see it with your own shoes before the fresco is cut.

### Does a work skirt without stretch let me sit and walk comfortably?

Yes, if it is cut to you. Stretch skirts are made in fixed sizes and rely on the fabric to give; this one gets its ease from the pattern, with room at the hip and a center back vent at the hem. The test skirt is where we check you can sit, climb stairs and walk without the skirt riding up.

### Is fresco wool good for a hot office or travel?

It is one of the better wools for both. The high-twist yarn and open weave let air through and help creases drop out, which is why fresco has long been chosen for summer suits. It is still wool, so it also stays comfortable in air conditioning.

### What is a business casual skirt women can wear every day?

A plain pencil skirt in a dark solid cloth, at the knee, is the business casual skirt women return to most, because it works with a blazer on Monday and a knit on Friday. Black, navy and charcoal in this fresco cover nearly everything; as a business casual black skirt it also takes a sweater and flat shoes, and a self stripe adds texture without looking formal.

### Why does this skirt have no waistband?

So the line from waist to hip stays smooth under a tucked top, with no band or button showing through a fine knit. The waist is finished with a facing inside. If you prefer a waistband, write it in the comment box on the measurement form, or email us with your order number.

### Can I change the length, add pockets or close the vent?

Yes. Write the change in the comment box on the measurement form when you order, or email us with your order number, and we include it in the pattern. The test skirt shows the change before the final skirt is made.

### Is the test skirt really free?

Yes. It is made in cotton from your measurements, sent to you at no charge, and yours to keep. It is unlined; your satin lining goes into the final skirt only.

### How long does it take?

The test skirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Once you approve the fit, the final skirt takes another 3 to 4 weeks and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. If you need it for a new job or a date, order as early as you can.

### How do I care for a fresco wool skirt?

Dry clean only, and not too often: brush it, hang it on a clip hanger and let it rest a day between wears, and most creases fall out on their own. Steam the hem lightly if it needs it.

### Which work-appropriate skirts suit a strict dress code?

A plain pencil skirt at or just below the knee, in black, navy or charcoal, fits even conservative codes; it is the black pencil skirt office wear has relied on for decades. Black dress skirts for women with slits, leather or flare are better kept for evenings.

### Is this a good black pencil skirt for work if I am between sizes?

Being between sizes is the usual reason clients come to us. There is no size chart here: the skirt is cut from your waist, hip and length, and the test skirt corrects what measurements miss, such as a fuller seat or a sway back.
