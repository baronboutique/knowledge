The Cashmere

## What Makes This Cashmere Soft, Light and Warm

Warmth in cashmere comes from fineness, not weight. Very fine fibers trap still air close to your skin, which is how a 70 gram scarf can feel warm around your neck. The close-up below shows the open handwoven weave of this scarf.

| 14 to 16 microns | Under US labeling rules, cashmere must average 19 microns or finer. This fiber is finer still, so it feels soft rather than prickly. |
| --- | --- |
| 35 to 55 mm staple | Longer fibers hold in the yarn better, so the scarf pills and sheds less as it ages. |
| 200/2 yarn | Two very fine threads twisted together give strength while keeping the scarf soft enough to knot without bulk. |
| About 70 g | Folds small enough for a handbag when you do not need it. |

To test any scarf at home, see our **[guide to telling real cashmere from a blend](https://baronboutique.com/aquamarine-blue-handwoven-cashmere-scarf-shawl/#bb-real)**.

Care

## Caring for a Pastel Cashmere Scarf

Hand wash in cold or lukewarm water with a mild detergent or wool wash, soak for 8 to 12 minutes, press the water through without wringing, rinse in cool water and dry it flat on a towel out of direct sun. Pastels fade in strong sun, so dry and store it in the shade.

Light colors show makeup at the neckline; dab marks with cool water before they set. For storage, fold it clean with cedar or lavender.

The open weave can catch on rings, bracelets, zips and bag straps. If a thread pulls, ease it back from the other side with a blunt needle. Never cut it.
