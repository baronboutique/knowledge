How to Drape It

## How to Wear a Draped Scarf: Four Ways, Step by Step

A draped scarf looks effortless when it is wide and light. At 40 x 80 inches this one is both, so a cashmere scarf drape takes seconds. These are the four ways it is worn in the photos.

The Open Drape

Hold the scarf open at full width behind your back and let it fall over both arms. As a draped shawl it moves with you and shows the sheer weave in light.

The Long Neck Drape

Fold it lengthwise, place it around the back of your neck and let both ends hang straight down the front. The simplest scarf drape, and the longest line.

The Neck Loop

Gather it narrow, wrap it twice around your neck and tuck the ends inside. Worn this way it becomes a yellow neck scarf that frames the face and keeps you warm.

Over the Shoulders

Drape it over both shoulders, then throw one end over the opposite shoulder. A cashmere shawl draped over shoulders like this stays put and leaves your hands free.

Searching for an elegant shawl draped over shoulders, or a scarf draped over shoulders, for an evening? Fold it in half lengthwise first, so the shawl scarf draped over shoulders looks neat rather than bulky. A cashmere stole draped over shoulders is warmer than a silk stole draped over shoulders, and it holds its folds better.

The Cashmere

## What Makes This Cashmere Soft, Light and Warm

Warmth in cashmere comes from fineness, not weight. Very fine fibers trap still air close to your skin, which is how a 70 gram scarf can feel warm around your neck.

| 14 to 16 microns | Under US labeling rules, cashmere must average 19 microns or finer. This fiber is finer still, so it feels soft rather than prickly. |
| --- | --- |
| 35 to 55 mm staple | Longer fibers hold in the yarn better, so the scarf pills and sheds less as it ages. |
| 200/2 yarn | Two very fine threads twisted together give strength while keeping the scarf soft enough to knot without bulk. |
| About 70 g | Folds small enough for a handbag when you do not need it. |

Looking for a cashmere pashmina scarf draped like this? Pashmina is the traditional name for very fine cashmere, but shops today often use it for blends, so check the label: this one is 100% cashmere. Compared with a silk scarf, elegant drape is the one thing they share; cashmere is the warmer of the two. Care

## Caring for a Light-Colored Cashmere Scarf

Hand wash in cold or lukewarm water with a mild detergent or wool wash, soak for 8 to 12 minutes, press the water through without wringing, rinse in cool water and dry it flat on a towel out of direct sun. Strong sunlight fades bright colors over time, so dry and store it in the shade.

Yellow shows makeup and marks at the neckline more than dark colors. Dab marks with cool water as soon as you see them, before they set. For storage, fold it clean with cedar or lavender.

The open weave can catch on rings, bracelets, zips and bag straps. If a thread pulls, ease it back from the other side with a blunt needle. Never cut it.
