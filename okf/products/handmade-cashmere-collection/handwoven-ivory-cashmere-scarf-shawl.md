Ivory or White

## What Color Is Ivory, and Is It White?

Ivory is white with a warm, creamy undertone. Next to bright white it looks softer and slightly yellow; next to cream it looks lighter. If you were searching for an off white scarf, elegant and warm rather than stark, or a cream cashmere scarf, elegant enough for evening, this is the color. A warm ivory scarf flatters most skin tones better than pure white, because it is less harsh at the face.

Choosing an ivory scarf color to match is simple: hold the scarf against what you plan to wear. Ivory matches ivory, cream and champagne fabrics; against a bright white dress it will read as cream.

In fashion, an ivory scarf goes with almost any outfit. An ivory scarf outfit can be tone on tone, as in the photos, or set against black, navy or camel.

The Cashmere

## What Makes This Cashmere Soft, Light and Warm

Warmth in cashmere comes from fineness, not weight. Very fine fibers trap still air close to your skin, which is how a 70 gram scarf can feel warm around your neck. That fineness is also why a cashmere scarf is a soft fabric to wear all day.

| 14 to 16 microns | Under US labeling rules, cashmere must average 19 microns or finer. This fiber is finer still, so it feels soft rather than prickly. |
| --- | --- |
| 35 to 55 mm staple | Longer fibers hold in the yarn better, so the scarf pills and sheds less as it ages. |
| 200/2 yarn | Two very fine threads twisted together give strength while keeping the scarf soft enough to knot without bulk. |
| About 70 g | Folds small enough for a handbag when you do not need it. |

How should cashmere scarf fabric feel? Soft and slightly hazy, never slick or shiny; a hard sheen often means a synthetic blend. To test any scarf at home, see our **[guide to telling real cashmere from a blend](https://baronboutique.com/aquamarine-blue-handwoven-cashmere-scarf-shawl/#bb-real)**.

Care

## Caring for an Ivory Cashmere Shawl

Ivory shows makeup, foundation and marks more than any other color. Dab marks with cool water as soon as you see them, before they set, and wear it over a collar rather than against makeup where you can.

Hand wash in cold or lukewarm water with a mild detergent or wool wash, soak for 8 to 12 minutes, press the water through without wringing, rinse in cool water and dry it flat on a towel out of direct sun. Store it folded, clean and wrapped in plain cotton, away from light, so the ivory does not yellow.

The open weave can catch on rings, bracelets, zips and bag straps. If a thread pulls, ease it back from the other side with a blunt needle. Never cut it.
