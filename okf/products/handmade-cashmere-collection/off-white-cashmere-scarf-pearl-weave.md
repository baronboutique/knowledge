The Texture

## What Is a Pearl Weave?

Pearl weave is our name for the small, raised texture on this scarf: tiny rounded points across the surface, a little like seed pearls. It comes from the weave and the thick two-ply yarn, not from anything sewn on, so there are no pearls attached. Up close, as a cashmere shawl texture close up shows, the cashmere scarf texture is soft and nubbly; from a distance it reads as a quiet, matte off white.

If you searched for a pearl scarf or a white pearl scarf, this is a cashmere scarf named for its texture, not a scarf with pearls on it.

Care

## Caring for a Medium-Weight Cashmere Scarf

Hand wash in cold or lukewarm water with a mild detergent or wool wash, soak for 8 to 12 minutes, press the water through without wringing, rinse in cool water and dry it flat on a towel out of direct sun. A thick scarf holds more water, so roll it in a dry towel to press it out before laying it flat.

Off white shows makeup at the neckline; dab marks with cool water before they set. For storage, fold it clean with cedar or lavender, away from light.
