## What Makes This a Fine Cashmere Shawl

The quality of a cashmere shawl can be measured, not just described. These are the numbers for this one and what they mean when you wear it.

| 14 to 16 microns | The fiber is finer than the US legal limit for cashmere, which is an average of 19 microns. Finer fiber feels softer on bare shoulders and the back of the neck. |
| --- | --- |
| 35 to 55 mm staple | Long fibers stay locked into the yarn, so the shawl sheds and pills less over the years. |

Care

## Caring for a Deep Green Cashmere Shawl

Hand wash it in cold or lukewarm water with a mild detergent or wool wash. Soak for 8 to 12 minutes, press the water through gently without wringing, rinse in cool water and lay it flat on a towel to dry. A steamer or a low iron takes out creases.

Two habits keep a deep color deep. Wash it on its own the first time, because any strongly dyed fiber can release a little color in its first wash. And keep it out of direct sun when drying and storing, since strong sunlight fades dyed fiber over time. Between wears hang it somewhere airy, and store it clean and folded with cedar or lavender.

If a thread catches on a ring or a clasp, ease it back into the weave from the other side with a blunt needle. Never cut it.
