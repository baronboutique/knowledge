How to read a yarn count: the first number is how fine each thread is (higher means finer), the second is how many threads are twisted together. A 200/2 yarn is very fine, so it weaves a sheer scarf; a 26/2 yarn is much thicker, so it weaves a dense, warm one. All three use the same 14 to 16 micron cashmere fiber.

The diamond weave is a small, repeating diamond pattern woven into the cloth. It is subtle, more a texture than a print, and it gives the wrap a slightly firmer hand than a plain weave.

Care

## Caring for a Royal Blue Cashmere Wrap

A strong color can release a little dye in its first wash, so wash it on its own the first time. Hand wash in cold or lukewarm water with a mild detergent or wool wash, soak for 8 to 12 minutes, press the water through without wringing, rinse in cool water and dry it flat on a towel out of direct sun.

Strong sunlight fades bright blues over time, so dry and store it in the shade. For storage, fold it clean with cedar or lavender.
