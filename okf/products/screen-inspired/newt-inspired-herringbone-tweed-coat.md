Not a theatrical costume. A serious outerwear piece that happens to carry one of the most iconic silhouettes in modern fantasy cinema.

The Cloth

## Authentic British herringbone tweed with dark leather accents

Wool-Rich Herringbone Tweed  
 Woven in Britain  
 Dark Leather Pocket Accents  
 Full Acetate Lining  
 Long Overcoat Silhouette  
 Canvas Front

The cloth is a wool-rich herringbone tweed, woven in Britain. Herringbone is a structured weave: the V-shaped pattern you see is built into the fabric at the loom, which means it does not pill or shift the way printed fabrics do, and it gives the coat a depth and texture that reads differently in daylight than it does indoors. In overcoat weight, herringbone has been the signature of serious British outerwear for over a century. It is the cloth a real 1920s Edwardian naturalist would have worn.

The dark leather accents on the pocket flaps are genuine leather, hand-stitched into position. They are not vinyl trim or bonded leather piping. They age, they develop character, and they make the coat feel crafted in a way that separates it immediately from the polyester alternatives sold elsewhere online. The lining is acetate, in your choice of color. The construction is canvas front throughout: the front of the coat is built around a floating canvas interlining that molds gently to your body over the first few wears.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this coat inspired by Newt Scamander’s screen coat?

Yes. The coat draws from the long, wide-lapelled silhouette worn by Redmayne as Newt Scamander across the film series, reimagined in authentic British herringbone tweed with genuine leather pocket accents. The fabric and leather details are our own interpretation of the Edwardian British adventurer aesthetic the screen wardrobe draws from.

### Why herringbone tweed instead of the blue-green wool from the film?

The screen coat is a distinctive teal blue wool. We chose British herringbone tweed for our version because it is truer to the Edwardian British outerwear tradition the character’s wardrobe references, and because it wears as serious everyday outerwear rather than a theatrical piece. Herringbone tweed in overcoat weight is a garment you can wear to dinner, to work, or to a convention. The teal screen color reads as costume outside a specific context. If you want the screen color in your lining or as an alternative outer fabric, let us know in the order notes and we will discuss options.

### Is the leather on the pockets real leather?

Yes. The accent trim on the pocket is genuine dark leather, stitched during construction. Not vinyl, not bonded leather, not heat-applied trim. It ages and develops character with the coat over time.

### Is this fabric real wool or a blend?

100% Wool British herringbone tweed, woven in Britain. The herringbone weave is structural, not printed, which means the pattern holds its definition across the life of the coat. The lining is acetate in your choice of color.

### How long does the coat take to make?

Usually 3 to 4 weeks per stage from the day your measurements are confirmed. The cotton test coat step adds a little time at the start, but it prevents the far bigger problem of a fit issue.

### What if the fit is not right when the test coat arrives?

That is exactly what the test coat is for. We make a cotton version of your coat first, before cutting the herringbone tweed. If anything needs adjusting, sleeves, shoulders, length, chest, you tell us and we update the pattern. Your real coat is then cut to the corrected fit. The cotton test coat is yours to keep at no charge.

### Can I customize the coat, different lapel, different leather color, no back belt?

Yes. The default coat is built to the screen silhouette, wide notch lapel, one-button front, back half-belt, dark leather pocket accents, but we will build it any way you prefer. Add your requirements to the order notes at checkout. We will confirm the full specification with you before production starts.

### Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship worldwide by DHL, UPS or FedEx with full tracking. Delivery is usually 3 to 7 business days once your coat leaves our workshop.

### How do I pay?

Secure checkout through PayPal. Pay with your PayPal balance or with any major credit or debit card through PayPal’s guest checkout. No PayPal account required.

### Will this coat work for everyday wear, not just for events?

Absolutely. British herringbone tweed in overcoat weight is one of the most versatile outerwear fabrics in menswear. It pairs with a suit for smart occasions, with smart trousers for everyday wear, or with dark jeans and boots. The long silhouette and leather accents read as considered outerwear to anyone who does not know the screen reference, and unmistakably as Newt’s coat to anyone who does.
