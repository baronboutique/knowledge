Yes. Both the interim cotton testing garment and the final custom 1989 Jester of Chaos Double-Breasted Coat ship worldwide using fully insured, trackable express courier services. Keep it inside a breathable garment bag within a cool, dry closet configuration to preserve the natural wool fibers.

Baron Boutique is deeply committed to long-term sartorial support.
