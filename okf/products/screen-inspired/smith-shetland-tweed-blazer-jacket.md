## W. Bill Shetland tweed from Harrisons of Edinburgh

100% Pure New Wool  
 390gsm / 12 to 13oz  
 Woven in Britain  
 W. Bill Shetland Bunch  
 Heritage Since 1863

The cloth is pure new wool, 390gsm, from the W. Bill Shetland Tweeds bunch by Harrisons of Edinburgh. The same English merchant house that has supplied Savile Row tailors and British costume departments for over 160 years. It is the cloth itself, not a “tweed-style” alternative, not a “Shetland-look” wool blend.

Why this matters. Shetland tweed is woven from undyed yarns of multiple natural shades, which is why a single jacket reads as a different color in afternoon sun than it does indoors at a convention. It photographs like a piece of British countryside. It softens and personalizes with every wear. And it lasts, in a way the polyester jackets sold elsewhere on the internet do not.

Questions & Answers

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this blazer drawn from the on-screen tweed jacket?

Yes. The blazer is hand-tailored from the W. Bill Shetland Tweeds bunch supplied by Harrisons of Edinburgh, the same English merchant that supplied the original. The buttons, lapel width, three-button cuffs, single back vent, and oval leather elbow patches are all matched to the on-screen reference.

### When was this jacket made famous on screen?

Smith wore it across his run in a long-running British science-fiction television role between 2010 and 2013. It is his second tweed in the role, the warmer and lighter Shetland weave that replaced his earlier darker Donegal jacket and became his signature on-screen look.

### Is the fabric real wool or a polyester blend?

100% pure new wool. The cloth is W. Bill Shetland tweed by Harrisons of Edinburgh, woven in Britain, 390gsm. No polyester. No acrylic. No “tweed-look” blend. The lining is acetate.

### How long does the blazer take to make?

Usually 3 to 4 weeks per stage from the day your measurements are confirmed. The cotton test jacket step adds a little time at the start, but prevents the much bigger delay (and disappointment) of a fit problem at the end.

### What if the fit is not right when it arrives?

That is exactly what the test jacket is for. We send you a free cotton version of your blazer first, and we only cut the Shetland tweed after you have confirmed the fit. If the test jacket needs adjusting, we adjust the pattern and make the real blazer to the corrected fit.

### Can I order it without elbow patches, or with a different lapel?

Yes. The default jacket is true to the on-screen original (elbow patches, notch lapel, three-button closure, single back vent), but we will build it any way you prefer. Add your customizations to the order notes at checkout and we will confirm before production.

### Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship worldwide by DHL, UPS or FedEx with full tracking. Delivery is usually 3 to 7 business days once your blazer leaves our workshop.

### How do I pay?

Secure checkout through PayPal. You can pay with your PayPal balance or with any major credit or debit card through PayPal’s guest checkout. No PayPal account required.

### Is this jacket good for everyday wear, not just for events?

Absolutely. Real W. Bill Shetland tweed reads as a heritage sport coat first and a fan reference second. It pairs with denim, chinos, cords, or wool trousers. It wears under a topcoat in winter and on its own in autumn. The on-screen details are subtle enough that anyone reads it as great tailoring; those familiar with the role spot it instantly.
