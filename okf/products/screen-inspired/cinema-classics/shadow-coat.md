## The Shadow Knows: The Vigilante’s Attire

Step from the darkness and become the master of men’s hearts. This is **not a costume**; it is the complete, four-piece uniform of the original pulp vigilante. We have meticulously recreated the iconic ensemble worn by Alec Baldwin in *The Shadow*, engineering a masterpiece of mystery, power, and timeless style.

### The Double-Breasted Overcoat

The foundation of your phantom persona is this sharp, double-breasted overcoat, tailored from **Super 130s’ worsted wool** for a commanding presence and a flawless drape.

- **Full Canvas Construction:** A hand-basted canvas front provides the sharp, authoritative structure of a 1930s gentleman, ensuring the coat molds perfectly to you.
- **Imposing Peak Lapels:** Classic, wide peak lapels create the powerful, formal silhouette essential for The Shadow’s look.
- **Engineered for Action:** A deep center-back vent allows for fluid, unrestricted movement as you dispense justice in the night.

### The Full-Length Cape

The soul of the silhouette, this dramatic, full-length cape attaches seamlessly to the overcoat with hidden hook-and-bar fasteners. It is designed to envelop you in darkness, creating the iconic, phantom-like presence of the character.

### The Classic Pleated Trousers

Tailored for action, the trousers feature double reverse pleats for classic styling and complete freedom of movement. Slanted front pockets and a traditional belt-looped waistband complete the authentic 1930s look.

### The Signature Red Scarf

No disguise is complete without the iconic red scarf. Crafted from **100% pure silk satin**, it’s the final, crucial element for concealing your identity and completing the transformation.

### Your Perfect Fit, Guaranteed: The Free Test Costume

Even a master of the night requires a flawless fit. We eliminate the risk of ordering online by sending you a **free test costume set** first.

1. **Receive Your Test Garment:** We mail you a test version of the coat, cape, and pants made in cotton to your exact initial measurements.
2. **Confirm Your Fit:** Try them on at home. You can then request any adjustments to the fit, style, and measurements.
3. **We Craft the Final Masterpiece:** We apply your feedback to tailor the final worsted wool ensemble for a perfect, screen-accurate fit.

The test costume is yours to keep.

*A complimentary allergy-protection service is available upon request. If you are sensitive to wool, we can extend the lining to the pants bottom hem at no extra charge.*
