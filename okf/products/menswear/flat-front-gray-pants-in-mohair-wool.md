Mohair at Work

## Why Mohair Makes Good Office Trousers

Why mohair trousers for work? Because the problems of office trousers are creasing, shine and heat, and mohair helps with all three.

| Creasing | Mohair fibers bend and spring back, so lap creases from sitting relax within minutes of standing |
| --- | --- |
| Shine | Mohair has a soft natural luster, so the cloth does not develop the flat, polished shine cheap trousers get at the seat |
| Heat | The smooth, open fiber breathes, keeping you cooler in heated offices and on commutes |
| Travel | Fold them in a bag, hang them on arrival, and they recover overnight |

Office trousers men wear four or five days a week need a cloth that tolerates that rhythm. A 10% mohair blend is a sensible level for daily wear: enough to change how the cloth behaves, not so much that it feels stiff or looks shiny. Mohair grey is also the easiest color in the range, sitting with navy, charcoal and tweed jackets alike.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Gray Mohair Pants

### Are mohair trousers good for the office?

Yes, they are one of the most practical choices. The mohair helps the cloth shed creases from sitting, it breathes in warm offices, and its soft luster keeps the trousers from developing a worn shine. In medium gray they go with nearly every shirt and jacket you already own.

### How much mohair is in mohair pants?

It varies: suiting blends usually carry between 10 and 30 percent mohair, with the rest wool, and some evening cloths go higher. Ours is 10% mohair with 90% merino, a balance chosen for daily wear: crisp and resilient but still soft against the skin.

### Gray mohair or gray worsted wool?

Plain gray worsted is softer and completely matte. Gray mohair wool is crisper, keeps its crease longer and has a gentle glow. If your trousers spend long days in a chair or in transit, mohair stays neater; if you prefer the softest hand, choose plain merino.

### Do mohair trousers travel well?

Very well. Mohair is springy, so folding marks and seat creases relax once the trousers hang overnight. Pack them folded along the crease, hang them as soon as you arrive, and a little steam from a shower finishes the job.

### Do mohair pants pill?

Mohair fibers are long and smooth, so they resist pilling better than short, fuzzy wools. The merino in the blend is fine and tightly spun as well. Rotating your trousers and brushing them occasionally keeps the surface clean.

### Classic or slim cut?

This pair is a classic straight leg, which hangs cleanly in a crisp cloth and is comfortable at a desk. A slimmer leg is available on request: write it in the comment box on the measurement form. For a slim mohair trouser built for evenings, see our black slim mohair pants.

### What should I wear with gray mohair trousers?

White or light blue shirts with black or dark brown shoes for the office; a navy blazer or tweed jacket as separates; a fine knit and loafers for smart casual. Avoid a gray jacket that nearly matches, which looks like a mismatched suit.

### How do I care for mohair wool trousers?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle; hang them by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and the sharpest crease. Between wears, hanging them overnight is usually all they need.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early if you need them by a date.
