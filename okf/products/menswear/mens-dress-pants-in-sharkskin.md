What Sharkskin Is

## What Sharkskin Pants Are

What are sharkskin pants? Trousers made from sharkskin cloth: a worsted wool woven so that a light yarn and a dark yarn alternate. The pattern of tiny steps this creates gives the cloth a smooth, two-tone surface that some say resembles the skin of a shark, which is where the name comes from. Nothing about it involves sharks.

| Sharkskin, this pair | Two colors of yarn in a fine twill; a two-tone sheen |
| --- | --- |
| Pick-and-pick | Two colors alternating in a plain weave; a pinpoint texture, less sheen |
| Plain worsted | One color; smooth and matte |
| High-twist wool | Springy, open cloth; crease resistant, no sheen |

Sharkskin was a favorite of mid-century tailoring, when sharp suits with a subtle shine were the height of city style, and it has never quite left. Today the sharkskin pants men buy are mostly worn as dress trousers on their own, and sharkskin trousers in navy or gray are among the most useful odd trousers because their texture keeps them from looking like half a suit. Questions & Answers

## Questions About Sharkskin Pants

### What are sharkskin pants?

Sharkskin pants are dress trousers made from sharkskin cloth, a worsted wool woven with alternating light and dark yarns. The weave creates a smooth, two-tone surface with a subtle sheen. They are worn like any good dress trouser, as part of a suit or on their own with a jacket or shirt.

### Is sharkskin made from sharks?

No. The name describes the look of the weave, which some compare to the texture of a shark’s skin. Sharkskin suiting is ordinary worsted wool; ours is 100% wool, with no animal skin of any kind.

### Is sharkskin shiny?

It has a soft sheen rather than a shine. The two colors of yarn catch the light differently, so the cloth seems to change tone slightly as you move. In navy and black the effect is subtle; in mid grays and blues it is more visible.

### What is the difference between sharkskin and pick-and-pick?

Both use two colors of yarn. Pick-and-pick alternates them in a plain weave, giving a pinpoint, matte texture. Sharkskin uses a twill-type weave, which produces a smoother surface with a stepped two-tone pattern and more luster.

### Can sharkskin trousers be worn all year?

Yes. Sharkskin is a smooth worsted of moderate weight, comfortable in offices and most climates through the year. Choose light gray or slate blue for summer and the darker colors for autumn, winter and evenings.

### What should I wear with navy sharkskin pants?

A white or light blue shirt, with dark brown shoes in daytime or black shoes in the evening. For a jacket, choose gray, camel or charcoal rather than navy, so the trousers do not look like a mismatched suit.

### Can I have pleats?

Yes, front pleats are available on request. They add a little room at the hip and a softer drape. Write it in the comment box on the measurement form when you order.

### How do I care for sharkskin trousers?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle; hang them by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and to keep the sheen even. Press through a cloth to avoid flattening the weave.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early if you need them by a date.
