## Narrow Chalk Stripe, Bold Chalk Stripe or Pinstripe

Chalk stripe trousers come in two spacings, and the choice changes the mood.

| Narrow chalk stripe, this pair | Lines set close together; reads as a refined texture from a distance and suits most offices |
| --- | --- |
| Bold chalk stripe | Lines set wider apart; more graphic, more of a statement, at home at events and in creative work |
| Pinstripe | A fine, crisp line; sharper and more corporate than either chalk stripe |

The chalk stripe has a reputation as a banker’s pattern, but that is only half the story. In navy it is one of the most flattering patterns in tailoring, because the vertical lines lengthen the leg and the soft edges keep it from looking severe. For a finer, crisper line see our **[grey pinstripe pants](https://baronboutique.com/mens-gray-pinstripe-pants/)**; for a chalk stripe with a touch of cashmere, see our **[chalk stripe merino wool dress pants with cashmere](https://baronboutique.com/merino-wool-dress-pants/)**.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Chalk Stripe Pants

### How do I wear navy chalk stripe pants without the suit jacket?

Treat the stripe as the only pattern. Pair the trousers with a solid jacket in gray, charcoal or camel, or skip the jacket and wear a plain white or light blue shirt. Avoid a navy jacket that nearly matches, which reads as a mismatched suit, and avoid striped shirts that compete with the trousers.

### Narrow or bold chalk stripe: which should I choose?

Narrow chalk stripes, like the pair in our photos, read as a soft texture from a distance and work in most offices. Bold chalk stripes are set further apart and look more graphic, better suited to events, creative workplaces and anyone who wants the stripe to be noticed. If this is your first striped trouser, choose narrow.

### Are chalk stripes only for bankers?

No. Chalk stripes became associated with finance, but in navy they suit weddings, dinners and smart casual outfits too, especially worn with knitwear or a contrasting jacket. The soft edge of the stripe makes it more relaxed than a pinstripe.

### What shoes go with navy chalk stripe trousers?

Black oxfords or monk straps, as in our photos, are the classic business choice. Dark brown or burgundy shoes soften the look for daytime and smart casual. Keep the belt the same color as the shoes.

### What shirt and tie go with navy striped pants?

Light blue or white shirts are the safest. Burgundy, as in our photos, is the classic tie color with navy; plain or small-patterned ties in navy, gray or green also work. Avoid striped ties and shirts so the patterns do not clash.

### Is merino chalk stripe good all year?

Yes. At 9 oz, Super 130s merino is a year-round weight: light enough for summer offices and evenings, warm enough under a coat in winter. Flannel chalk stripes are warmer and fuzzier, but they are a cold-weather cloth.

### Can navy chalk stripe trousers be worn to a wedding?

Yes, with the matching jacket they make a classic suit for a daytime or city wedding, and as separates they suit a guest at a smart casual wedding. For black tie, choose plain evening trousers instead.

### How do I care for chalk stripe trousers?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle; hang them by the hem to dry so the stripes fall straight. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and a sharp crease. Press through a cloth to avoid shine on navy.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early if you need them by a date.
