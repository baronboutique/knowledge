What Flannel Is

## What Flannel Trousers Are

What are flannel trousers? Trousers made from flannel, a wool cloth that is milled and brushed after weaving so the fibers lift into a soft nap. The nap blurs the weave, which is why flannel looks matte and gently textured rather than smooth and shiny like a worsted suiting. Wool flannel pants feel warm and soft, and the nap makes small creases disappear.

Gray is the classic flannel color because the brushed surface turns it into a soft, cloudy tone that sits with every jacket. Questions & Answers

## Questions About Grey Flannel Trousers

### What are flannel trousers?

They are trousers made from wool flannel, a cloth that is milled and brushed after weaving to raise a soft nap. The nap gives flannel its matte, slightly fuzzy look, its warmth and its soft hand. Wool flannel trousers are a classic odd trouser, worn with a blazer or sports jacket rather than as part of a suit, though flannel suits exist too.

### Can you wear grey flannel trousers in summer?

Heavy flannel is too warm for summer, which is why it is thought of as an autumn and winter cloth. Our flannel is a lightweight Super 120s, so it is comfortable indoors and in mild weather for most of the year. In real heat a tropical wool or linen trouser is the better choice; light grey flannel is the coolest of the flannel colors to look at, but the cloth is the same weight.

### What is the difference between flannel and regular wool trousers?

Regular dress trousers are usually worsted wool: smooth, crisp and slightly lustrous, holding a sharp crease. Flannel is brushed, so it is softer, warmer and matte, and its crease is softer too. Flannel looks more relaxed and textured; worsted looks sharper and more formal. Many men own both, a gray worsted for the office and gray flannel for the rest of the week.

### What do you wear with grey flannel trousers?

A navy blazer with a white or light blue shirt is the classic answer, and a tweed jacket, a camel sweater or a rollneck all work well too. Brown leather or suede shoes suit flannel’s soft texture; black shoes make it more formal. Avoid a jacket in a gray very close to the trouser, which looks like a mismatched suit.

### Mid grey, light grey or charcoal flannel: which should I choose?

Mid gray is the most versatile and the one in our photos; it works with navy, brown, green and burgundy jackets. Light gray is softer and more casual, a good spring and summer shade. Charcoal is the most formal and closest to a suit trouser. If you can own only one pair, choose mid gray.

### Should flannel trousers have pleats or a flat front?

Both are correct. A flat front is cleaner and suits a slimmer build or a modern look; pleats add room at the hip and let soft flannel drape, which is why traditional flannel trousers often have them. Ours are flat front by default, with single or double pleats, side adjusters and cuffs on request.

### Do flannel trousers bag at the knee?

Wool flannel recovers well if you let it rest: hang the trousers by the hem overnight and the knees relax back into shape. Rotating between two pairs helps any wool trouser last longer. A trouser cut to your measurements also bags less, because the cloth is not straining when you sit.

### How do I care for them?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle cycle and should be hung by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and to keep the nap soft. Brush flannel after wearing to lift dust out of the surface, and steam rather than press hard so the nap does not flatten or shine.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early if you need them for the start of the cold season.
