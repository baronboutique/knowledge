### Wash & Care Instructions

Proper care preserves your plaid cashmere scarf’s softness, warmth, and heritage quality for decades. Follow these simple steps:

#### Cleaning Method (Choose One)

**Hand Wash (Recommended):**

- Fill a basin with cold or lukewarm water and add mild detergent.
- Soak the shawl for 8–12 minutes, then gently rub soiled areas with your fingers.
- Rinse thoroughly in fresh, cool water until soap is gone.
- Gently squeeze out excess water—do not wring.

Dry Clean:

- Request gentle or delicate dry cleaning to preserve fiber integrity.

#### Drying & Storage

**After Washing:**

- Lay flat on a clean, dry towel or hang in a well-ventilated area to air dry.
- Once dry, use a garment steamer or low-heat iron if needed to remove wrinkles.

**Long-Term Storage:**

- Store flat or loosely rolled in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
- Place cedar chips or lavender sachets nearby to prevent moths and maintain freshness.

**Daily or Short-Term Use:**

- Hang in a well-ventilated area between wears to allow natural fiber recovery.
