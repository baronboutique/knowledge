High quality, comfortable, and open to changes This was my second order from Baron Boutique, my first being their reproduction of the Black Velvet Frock. I was very impressed with the first coat, so I went for this one, I had been wanting a replica of the twelfth doctor's signature coat, but the thicker wool that most companies make it out of was off-putting being that I live in a warm country, so seeing a thinner version here was exactly what I was looking for. I ordered the coat with the default colour and lining, but I requested the red middle cuff button, which Baron were happy to add. Once the coat arrived it was beautiful, but too long, so at no extra cost they offered to remove some of the length. Upon getting it back it was absolutely perfect, the wool feels more like that of a high quality suit jacket, which is exactly what I was hoping for, and captures the very dark blue of the original. The lining feels very silky and is perfectly added, being very close to the edge but with a thin strip, just like the actual coat. It has two inside pockets, and the two outside pockets are extremely deep. The buttons look and feel great, with no worry of becoming loose. Overall I'm very happy once again, and am already looking at ordering a third coat from Baron Boutique.
