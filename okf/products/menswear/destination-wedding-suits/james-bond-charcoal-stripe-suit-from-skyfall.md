We at Baron Boutique are committed to providing better quality custom-made suits for our customers. Taking inspiration from Skyfall, the charcoal grey pinstripe suit to cosplay James Bond for modern men who appreciate style and understand quality. Daniel Craig charcoal pinstripe suit is a traditional suit in a very fashionable fit still going strong and lends itself perfectly to the trend.

Step into the lethal elegance of Skyfall. This is more than a suit; it’s Bond’s armor, custom-tailored from a sharp Super 120s’ worsted serge wool in a classic charcoal rope stripe.

#### A Silhouette of Lethal Precision

The genius of this suit lies in its cut. A hand-basted full canvas front, narrow shoulders, and a low 3-button stance are engineered to broaden the chest and create a formidable silhouette. The trousers are equally precise, featuring a clean flat front, classic cuffed hem, and buckle side-adjusters for a sharp, beltless line. Available in 4 iconic pinstripe colors, it pairs perfectly with [tailored dress shirts](https://baronboutique.com/menswear/shirts).

#### A Fit of MI6 Precision

A suit this iconic demands a flawless fit. Your order includes a complimentary test suit to finalize all measurements before we craft your final garment, eliminating all sizing risk. The test suit is yours to keep. We also provide complimentary allergy-friendly lining options upon request.
