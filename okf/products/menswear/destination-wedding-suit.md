## Questions About the Cape Wedding Suit

### Why is Fresco wool specifically good for a destination or outdoor wedding?

Fresco wool is a high-twist, open-weave cloth. The open weave allows air to circulate through the fabric rather than trapping heat against the body, which is the defining problem with standard wool at an outdoor wedding in warm weather. It also has natural wrinkle resistance because the high twist of the yarns helps the fabric spring back to shape. For a suit that needs to travel, stay pressed through hours outdoors, and look correct in photographs taken at any point in the day, Fresco wool is the correct fabric choice in a way that most alternatives are not.

### Can the tunic and cape be different colors?

Yes. The tunic and trousers are one fabric selection, and the cape is a separate selection. Both come in the same 13 Fresco wool colors. You can order the full ensemble in black for a unified look, or choose a contrasting combination. The satin silk lining of the cape is a third independent selection from 20 colors, visible when the cape moves. The price is the same regardless of color combination.

### How does the cape attach and stay in place?

The cape is secured at the chest by a hand-made silver metallic brooch and chain fastening. It is not attached to the tunic itself, so it moves independently. The floor-length pleated back gives it weight and drape so it falls cleanly rather than floating or moving unpredictably. In movement, it sweeps naturally. In photographs, especially at outdoor venues with any wind, it creates a significant visual effect.

### What does the free test suit process involve for a wedding order?

You enter your measurements on the product page when you order. We build a complete test version, tunic, trousers, and cape, in cotton, to your initial measurements and ship it via trackable courier. You try it on, check every element, and send us your adjustment notes. We apply every correction to the final pattern before cutting the Fresco wool. The test suit is yours to keep. For a wedding order, we strongly recommend allowing enough time for the full process. If you have a wedding date, tell us when you order and we will confirm the timeline honestly.

### How long does the full process take?

Allow 6 to 8 weeks from order to final suit in your hands. The test suit takes 3 to 4 weeks to build and ship. Once you confirm fit, the final suit takes 3 to 4 weeks in production. For a destination wedding, order as early as possible and tell us your date when you do. We will confirm whether the timeline is workable before you commit.

### Can I order this in a color other than black?

Yes. The 13 available Fresco wool colors include black, charcoal, dark grey, grey, light grey, navy blue, beige, white, tan, chocolate brown, blue, purple, and red. The cape and tunic can be ordered in the same color or different colors. The satin silk lining of the cape is a separate selection from 20 colors. All combinations are the same price.

### Can I wear this suit outside the wedding context?

Yes, and many of our customers do. The tunic and trousers function as a distinctive formal ensemble independent of the cape. The cape works as a statement outerwear piece for black-tie events, theatrical occasions, and collector use. Many customers order this ensemble with the wedding as the primary context and subsequent events in mind. The construction quality, seam allowances, and Fresco wool hold up across years of wear.

### Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Both the test suit and the final suit ship worldwide via trackable courier. For a destination wedding, both shipments can go to different addresses if needed. Delivery times vary by destination.
