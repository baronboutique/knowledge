Bond's navy blue coat from Spectre is sure to add a touch of sophistication this season. The custom-made Craig's Spectre coat is essential and synonymous with the spy lifestyle. Show your depth of fandom in this 3/4 long length navy blue coat with a matching velvet collar.

This is the definitive navy overcoat from Spectre—an artifact of understated power and lethal elegance. We commission it in a fine Super 120s’ wool flannel, a fabric with the requisite structure and drape for a garment of this caliber.

Its defining feature is the blue velvet collar, a touch of dark luxury against the austere flannel. A concealed fly front ensures a clean, uninterrupted line—the epitome of discretion. The silhouette is sharp and functional, finished with a classic chest welt pocket and a deep center-back vent for unrestricted movement. It provides the essential final layer over a tailored white shirt. Available for commission in a curated palette of ten colors.

A garment of this standing demands a perfect foundation. Your commission begins with a preliminary fitting coat—a toile—crafted to your initial measurements. The fitting canvas is, naturally, yours to retain.
