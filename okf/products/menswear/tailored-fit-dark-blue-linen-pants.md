Questions & Answers

## Questions About Tailored Linen Pants

### Are linen pants appropriate for the office?

Tailored linen pants are, especially in dark colors and warm climates. A flat front, belt loops and a crease make them read as dress trousers; pair them with a collared shirt and leather shoes. In formal offices, keep to navy, charcoal or dark blue linen.

### How should tailored linen trousers fit?

Closer than relaxed linen, but with a little more room at the seat and thigh than wool trousers, because linen does not stretch. The leg should hang straight from the knee and the hem should just touch the shoe. Your test trouser lets you confirm that ease before we cut.

### Why does linen wrinkle, and is that a problem?

Linen fibers are stiff and do not spring back the way wool does, so the cloth creases where it bends. Soft creases are part of linen’s look and are expected in summer. Dark linen hides them better, and a well-fitted trouser creases less because the cloth is not strained.

### Are dark linen pants see-through?

Far less than white or pale linen. Dark blue linen is opaque in normal light, and the satin lining at the front to the knee adds another layer where it matters most.

### Linen or linen-cotton for trousers?

Pure linen is the coolest and most breathable, with the most character. Linen-cotton blends crease a little less but feel warmer and less crisp. For hot climates and summer, pure linen is the better choice.

### Will dark linen fade?

Linen softens and can lighten slightly over many washes, especially in strong sun. Wash cool, dry in the shade, and turn the trousers inside out to keep dark blue deep for longer.

### What should I wear with dark blue linen trousers?

A light blue or white shirt, a pale unstructured jacket if needed, and tan or brown suede shoes. White leather sneakers make it casual. Avoid heavy black shoes, which look out of place with linen.

### How do I care for linen trousers?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle cycle; hang them to dry and press while slightly damp for a crisp crease. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life. Linen gets softer with every wash.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order before summer if you can.
