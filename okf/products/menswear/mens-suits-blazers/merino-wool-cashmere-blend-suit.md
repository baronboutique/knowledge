## Short Description

A cornerstone of the sophisticated wardrobe, this charcoal grey suit is tailored from a luxurious blend of fine merino wool and soft cashmere. The cloth is prized for its supple drape and exceptional feel. Custom-tailored with a full canvas front, this suit is an investment in timeless style and impeccable quality.

The addition of fine cashmere to merino wool creates a cloth of exceptional character, prized by discerning dressers for its luxurious softness and sophisticated, supple drape. Our interpretation is commissioned in a versatile charcoal grey, a foundational color that commands authority in any setting.

#### The Architecture of the Merino Wool Cashmere Blend Suit

The jacket is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an unseen architecture that allows the garment to mold to the wearer over time. The trousers are designed with a full skirt-lining in the waistband for a clean, comfortable drape. Every detail, from the hand-carved horn buttons to the final silhouette you define, is executed with precision.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your suit is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define the final silhouette—from a classic tailored fit to a more relaxed interpretation—and specify personal details, such as the inclusion of a ticket pocket. Accommodations, such as a fully extended trouser lining, are naturally part of our bespoke service.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of such textural luxury demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission, therefore, begins with a preliminary fitting suit—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the meticulous refinement of the suit’s balance and line, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
