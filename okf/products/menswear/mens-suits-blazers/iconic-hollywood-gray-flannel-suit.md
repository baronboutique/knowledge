## Short Description

A grey flannel suit is the outfit of preference and symbol of loyalty. You’ll definitely make a statement in this look. For men who are style-conscious, this iconic Hollywood grey flannel suit is the right choice. Customize your flannel suit to a style that spans years.

The grey flannel suit is an artifact of quiet, masculine authority. It is the definitive uniform of the mid-century executive, a garment that communicates an assured, intellectual confidence. Our interpretation honors this heritage, commissioning the suit in a fine Super 120s’ wool flannel—a cloth with a soft, brushed hand and a distinctly supple drape.

#### The Architecture of the Grey Flannel Suit

The jacket is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an unseen architecture that allows the garment to mold to the wearer over time. The two-button stance and classic side vents create a timeless silhouette. The trousers are designed with a full skirt-lining in the waistband for a clean, comfortable drape. Every detail, from the hand-carved horn buttons to the final silhouette you define, is executed with precision.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your suit is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define the final silhouette—from a classic tailored fit to a more relaxed interpretation—and specify personal details, such as the inclusion of a ticket pocket. Accommodations, such as a fully extended trouser lining, are naturally part of our bespoke service.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of such foundational importance demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting suit—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the meticulous refinement of the suit’s balance and line, ensuring the final flannel piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
