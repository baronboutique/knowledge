## Short Description

Every man should have wool flannel suits in his wardrobe. Start with a simple solid navy flannel suit with a perfect sleeve-length jacket and notch lapel front. Wool flannel suits are fashionable and comfortable for all seasons. Crafted from 100% flannel cloth and custom-made to order, the navy flannel suit is sure to become a favorite.

The navy flannel suit is a cornerstone of the sophisticated masculine wardrobe, a versatile garment that bridges the gap between formal and casual with unparalleled elegance. Our interpretation is commissioned in a fine Super 120s’ wool flannel—a cloth with a soft, brushed hand and a distinctly supple drape, offering the visual depth of traditional flannel without the weight.

#### The Architecture of the Navy Flannel Suit

The jacket is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an unseen architecture that allows the garment to mold to the wearer over time. The trousers are designed with a full skirt-lining in the waistband for a clean, comfortable drape. Every detail, from the hand-carved horn buttons to the final silhouette you define, is executed with precision.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your suit is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define the final silhouette—from a classic tailored fit to a more relaxed interpretation—and specify personal details, such as the inclusion of a ticket pocket. Accommodations, such as a fully extended trouser lining, are naturally part of our bespoke service.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of such foundational importance demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting suit—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the meticulous refinement of the suit’s balance and line, ensuring the final flannel piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
