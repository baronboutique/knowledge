Light to Charcoal

## Light Gray, Mid Gray or Charcoal Pinstripe: Which One?

The shade decides where the suit works best. The stripe decides how loud it is. A faint pinstripe is quiet and formal; a bold rope stripe is confident and reads from across a room.

| Light gray pinstripe | Daytime, spring and summer, travel, daytime weddings. The lightest and most relaxed of the three, and the easiest to wear with brown shoes. This is the suit in the photos. |
| --- | --- |
| Mid gray striped suit | The office suit that works all year. Formal enough for client meetings, easy enough to wear three days a week. |
| Charcoal or dark gray pinstripe | Evenings, winter, interviews, board meetings and formal occasions. Closest to navy in formality, and the natural partner for black shoes. |

Unsure? A mid gray with a clear pinstripe covers the most occasions. If you already own a navy suit, a light gray pinstripe gives you the widest range between the two.

What to Wear With It

## Gray Pinstripe Suit Combinations: Shirt, Tie and Shoes

Gray is the most forgiving suit color there is: almost every shirt and tie color works with it. The stripe is the one thing to respect. Keep the shirt plain or finely patterned, and pick up the stripe color in your tie.

| Light gray | Shirt: white, pale blue or pale pink. Tie: navy, burgundy, lavender or a soft pattern. Shoes: brown, tan or dark brown, with a matching belt. |
| --- | --- |
| Mid gray | Shirt: white or light blue. Tie: navy, burgundy, forest green or silver. Shoes: dark brown or black, with a matching belt. |
| Charcoal | Shirt: white, or light blue for daytime. Tie: burgundy, deep red, silver or black. Shoes: black oxfords, with a black belt. |

Three rules that keep any combination right:

- Echo the stripe: a blue stripe with a navy tie, a red stripe with a burgundy tie, a pink stripe with a pink or plum tie.
- Never wear a striped shirt or tie with stripes the same width as the suit stripe; the lines fight each other.
- Black shoes work with every gray; brown shoes are best with light and mid gray.

Cloth Care

## Super 130s and 150s Wool: Which Cloth, and How to Look After It

The Super number describes how fine the wool fibers are: the higher the number, the finer the cloth. The Super 150s cloths, including the light gray in the photos, are 9 oz: light enough for warm weather and air-conditioned offices, and fine enough to hold a crisp stripe and a clean drape. The Super 130s cloths, mostly the bold and rope stripes, are a little more hard-wearing and a good choice if you will wear the suit several days a week. The honest trade-off: the finer the wool, the more it rewards a little care. Treated well, either cloth looks just as sharp in year five as it does the week it arrives.

- Rest it a day between wears on a shaped wooden hanger, so the wool recovers its shape.
- Brush it after wearing, and steam out creases rather than pressing hard.
- Dry clean only when it needs it, a few times a year at most; frequent cleaning wears fine wool faster than wearing does.
- If you will wear it several days a week, consider a second pair of trousers, which wear out before the jacket.

## Questions About This Gray Pinstripe Suit

### What shirt and tie go with a gray pinstripe suit?

A white or light blue shirt works with every shade of gray. For the tie, pick up the stripe color: navy with a blue stripe, burgundy with a red stripe, pink or plum with a pink stripe. Avoid a striped shirt or tie with stripes the same width as the suit stripe.

### What color shoes go with a gray pinstripe suit?

Black shoes work with every gray and are the right choice with charcoal. With light or mid gray, brown, tan or dark brown shoes look relaxed and polished. Match your belt to your shoes.

### Should I choose a light gray or a charcoal pinstripe suit?

Light gray is for daytime, warm weather and travel; charcoal is for evenings, winter and formal meetings. A mid gray with a clear stripe covers the most occasions if this is your only striped suit. If you already own navy, a light gray pinstripe adds the most range.

### Is a grey striped suit right for the office?

Yes. A grey striped suit is one of the most traditional business suits. A faint pinstripe or dark stripe reads quiet and formal; a bold rope stripe says more, and suits roles where a little presence helps.

### Can I wear a light grey pinstripe suit to a wedding?

Yes, especially a daytime or outdoor wedding. Wear it with a white shirt and a soft-colored tie. For an evening wedding, a charcoal pinstripe is the more formal choice.

### What is the difference between a pinstripe and a rope stripe?

A pinstripe is a single thin, sharp line. A rope stripe is made of tiny twisted lines, so it looks bolder from a distance and textured up close. Both are on this page, from a faint pinstripe to a bold white rope stripe.

### Super 130s or Super 150s: which should I choose?

Super 150s is finer, lighter and smoother, and rewards a little care: rest it a day between wears, brush it after wearing and dry clean it only when needed. Super 130s is a little more hard-wearing, so it suits a jacket you will wear several days a week. Each cloth above shows its grade.

### Can I wear this suit in warm weather?

Yes. The Super 150s cloths are 9 oz, light enough for warm days and air-conditioned offices, and wool breathes better than polyester blends. The light gray cloth with faint blue pinstripes is the coolest-looking choice for summer.
