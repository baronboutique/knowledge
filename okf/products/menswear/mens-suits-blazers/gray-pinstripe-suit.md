## Short Description

The definitive grey pinstripe suit, an artifact of timeless sartorial authority. Tailored from a fine, lightweight Merino wool, it is an ideal choice for warm-weather business and travel. The classic two-button construction and sharp pinstripe create a look of enduring, intelligent style, with customizable details to match your personal expression.

The pinstripe suit is an artifact of pure sartorial authority, a foundational garment with a rich heritage in finance and halls of power. Our interpretation is commissioned in a fine, 9-ounce Super 150s’ Merino wool, a cloth chosen for its crisp hand and the sharp, graphic clarity of its stripe.

#### The Architecture of the Grey Pinstripe Suit

The jacket is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an unseen architecture that allows the garment to mold to the wearer over time. The trousers are designed with a full skirt-lining in the waistband for a clean, comfortable drape. Every detail, from the hand-carved horn buttons to the final silhouette you define, is executed with precision. It is a versatile canvas that can be tailored with classic details, such as double pleats, to evoke the golden age of Hollywood style.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your suit is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define the final silhouette—from a classic tailored fit to a more relaxed interpretation—and specify personal details, such as the inclusion of a ticket pocket. Accommodations, such as a fully extended trouser lining, are naturally part of our bespoke service.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of such graphic precision demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting suit—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the meticulous refinement of the suit’s balance and line, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
