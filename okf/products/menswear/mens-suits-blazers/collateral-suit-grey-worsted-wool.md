## Questions About the Collateral Suit

### What makes the Collateral suit different from a standard single-button grey suit?

Several details mark this suit as specific to the Collateral character rather than a generic single-button suit. The lapels are narrow, between 2.75 and 3.25 inches, which was unusual at the time and remains distinctive. All three pockets are piped with no flap, giving the front a very clean line. The trouser waistband uses side-tab adjusters with no belt loops, a detail that most off-the-rack single-button suits do not carry. The double vents, rounded jacket hem, and single working sleeve cuff complete the construction. Taken together these details produce a suit that a knowledgeable observer recognizes immediately and everyone else simply reads as exceptionally well-dressed.

### Why does canvas front construction matter for this specific suit?

A fused front jacket uses adhesive to bond the facing to the outer fabric. Over time and dry cleaning, the adhesive breaks down and the fabric bubbles. You will not know it has happened until it has happened, and it cannot be corrected. A canvas front uses a layer of woven horsehair canvas floating between the outer fabric and the lining. It molds to the wearer’s chest over time and holds its shape indefinitely. For the Collateral suit specifically, where the entire aesthetic depends on a clean, flat jacket front with no visual interruption, a fused front that eventually bubbles destroys the suit. Canvas front is not a luxury upgrade here. It is the correct construction for a suit that is supposed to last.

### Can I wear this as an everyday suit rather than a screen reference?

Yes, and this is increasingly the reason people order it. The Collateral suit is a study in restraint. Single button, narrow notch lapels, no decorative pockets, clean front, beltless trousers. In light grey, it reads as a very well-made minimalist suit to anyone who does not recognize the reference. In any of the other 11 available colors it moves further from the character association and closer to a distinctive everyday suit. It works for formal business settings, events where a suit is required, occasions where you want to be well-dressed without making a statement about it. The construction is built for repeated wear.

You enter your measurements on the product page when you order. We build a complete test suit in cotton to your measurements, jacket and trousers, and ship it to you via trackable courier. You try it on and check the shoulder line, chest fit, jacket length, and trouser break. You send us your adjustment notes. ### What colors are available?

Twelve worsted wool colors: Light Grey (screen-inspired default), Grey, Melange, Charcoal Grey, Black, Navy, Oxford Blue, Blue, Brown, Tan, Beige, and White. All are the same price. Light grey is the classic choice for the look. The other colors are popular for people who want the cut and construction of the Collateral suit as an everyday piece in a color that suits their wardrobe.

### How long does the whole process take?

Plan on 6 to 8 weeks from order to final suit in your hands. The test suit takes 3 to 4 weeks to build and ship. Once you confirm the fit, the final suit takes 3 to 4 weeks in production. If you have a specific event date, tell us when you order and we will confirm honestly whether we can meet it.

### Do you ship internationally?

Yes, both the test suit and the final suit ship worldwide via trackable courier. Delivery times vary by destination. We ship to every country we are aware of buyers ordering from.

### Can I get allergy-friendly lining?

Yes. If you have sensitivities to standard acetate lining, we can accommodate this. Note it when you order or contact us before placing your order and we will confirm the available options at no additional charge.

### Is this suit appropriate for a wedding or formal event?

For most formal occasions, yes. The Collateral suit is a single-button suit in lightweight worsted wool and works well for smart-formal events, cocktail occasions, and business formal settings. It is not appropriate for black tie. The minimal construction and clean lines make it one of the more versatile formal suits, and the side-tab beltless trouser sits better under a jacket at a formal event than a belted trouser does.
