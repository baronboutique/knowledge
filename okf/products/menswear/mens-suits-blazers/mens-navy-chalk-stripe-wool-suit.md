Not your ordinary navy chalk stripe suit. These mens chalk stripe suits are carefully constructed from year-round lightweight pure wool cloths for a refined yet effortless look for any occasion. Two-button closure navy chalk stripe suit with notch lapel and 4 button sleeve cuffs makes it really special with a flattering shape.

The navy chalk stripe suit is the definitive uniform of the global business elite, a garment that communicates an unwavering sense of purpose and sartorial intelligence. Our interpretation is commissioned in a fine Super 130s’ wool, a cloth chosen for its crisp hand and the sharp, graphic clarity of its stripe.

#### The Architecture of the Navy Chalk Stripe Suit

The jacket is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an unseen architecture that allows the garment to mold to the wearer over time. The trousers are designed with a full skirt-lining in the waistband for a clean, comfortable drape. Every detail, from the hand-carved horn buttons to the final silhouette you define, is executed with precision.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your suit is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define the final silhouette—from a classic tailored fit to a more relaxed interpretation—and specify personal details, such as the inclusion of a ticket pocket. Accommodations, such as a fully extended trouser lining, are naturally part of our bespoke service.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of such graphic precision demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting suit—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the meticulous refinement of the suit’s balance and line, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
