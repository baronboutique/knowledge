Stripe Types

## Pinstripe, Chalk Stripe, Dotted Stripe or Bold Stripe: What’s the Difference?

From a distance, every striped suit looks alike. Up close, the width of the line, how sharp it is and how far apart the lines sit change the whole character of the suit. The narrower and closer the stripe, the more formal and quiet it reads.

| Pinstripe | A very thin, sharp line, about the width of a pin. The most formal and conservative stripe, the classic choice for business and ceremony. |
| --- | --- |
| Dotted (beaded) stripe | A pinstripe made of tiny dots instead of a solid line. Just as formal, with a little more texture up close. |
| Chalk stripe | A wider, softer line with blurred edges, like a tailor’s chalk mark, usually on a brushed cloth. Still businesslike, a little warmer and more relaxed. |
| Bold stripe | A wide, clearly spaced line that stands out from across a room. Confident and less formal, best when you want the stripe to be the statement. |

Vertical stripes draw the eye up and down, so any of these makes the figure look longer. For a quiet, all-occasion suit, choose a pinstripe or dotted stripe. For more presence, choose a chalk or bold stripe.

The Waistcoat

## A Waistcoat That Covers the Waistband

The most common fault in a three-piece suit is a strip of shirt, or the trouser waistband and belt, showing between the bottom of the waistcoat and the top of the trousers. It happens when the waistcoat and trousers come in separate standard sizes: the waistcoat is too short for your torso, or the trousers sit too low for the waistcoat.

Because all three pieces are made to your measurements together, we set the waistcoat length and the trouser rise as a pair, so the waistcoat point covers the waistband when you stand, sit and reach. The back has a belt and buckle adjuster, so you can fine-tune the fit through the day. Leave the bottom button undone: the waistcoat is cut to sit that way.

Belt or Braces

## Why a Belt Doesn’t Belong Under a Waistcoat

A belt buckle under a waistcoat makes a bump right where the waistcoat should lie flat. The trousers come with an extended square button tab and a grip-lined waistband, so they stay put without much help from a belt. If you plan to wear the waistcoat most of the time, ask for side adjusters or brace buttons instead of belt loops in the comment box on the measurement form. Both come at no extra cost.

## Questions About This Navy Pinstripe Three-Piece Suit

### What is the difference between a pinstripe and a chalk stripe?

A pinstripe is a very thin, sharp line, about the width of a pin. A chalk stripe is wider and softer at the edges, like a line drawn with tailor’s chalk, and usually sits on a brushed cloth. Pinstripes read more formal; chalk stripes read a little warmer and more relaxed.

### Which stripe is the most formal?

A fine pinstripe or dotted stripe on dark navy is the most formal, and works for business, interviews and weddings. Chalk stripes sit in the middle, and bold, widely spaced stripes are the least formal and most eye-catching.

### Is a navy pinstripe 3-piece suit appropriate for a wedding?

Yes. A navy pinstripe three-piece is a classic choice for a groom, the father of the bride or groom, or a guest at a formal daytime wedding. For the most formal look, choose a fine pinstripe or dotted stripe on dark navy.

### Should the waistcoat cover the trouser waistband?

Yes. The waistcoat should cover the waistband completely, with no shirt showing between them when you stand or move. We set the waistcoat length and the trouser rise together to your measurements, and you can check it on the free test suit before we cut your cloth.

### Do you button the bottom button of a waistcoat?

No. The bottom button of a single-breasted waistcoat is traditionally left undone, and the pointed front is cut to sit that way. Fastening it pulls the cloth and causes creasing across the front.

### Can I wear a belt with a three-piece suit?

You can, but the buckle shows as a bump under the waistcoat. The trousers have an extended button tab and a grip-lined waistband, and you can ask for side adjusters or brace buttons instead of belt loops in the comment box on the measurement form.

### What shirt and tie go with a navy pinstripe suit?

A white or light blue shirt is the safest choice, because it lets the stripe stand out without competing with it. Pair it with a solid or small-patterned tie in burgundy, pink, silver, gray or navy. Avoid a striped shirt with stripes the same width as the suit.

### What does Super 130s or 150s mean?

The Super number describes how fine the wool fibers are: the higher the number, the finer and softer the cloth. Super 130s is a durable, everyday fine wool; Super 150s feels a little lighter and smoother. All 10 cloths here are 100% worsted wool.
