## Short Description

A grey tropical wool suit exhibiting confident tailoring for the utmost formal look is also an ideal wool suit in summer. The grey wool suit is perfect for any occasion with its contemporary design and styling. A well-tailored grey summer suit is a full-fashion impact for weddings or garden parties.

The tropical wool suit is an engineering marvel of classic menswear, created for the uncompromising demands of warm-weather tailoring. Its distinctive, open-weave structure creates a cloth of unparalleled breathability and a crisp, elegant drape. It is the definitive choice for the gentleman who requires an impeccable appearance, regardless of the climate.

#### The Architecture of the Grey Tropical Wool Suit

Our interpretation is commissioned in a fine, 8-ounce Super 140s’ worsted wool. The jacket is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an unseen architecture that provides structure without weight. The two-button stance and classic notch lapels create a timeless silhouette, while the flat-front trousers maintain a clean, modern line.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your suit is a collaborative process. You will define the final silhouette—from a sharp, slim fit to a more relaxed interpretation—and specify personal details that make the garment uniquely yours. Accommodations, such as a fully extended trouser lining, are naturally part of our bespoke service.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment engineered for performance demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting suit—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the meticulous refinement of the suit’s balance and line, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
