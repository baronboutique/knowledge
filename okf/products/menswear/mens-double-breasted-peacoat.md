## Why a Greatcoat Reads Differently

The 8×4 closure gives a deeper overlap across the chest than a standard double-breasted front, which is what creates the broad, squared line through the body. The enhanced shoulder flaps hold that line out at the shoulder rather than letting the cloth fall away. The waist belt does the work a vent cannot, drawing the coat in so the length reads as deliberate instead of simply long. The sleeve tabs and 34mm horn buttons are scaled to match. None of it is decoration for its own sake. Each detail exists to give a long coat structure, because a long coat without structure is a dressing gown.

## The Fitting Toile: Your Length Is Settled Before We Cut

On a belted greatcoat, length is not one decision. It is three. The waist belt divides the coat in two, and where the hem falls decides whether the length below that belt balances what sits above it or swamps it. The 8×4 button stance is spaced against the same line. The patch pockets have to land where your hands actually fall, which changes the moment the hem moves. Alter the length and all of it shifts together, and none of it can be corrected once the Melton is cut.

So your commission begins with a fitting coat, **a free toile**, made to the measurements you give us. You try it on at home, see the belt line and the hem on your own body in your own mirror, and tell us what to change. Only then do we cut the final cloth. The toile is yours to keep, and delivery of both pieces is included as part of our service.
