#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your waistcoat is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define its final character—from a classic V-neckline to the addition of lapels for a more formal expression. Each detail is an opportunity for personal expression.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment designed to sit with such precision against the torso demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting waistcoat—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
