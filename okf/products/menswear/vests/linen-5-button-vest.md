Why Pure Linen

## Why a Pure Linen Waistcoat for Summer

Linen is spun from flax. Its long, smooth fibers let air through and carry moisture away from the skin, which is why a linen waistcoat feels cooler than any wool one in real heat.

| Pure linen, this waistcoat | Coolest and most breathable; creases softly, which is part of its relaxed look |
| --- | --- |
| Linen blend tailored waistcoat | Mixed with cotton or synthetics to crease less, but warmer and with less of linen’s character |
| Wool waistcoat | Crisper and more formal; for cooler months see our **[men’s wool waistcoat](https://baronboutique.com/100-percent-wool-vest-for-all-season/)** |

Many shops sell a linen blend tailored waistcoat to avoid creases. We prefer pure linen for summer: the creases are soft and expected, and the pre-washed finish keeps them gentle. A light steam before the event is all it needs.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Men’s Linen Waistcoat

### Is a linen waistcoat appropriate for a wedding?

Yes, for summer, beach, garden and destination weddings it is one of the most comfortable choices, for the groom, groomsmen or guests. With matching linen trousers it looks complete without a jacket, which matters in the heat.

### Pure linen or a linen blend waistcoat?

Pure linen is cooler and more breathable, and its soft creases are part of its charm. A blend creases less but feels warmer and flatter. For real summer heat we recommend pure linen; ours is pre-washed, so it is softer from the start.

### Does a linen waistcoat crease?

A little, as all linen does. Pre-washing keeps the creases soft, and a few minutes of steam before an event brings it back. Keeping the fit right also helps, since a tight waistcoat creases more across the front.

### What do you wear with a white linen waistcoat?

Matching white or natural linen trousers for a beach look, or blue, tan or gray linen trousers for contrast. A white, light blue or fine-striped shirt and a soft blue tie, as in our photos, are easy choices.

### Can I order matching linen trousers?

Yes. We make linen trousers to your measurements in the same linens; choose the same color for a matching set. For a full three-piece, see our white linen suit with vest.

### How long should a linen waistcoat be?

It should cover the trouser waistband by about an inch, front and back, so no shirt shows between them when you move. We cut the length from your measurements and confirm it on the test waistcoat.

### Can I add lapels or a collar?

Yes, free of charge. Write it in the comment box on the measurement form, or email us with your order number.

### How do I care for a linen waistcoat?

Dry clean only, because the satin back and lining need it. Between cleanings, hang it on a hanger, let it air, and steam out any creases.

### Is the test waistcoat free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test waistcoat is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final waistcoat takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. For a summer wedding, order as early as you can.
