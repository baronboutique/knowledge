Our interpretation is rendered in a fine Super 160s’ worsted wool from the historic Vitale Barberis Canonico mill—a fabric prized for its luminous quality and impeccably smooth hand.

#### The Foundation of a Mens Wool Dress Vest

This garment is an exercise in versatility. It is designed to complete a formal suit, to be worn over a crisp shirt and fine [dress pants](https://baronboutique.com/mens-pure-new-wool-wedding-pants), or to lend a touch of tailored structure to a more casual pairing. The construction is meticulous, featuring hand-carved horn buttons and a silk back with a traditional adjuster for a precise fit.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your waistcoat is a collaborative process. The design can be rendered in its classic, lapel-less form, or commissioned with the addition of a collar and lapels for a more formal, vintage-inspired expression. Each detail is an opportunity for personal expression.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment designed to sit with such precision against the torso demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting waistcoat—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the meticulous refinement of the waistcoat’s balance and line, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
