A timeless fourth Doctor Baker Horror Of Fang Baker coat is truly an iconic coat essential for your cosplay collection. The Fang Rock coat will not only look amazing but will also stand the test of time. Made from Melton wool this fourth Doctor coat will get you plenty of wear for many years.

Commissioned in a dense Melton wool, this garment is a direct recreation of the formidable greatcoat from The Horror of Fang Rock. It is an artifact of the Fourth Doctor’s tenure, built not as a costume, but as a functional piece of outerwear with a specific, atmospheric provenance.

#### Form and Substance of the Fang Rock Coat

Its identity is forged in the contrast between the hard-wearing Melton wool and the soft, professorial corduroy at the collar and cuffs. The double-breasted front forms a fortress against the elements, while its structure is assured by a full, hand-basted canvas front. Every detail, from the signature elbow patches and numerous pockets to the unique off-center back vent, has been meticulously recreated to honor the original garment’s eccentric, utilitarian character.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your coat is a collaborative process. While light grey is the screen-accurate choice, we can commission this piece from a curated palette of thirteen colors. You will define the final length and silhouette, assured that the fundamental principles of its bespoke construction are held to the highest standard, including hand-set armholes and hand-carved horn buttons. It stands as a singular piece, or as the anchor to a complete [Fourth Doctor](https://baronboutique.com/fourth-doctor-12th-season-corduroy-coat) wardrobe.
