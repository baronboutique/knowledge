Questions & Answers

## Questions About Blue Tuxedo Pants

### Are blue tuxedo pants acceptable for black tie?

Yes. Midnight blue has been accepted for black tie for about a century, and navy is now widely worn as well, especially with a matching or black dinner jacket. For the most traditional events choose midnight blue or black; for weddings, galas and parties, navy is a confident, correct choice.

### What is the difference between navy and midnight blue?

Navy is a clear deep blue that always reads as blue. Midnight blue is darker, close to black, and was popular for evening wear because under artificial light it can look deeper than black. Both are offered in this herringbone cloth.

### Do tuxedo pants have belt loops?

Traditionally, no. Tuxedo trousers are held up by side adjusters or braces so nothing interrupts the line of the waist under the jacket. Ours are tuxedo pants, side adjusters included, with brace buttons on request.

### Do tuxedo pants need a satin stripe?

A satin or grosgrain stripe down the outer leg is the traditional mark of tuxedo trousers and matches the lapels of a dinner jacket. It is not compulsory for modern evening wear. The pair in our photos has no stripe; we can add one on request.

### Can I wear navy tuxedo pants with a black jacket?

Yes. A black dinner jacket over navy or midnight trousers is a stylish combination, and so is a velvet jacket in a deep color. Keep the shirt white and the shoes black so the outfit stays formal.

### What is herringbone?

Herringbone is a twill weave in which the diagonal lines change direction at regular intervals, making a zigzag pattern like the bones of a fish. In fine worsted navy the pattern is subtle and gives the cloth depth without looking busy.

### What shoes and shirt go with blue tuxedo pants?

A white dress shirt, with French cuffs or a pleated front, and black patent or polished black oxfords. A black or navy bow tie completes the outfit; avoid brown shoes and patterned shirts with evening trousers.

### How do I care for tuxedo trousers?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle; hang them by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life, especially if a satin stripe is added. Hang them between wears so the herringbone recovers.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early if you need them for an event.
