## Short Description

Well-made men's 2 button silk and wool suit in the quality fabric makes the perfect addition to your wardrobe. This is the practical silk and wool suit for office wear, travel, summer party, weekend out with friends, and weddings. Tailored natural and soft shoulder with higher armholes for cleaner look preserves the original sense of classic 2 button suit.

The introduction of silk to fine worsted wool creates a cloth of exceptional character, prized by discerning dressers for its subtle lustre, luxurious hand, and sophisticated drape. It is a suit that communicates an appreciation for the nuances of fine textiles, a definitive step beyond the standard business uniform.

#### The Architecture of the 2 Button Silk and Wool Suit

Our interpretation is commissioned in a fine Super 130s’ blend. The jacket is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an unseen architecture that allows the garment to mold to the wearer over time. The classic two-button stance provides a timeless silhouette, while hand-carved horn buttons offer a point of quiet, natural integrity.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your suit is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define the final silhouette—from a classic tailored fit to a more relaxed interpretation—and specify personal details, such as the inclusion of a ticket pocket. Accommodations, such as a fully extended trouser lining, are naturally part of our bespoke service.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of such textural character demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting suit—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the meticulous refinement of the suit’s balance and line, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
