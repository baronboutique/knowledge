## Short Description

This easy-wearing mens silk blazer in 100% dupioni silk is one of our most popular among fashionable men. And, judging from your comments, there are a couple of reasons why. These dupioni silk blazers are all about the feel of luxury and looking rich with their depth of colors.

The Dupioni silk blazer is a statement of pure, unapologetic elegance. It is a garment defined by the unique character of its cloth—a silk prized for its distinctive, subtly irregular texture and its vibrant, low lustre. This is the definitive choice for the gentleman who understands that true style is a language of nuance and personal expression.

#### The Architecture of the Men’s Silk Blazer

The blazer is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an unseen architecture that ensures a flawless silhouette. Its classic two-button stance and notched lapel create a timeless frame. The armholes are set by hand, a hallmark of bespoke tailoring that provides superior comfort and movement. It is the perfect finishing element over a pair of matching [silk trousers](https://baronboutique.com/mens-dupioni-silk-dress-pants) or provides a point of luxurious contrast to fine woolens.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your blazer is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define the final silhouette—from a classic tailored fit to a more relaxed interpretation—and specify the details that make the garment uniquely yours.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of such distinct character, rendered in a material as unique as Dupioni silk, demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting jacket—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the meticulous refinement of the blazer’s balance and line, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
