The Silk

## Why dupioni photographs differently from any other fabric

100% Pure Silk  
 Natural Horizontal Slub  
 22 Colors  
 Light-Responsive  
 Wedding-Grade

Dupioni is woven from the reeled thread of two silkworms whose cocoons merge during spinning. The result is a natural horizontal slub – an irregular crosswise texture that cannot be replicated in plain-weave or synthetic silk. It is this slub that gives dupioni its directional light response: the cloth holds light differently at every angle, which is why it does not look the same in two photographs taken at the same event from different positions. In silver, that property is most visible. Restrained and cool in natural daylight. Luminous under the warm light of a ceremony or reception hall.

**A note on slub direction.** Every dupioni suit in the market runs the slub horizontally – the texture crosses the body. Baron has made dupioni suits with the slub running vertically, changing how the cloth drapes and how it catches light. In black dupioni, vertical slub reads as architectural. In silver, horizontal slub reads as luminous. Both are available on commission. [See what vertical slub does in black dupioni.](https://baronboutique.com/black-dupioni-silk-suit-3-roll-2/)

## Frequently asked questions

Is a gray dupioni silk suit the same as a silver dupioni silk suit?

Yes. Silver and gray describe the same cloth under different light. In natural daylight, silver dupioni reads as cool steel grey. Under the warm directional lighting at a wedding venue or formal event, the same cloth shifts toward luminous silver. Both search terms describe this product. If you are searching for a gray dupioni silk suit for a wedding, this is it.

What makes dupioni silk look different from other silk?

Dupioni is woven from the reeled thread of two silkworms whose cocoons merge during spinning. This produces a natural horizontal slub – an irregular crosswise texture – that no flat-weave or synthetic silk can replicate. The slub gives the fabric its directional light response: it reads differently from every angle and under every light source. This is why dupioni photographs with depth that polyester and plain-weave silk cannot produce.

What is the difference between horizontal and vertical slub in dupioni?

In a standard dupioni suit, the slub runs horizontally across the body. Light catches it laterally as you move, adding texture that reads as depth from the front. Baron has made dupioni suits with the slub running vertically – the texture falls with the garment instead of across it. The visual effect is elongating and architectural. In black dupioni, vertical slub changes the entire character of the cloth. See the black dupioni 3-roll-2 suit for an example.

What is the 5-roll-2 button stance?

A 5-roll-2 jacket has five buttons on the front placket, but the lapel rolls naturally to the second button. Only the lower two or three buttons are ever fastened. The effect is a longer lapel roll than a standard two-button jacket – the roll point sits higher on the chest, making the silhouette more vertical and more formal. It is the configuration tailors choose for themselves when they want a single-breasted suit that reads as genuinely tailored, not default.

How does the free test suit work?

After you place your order and submit your measurements, we make a full test suit in a comparable weight fabric before touching the dupioni. We ship it to you at no charge. You try it on at home, send your fit notes and photos, and we adjust the pattern where needed. Once you confirm the fit, we cut the silk. The test suit is yours to keep. We only cut the dupioni when the pattern is confirmed.

Can I change the lapel width, add a vest, or request a custom detail?

Yes. The default is a wide portrait lapel at 3.5 to 4.5 inches, high double back vents, and a two-piece configuration. A narrower lapel, a single vent, a vest to make it a three-piece, or a different trouser silhouette are all available. Add your requests in the order notes at checkout and we will confirm before production begins.

Is this suit appropriate for a wedding?

Yes. Dupioni silk is a formal fabric. Silver is the most requested dupioni color for weddings – it photographs under both natural and event lighting without looking flat, it pairs with any palette from botanical to minimalist, and the 5-roll-2 construction places it at the formal end of single-breasted tailoring. Order with the full 6-week lead time when buying for a specific event date.

How long does it take?

6 to 8 weeks total from order to delivery, including the free test suit. The test suit typically takes 3 weeks. Construction takes 3 to 4 weeks once fit is confirmed. We ship worldwide via trackable courier and provide tracking from dispatch. If you have a firm event date, contact us before ordering and we will confirm whether the timeline is achievable.

How do I care for a dupioni silk suit?

Dry-clean only. Do not spot-clean with water – dupioni marks easily from direct moisture contact. Hang on a wide suit hanger between wears and allow it to breathe. The natural slub hides minor surface creases better than plain-weave silk does. Store in a breathable garment bag between seasons. With proper care, a dupioni silk suit holds its structure and character for many years.
