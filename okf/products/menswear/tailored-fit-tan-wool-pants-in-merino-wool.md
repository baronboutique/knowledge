Why Tan

## Tan, Khaki and Light Brown

Tan, khaki, beige and light brown are close cousins, and people search for them interchangeably. The differences are small but they change what you wear with them.

| Tan, this pair | A warm, sandy light brown; the most versatile of the group |
| --- | --- |
| Khaki | Slightly greener and duller; the classic chino color |
| Beige | Paler and cooler, close to stone |
| Light brown | Deeper than tan, closer to camel |

Tan khakis are casual by nature because they are usually cotton twill. Tan wool trousers sit higher: the same color in a fine worsted reads as a dress trouser, so tan dress pants men wear with a navy blazer look deliberate rather than weekend. Light tan dress pants suit summer and dark tan dress pants suit autumn; this tan sits in the middle. Tan khaki dress pants, the cotton version, are a weekend trouser; tan dress slacks in wool are not. As tan suit pants with a matching jacket, the cloth makes a summer suit; as tan slacks, men’s most useful odd trouser after gray. Among men’s wool dress pants, tan is the color tan trousers men wear for warmth and ease, and in fine wool they count as tan formal pants for daytime events.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Tan Dress Pants

### What color shirt goes with tan pants?

White and light blue are the easiest and most formal; pale pink, lavender and navy also work well. The aim is contrast, so avoid a shirt in the same sandy shade. For smart casual, a navy polo or a navy knit over a collar looks sharp with tan.

### What shoes go with tan dress pants?

Shoes should be darker than the trousers, so mid or dark brown, burgundy and brown suede are the safe choices; our photos show brown. Tan shoes can work, but only when everything above the waist is light too. Black shoes look harsh with tan in daylight and are better kept for gray and navy.

### What is the difference between tan and khaki?

Tan is a warm, sandy light brown; khaki is a little duller and greener, the color of classic chinos. In practice the words overlap, and many people call any light brown trouser khaki. Ours is a true tan in wool, so it reads as a dress trouser rather than a chino.

### Are tan pants formal enough for the office?

In wool, yes. Tan dress pants with a white shirt and a navy blazer are a classic business casual outfit, and in most offices they are appropriate any day without a suit. For a formal meeting or an evening event, gray, navy or charcoal is safer.

### Tan wool pants or tan chinos?

Chinos are cotton: casual, cheaper, and they crease and fade quickly. Tan wool trousers drape better, hold a crease, breathe in warm weather and keep their color for years. If you want one pair that goes from office to dinner, wool is the better buy.

### When can you wear tan trousers?

Tan is traditionally a spring and summer color, but this tan and this weight of wool work from spring into autumn. In winter, it pairs well with darker browns and navy knitwear. Lighter tan and linen suit the hottest weather.

### Can I get pleats, brace buttons or turn-ups?

Yes, all three are available on request. Write what you want in the comment box on the measurement form. Pleats add room at the hip, brace buttons let you wear braces instead of a belt, and turn-ups add weight at the hem.

### How do I care for light-colored wool trousers?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle; hang them by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and a sharp crease. Deal with marks quickly by blotting with cold water rather than rubbing, and brush the trousers after wearing.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early if you need them for a date.
