## A Free Test Trouser to Set the Pleats

You first receive a test trouser in cotton, made to your measurements, free and yours to keep. It is unlined; the satin goes into the wool pair.

Pleats only look right when the trouser fits at the hip: they should lie closed when you stand and open slightly when you sit or walk. If they spread open while standing, the hip is too tight. Check that in the test pair, along with the waist height, which side adjusters fine-tune but do not replace.

1

**Measure.** Waist, seat, thigh, knee, hem and inseam on the form; see the **[men’s pants fit guide](https://baronboutique.com/mens-pants-fit-guide/)** for help.

2

**Test trouser in cotton.** Your test trouser takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch.

3

**Watch the pleats.** Photos from the front, side and back, standing and seated, with your notes.

4

**Your navy trousers.** Once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch.

## What Shirt and Shoes Go With Navy Dress Pants

The most common questions about navy dress pants are about the shirt and the shoes. Here is what works.

| White shirt | The cleanest pairing; a white dress shirt with navy dress pants is right for any office or event, as in our photos |
| --- | --- |
| Light blue or pink | Softer than white for daytime; both sit well with navy |
| Black shirt | Works for evening if the navy is clearly navy; keep the shoes black |
| Shoes | Black for formal, dark brown or burgundy for daytime and business casual |

A navy dress pants outfit is easy to build: for work, a white shirt, dark shoes and a gray or camel jacket. For navy dress pants formal enough for a wedding, add a tie and black oxfords; a formal navy pants outfit, men’s evening version, swaps the tie for a bow tie. Navy dress pants, white shirt: men have relied on that pair for decades. A black dress shirt with navy dress pants is a modern evening look; the two colors should be clearly different, which is why it works better with a mid navy than with near-black. A monochrome navy outfit, the simplest tonal look, pairs these trousers with a navy knit or shirt in a slightly different shade.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Navy Dress Pants

### What color shirt goes with navy dress pants?

White and light blue are the easiest and most formal; pink, lavender and fine checks also work well. For evening, a black shirt can look sharp if the trousers are clearly navy. Avoid a shirt in almost the same navy, which looks like a mismatch rather than a choice.

### Can you wear a black shirt with navy dress pants?

Yes, as an evening or modern look. The contrast between black and navy needs to be visible, so it suits a mid to deep navy like this one rather than near-black midnight blue. Keep shoes and belt black; with side adjusters you can skip the belt.

### What shoes go with navy dress pants?

Black oxfords for formal and business, as in our photos. Dark brown and burgundy shoes suit daytime and business casual, and suede loafers work for smart casual. Avoid light tan shoes with dark navy in formal settings.

### What is a reverse pleat?

A pleat that folds toward the pocket rather than toward the fly. It adds room at the hip and thigh for comfort when sitting and gives the leg a smooth, straight drape. If you prefer a flat front, it is available on request.

### Do these trousers need a belt?

No. They have self-tie buckle adjusters at the waist instead of belt loops, so the waistband is adjusted at the sides and stays clean under a jacket. If you prefer belt loops, ask when you order.

### Are tropical wool trousers good for hot weather?

Yes. Tropical wool is woven light and open so air passes through, and wool handles moisture better than cotton or polyester. At 8 oz this cloth is one of the coolest dress trouser weights.

### Navy or dark navy dress trousers?

This navy is a deep, classic shade. Dark navy dress trousers men buy for evening are usually a shade closer to midnight. Darker navy and midnight blue read almost black and suit evening; brighter blues read more casual. If you want a darker or lighter blue, the selector offers Oxford blue and blue in the same cloth.

### How do I care for navy dress pants?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle; hang them by the hem to dry and away from strong sun so the navy stays deep. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and a crisp pleat.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early if you need them by a date.
