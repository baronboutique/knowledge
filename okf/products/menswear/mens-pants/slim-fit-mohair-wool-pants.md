What Mohair Does

## What Mohair Pants Are

What are mohair pants? Trousers woven from mohair or, much more often, from wool blended with mohair. Mohair fibers are long, smooth and stiff compared with sheep’s wool, so they reflect light, resist crushing and let air move through the cloth. Blends usually carry 10 to 30 percent mohair; ours is 10 percent, which keeps the soft feel of merino with the spring and sheen of mohair.

| Plain merino wool | Soft and matte; creases more easily |
| --- | --- |
| Merino with 10% mohair, this pair | Soft with more spring; low sheen; creases drop out |
| High-mohair blends | Crisper and shinier; very cool, more formal |
| Flannel | Brushed and warm; no sheen, a soft crease |

Mohair has been a favorite for warm-weather and evening tailoring for generations because it is cool, it travels well and it keeps a sharp line through a long night. In black, the subtle luster makes these work as black wool dress pants for the office by day and as evening trousers at night. Questions & Answers

## Questions About Mohair Pants

### What are mohair pants?

Mohair pants are trousers made from mohair, the hair of the Angora goat, usually blended with sheep’s wool. The mohair adds a crisp drape, a subtle sheen and strong resistance to creasing. Ours are 90% merino and 10% mohair, a blend that keeps merino’s softness while gaining mohair’s spring.

### Is mohair itchy?

Mohair sweaters can be, because they are knitted from fluffy, brushed yarn. Mohair suiting is completely different: the fibers are spun tight and woven smooth, and in our blend they are mixed with fine merino. The result feels smooth against the skin, and the satin lining at the front of the leg adds another soft layer.

### Is mohair good for hot weather?

Yes. Mohair is one of the classic warm-weather tailoring fibers because it is breathable, it does not cling, and it stays crisp in humidity. A 10% blend is not as cool as a high-mohair cloth, but it is comfortable from spring through autumn and in warm evenings.

### Do mohair pants wrinkle?

Much less than plain wool or cotton. Mohair fibers are springy and spring back after being bent, so creases from sitting drop out after the trousers hang for a while. That is why mohair is a favorite for travel and long days.

### Do mohair pants look shiny?

They have a low luster rather than a shine, a soft glow that shows more in black and darker colors. It reads as richer cloth, not as a synthetic gloss. Blends with more mohair are shinier; at 10% the effect is subtle.

### Mohair or plain wool for dress pants?

Plain merino is softer and more matte, and the easier choice for everyday office wear. Mohair wool is crisper, holds its crease better and looks dressier, so it suits evenings, events and anyone who wants trousers that still look pressed at night. For men’s wool pants worn several days a week to work, either works; mohair stays sharper.

### Side adjusters or belt loops?

Belt loops are standard and practical. Side adjusters, buckled tabs at the waist, give a clean line with no belt and are traditional on dress and evening trousers, which suits mohair. Both are available; ask for side adjusters in the comment box when you order.

### How do I care for mohair pants?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle; hang them by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and to keep the crease crisp. Between wears, hang them overnight and the mohair will shake out most creases on its own.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early if you need them for an event.
