Mélange is not a pattern, but a process. It is the art of dyeing wool fibers before they are spun into yarn, creating a cloth with a soft, heathered character and unique tonal depth. Our interpretation is commissioned in a fine, 8-ounce Super 140s’ worsted wool, resulting in a trouser of exceptional visual interest and versatility.
