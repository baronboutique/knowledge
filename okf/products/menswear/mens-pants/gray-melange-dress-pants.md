What Melange Is

## What Melange Pants Are, and Why Melange

Melange is a process, not a pattern. Wool fibers are dyed in two or more shades, blended loose, and only then spun into yarn. Every thread carries several tones, so the woven cloth has a fine, heathered surface. Heather gray is the same idea under another name, and grey melange is simply the British spelling of gray melange. Dark grey melange works the same way with deeper fibers; ours is the light version.

| Plain gray | Yarn dyed one shade; flat and even, the most formal |
| --- | --- |
| Gray melange | Fibers in several shades spun together; soft depth, hides creases and lint |
| Pick-and-pick | Two colors woven alternately; a pinpoint texture |
| Flannel | A brushed, heavier cloth; fuzzy surface, made for cold weather |

Why choose melange over plain gray? Because the mixed tones forgive. Small creases, a little dust and the wear of a long day disappear into the texture, which is why melange trousers look fresher at six in the evening than plain light gray trousers do. Melange clothing also sits between formal and casual: these are proper dress pants with a crease, but the texture lets them sit happily under a knit or an unstructured jacket. That is the melange pants meaning most people are after: gray melange pants that dress up or down. Melange pants men wear to the office are usually this kind: light gray wool dress pants cut with a crease, while the casual version is cut fuller.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Melange Pants

### What are melange pants?

Melange pants are trousers made from melange cloth, where fibers are dyed in different shades and blended before spinning. The result is a heathered, slightly mottled color with more depth than a flat dye. The word comes from the French for mixture. It describes the cloth, not the cut, so melange pants can be tailored dress trousers like these, relaxed wide-leg trousers or casual joggers.

### What is the difference between gray and gray melange?

Plain gray cloth is woven from yarn dyed one even shade, so it looks smooth and flat. Gray melange mixes lighter and darker fibers in every thread, which gives a soft texture you notice up close. Plain gray reads a little more formal; melange is more forgiving, because creases and lint blend into the texture. From a few steps away both read as gray dress pants.

### Is melange the same as heather gray?

Yes, in practice. Heather is the term used more in the United States and for knitwear, melange in Europe and for woven cloth, and both describe fibers of mixed shades spun together. Heather gray dress pants and gray melange dress pants are the same idea; ours is woven in fine tropical wool, not jersey.

### What should I wear with light gray dress pants?

Go darker on top or underfoot. A white or light blue shirt with a navy jacket is the office standard, and a navy knit or polo works for business casual. Black shoes are the most formal, dark brown or suede shoes are easier in daylight, and white leather sneakers suit the weekend. Avoid an all-light outfit, which washes out under office lighting.

### Are these the relaxed melange pant people talk about?

The pair in our photos is a tailored straight leg, the dress version of melange. If you want the relaxed, fuller-leg melange pant, write relaxed or wide leg in the comment box on the measurement form and we cut the pattern that way, still from your own measurements. Your test trouser lets you check the drape before we cut the wool.

### Can I wear light gray wool trousers all year?

Tropical wool is made for warmth, so these are most at home from spring to autumn and in heated offices in winter. Wool breathes and handles moisture better than polyester, which is why light wool trousers feel cooler than synthetic ones on a hot day. For real cold, a flannel trouser is the warmer choice.

### What is an extended waistband tab?

It is a short extension of the waistband that closes with a hook beyond the fly, so the front lies flat and the closure takes the strain instead of a button. It gives a cleaner line under a jacket. These trousers also have belt loops, so you can wear them with or without a belt.

### How do I care for them?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle cycle and should be hung by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and a sharper crease. Between wears, hang them and let the wool recover overnight; light grays also benefit from a quick brush.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can if you need them for a date.
