## What Makes Wool All Season

What makes wool all season? Weight and weave. Cloth is measured in ounces per running yard: below about 8 oz is tropical and summery, above about 12 oz is winter, and 9 to 11 oz is the range tailors call all season. At 9 oz in a plain weave, this cloth is at the lighter end of that range, so it stays comfortable in warm offices and on summer evenings and works under a coat in winter.

| Tropical wool, 7 to 8 oz | Coolest; for hot climates and high summer |
| --- | --- |
| All season, 9 to 11 oz, this pair | Year round in most climates and in air-conditioned offices |
| Flannel, 11 oz and up | Soft and warm; autumn and winter |
| Cotton chinos | Casual; warmer in heat than light wool, and they crease and stain more easily |

Super 130s describes the fineness of the wool fiber: the higher the number, the finer the yarn and the smoother the cloth. 130s is fine enough to feel soft and drape well, but not so fine that it wears thin quickly, which makes it a good choice for trousers you will wear several times a week. The 2% cashmere adds softness to the hand without weakening the cloth. For the warmest weather, see our **[high-twist wool work pants](https://baronboutique.com/flat-front-work-pants-in-high-twist-wool/)**; for winter, our **[grey flannel trousers](https://baronboutique.com/mens-gray-wool-flannel-pants/)**.

## Questions About All Season Wool Dress Pants

### What does all season wool mean?

It means a wool cloth light enough to be comfortable in warm weather and substantial enough for cool weather, usually around 9 to 11 oz per yard in a smooth weave. Wool’s ability to breathe, wick moisture and trap warm air is what lets one weight work across seasons. Ours is a 9 oz Super 130s, the lighter end of the range, which suits heated and air-conditioned offices all year.

### Can you wear wool dress pants in summer?

Yes, light wool is often cooler than cotton in summer. Wool fibers move moisture away from the skin and dry quickly, so the cloth does not cling, and a plain weave lets air through. In very hot, humid climates a tropical wool or linen trouser is cooler still, but for most offices and summer evenings this cloth is comfortable.

### Are wool dress pants washable?

These are washable wool dress pants, which is rarer than it should be. They can be machine washed on a cool, gentle wool cycle and hung by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is still recommended for a longer life and a sharper crease, and wool rarely needs washing anyway because it resists odor; airing them overnight between wears is usually enough.

### Wool vs cotton dress pants: which is better?

Wool drapes better, holds a crease, resists wrinkles and odor, and regulates temperature in both heat and cold. Cotton is cheaper and more casual, but it creases quickly, holds sweat and loses shape at the knee. For trousers worn to work several days a week, wool lasts longer and looks sharper at the end of the day.

### What does Super 130s mean?

It is a measure of the fineness of the wool fiber; the higher the number, the finer the fiber and the smoother the cloth. Super 130s is fine and soft with a good drape, while still durable enough for everyday trousers. Very high numbers like 180s feel silkier but wear faster.

### Does the 2% cashmere make a difference?

It softens the hand of the cloth, so the trousers feel smoother and warmer to the touch than plain worsted. At 2% it does not change how the cloth wears or how you care for it. Most of the performance comes from the fine merino. Worsted wool pants made from combed merino like this are smoother and stronger than woolen cloth.

### Is petrol blue the same as navy?

No. Petrol blue is a deep blue with a hint of green, a little lighter and richer than navy. It works in all the same outfits as navy and pairs especially well with gray and brown.

### Flat front or pleated dress pants?

A flat front gives a cleaner, slimmer line and is the modern default; it is what the photos show. Pleats add room at the hip and thigh, which can be more comfortable for a fuller build or if you sit a lot. Pleats are available on request: write it in the comment box on the measurement form.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early if you need them for a date.
