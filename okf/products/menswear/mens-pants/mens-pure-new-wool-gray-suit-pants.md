## Questions About Light Gray Dress Pants

### Are these good grey office pants?

Yes. As grey office pants or grey business pants they work with or without a blazer, and worsted wool looks sharper than a blend. Think of them as dress slacks that fit.

### What goes with light gray dress pants?

Blue and white shirts, a navy blazer and brown shoes are the classic combination. For evening, a white shirt and black shoes.

### Light gray or medium gray dress pants?

Light gray wool dress pants for summer, daytime and warm climates; medium gray if you want one pair for every season.

### Are these suit pants?

They are dress trousers to wear on their own or with a jacket, like gray suit pants without the suit. If you want a matching jacket, ask us before ordering and we will tell you what we can do.

### What does Super 160s mean?

It describes the fineness of the wool fiber; Super 160s is very fine, so the cloth is smooth and drapes well.

### Can I get them unhemmed or with cuffs?

Yes, both on request. The standard 3 inch blind hem also leaves room to let the length down later.

### Brown or black shoes with gray dress pants?

Brown shoes for daytime and business casual; black for formal and evening.

### How do I care for them?

They can be machine washed on a cool, gentle cycle; dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life. Light colors show marks sooner, so spot clean promptly.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
