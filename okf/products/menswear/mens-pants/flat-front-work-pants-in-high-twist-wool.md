High-Twist Wool

## What High Twist Wool Trousers Do Differently

High twist wool is wool spun with extra turns per inch, then woven fairly open. The twisted yarn behaves like a spring, so the cloth pushes creases back out; the open weave lets air move through it. Fresco is the classic name for this kind of cloth, and high-twist tropical wool is its lighter cousin; high twist tropical wool trousers and these share the same springy yarn, and high twist wool trousers summer wardrobes rely on are usually one or the other.

| Crease recovery | The springy yarn recovers after sitting and travel; hang overnight and the knees relax |
| --- | --- |
| Breathability | Open weave; cooler than a standard worsted of the same weight |
| Feel | Dry and slightly crisp, not soft or slippery |
| Season | Year round in an office; especially good in warm weather |

Compared with ordinary worsted wool pants, high-twist wool pants feel drier and look sharper later in the day. Compared with wool chinos, they are far dressier: a crease, a waistband and a cloth that reads as tailoring. High twist trousers are also what tailors recommend for travel, because they come out of a suitcase looking ready.

## Questions About High-Twist Wool Trousers

### What is high-twist wool?

Wool spun with extra twist in each yarn and woven open. The cloth is springy, breathable and naturally crease resistant, which is why it is used for travel and work trousers.

### Are high twist wool trousers good for summer?

Yes. The open weave lets air through, so they feel cooler than a standard wool trouser of the same weight.

### Do they really resist wrinkles?

They recover from creases much faster than ordinary wool. Knees and lap may crease after a long sit; hang them overnight and the creases relax.

### Can I get a higher rise?

Yes. Note it on the measurement form and we cut the rise higher; you see the result on the free test trouser.

### What is the difference from your charcoal dress pants?

Same cloth, different cut. These are a classic straight work trouser; the charcoal dress pants are slimmer and cut to lengthen the leg.

### Can I get them unhemmed or with cuffs?

Yes, both on request; write it in the comment box on the measurement form.

### How do I wash them?

Machine wash on a cool, gentle cycle and hang to dry, or dry clean for a longer life; the high-twist yarn holds its shape either way.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
