A slim trouser shows every fitting error, so the test pair is where we get it right. ## Charcoal Grey Formal Trousers, and Why Charcoal

Charcoal is a dark gray, darker than dark gray and lighter than black, named after the color of burnt wood. It is the gray most offices run on because it reads formal without the starkness of black, and it shows less lint and wear than black cloth.

| Dark gray | One step lighter; the dark gray dress pants men wear to the office, and the dark grey pants men choose for business casual |

Whether you call them charcoal gray dress trousers, charcoal dress trousers or charcoal gray dress slacks, they are the same garment: formal charcoal trousers with a sharp crease. As charcoal grey formal trousers men wear with a suit jacket in a different color, they make a smart odd-trouser combination; as charcoal formal trousers men wear with the matching jacket, they become a suit.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Charcoal Gray Trousers

### What color shirt goes with charcoal gray pants?

White, light blue and black are the safest; pink and lavender also work. White is the most formal, black the most evening, light blue the easiest for daytime office wear. With a white shirt, charcoal gray pants men wear to the office work just as well on weekends.

### What about a black shirt with grey trousers?

It works when the trousers are lighter than the shirt. With a black shirt, gray pants men usually pick are charcoal or mid gray, with black shoes and a slim black belt.

### Brown or black shoes with charcoal trousers?

Both. Black shoes are more formal and sharp with a white or black shirt; dark brown shoes soften the look for business casual.

### What is the difference between charcoal and dark gray?

Charcoal is darker, close to black; dark gray is a step lighter. Charcoal reads more formal and works into the evening.

### Are these slim fit?

Yes, slimmer than a classic trouser and shaped to lengthen the leg, but cut from your seat and thigh measurements so they never pull. Straight fit is available on request.

### Can I get them unhemmed or with cuffs?

Yes, both on request. Write it in the comment box on the measurement form.

### What is high-twist wool?

Wool spun with extra twist in the yarn, which makes the cloth springy, breathable and naturally crease resistant.

### How do I care for them?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle cycle; dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life. Hang them by the hem to let creases drop out.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
