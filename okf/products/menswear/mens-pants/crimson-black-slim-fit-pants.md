## How Slim Fit Pants Should Fit

How should slim fit pants fit? Close, but never tight. The seat should lie smooth with no horizontal lines, the thigh should have enough room to pinch a little cloth on each side, and the leg should narrow from the knee to a hem that just touches the top of your shoe with little or no break. If you can see pockets flaring open or lines radiating from the crotch, the trouser is too tight.

| Skinny | Tight from hip to ankle; casual only, not a dress cut |

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Black Slim Fit Pants

### How should slim fit pants fit?

Close to the body without pulling. Stand in front of a mirror: the seat should be smooth, with no horizontal lines, and the front pockets should lie flat rather than flare open. Sit down: you should not feel the cloth straining across the thigh. The leg should narrow from the knee to a hem that just meets your shoe. If any of those fail, the trouser is too tight, not slim. Black slim dress pants show strain lines more clearly than lighter colors, so it is worth getting right.

### What is the difference between slim fit, skinny fit and slim straight?

Skinny is tight all the way from hip to ankle and belongs with casual clothes. Slim is close through the thigh and narrows below the knee, which is the cut in our photos and works for dress and smart casual. Slim straight stays close at the thigh but runs straight from the knee down, a slightly more classic line. Tapered starts fuller at the hip and narrows sharply. Write any of these in the comment box and we cut that shape.

### Are slim fit pants comfortable for sitting all day?

They are when they are cut for you. Most complaints about black slim fit pants men buy ready-made come from a trouser cut on a narrow block that does not match your seat and thigh. Ours are cut from your measurements, have a grip waistband, and a split at the back of the waistband that gives a little when you sit. The free test trouser lets you check comfort before we cut the wool.

### What shoes go with black slim fit trousers?

Black leather shoes are the formal choice, especially in the evening. Dark brown shoes, as in our photos, soften black for daytime and smart casual; burgundy works too. Loafers and clean leather sneakers suit a smart casual look. With a slim leg, keep the shoe sleek; chunky soles look heavy under a narrow hem.

### Can you wear black trousers for smart casual?

Yes. Black trousers men wear for smart casual just need softer pieces around them. Pair black slim fit trousers with a gray, camel or navy knit, a crisp white shirt or an open-collar shirt and loafers. The wool and the crease keep them smarter than chinos, while the knit takes the formality down. Avoid a full black outfit in daylight, which can look severe; add one lighter piece.

### What is an after-dinner split?

It is a small split at the center back of the waistband, a traditional tailoring detail. It lets the waistband give slightly when you sit or after a large meal, which is where the name comes from, and makes the waistband easier to alter later.

### Is black or charcoal better for work trousers?

Charcoal is easier in daylight and matches more jackets; black is sharper and the natural choice for evening and formal events. If your office is formal or you wear them mostly at night, black is right. If they will be your everyday trouser with mixed jackets, charcoal is more versatile.

### How do I care for them?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle cycle; hang them by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and to keep the black deep. Black wool shows lint, so a quick brush before wearing keeps them sharp.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
