## Why Navy Wool Dress Trousers

Why navy wool trousers? Because navy is the most adaptable dark color in tailoring.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Navy Wool Dress Trousers

### Why are navy wool trousers a wardrobe essential?

Navy goes with more shirts, shoes and jackets than almost any other trouser color, and it is formal enough for work while relaxed enough for weekends. In wool it also keeps its shape and color for years, which is why many men own navy trousers before any other dark pair.

### What jacket goes with navy wool dress trousers?

Contrast is the rule: gray, camel, brown tweed and herringbone jackets all work. Avoid a navy blazer in a different shade or cloth, which looks like a mismatched suit rather than separates.

### Brown or black shoes with navy trousers?

Both work. Brown, as in our photos, is relaxed and suits daytime and business casual; black is more formal and suits evening. Burgundy is a stylish third choice.

### What does the sharkskin weave add?

Texture and a soft luster. Light and dark yarns are woven together, so the navy has depth up close while still reading as a solid color from a distance. It makes the trousers look a little richer than plain navy.

### Navy wool trousers or navy chinos?

Chinos are cotton: casual, cheaper and quick to crease and fade. Wool trousers drape better, breathe better and hold a crease, so they look sharper at the end of the day and last much longer.

### Is dark navy too close to black?

In dim light dark navy can look nearly black, which is part of its appeal; in daylight it is clearly blue. If you want a more obvious blue, choose blue or slate blue from the same cloth.

### What shirts go with navy wool trousers?

Almost any: white, light blue, pink, gingham, fine stripes and checks. A blue gingham shirt, as in our photos, is a relaxed office choice; white is the most formal.

### How do I care for navy wool dress trousers?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle; hang them by the hem in the shade to dry so the navy stays deep. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and a sharp crease.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early if you need them by a date.
