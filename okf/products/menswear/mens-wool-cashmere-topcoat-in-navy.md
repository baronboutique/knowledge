## Cut to Go Over a Suit, Not Just Over You

A ready-made overcoat is sized for a body. It is not sized for a body wearing a suit jacket, which is why so many of them strain across the back over tailoring and hang loose without it. This coat is cut over your jacket measurements, and every detail follows from that. The fly front conceals the buttons so the front reads clean over a lapel. The center back vent lets the coat sit over a jacket without pulling across the seat. At 14 ounces the cloth drapes over a tailored shoulder rather than stacking bulk on it, which is where heavier coats go wrong. And the length covers the hem of your jacket, which is the practical reason dress overcoats are long in the first place. If you wear it mostly over knitwear rather than tailoring, say so on the measurement form and we will cut it closer.

## The Fitting Toile: Tried On Over Your Own Jacket

An overcoat has to work over tailoring, and that is the one thing no size chart can tell you. Shoulders that sit clean over a shirt can bind over a jacket. A center back vent that hangs straight on its own can pull open once there is a suit underneath it. A length that looks right in the mirror on its own can leave your jacket hem showing beneath it. Every one of those is settled by a quarter inch, and none of them can be corrected once the wool cashmere is cut.

So your commission begins with a fitting coat, **a free toile**, made to the measurements you give us. Put on the suit you actually wear, put the toile on over it, and see the coat the way it will be worn rather than the way it looks on a hanger. Tell us what to change. Only then do we cut the final cloth. The toile is yours to keep, and delivery of both pieces is included as part of our service.
