#### Architecture of an Off-White Dinner Jacket

The jacket is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an unseen architecture that ensures a flawless silhouette. The peak lapels are defined by meticulous hand-pick stitching, and the buttonholes are sewn entirely by hand. The armholes are also hand-stitched, a hallmark of bespoke tailoring that provides superior comfort and movement. Buttons are covered in the jacket’s own cloth, a final, understated detail that signals true custom work.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your dinner jacket is a collaborative process. You will define the final silhouette—from a classic, tailored fit to a more relaxed interpretation—and specify personal details.
