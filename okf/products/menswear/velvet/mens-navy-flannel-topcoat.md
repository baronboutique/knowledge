## Short Description

Men’s navy topcoat goes well with pretty much anything. It is a perfect velvet collar topcoat for layering over a jacket or sport coat, or just for throwing on over a shirt or sweater. Single breasted three button fly front closure men’s navy topcoat with four interior pockets makes a great place to stash your valuables. A tailoring classic, this velvet collar top coat will keep you warm and looking like a well-dressed grown-up for many years to come.

This is the definitive men’s navy topcoat, a cornerstone of the sophisticated masculine wardrobe. We commission it in a fine Super 120s’ wool flannel, a cloth with an impeccable drape and an exceptionally soft hand.

#### Form & Substance

The design is an exercise in powerful minimalism. A concealed fly front closure ensures an uninterrupted, austere line, while the velvet collar offers a point of quiet, textural distinction. The architectural drape of this men’s navy topcoat is assured by an unseen foundation: a full, hand-basted canvas front, the hallmark of true bespoke tailoring.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of this stature is perfected through a meticulous fitting process. Your commission, therefore, begins with a preliminary fitting coat—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This essential step allows for the refinement of silhouette and balance, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, naturally, yours to retain, and the delivery of both pieces is included as part of our service.
