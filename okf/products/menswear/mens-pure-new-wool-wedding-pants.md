## A Free Test Trouser Well Before the Day

Before we cut your wool, we send you a test trouser in cotton, made to your measurements, free and yours to keep. It is unlined; your satin goes into the final pair.

Wear the test pair with the shoes you plan to wear on the day, because the shoe sets the hem. Sit, stand, and try a few dance steps: the waist should stay put, the seat should not pull, and the hem should touch the shoe with a slight break or none. Your notes go into the pattern before we cut the wedding trousers.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Wedding Pants for Men

### What pants should a man wear to a wedding?

Follow the dress code. For black tie, tuxedo trousers. For formal and cocktail weddings, dark wool dress trousers in charcoal, dark gray, navy or midnight blue, usually with a jacket. For daytime and semi-formal weddings, mid or light gray or navy wool, and for beach weddings light gray wool or linen. Avoid jeans, chinos and anything that looks like everyday office wear unless the invitation says casual.

### What color pants for a wedding guest?

Gray and navy are always safe; they suit any season and do not compete with the wedding party. Avoid white or ivory unless asked, and avoid black at a daytime wedding unless the dress code is formal. If you know the wedding colors, a tie or pocket square is the better place to pick them up.

### Should groomsmen pants match exactly?

Yes, ideally the same cloth and color, because differences in shade show clearly in group photos. The easiest way is to order all the trousers in the same cloth, each cut to the man wearing it. The groom can then stand out with a different jacket, waistcoat or tie.

### Can I wear dress pants without a suit jacket to a wedding?

At a semi-formal or daytime wedding, yes, if the trousers are tailored and paired with a jacket in a contrasting color, or with a good shirt and tie in hot weather. At a formal or black tie wedding, wear a matching jacket.

### How far ahead should I order wedding trousers?

Allow for the test trouser, your fitting notes and the final pair. The test trouser takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; the final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks once you confirm the fit, and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as soon as the date is fixed, especially for a wedding party, where everyone needs to send their notes.

### Black, midnight blue or dark gray for an evening wedding?

All three are correct. Midnight blue is the traditional evening alternative to black and can look richer under artificial light. Dark gray is less severe and works for both the ceremony and the reception. Black is the most formal and matches black tie accessories.

### What shoes go with wedding trousers?

Black oxfords for formal and evening weddings; dark brown oxfords or derbies, as in our photos, with gray, navy or light colors in the daytime. Hem the trousers to the shoes you will actually wear, which is why we suggest wearing them with the test trouser.

### How do I care for the trousers after the wedding?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle, hung by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and a sharp crease. Because they are cut in a classic dark wool, they will keep working after the wedding as dress trousers for work and events.

### Is the test trouser free?

Yes, every person who orders gets a free test trouser in cotton, made to their measurements and theirs to keep. It is how we make sure each pair fits before the wool is cut.
