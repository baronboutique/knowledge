I needed a coat for a formal I'm attending in a few months, and I always liked the style of the twelfth Doctor, and knew I wanted something like what he wears. I loved the look of this coat but wasn't keen on the blue lining, and Baron interested me with their brilliant customisation options. I choose a black outside colour but a maroon lining, and I could not be happier. It fits perfectly, I was worried it would be too small to wear with a hoodie underneath like the Doctor does, but it somehow feels like it fits perfectly regardless of whether there's a hoodie underneath or not. The maroon lining is great, in darker lighting it isn't noticeable but if it's bright it adds a lot of subtle flair. The velvet is high quality too, it feels great and very soft, and the coat as a whole is the perfect thickness, not too heavy to the point where it's always hot but not so thin that it doesn't do its job. The process of getting the coat was around 2 months from initial ordering to receiving the final product, as they will send you a test coat after a month to be sure the sizing is all good, but the wait is definitely worth it for a very high quality product that I'll be wearing out and about in my day to day life as it looks so good.
