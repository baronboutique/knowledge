## Short Description

The raw silk shirt is an exercise in textural luxury. Rendered in a sophisticated grey, its unique, nubby surface provides a quiet foundation for any ensemble, while a sharp cutaway collar offers a modern, architectural line. A discreetly hand-stitched chest pocket adds a touch of classic utility. While grey offers a versatile canvas, this piece can also be commissioned in a library of expressive hues—from vibrant petrol blue to hot pink—for a more audacious personal statement.

Raw silk is a connoisseur’s cloth, a textile prized for its unique, nubby texture and its sophisticated, low lustre. It is crafted from the shorter fibers of the silk cocoon, resulting in a fabric with a characterful, irregular surface that is both visually intriguing and exceptionally soft to the touch. This shirt is a definitive statement of relaxed, textural luxury.

#### The Foundation of the Gray Raw Silk Shirt

The shirt’s unique character resides in the inherent quality of its cloth. The construction is meticulous, featuring a modern cutaway collar that provides a sharp, architectural frame for the face. #### A Personal Commission

The creation of your shirt is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define its final character—from the precise fit and collar style to the choice of long or short sleeves. Each detail is an opportunity for personal expression.
