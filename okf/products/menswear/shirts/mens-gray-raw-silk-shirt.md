## A Free Test Shirt to Set the Sleeves

Your shirt is made first in cotton from your measurements and sent to you free; you keep it. It may not carry every style detail of the silk shirt, because its job is the fit.

On short-sleeve shirts, the sleeve is what we adjust most often after the test: its length and its width. A good short sleeve ends around the middle of the upper arm and leaves about a finger’s room at the opening, so it neither grips the arm nor stands away from it. Raise your arm and look in a mirror; then tell us if the sleeve should be shorter, longer, slimmer or roomier, along with any change to the body.

Short Sleeves

## Why Raw Silk Works for Short Sleeves

Short sleeves change what the cloth has to do. A loose silk shirt in a smooth weave can cling in humid heat; raw silk has more body, so it holds the shirt away from the skin and lets air move. Its matte, slubbed surface also hides small creases from a day of wear, which matters more on a shirt worn on its own without a jacket.

| Raw silk, this shirt | Slubbed, matte, crisp body; stays off the skin; dry clean only |
| --- | --- |
| Smooth silk | Glossy and fluid; dressier, clings more in humid heat; see our **[satin silk shirt](https://baronboutique.com/satin-silk-shirt/)** |
| Cotton or linen | Washable and easy; linen creases more, cotton feels warmer than silk |

The shirt is cut with a French placket, a one-piece shoulder yoke and side pleats at the back for room to move, and finished with a curved hem that can be worn in or out.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Short-Sleeve Raw Silk Shirt

### Is a short-sleeve raw silk shirt good for hot weather?

Yes. Silk is light and breathes, and raw silk has enough body to stand away from the skin, so it stays comfortable in heat and humidity.

### How long should a short sleeve be?

Usually around the middle of the upper arm, with about a finger’s room at the opening. On this shirt both the length and the width are set on your test shirt.

### Can I wash it at home?

No, dry clean only. Washing raw silk in water can change its size and texture; dry cleaning keeps it stable.

### Does raw silk stretch?

It may relax slightly with wear, but it does not go baggy, which is why the fit is set closely on the test shirt.

### Can I have a cutaway collar or long sleeves?

Yes. Write a cutaway collar in the comment box on the measurement page. For long sleeves, see our gold raw silk shirt, which is offered in the same colors.

### Is raw silk real silk?

Yes, 100% silk. The name describes the slubbed, matte look of the cloth, not a blend.

### Which color should I choose?

Gray is the quietest and works with navy, white, olive and tan. Natural and Unprocessed are lighter neutrals; petrol blue, red and hot pink make the shirt the statement.

### Is the test shirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test shirt is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final shirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch.
