What Is Raw Silk

## What Is Raw Silk?

Raw silk is a name for silk woven with its natural irregularities left in: thicker and thinner threads give the slubs and a dry, matte hand. It is the opposite of a smooth satin.

| Raw silk, this shirt | Textured and matte with a soft glow; relaxed, breathes well, creases softly |
| --- | --- |
| Satin silk | Smooth and glossy; dressier and more formal; see our **[satin silk shirt](https://baronboutique.com/satin-silk-shirt/)** |
| Polyester ‘silk’ | Uniform and shiny, without the texture or the breathability of real silk |

Silk breathes, so a pure silk shirt stays cool in heat, and raw silk adds a crisp body that keeps the shirt from clinging. That is why a pure silk shirt in raw silk is a favorite for summer weddings, festivals and evenings in warm places. The label reads 100% silk; the texture is part of the cloth, not a print.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Men’s Raw Silk Shirt

### What is a raw silk shirt?

A shirt in raw silk, a silk cloth woven with its natural slubs and irregular threads left in. It has a textured, matte surface with a soft glow, unlike smooth, glossy satin silk. This one is 100% silk.

### Is raw silk real silk?

Yes. Raw silk is 100% silk; the name describes the textured look of the cloth, not a blend. A label of 100% silk is what to look for.

### Is raw silk better than smooth silk?

Neither is better; they suit different uses. Raw silk is textured, matte and relaxed, and it hides small creases. Smooth silk such as satin is glossy and dressier. Choose raw silk for warm days and smart casual wear, smooth silk for formal evenings.

### Is a raw silk shirt good for summer?

Yes. Silk breathes and stays light, and the crisp body of raw silk keeps it from clinging in heat. Short sleeves are available for the hottest days.

### Does raw silk wrinkle?

It creases softly, like linen, and the texture hides small creases well. Hang it after wearing and steam it lightly; avoid pressing hard, which can flatten the slubs.

### Can I get short sleeves?

Yes. Choose short sleeves when you order, or write it in the comment box; the test shirt shows where the sleeve will end.

### Does raw silk shrink or stretch?

Kept to dry cleaning, it holds its size. With wear it may relax slightly, but it does not go baggy. Washing in water is not recommended; it can change the size and texture of the cloth.

### How do I care for a raw silk shirt?

Dry clean only. Keep it on a broad hanger, away from perfume and deodorant until they dry, and steam rather than iron.

### Is the test shirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test shirt is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final shirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch.
