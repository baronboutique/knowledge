Twill Button-Down

## Why a Twill Button-Down

What does a twill shirt look like? Close up, fine diagonal lines run across the cloth; from a step away it reads as a smooth, slightly lustrous navy. That small sheen is what separates it from an Oxford, whose basket weave is matte and textured.

| Twill button-down, this shirt | Smooth with a slight sheen; dressier than an Oxford, relaxed enough without a tie |
| --- | --- |
| Oxford button-down | Matte basket weave; the most casual collared shirt; see our **[pink Oxford shirt](https://baronboutique.com/mens-pink-oxford-cotton-shirt/)** |
| Twill dress shirt | Same cloth with a business collar for a tie; see our **[light blue twill dress shirt](https://baronboutique.com/mens-light-blue-twill-cotton-shirt/)** |

Is twill too heavy for summer? Work-shirt twills are heavy, but this is 2-ply 80s, a fine dress-shirt twill, so it is comfortable in air-conditioned offices and mild summers. It is a little warmer than poplin; for real heat, linen or raw silk breathes more.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Navy Twill Button-Down Shirt

### What does a twill shirt mean?

A shirt woven in twill, where each thread steps over at the next row so fine diagonal lines run across the cloth. Twill drapes well, resists creasing and has a slight sheen.

### Is twill too heavy for summer?

Not at this weight. This is a fine 2-ply 80s dress-shirt twill, not a work-shirt twill. It is a little warmer than poplin; for real heat choose linen or raw silk.

### Can I wear a button-down shirt with a suit?

With a sport coat or a relaxed suit and a knit tie, yes. For a formal business suit, a twill dress shirt with a business collar looks sharper.

### Is it non-iron?

No. The cotton is plain and untreated, so it needs a quick iron after washing, but it keeps the soft, breathable feel that non-iron finishes take away.

### Can I change the back or the collar?

Yes. The box pleat and locker loop are standard; side pleats, a plain back or another collar can be requested in the comment box on the measurement page or by email with your order number.

### Should I wear it tucked or untucked?

Either. Tell us which you prefer when you order and we cut the length for it; the test shirt shows you the result before the twill is cut.

### How do I wash it?

Machine wash on a gentle cycle, hang to dry and iron while slightly damp.

### Is the test shirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test shirt is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final shirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch.
