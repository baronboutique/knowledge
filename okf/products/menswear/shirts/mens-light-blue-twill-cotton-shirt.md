## Short Description

Men's twill dress shirts featuring fine craftsmanship, and offer a choice of button cuffs or French cuffs for the man who enjoys wearing cuff links. If you’re looking to refresh your dress shirts wardrobe with a new twill piece then you’ll love the range of contemporary twill dress shirts we hand tailor for you.

The twill dress shirt is an exercise in textural sophistication. Its distinctive diagonal weave creates a cloth of superior drape, subtle luster, and exceptional wrinkle resistance. It is the definitive foundation upon which a well-considered tailored ensemble is built.

#### The Foundation of Men’s Twill Dress Shirts

The unique character of the twill weave provides a versatile canvas, pairing with the smooth finish of a worsted wool suit or providing a point of textural contrast to a flannel or tweed. It is a garment of quiet authority, the essential and reliable core of a gentleman’s shirting collection, particularly when worn with our [finest suits](https://baronboutique.com/menswear/mens-suits-blazers).

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your dress shirt is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define its final character—from the precise spread of the collar to the choice of a classic barrel or formal French cuff. Each detail is an opportunity for personal expression.

#### The Bespoke Promise: A Flawless Foundation

A garment of such foundational importance demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting shirt—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the meticulous refinement of the shirt’s balance and line, ensuring the final twill piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
