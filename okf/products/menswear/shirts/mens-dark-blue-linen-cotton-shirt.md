Linen or Cotton

## Linen or Cotton? Why a Blend

Linen or cotton? A blend answers both. Pure linen is the coolest cloth for heat but creases quickly; pure cotton is soft and smooth but holds warmth. At 55% linen and 45% cotton, this cloth keeps most of linen’s airiness and dry feel while the cotton softens the hand and calms the creasing.

| 55% linen, 45% cotton, this shirt | Cool, soft, creases less than linen; easy machine wash |
| --- | --- |
| 100% linen | Coolest and driest, creases more; see our **[tailored linen shirt](https://baronboutique.com/tailored-linen-shirts/)** |
| 100% cotton | Smooth and soft, warmer in heat |

Most linen-cotton shirts are sold in loose standard sizes without the blend stated. Here the ratio is on the label and the shirt is cut to your body.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Linen-Cotton Shirt

### Is a linen-cotton shirt better than pure linen?

It depends on what you need. Pure linen is cooler and drier; the blend is softer and creases less. For long days and travel the blend is easier; for the hottest weather, pure linen.

### What is the blend?

55% linen and 45% cotton, the same in every color.

### Does a linen-cotton blend wrinkle?

Less than pure linen. It still creases a little where it folds, but the cotton keeps the creases soft and they relax quickly.

### Can I have long sleeves or a curved hem?

Yes, at no extra cost. Write it in the comment box on the measurement page, or email us with your order number. A button-down collar can be requested the same way.

### Can I wear it to a wedding?

Yes, where short sleeves suit the setting, such as a beach or resort wedding in the heat. Light colors suit daytime; for a more formal look, choose long sleeves.

### Should it be tucked in?

This cut is made to be worn untucked, with a straight hem and side slits. If you prefer to tuck it in, ask for a curved hem.

### How do I wash it?

Machine wash on a gentle cycle and hang to dry; a quick iron or steam brings it back.

### Is the test shirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test shirt is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final shirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch.
