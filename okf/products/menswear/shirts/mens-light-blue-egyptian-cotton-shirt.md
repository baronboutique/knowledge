220 Thread Count

## What 220 Thread Count Means

Thread count is the number of threads in one square inch of cloth, lengthwise and crosswise together. At 220, often written as two twenty thread, Egyptian cotton makes a fine shirting: dense enough to look smooth and hold a press, light enough to stay comfortable under a jacket. A higher number is not automatically better; very high counts often come from counting twisted yarns more than once, and the cloth gets heavier rather than finer.

The cloth is 100% cotton, with no polyester, in every color. Close up the surface is smooth with a soft sheen and no fuzz. For a cotton with a diagonal texture that creases even less, see our **[light blue twill dress shirt](https://baronboutique.com/mens-light-blue-twill-cotton-shirt/)**.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Men’s Egyptian Cotton Dress Shirt

### Are Egyptian cotton dress shirts worth the price?

They are if the shirt fits. The cloth is softer, more breathable and longer wearing than ordinary cotton, but those gains are wasted on a shirt that pulls at the shoulders or balloons at the back. That is why we pair it with a test shirt cut to your measurements.

### What does 220 thread count mean?

220 threads in one square inch of cloth, counted both ways; two twenty thread is the same thing. For a dress shirt it gives a fine, smooth cloth that is still light.

### How long does an Egyptian cotton shirt last?

Many years with proper care and proper ironing. The easy-iron finish lasts a long time, and the shirt keeps its quality; as with any shirt worn often, the underarm is where wear shows first.

### Does it need ironing?

A little. The easy-iron finish means it comes out of the wash smoother and irons quickly, but it is not a no-iron shirt.

### Why does the back of a dress shirt puff out when tucked in?

Because ready-made shirts carry extra cloth through the waist. Cut to your measurements, the center pleat opens only when you move, and the back lies flat when you stand.

### Can I have a white collar and cuffs, or a different collar?

Yes. Write it in the comment box on the measurement page, or email us with your order number. French or barrel cuffs and a shirt without a pocket can be requested the same way.

### How do I wash it?

Machine wash on a gentle cycle, hang to dry and iron while slightly damp.

### Is the test shirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test shirt is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final shirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch.
