Free Test Shirt

## A Free Test Shirt to Settle the Fit

We make your shirt first in cotton from your measurements and send it to you free; you keep it. It may not carry every style detail of the linen shirt, because its job is the fit.

The most common change after a linen test shirt is the fit itself. Many clients ask for a close, slim fit at first, then wear the test shirt for an afternoon and ask for something more comfortable. Linen does not give the way stretch cotton does, so room matters: sit down, reach across a table and raise a glass, and if the buttons pull or the sleeves bind, tell us. Measurements are adjusted at the same time, so the linen shirt is cut once and right.

Tailoring Linen

## Why Fit Matters More in Linen

Linen creases. That is part of its character, and no cut removes it, but fit decides where the creases go. A shirt with enough room through the chest and back creases softly when you move; a tight one creases hard across the buttons and under the arms. The box pleat at the center back gives room to reach without pulling, and a curved hem keeps the shirt neat tucked or untucked.

| 100% linen, this shirt | Coolest and quickest to dry; creases softly; softens with every wash |

Questions & Answers

## Questions About the Tailored Linen Shirt

### Is a linen shirt right for a wedding?

Yes, for beach, garden and summer weddings. Linen keeps you cool through the ceremony and photographs; white, off white and light blue are the classic choices, darker linen for evening receptions.

### Can linen shirts be tailored?

Yes. Linen has no stretch, so fit matters more than with cotton. This shirt is cut from your measurements, and the fit is checked on a free test shirt before the linen is cut.

### What fit should I choose for linen?

Slightly more relaxed than you would choose in cotton. Many clients first ask for a slim fit, then move to a more comfortable one after wearing the test shirt, because linen pulls and creases where it is tight.

### Can a whole wedding party order the same shirt?

Yes. Each person orders the same linen and style with his own measurements and receives his own test shirt. Order early enough for the test shirt and final shirt stages.

### Can I change the collar or have short sleeves?

Yes. The standard is a button-down collar with long sleeves; any other collar and short sleeves can be requested in the comment box on the measurement page or by email with your order number.

### Does linen wrinkle?

Yes, softly, and that is part of its look. A good fit keeps creases soft rather than sharp; steam it or iron it slightly damp before an event.

### How do I wash a linen shirt?

Machine wash on a gentle cycle, then hang to dry. Linen softens with every wash.

### Is the test shirt free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test shirt is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final shirt takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch.
