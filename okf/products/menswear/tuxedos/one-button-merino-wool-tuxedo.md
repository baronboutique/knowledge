Put together a custom look for any occasion where you need the best tuxedo. The selection of a notch lapel for an evening suit is a statement of modern minimalism. It is a confident, understated choice that borrows its clean, angular lines from the classic suit, yet remains unequivocally formal. We commission this one-button tuxedo in a fine Super 130s merino and cashmere blend—a cloth chosen for its soft hand and sophisticated performance under evening light.

#### The Architecture of a Modern One-Button Tuxedo

The clean line of the notch lapel, faced in pure silk satin, complements the deep, flattering V-shape created by the single-button stance. The jacket is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an essential foundation that allows the garment to mold to the wearer over time. The trousers are designed for an uninterrupted line, featuring a traditional satin outseam and a clean, beltless waistband.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your tuxedo is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define the final silhouette and specify personal details, assured that the fundamental principles of its bespoke construction are held to the highest standard. Accommodations, such as a fully extended trouser lining, are naturally part of our service.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of such clean lines demands absolute precision. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting suit—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the refinement of the tuxedo’s balance and line, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
