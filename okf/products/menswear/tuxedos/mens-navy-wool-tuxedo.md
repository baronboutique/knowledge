## Short Description

Richly styled slim fit peak lapel tuxedo tailored from Super 140s' 8oz 100% worsted wool in plain cloth with a slight sheen. This single button tuxedo suit for men with satin silk trimmed peak lapels assure a full-fashion impact. The tailored slim-fit tuxedo suit adds a look perfectly in tune with the season’s spirit of effortless elegance.

The peak lapel is a definitive statement of formal intent. Its sharp, architectural lines create a powerful, ascending silhouette, drawing the eye upwards and broadening the shoulder. It is a choice for the man who understands that the language of formalwear is one of nuance and authority.

#### The Architecture of the Peak Lapel Tuxedo

Our interpretation is commissioned in a fine, summer-weight wool. The jacket is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an unseen architecture that ensures a flawless drape. The lapels, faced in pure silk satin, provide a sharp, textural contrast. The trousers are designed with a single, reverse-facing pleat, a classic detail that offers both comfort and an elegant line.

#### A Personal Commission

The creation of your tuxedo is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define the final silhouette—from a sharp, slim fit to a more relaxed interpretation—and specify personal details that make the garment uniquely yours. Accommodations, such as a fully extended trouser lining, are naturally part of our bespoke service.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of such specific character demands an unimpeachable fit. Your commission therefore begins with a preliminary fitting suit—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This critical step allows for the meticulous refinement of the suit’s balance and line, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, of course, yours to retain.
