Slate blue wedding suit perfectly tailored to your body and styled to your personality. Use our free customizer to choose lapel style, lining, and buttons for a look that is exclusively yours. It is a custom-made slate blue wedding suit with ultimate confidence and timeless style.

To select a slate blue suit for a wedding is a statement of modern, sophisticated taste. Our interpretation elevates this choice further by commissioning it in a fine Super 120s’ sharkskin wool—a cloth prized for its unique, two-toned lustre and its impeccably smooth hand. The jacket is built upon a full, hand-basted canvas front, an unseen architecture that ensures a flawless silhouette. Its classic two-button stance and notched lapel create a timeless frame. The trousers are designed with a clean, flat front and a full skirt-lining in the waistband for a comfortable, uninterrupted drape. Every detail, from the hand-carved horn buttons to the final silhouette you define, is executed with precision.

The creation of your suit is a collaborative process. Through our customizer, you will define the final silhouette—from a classic tailored fit to a more relaxed interpretation—and specify the details that make the garment uniquely yours. Accommodations, such as a fully extended trouser lining, are naturally part of our bespoke service.
