What Dupioni Is

## Dupioni Silk Pants, and How They Differ From Satin

Dupioni, also spelled dupion, is a silk woven from uneven yarn, traditionally reeled from double cocoons where two silkworms spun together. The result is a cloth with small slubs across its surface, a crisp, papery hand and a bright sheen. Unlike fluid silks, it holds a shape, which is why dupioni silk pants keep a crease and stand slightly away from the leg.

| Dupioni silk | Crisp, slubbed, structured, with a sheen; tailors well; this trouser |
| --- | --- |
| Satin silk | Smooth and glossy, soft and fluid; drapes against the leg |
| Wool | Matte and resilient; the everyday dress trouser |

If you were looking for satin pants for men, satin trousers men wear to parties or men’s satin dress pants, dupioni is the more tailored alternative: it has a shine, but a textured one, and it does not cling. Silk satin pants men wear for evening drape and slide; silk dupion trousers keep their line. They are silky dress pants that still look like trousers, and dupioni pants keep their line through an evening. Not every pair of silky pants for men is silk; silky trousers men buy in polyester shine flat, while silk has depth. Dupioni is also not the cloth for draped trousers men wear loose and soft: drape pants men like are cut in fluid satin or crepe, and drape trousers men want for that look need a softer silk.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Silk Trousers

### What is dupioni silk?

A silk woven from uneven yarn, which gives it small slubs, a crisp hand and a bright sheen. It holds a tailored shape better than fluid silks.

### Are silk pants for men comfortable?

Yes. Silk breathes and feels cool, and the front lining to the knee keeps the silk smooth against the leg. Cut to your measurements, they do not pull when you sit.

### Is dupioni the same as satin?

No. Satin silk is smooth, glossy and fluid; dupioni is textured and crisp. Dupioni is the better choice when you want trousers that keep a crease.

### When can I wear silk dress pants?

Weddings, festivals, parties, evening events and summer occasions. Darker colors such as black and chocolate also work at a formal dinner.

### Can I get them unhemmed or with cuffs?

Yes, both on request. The standard is a 3 inch hand-stitched blind hem.

### Does dupioni silk wrinkle?

It creases at the knee and lap when you sit, like linen, and the creases relax when you hang the trousers. Dry clean only; press with a cool iron through a cloth.

### Can I order a matching jacket?

Ask us before ordering and we will tell you what we can make in the same silk.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order as early as you can.
