Navy or grey flannel? Both are classics, and they do different jobs.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Navy Flannel Pants

### Navy flannel or grey flannel: which should I buy first?

If you mostly wear a navy blazer, grey flannel is the easier first pair. If your jackets are gray, brown or tweed, or you want flannel trousers that can also form a suit, navy is more useful. Many men end up owning both, because they cover different jackets.

### What jacket goes with navy flannel trousers?

Gray herringbone, brown tweed and camel sport coats all work, because they contrast clearly with navy. Avoid a navy jacket in a different cloth, which looks like a mismatched suit; the only navy jacket that works is the matching flannel one.

### Is navy flannel too warm for spring?

Super 120s flannel is a lighter weight than traditional heavy flannel, so it is comfortable from autumn through spring and in heated offices. In real summer heat a tropical wool or linen trouser is the better choice.

### Can I order a matching flannel jacket?

These trousers are sold on their own, but navy flannel makes a classic suit. Ask us when you order and we will tell you what is possible in the same cloth.

### Does wool flannel pill?

Any brushed cloth can form a little pilling where it rubs, such as the inner thigh, especially at first. Brushing the trousers after wearing and rotating between pairs keeps the surface neat; a fabric comb removes any pills that appear.

### Can I have side adjusters instead of belt loops?

Yes. Side adjusters are a traditional detail on flannel trousers and give a clean, beltless waistband. Write it in the comment box on the measurement form.

### Are flannel trousers formal enough for the office?

Yes. Flannel is a dress cloth, and navy flannel with a shirt and tie or a tailored jacket is appropriate in most offices from autumn to spring. It reads a little softer than worsted, which many people prefer.

### How do I care for navy flannel trousers?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle; hang them by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life. Brush flannel after wearing to lift dust from the nap, and steam rather than press hard so the surface keeps its softness.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early for the start of the cold season.
