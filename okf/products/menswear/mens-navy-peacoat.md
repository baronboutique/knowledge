Slim Fit, Made to Your Measurements

## A navy peacoat that actually fits you

Most navy peacoats fall into two categories. The cheap ones are shapeless rectangles in thin wool blends that lose their shape after one winter. The expensive ones are rigid, broad-shouldered, built for a 1940s frame, with sleeves that swallow your hands.

This is the third option. Slim-fit, sharp through the chest, clean at the waist, sleeves that end where they should. Made to your measurements in a dense 18-ounce wool-cashmere blend that holds its shape and softens with wear. And before we cut a single inch of wool, we send you a free test coat in cotton to try on at home. You confirm the fit. Then we make your peacoat.

The Fabric

## Why an 18-ounce wool-cashmere blend matters in November

18 oz Wool-Cashmere  
 Dense Weave  
 Wind-Resistant  
 Holds Its Shape  
 Softens With Wear

Most men have owned a peacoat that looked great in the shop and disappointing by January. The reason is almost always the fabric weight. Department-store peacoats run 12 to 14 ounces. They are too thin to block wind and too soft to hold a sharp lapel after a few wears.

Ours is 18 ounces, woven dense, with a measured cashmere content that gives the surface a soft hand without making it pill. It blocks wind across the chest, holds its silhouette through a wet commute, and develops a quiet sheen over years rather than going flat after a season.

Questions & Answers

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a custom navy peacoat cost?

Our wool-cashmere peacoat starts at $599. The free cotton test coat is included in the price, there is no separate fitting fee. The price does not change based on color or lining choice.

### Can I have it longer or shorter than a standard peacoat?

Yes. Peacoat length is a choice, not a fixed rule. The traditional naval cut ends at the hip and reads sharp and military. A few inches longer covers the seat and reads closer to a short overcoat. You specify the length you want on the measurement form, and you will see it on the cotton test coat before we cut any wool, so you can change your mind at no cost.

### What is the wool-cashmere blend?

It is a dense 18-ounce blend of wool and cashmere. The wool gives it shape, weight and durability. The cashmere gives it a softer hand and a quiet sheen. The fabric blocks wind and holds its silhouette through years of wear, unlike thinner department-store peacoats.

### How long does it take to make?

Usually 4 to 6 weeks from the day your measurements are confirmed via the test coat. The test coat itself takes 2 to 3 weeks. So plan on 6 to 9 weeks total from order to final coat in your hands. If you have a deadline, contact us and we will tell you honestly whether we can meet it.

### What if the test coat doesn’t fit perfectly?

That is exactly what the test coat is for. You try it on, send us pictures and your notes, and we adjust the pattern. There is no extra charge for fit corrections. We only cut the wool-cashmere after you have confirmed the fit on the cotton.

### Can I order this in a fit other than slim?

Yes. The default cut is slim, but because every coat is made to your measurements, we can adjust the silhouette to a regular or fuller fit. Add a note at checkout and we will confirm before production.

### Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship worldwide by DHL, UPS or FedEx with full tracking. Delivery is usually 3 to 7 business days once your coat is finished.

### How do I pay?

Secure checkout through PayPal. You can pay with your PayPal balance or with any major credit or debit card through PayPal’s guest checkout, no PayPal account required.

### What colors and linings are available?

Six wool-cashmere outer colors: navy, charcoal, black, camel, brown, and coffee brown. Thirty-three acetate lining colors and patterns, including burgundy, navy, gold, paisley and many more. The full list is in the color and lining selectors on this page.

### How do I take care of it?

Dry clean only. With reasonable care, an 18-ounce wool-cashmere peacoat will hold its shape and look for a decade or more. Hang it on a wide wooden hanger between wears, brush it down with a soft clothes brush after wet weather, and dry-clean it once or twice a season.
