What Is a Car Coat

## What Is a Car Coat, and Why Tweed

What is a car coat? Men’s tailors use the name for a short overcoat, usually ending between the hip and the middle of the thigh, first worn in the early days of motoring when cars were open or unheated. The length is the point: a long coat bunches and creases when you sit behind the wheel, while a car coat clears the seat. For men today a wool car coat is the coat for the commute, the weekend and the evening walk, worn over a suit or with jeans.

| Car coat | Hip to mid-thigh; covers a jacket; easy to sit and drive in |
| --- | --- |
| Overcoat or topcoat | Knee length or longer; more formal; a herringbone tweed overcoat is the long cousin of this coat |
| Tweed jacket or blazer | A men’s herringbone tweed blazer is worn as a jacket, not over one; ends at the seat |

Car coats for men come in many cloths, but tweed is the classic one: it was made for wind and cold, and the texture looks right with both tailoring and casual clothes. A men’s wool car coat in herringbone is the most versatile version, quiet enough for a suit and relaxed enough for a sweater: shorter and easier to live with than a men’s herringbone overcoat, and warmer than a men’s herringbone jacket.

The Cloths

## Wool Blend Herringbone Tweed, 330 g per Meter

Tweed vs herringbone is a common question, and the answer is that they are not opposites. Tweed is a cloth: a rough-surfaced wool, traditionally woven for cold, wet weather. Herringbone is a weave: a twill that reverses direction every few threads, so the lines form a zigzag of V shapes like the bones of a fish. This coat is both, a men’s herringbone tweed coat. The same pattern in a smooth worsted gives the herringbone suit pattern; in tweed the zigzag is softer and more textured.

| Composition | 90% wool, 10% viscose; a wool blend herringbone tweed |
| --- | --- |
| Weight | 330 g per meter, a middle weight for coating |
| Feel | Textured and dry, with a soft surface; the viscose adds a little drape |
| Season | Fall, winter and cool spring days; layer a sweater under it for real cold |

At 330 g per meter the cloth is lighter than the heavy country tweeds of the past, which is what you want in a coat worn over a jacket: warm, without the bulk that makes a layered coat stiff. Wool does the work of warmth and wind resistance; the 10% viscose softens the hand and helps the coat hang.

Football buttons are the domed, paneled buttons long worn on tweed; they sit well against a textured cloth and are easy to work in gloves. Each one is hand-stitched and reinforced, because a coat button takes more strain than a jacket button.

On a men’s herringbone wool coat, the angled welt pocket is the car coat pocket: set on a slant, it takes your hand naturally while you walk, and the welt keeps the edge flat. Some call it a wool car jacket; whatever the name, the canvas front gives the chest its shape so the coat does not collapse over a sweater, and the topstitching along the collar and seams is the sporting finish that separates a tailored car coat from a city overcoat.

## Questions About the Herringbone Car Coat

### What is a car coat for men?

A car coat is a short overcoat that ends between the hip and mid-thigh, first made for driving in open or unheated cars. It is long enough to cover a suit jacket and short enough to sit in without bunching.

### What is the difference between tweed vs herringbone?

Tweed is a type of cloth and herringbone is a weave pattern, so a coat can be both. This one is a herringbone tweed: a textured wool blend cloth woven in a zigzag of V shapes.

### Is 330 g per meter warm enough for winter?

Yes, with a layer under it. At 330 g per meter it is a middle-weight tweed, warm over a jacket or sweater and still light enough to wear from fall into spring.

### Why 90% wool and 10% viscose?

The wool gives warmth, wind resistance and the tweed texture; the viscose softens the hand and helps the coat drape. It is a wool blend herringbone tweed rather than a 100% wool cloth.
