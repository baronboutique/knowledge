## Short Description

All purpose black wool topcoat in quality Melton wool with a lovely touch of hand carved horn buttons, silk like acetate lining, and velvet collar makes it the best classic black topcoat ever. It is surprising how much such a classic topcoat can boost your personality. The classic black topcoat is dashing and effortlessly cool making it a quality coat worth investing. With proper care, it should be all purpose topcoat in your wardrobe for years to come.

This is the definitive black wool overcoat—an artifact of pure form and function. We commission it in a substantial 18-ounce Melton wool, a fabric felted and heavily brushed to an impenetrably soft hand. It is the final, essential layer of a serious wardrobe.

#### An Exercise in Minimalism

The design is an exercise in powerful minimalism. A fly front closure ensures an uninterrupted, austere line, while the velvet collar offers a point of quiet, textural distinction. Its architectural drape is assured by an unseen foundation: a full, hand-basted canvas front, the hallmark of true bespoke tailoring.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of this stature is perfected through a meticulous fitting process. Your commission, therefore, begins with a preliminary fitting coat—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This essential step allows for the refinement of silhouette and balance, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, naturally, yours to retain, and the delivery of both pieces is included as part of our service.
