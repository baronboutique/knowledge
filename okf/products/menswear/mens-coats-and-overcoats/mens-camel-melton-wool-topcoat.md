## Short Description

Sophisticated camel topcoat in English Melton wool is versatile enough to add a sense of refinement to both tailored and casual ensembles. The distinctive velvet collar and insulating properties of Melton wool are well-balanced with hand-stitched elegance.

This is the definitive winter topcoat, rendered in a formidable 20-ounce Melton wool. It is not merely a garment but a statement of intent—a cornerstone piece for the discerning wardrobe.

#### Form & Substance

The soul of the coat resides in its dense Melton wool, a fabric felted and heavily brushed to an impenetrably soft hand. The point of quiet distinction is its velvet collar, a textural contrast that elevates the entire piece. Its architectural drape is assured by an unseen foundation: a full, hand-basted canvas front, the hallmark of true bespoke tailoring.

#### The Bespoke Promise: Your Fitting Canvas

A garment of this stature is perfected through a meticulous fitting process. Your commission begins with a preliminary fitting coat—a toile—crafted to your initial specifications. This essential step allows for the refinement of silhouette and balance, ensuring the final piece is achieved with absolute precision. The fitting canvas is, naturally, yours to retain, and the delivery of both pieces is included as part of our service.
