The coat’s character is defined by its tactile, big-wale corduroy. A full, hand-basted canvas front provides the necessary structure, while the signature frock-style back creates its unmistakable silhouette. The single-breasted, three-button closure maintains a clean, utilitarian line.

While black is the definitive choice, this piece can be commissioned in a curated palette of eleven corduroys, ranging from rich oxblood to deep greens and classic tans. The choice of wale—wide or thin—further refines its character, making the final garment a truly personal statement.
