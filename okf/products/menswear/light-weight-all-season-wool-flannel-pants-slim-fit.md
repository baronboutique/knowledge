## A Free Test Trouser for a Beltless Waist

We begin with a cotton test trouser cut to your measurements, free and yours to keep. It comes without lining; your satin is sewn into the flannel pair.

A slim trouser with no belt depends on the waist fitting exactly, so the test pair is where we get it right. The waistband should sit level and stay up on its own before the side adjusters are touched; the adjusters are for fine tuning. Check the thigh when you sit, too, since slim flannel shows strain as faint lines in the nap.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Burgundy Flannel Dress Pants

### What do you wear with burgundy dress pants?

Keep the rest of the outfit neutral: a white or pale blue shirt, a navy, charcoal or black jacket, and black or dark brown shoes. A black tie, as in our photos, makes it sharp for evening; a cream or gray knit makes it relaxed. Avoid matching burgundy elsewhere in the outfit.

### What is the difference between burgundy and maroon?

Burgundy is a deep red with a slight purple tone; maroon leans slightly brown. They are close enough that people use the words interchangeably, and both are styled the same way.

### Are burgundy pants formal?

In a fine wool and a tailored cut, yes, for evening and festive events such as holiday parties, dinners and some weddings. For business settings they are a statement; gray, navy or charcoal are safer for daily office wear.

### What shoes go with maroon dress pants?

Black shoes are the safest and most formal; dark brown works for daytime. Avoid shoes in the same burgundy, which can look forced, and avoid light tan, which fights the depth of the color.

### Does flannel work in a slim fit?

Lighter flannel does. At 10 oz this cloth drapes close to the leg without bulk, so a slim cut stays neat. Heavy traditional flannels are better in a straighter cut.

### Why side adjusters instead of belt loops?

Side adjusters keep the waistband clean and uninterrupted under a jacket, which suits a slim, dressy trouser. They fine-tune the fit by a small amount, so the waist itself is set from your measurements and confirmed with the test trouser.

### Is 10 oz flannel too warm?

It is a cool-weather cloth, comfortable from autumn to spring and in heated rooms. In summer heat a tropical wool or linen trouser is more comfortable.

### How do I care for flannel dress pants?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle; hang them by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and to keep the color rich. Brush the nap after wearing and steam rather than press hard.

### Is the test trouser free, and how long does it take?

Yes, the test trouser is free and yours to keep. It takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch; once you confirm the fit, your final pair takes 3 to 4 weeks to make and arrives 5 to 10 days after dispatch. Order early for the holiday season.
