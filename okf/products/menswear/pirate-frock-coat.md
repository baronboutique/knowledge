The Fit Guarantee

## Why a Velvet Coat in the Wrong Fit Is Unwearable

Velvet has directional pile. When it sits correctly on the body, the pile catches light and creates depth — the coat looks alive. When it pulls across the chest or sits wrong at the shoulder, the pile lies flat in the wrong direction, creating patches that read as distortion. You cannot steam or press this out. The fit either works or it does not.

Before we cut your velvet pirate coat, we build a complete test version in cotton to your measurements and ship it to you via trackable courier. You try it on at home. You check the shoulder line, the chest, the sleeve length, the coat length. You send us your notes. We correct every note before we touch your velvet. The test coat is yours to keep.

This step is not optional — it is how we ensure a coat this distinctive arrives looking exactly as it should.

### How long does a well-made velvet frock coat last compared to a costume-shop version?

A bespoke velvet frock coat built on a canvas front with quality poly-cotton velvet will last 15 to 20 years with correct care, where a costume-grade polyester version typically loses its pile and shape after three to five wears. The difference is the construction and the fabric weight: costume velvet is thin and the pile is shallow, so it flattens quickly under wear and cleaning. A heavier poly-cotton velvet with deeper pile holds its appearance through repeated use. The canvas front matters too — it maintains the coat’s silhouette through every wearing rather than collapsing like a fused front does after dry cleaning.

### What is a pirate frock coat and how is it different from a regular frock coat?

A pirate frock coat takes the 18th-century naval officer’s frock coat silhouette — fitted body, above-knee length, flared hem, stand-up collar — and builds it in velvet with theatrical details including contrast collar trim, decorative buttons, and silk piping on the sleeves. A standard frock coat from the Victorian tradition is typically made in wool and worn as formal daywear. The pirate frock coat is the stage and ceremony version: the same fitted, dramatic silhouette but in materials and detailing designed to read as theatrical and memorable rather than conventional.

### Why does velvet pill or flatten, and how does this coat avoid that?

Velvet piles flatten when the fibers are too short, too fine, or made from synthetic materials that compress under pressure. Polyester velvet used in costume-grade coats has a shallow pile that flattens in the first few wears and cannot be lifted back. The premium poly-cotton velvet used in this coat has a deeper pile and a denser weave, which holds its texture longer and responds better to steaming when needed. Dry cleaning is still required — velvet should never be washed — but the pile recovery after a proper dry clean is significantly better in a quality velvet than in costume fabric.

Musicians, theatrical performers, and event hosts who wear this silhouette regularly have reported that a properly fitted version has a noticeably different presence on stage.
