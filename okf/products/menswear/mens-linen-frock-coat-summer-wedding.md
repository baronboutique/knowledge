## Try It Before We Cut the Real Thing

A frock coat has a silhouette that cannot be adjusted after the fact. ## FAQ

### How long does a well-made linen frock coat last if worn to weddings and formal events a few times a year?

A linen frock coat worn two to four times a year with proper dry-cleaning will hold its shape and structure for 15 to 20 years or longer. The limiting factor in most garments is the interlining — fused interlinings delaminate after repeated cleaning, causing bubbling and collapse. Baron Boutique frock coats use a canvas front rather than fusing, which means the structure is built from interlocked fibers rather than adhesive. That distinction is why the coat continues to shape correctly a decade after purchase instead of losing its chest architecture after three dry cleans.

### What is the most important measurement for a men’s linen frock coat?

The chest measurement is the most critical dimension for a frock coat — more so than for a standard suit jacket because the frock coat’s silhouette is built around the chest-to-waist relationship, and linen has less stretch than wool to accommodate variation. A chest that is half an inch off in a wool jacket can be disguised; in a structured linen coat, it shows in how the front panels hang. You enter all measurements directly on the product page, and before we cut the linen, we build a cotton test coat in those measurements so you can verify the fit on your body before the final garment is made.

### Is this frock coat made to my measurements or does it come in standard sizes?

Every men’s linen frock coat from Baron Boutique is made entirely to your measurements — there are no standard sizes, no size charts, and no stock. You enter your dimensions on the product page and the coat is cut from those numbers. This matters for a frock coat more than most garments because the hem length, waist seam position, and skirt flare are all set at the cutting stage and cannot be adjusted afterward the way a jacket hem or sleeve can be.

### What is a frock coat and how is it different from a suit jacket?

A frock coat is a knee-length tailored coat that was the primary formal garment for men in the 19th century — longer than a suit jacket, more structured, and cut with a waist seam that creates a two-part silhouette distinct from the single-panel suit jacket. Where a suit jacket ends at the hip, a frock coat continues past the knee with a pleated or flared lower half called the skirt. The silhouette is inherently more formal and more dramatic than a suit jacket, which is why it remains the choice for ceremonial occasions, formal weddings, and collectors who want a garment with genuine presence.

### What is the best coat for a summer garden wedding?

Linen is the most practical formal fabric for a summer outdoor wedding — it regulates temperature better than wool, holds a structure better than cotton, and photographs with a natural texture that reads as deliberate rather than casual. A tailored linen frock coat specifically suits a garden or vineyard setting better than a morning coat or a standard suit because the silhouette references formal ceremony without borrowing from the office. Baron Boutique’s linen frock coat is made to your measurements in 18 linen colors, so the garment is as specific to the occasion as the wedding itself.

### Can I choose a different collar trim than the standard brown velvet?

Yes — the collar trim is fully selectable at the product level. The default is brown velvet, but you can choose from 16 collar and trim options including black velvet, navy velvet, maroon velvet, black and brown leather, black and navy corduroy, and “same as main fabric” if you want a clean single-material look. The trim choice is one of the three selectable attributes on the product page alongside linen color and lining color.

### What does a men’s linen frock coat look like at a formal event?

A tailored linen frock coat at a formal event reads as deliberate and ceremonial in a way that a suit jacket does not — the below-knee length, waist seam, and pleated skirt create a silhouette that signals formality without requiring a morning coat or white tie. In photographs it reads as structured and intentional. The linen texture adds a warmth and tactile quality that wool lacks in outdoor light, which is why it works particularly well for summer weddings and garden receptions where photographs are taken in natural light.

### Most frock coats I’ve seen look theatrical. Will this look like a costume?

That concern is exactly right for most of what’s available — and it comes down to construction and fit. A frock coat looks theatrical when the proportions are wrong for the body wearing it, when the fabric is synthetic, or when the buttons and details are costume-grade. A tailored frock coat cut to your measurements in pure linen with horn buttons and a canvas front reads as a formal garment, not a theatrical one, for the same reason a bespoke suit reads as professional and a cheap suit reads as dressed-up. The difference is whether the garment was made for you or made for a photograph.

Any fit adjustments are made before the final coat is cut. By the time your linen frock coat ships, the fit has already been confirmed on your body — not estimated from a size chart. If something still needs attention on arrival, contact us directly and we address it.
