## What Pants to Wear to a Beach Wedding

What pants should a man wear to a beach wedding? Tailored linen trousers are the safest answer for guests, groomsmen and grooms alike, because they respect the occasion and the climate at the same time. Here is how the usual choices compare for a ceremony on the sand.

| Tailored linen, this pair | Coolest cloth, wedding-appropriate with a zip fly, crease and belt loops; creases softly during the day |
| --- | --- |
| Drawstring linen | Cool and relaxed; right for a casual or barefoot wedding, too casual for most ceremonies |
| Tropical wool | Smoother and more crease resistant; warmer than linen in direct sun |
| Cotton chinos | Easy and familiar; heavier and hotter than linen, and they show sweat |

Men’s linen pants for wedding guests and the wedding party differ mainly in color. For a groom or groomsmen, matching linen trousers in white, off white or natural with linen shirts are the classic beach wedding attire for men. For guests, a light color that does not compete with the wedding party is the safe choice: soft tan, khaki, light blue or light gray linen. If the dress code is formal or the reception runs into the evening, a darker linen or our **[wool wedding pants](https://baronboutique.com/mens-pure-new-wool-wedding-pants/)** are the dressier choice.

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Linen Wedding Pants

### What pants should a man wear to a beach wedding?

Tailored linen trousers in a light color are the most reliable choice: they are cool, they look like wedding clothes, and they suit sand and sun. Choose a zip fly and belt loops over a drawstring unless the invitation says casual or barefoot. Guests usually wear tan, khaki, light blue or light gray, leaving white and ivory to the groom and groomsmen unless asked otherwise.

### Are linen pants appropriate for a wedding?

Yes, for a daytime, outdoor or warm-weather wedding they are often the most appropriate choice. What makes linen pants look formal enough is the tailoring: a clean flat front, a pressed crease, a fitted seat and a hem cut to the right length. For an evening or black tie wedding, linen is too casual; choose wool instead.

### My linen pants are see-through. Will these be?

Light linen can show through in bright sun, which is a common worry. Ours are lined to the knee in satin at the front, which keeps the most visible area opaque and helps the leg hang cleanly. Wear underwear close to your skin tone rather than white, which shows more under white cloth. If you are concerned, natural, soft tan or light gray linen are less sheer than bright white.

### How do I stop linen pants from wrinkling?

You cannot stop linen creasing completely; soft creases are part of its character and most people expect them at a summer wedding. Hang the trousers the night before, steam them on the morning, and stand rather than sit until after the photos if you want them crisp in pictures. A trouser that fits well creases less, because the cloth is not pulled tight across the lap.

### Linen, cotton or tropical wool for a hot wedding?

Linen is the coolest and most breathable, and it looks most at home on a beach. Tropical wool is smoother, keeps its crease better and handles evening better, but feels warmer in direct sun. Cotton chinos are the least suited: heavier, slower to dry and more likely to show sweat.

### What should I wear with linen pants to a beach wedding?

A linen or fine cotton shirt in white, pale blue or a light stripe, with suede loafers, suede derbies or espadrilles in tan or brown. Add an unstructured linen jacket for the ceremony if the dress code asks for one. Avoid black leather shoes and heavy belts, which look out of place with light linen on sand.

### What is an after-dinner split?

It is a small split at the center back of the waistband. It lets the waistband give slightly when you sit down to eat, which is how it got its name, and it makes the waistband easier to let out later. It is a traditional tailoring detail you rarely find on ready-made trousers.

### How do I care for linen pants?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle cycle; hang them to dry and iron them while still slightly damp for the sharpest finish. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and the crispest press before a wedding. Linen softens and becomes more comfortable with every wash.

Order as soon as the date is set, and allow time to send us your notes on the test pair.
