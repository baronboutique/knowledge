### What is a pocket square?

A pocket square is a hemmed square of a thin fabric that sits in the breast pocket of your suit jacket as a decorative accessory, providing a stylish hint to increase visual appeal.

### Can you iron silk pocket squares?

Yes, you sure can iron a silk pocket square. Iron the silk pocket square on a low heat on the reverse side. It should get rid of wrinkles. If you are using medium to high heat, use a towel over your pocket square and iron over it.

### Can you steam silk pocket squares?

Yes, you sure can steam a silk pocket square. To steam a silk pocket square, use a towel over your pocket square and iron over it. This will avoid the steam stain.

### Can you wash silk pocket squares?

Yes, you sure can wash silk pocket squares. Handwash your silk pocket square in cold to lukewarm water. Use a small amount of soap. Air dry in shade.

Your silk pocket-handkerchief squares are delicate and treated as such. First, roll your pocket squares in a towel to soak up excess moisture. Then lay them out to air dry, making sure to avoid any potential heat source or direct sunlight.

### How to store silk pocket squares?

The best way to store silk pocket squares is a plastic container. Fold your silk pocket squares neatly, and place them in the container. Stack them one on top of the other. Start with the larger pocket squares first, and stack the smaller ones on top.
