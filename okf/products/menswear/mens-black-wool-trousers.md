Keeping Black Black

## How to Keep Black Wool From Fading

Black fades when dye is stripped by hot water, harsh detergents and sunlight, and it turns shiny when fibers are flattened by heat and friction. A little care keeps these trousers deep black for years.

| Wash cool | A cool, gentle wool cycle; hot water strips dye |
| --- | --- |
| Mild detergent | A wool detergent without optical brighteners, which dull dark colors |
| Dry in shade | Hang by the hem, away from direct sun |
| Press through a cloth | Prevents the shine an iron leaves on dark wool |
| Rotate | Wearing a pair every other day lets the wool recover and reduces wear at the seat |

Questions & Answers

## Questions About Black Wool Trousers

### What should I look for in black work pants?

Fit first: trousers cut to your seat and thigh will not pull or bag. Then the cloth: wool breathes, resists odor and stays matte, while polyester traps heat and shines with wear. Finally, construction such as a grip waistband, a lined front and seam allowances that let the trousers be altered as you change.

### Why do black trousers go shiny?

Shine appears when fibers are flattened by friction and heat, usually at the seat and knees, and when a hot iron presses directly on dark cloth. Synthetic fibers shine fastest. Fine merino resists it; pressing through a cloth and rotating between pairs keeps it away.

### How do I stop black wool trousers from fading?

Wash them cool on a gentle wool cycle, use a wool detergent without optical brighteners, and dry them in the shade. Hot water, harsh detergent and strong sunlight are the main causes of fading. Washing less often also helps, and wool rarely needs frequent washing because it resists odor.

### Can you wear brown shoes with black trousers?

It is possible but tricky; dark brown or burgundy can work in a relaxed, smart casual outfit. For work and formal occasions black shoes are the safe choice with black trousers, with a black belt to match.

### Is black too formal for everyday office wear?

Not in most offices. Black trousers with a white or blue shirt are standard business wear, and a knit makes them softer for casual days. If your office is relaxed, charcoal from the same cloth gives a similar look with less contrast.

### Wool or polyester black trousers?

Polyester is cheaper and easy to wash, but it traps heat and sweat, holds odor, and develops a shine. Wool breathes, drapes and stays matte, and it lasts much longer. For trousers worn several times a week, wool is the better value over time.

### Classic or slim cut black trousers?

This pair is a classic straight leg, which is comfortable for long days and suits most builds. A slimmer leg is available on request, and our black slim fit pants are cut slim from the start in a firmer worsted. Choose classic if you sit a lot or prefer room at the thigh.

### How do I care for these trousers?

They are machine washable on a cool, gentle wool cycle; hang them by the hem to dry. Dry cleaning is recommended for a longer life and a sharp crease. Between wears, hang them overnight and brush off lint, which shows easily on black.
