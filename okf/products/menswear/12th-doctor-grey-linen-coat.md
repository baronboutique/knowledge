## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which series is the grey linen coat from?

The grey distressed linen frock coat was introduced mid-Series 10 (2017), Peter Capaldi’s final season as the 12th Doctor. Costume designer Hayley Nebauer introduced it as a departure from the structured Crombie coats of Series 8 and 9. It is lighter in weight and more worn in character, suited to the Doctor’s final arc.

### Is this the rarest 12th Doctor coat variant?

Yes. The grey slub linen frock coat is the least-replicated of all 12th Doctor variants. The navy Crombie and velvet coats are widely available from various suppliers. A screen-accurate replica of the Series 10 grey linen coat is extremely rare which is why we made one.

### What lining color is used on screen?

The on-screen coat features an electric blue lining, consistent with Capaldi’s Series 10 coat palette. We offer this as the default lining for screen accuracy, but you can request any lining color at no additional charge.

### What are the gold buttons on the back of the coat?

The back features two decorative gold dome buttons positioned above the pleated back skirt panels. A distinctive construction detail that anchors the dramatic pleated silhouette and is one of the coat’s most recognizable screen-accurate features.

### Can this coat be worn as everyday outerwear, not just for cosplay?

Absolutely. Because the grey linen frock coat reads as a distinctive tailored coat rather than an obvious costume piece, it is well suited to everyday wear particularly in creative environments, at cultural events, or as a statement overcoat in warmer weather. Linen’s breathability makes it comfortable in temperatures where wool coats would be impractical.

### How long does the full order take?

The test coat takes 18-21 business days to arrive. After you approve the fit, the final linen coat takes a further 18-21 business days. Total time from order to final coat is typically 6-8 weeks. If you have a convention or event deadline, contact us and we will do our best to accommodate it.

### Do I need to return the test coat?

No. The test coat is yours to keep at no extra charge. We retain a copy of the pattern. You do not need to return it under any circumstances.

### Is this available for women as well?

Yes. Because every coat is built entirely to your measurements, there is no separate women’s version required. The pattern is constructed to fit your body regardless of gender. Simply submit your measurements and we will tailor accordingly.
