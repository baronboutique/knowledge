## Questions & Answers

**Q:** Should I choose the leather version or the Melton wool version?  
**A:** We make this coat in both full-grain leather and Melton wool. The wool version reads as a soft, theatrical coat with more drape. The leather version is for buyers who want the silhouette as a long-term wardrobe piece, something worn regularly, in all weather, that improves with age. Full-grain leather lasts decades and registers as outerwear with serious presence. Both are built to the same pattern and the same measurements process. The choice comes down to material and intent.

**Q:** What is cow Nappa and why is it used for this coat?  
**A:** Cow Nappa is a full-grain cowhide leather that has been tanned for softness while retaining the structural weight of the hide. It is soft enough to drape through the body of the coat but firm enough to hold the wide peak lapels and flared cuffs in shape without internal support. Lambskin is softer and lighter, better for a more relaxed drape. Both are genuine leather, not bonded or synthetic. The difference in feel is significant: cow Nappa has more presence; lambskin has more softness.

**Q:** Why does the test coat matter more for leather than for other materials?  
**A:** Because leather cannot be corrected after cutting. With wool you can press, adjust, and sometimes re-cut using the ample seam allowances. With leather, once it is cut and stitched, the garment is permanent. A sleeve that lands wrong stays wrong. A shoulder that is too wide stays too wide. The test coat in cotton lets you see and correct every element of the fit before any leather is touched. It is not optional for this garment.

**Q:** Can I get this in a color other than black?  
**A:** Yes. Dark Brown Lambskin and Black Lambskin are available as alternatives to the default Cow Nappa Black. For the lining, 33 acetate colors are available at no extra charge. Black acetate is the default.

**Q:** Can I specify a custom coat length?
