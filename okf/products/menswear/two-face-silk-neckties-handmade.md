#### Caring For Your Silk

To maintain the integrity and luster of the silk, we recommend dry-cleaning only.

##### How to store silk neckties?

Store the silk neckties by rolling or hanging. You can store silk neckties in many other ways, but these are the most common two.

**Rolling**: Fold the necktie in half without the knots and roll from the thinner end. Store your rolled ties in a drawer with a drawer organizer or in a container. Do not stack rolled ties or put anything on top, as any weight will cause the neckties to lose their shape.

**Hanging**: The best way to hang a silk necktie is to hang it on a tie rack. This is the most wrinkle-free way to store silk neckties. The tie rack should be placed in a cool, dry area, or in the closet.
