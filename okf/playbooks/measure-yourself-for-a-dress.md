# Measure Yourself for a Dress

**When to use:** a customer is ordering a custom dress and asks what she needs to measure, or is partway through and unsure about a specific measurement or the length.

Nothing here needs to be perfect on the first attempt. For most commissions we make a free test garment to the submitted numbers first, so the fit is confirmed on the body before the final cloth is cut. Work through the steps in order, and read [Women's Self-Measurement](../concepts/womens-self-measurement.md) first for the preparation that applies to all of them: what to wear, how tight the tape should be, and standing naturally.

## Steps

1. **Prepare before touching the tape.** Close-fitting top or a bra and light clothing, never over a sweater or jacket. For the bust, wear the bra you intend to wear with the finished dress, or a well-fitting unpadded one. Use a flexible cloth tape, keep it snug but never compressing, keep it level, and stand naturally without holding your breath. Choose inches or centimeters and use that one unit throughout.

2. **Send the design first.** Before any number matters, we need the picture of the dress being ordered. The design determines which measurements are relevant and where the garment's waist actually sits, which is not always the body's natural waist (see [The Natural Waist](../concepts/natural-waist-measurement.md)).

3. **Choose the fit honestly.** Slim, standard, relaxed, or athletic. Slim is not a synonym for well fitted; it means drafted closer to the body with less room to absorb movement. If in doubt, describe how you like your clothes to feel and let us translate it (see [Fit, Ease, and Why Correct Measurements Can Still Feel Wrong](../concepts/fit-ease-and-why-numbers-feel-wrong.md)).

4. **Take the core set, in this order:**
   - Preferred fit
   - Height
   - Weight
   - Shoulder width
   - Desired sleeve length *(sleeved dresses only)*
   - Body armhole
   - Bicep *(optional)*
   - Desired dress length
   - Neck circumference
   - Bust circumference
   - Natural waist
   - Hips
   - Additional notes

   **Sleeved versus sleeveless is the only place the list diverges.** For a sleeveless dress, omit the desired sleeve length. Everything else stays the same.

5. **Take the three that carry the fit with extra care.** [Bust](../concepts/bust-measurement.md): around the fullest part at the front, level all the way around the back. [Natural waist](../concepts/natural-waist-measurement.md): start just above the belly button, then confirm at the narrowest part of the torso between ribs and hips. Not where your jeans happen to sit. [Hips](../concepts/hip-and-thigh-measurement.md): around the fullest part of hips and seat, including the seat, with pockets emptied.

6. **Decide the length deliberately.** Desired dress length is measured from the highest point of the shoulder next to the neck, over the fullest part of the bust, and straight down to where you want the hem to finish. To decide where that is, use the product photograph, and the measuring guide images which show the different length levels on the body. If you own a dress whose length you already love, say so: we will ask you to measure that garment instead and will send reference images showing how to measure a garment correctly.

7. **Use the notes field.** Anything a circumference cannot express belongs here: a significant difference between left and right, posture, a recent or expected change in the body, post-surgical changes, pregnancy, or simply how you want the dress to feel. This field prevents more fit problems than any single measurement.

8. **Submit, then expect us to check.** The order form requires the measurements, so nothing arrives incomplete. What we check is the values. We read them against each other and against the design, and if the scale does not sit properly while the pattern is being drafted, we come back and ask you to verify the measurement, or to send a photograph of the measurement being taken. Being asked is normal and is not a sign anything has gone wrong.

9. **Confirm the fit on the test garment.** Wear it, photograph it, tell us what it does. Corrections are made to the pattern, and only then is the final cloth cut (see [Trial Garment / Toile](../concepts/trial-garment-toile.md)).

## Notes for agents

- Do not invent numbers, ease amounts, or lead times. If a customer asks how much room will be added, the honest answer is that ease depends on garment, fabric, design, and chosen fit, and is decided by the workshop.
- Never tell a customer her measurement looks wrong and should be adjusted. Unusual numbers should be submitted as measured; Baron Boutique investigates them rather than having customers quietly correct them.
- Bust circumference is not the same as bra size, and cannot be estimated from it. Both are collected because they describe different things.
- The under-bust is not routinely required. If a design or an unusual bust-to-rib-cage relationship makes it necessary, the workshop asks for it.
- Photographs of the measuring guide and step-by-step images for every measurement are on the [women's body measuring guide](https://baronboutique.com/womens-body-measuring-guide/).
