# Concepts

Hand-authored craft knowledge from the Baron Boutique workshop, one concept per file.

## Construction and Craft

* [Construction](construction.md) — How Baron Boutique builds the final garment, and how the workshop decides between canvas types, hand work, and machine work for each project.
* [Fittings](fittings.md) — How Baron Boutique reads fit from customer photos, translates plain-language feedback into corrections, and decides when the fit is final.
* [Pattern Drafting](pattern-drafting.md) — How Baron Boutique drafts an individual pattern from scratch for each commission, and how it decides proportions when the reference picture and the customer's body disagree.
* [Trial Garment (Toile)](trial-garment-toile.md) — Why Baron Boutique ships a test garment before cutting the final fabric, what it is made from, and how the workshop decides when one is enough.

## Fabric and Materials

* [Dupioni Silk](dupioni-silk.md) — Why Dupioni silk is a Baron Boutique signature for occasion garments, and the one question that decides whether we recommend it.
* [How We Choose Fabric](fabric-selection.md) — How Baron Boutique advises fabric, starting from the customer's objective rather than the cloth, with the workshop's reasoning on weight, climate, and trade-offs.
* [Linen and Himalayan Hemp](linen-and-himalayan-hemp.md) — How Baron Boutique decides between linen and Himalayan hemp, and the single question about wrinkles that usually decides it.
* [Velvet](velvet.md) — How Baron Boutique chooses among velvets for frock coats, evening jackets, and gowns, what velvet demands in the workshop, and how to live with a velvet garment for years.
* [Understanding Wool](wool.md) — How Baron Boutique chooses among worsted, tropical, flannel, tweed, and boiled wool, categorized by the problems each solves rather than by technical definitions.

## Measurement and Fit

* [Bust Measurement](bust-measurement.md) — Where the bust measurement is actually taken, why Baron Boutique asks for bra size as well, and what a wrong bust number does to a garment.
* [Fit, Ease, and Why Correct Measurements Can Still Feel Wrong](fit-ease-and-why-numbers-feel-wrong.md) — Why a garment cannot equal the body measurement, how Baron Boutique diagnoses a waist that pulls at the hips, and what fabric does to fit.
* [Hip and Thigh Measurement](hip-and-thigh-measurement.md) — Why Baron Boutique does not use a fixed distance below the waist for hips, how to take the thigh, and why trousers punish a hip error hardest.
* [Measurement](measurement.md) — How Baron Boutique collects, verifies, and reuses customer measurements for remote bespoke tailoring, and how experience catches the numbers that lie.
* [The Natural Waist](natural-waist-measurement.md) — The difference between the natural waist and the waistband measurement, how to find each, and why "waist" alone is never enough information.
* [Women's Self-Measurement](womens-self-measurement.md) — How Baron Boutique asks women to measure themselves at home, and why preparation decides whether the numbers describe the body or the clothing.

## Ordering and Process

* [The Bespoke Commission Process](bespoke-commission-process.md) — How a Baron Boutique bespoke commission works from first inquiry to delivered garment, and the thinking behind each stage of the journey.
