## What We Will and Will Not Promise

Most of what follows is the boring part of a service agreement. It is here because a promise you cannot keep costs more than a sale you did not make.

- **Everything is made to order.** Production and delivery timelines are estimates, and they move with design complexity, material availability, how fast you come back to us, and shipping conditions.
- **Measurements are yours to get right.** We guide the process and provide instructions, but we cannot be responsible for a fit problem caused by a measurement that was wrong when it reached us.
- **Shipping is handled by couriers, not by us.** We coordinate and monitor, but delays at customs or with the courier are outside our control.
- **We can decline work.** Requests are reviewed for feasibility, material availability and design constraints. If we do not think we can make it well, we would rather say so than take the order.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between bespoke and made to your measurements?

Both start from a pattern cut for you rather than a standard size. Bespoke adds a fitting prototype, a test garment made up before your real cloth is touched, so the shape can be corrected on your body. That takes longer and costs more. Made to your measurements skips the prototype stage and uses a free test fit instead, which is accurate enough for most garments and noticeably faster. If the garment is complicated, or your posture is unusual, bespoke is worth the extra step.

### Do you sell ready-made clothing?

No, and we never have. Nothing is cut before it is ordered. Every garment on this site is made as part of a service request, for one named customer, which is also why a finished piece cannot be resold or repurposed if an order falls through.

### Is there a minimum order?

Not for individuals. Bespoke, made to your measurements, CMT and alterations are all single-garment services. Prototype development also has no minimum. Small-batch production starts at 5 pieces per style, because a production run has setup costs that a single piece cannot carry.

### How long will my garment take?

It is quoted with your project rather than published as a single number, because a shirt and a hand-padded overcoat are not the same job. Complexity, material availability and how quickly your measurements come back to us all move the date. If you have a fixed deadline, tell us at the start and we will tell you honestly whether it is achievable.

### How does the online consultation work?

It starts with a discussion of your style preferences, what the garment is for, and your fit requirements. We then guide you through measuring, with step-by-step instructions and visual aids, so it can be done accurately at home. Submit the form with your requirements to begin.

### How do I take my own measurements accurately?

We send written instructions and visual aids, and we check the numbers for internal consistency when they arrive. If something looks wrong against the rest of your measurements, we come back to you before cutting rather than after. Where possible, have someone else measure you, and wear the kind of shirt you would normally wear under the garment.

### What communication can I expect while my garment is being made?

Regular updates on progress, including photographs or video at key stages. Our team is available by email throughout if you have questions.

### I am not sure about the design. Will you advise?

Yes. We give personalized recommendations based on what the garment is for and how you want to wear it. We will also tell you when an idea will not work the way you are picturing it, which is the more useful half of the advice.

### Can I supply my own fabric?

Yes, through our CMT service. Send it to us and we will cut from it. We will tell you before production whether the cloth behaves the way your design assumes, because some fabrics will not hold a structured shoulder or a sharp pleat no matter who sews them.

### What are the shipping options and delivery times for international orders?

We ship worldwide through FedEx, DHL and UPS. Delivery estimates vary with your location, courier operations and customs processing. Tracking details are provided once your order is dispatched. Import duties and taxes are payable by you in the receiving country.

### What if I need alterations after delivery and I live abroad?

Bespoke and made-to-your-measurements orders include a complimentary test fit, which is there precisely to avoid this. If a minor adjustment is still needed, a sleeve or a trouser length, a trusted local tailor is faster and cheaper than shipping. For anything significant, contact us and we will work it out with you.

### Will you alter a garment I bought somewhere else?

Yes. We assess it on construction and feasibility first, then quote on the complexity of the work. You cover shipping in both directions.

### Can I see examples of previous custom projects?

Yes. We have photographs of garments made for clients worldwide through our online service. Contact us and we will share examples relevant to what you are planning.

### Where are the garments actually made?

In our own atelier in Kathmandu, by our own tailors. Nothing is subcontracted, so if a quality problem appears it is ours to fix and there is no third party for either of us to chase.
