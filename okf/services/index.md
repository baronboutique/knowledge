# Services

- [Private Label](private-label.md): Baron Boutique produces cashmere knitwear, handwoven cashmere accessories and tailored garments for established fashion brands under the brand's own label.
- [Custom Cashmere Gifts](custom-cashmere-gifts.md): Baron Boutique offers a bespoke cashmere gifting service for life's most meaningful occasions, including weddings, corporate milestones, baby showers, and personal celebrations.
- [Retail Partnerships](retail-partnerships.md): Baron Boutique partners with boutique retailers, specialty stores, and online retailers worldwide, producing custom tailored menswear, womenswear, screen-inspired garments, and handwoven cashmere to o…
- [Designer Program](designer-program.md): Baron Boutique's Designer Program produces garments for emerging and independent fashion designers with a minimum order of one piece.
- [Natural Hemp Fabric & Clothing](natural-hemp-fabric-clothing.md): Baron Boutique produces 100% natural hemp fabric and hemp clothing from hemp grown between 1,500m and 3,000m in the Nepal Himalayas without pesticides, herbicides or synthetic fertilizers.
- [Custom Corporate Gifts](custom-corporate-gifts.md): Baron Boutique produces branded corporate cashmere gifts to order: scarves, shawls, wraps and pashminas, hand-knitted beanies, gloves and socks, throws, and boxed gift sets.
- [Our Services](our-services.md): Baron Boutique operates as a made-to-order tailoring house. Every garment is cut after it is ordered, to one person's measurements, in the fabric they chose, and no ready-made stock is held.
