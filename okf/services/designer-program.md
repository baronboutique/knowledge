Baron Boutique's Designer Program produces garments for emerging and independent fashion designers with a minimum order of one piece. The single-piece minimum is deliberate: every other Baron Boutique program carries a minimum because production economics require it, and this one does not, so a designer testing an idea does not have to fund a production run to find out whether it works. A prototype is quoted per design and takes about 3 to 4 weeks. Small batches of 10 to 50 pieces take about 4 to 6 weeks and larger runs about 8 to 12 weeks. Patterns are cut from scratch and made up in calico first, so the shape can be corrected before the cloth is cut. Design consultation on feasibility, construction and materials is included. A non-disclosure agreement is signed on request at no cost before designs are shared. Materials include superfine wool, wool-cashmere blends, silk blends, linen and linen blends, and 100% Grade A cashmere, and designers may supply their own cloth. Everything is cut and sewn in Baron Boutique's own atelier in Kathmandu with nothing subcontracted, and export documentation and worldwide shipping are handled in house.

## We Understand Your Journey

Starting a fashion design career is challenging. Whether you’re a talented designer working a 9-to-5 job to pay the bills, a recent graduate with bold ideas but limited capital, or someone transitioning from another career into fashion, you deserve a partner who believes in your vision.

The barriers to entry are real: expensive manufacturing minimums, high upfront costs, complex supply chains, and the risk of unsold inventory. These obstacles have stopped countless talented designers from pursuing their passion.

Baron Boutique was founded on a simple principle: **great design shouldn’t require great wealth.**  
 That is why the minimum here is one piece. Every other program we run has a minimum, because production economics are real. This one does not, deliberately, because a designer testing an idea should not have to fund a production run to find out whether it works. The minimum is one because that is what it takes to get started.

## From Sketch to Garment

The distance between a drawing and something a customer can wear is where most first collections stall. This is what that distance actually looks like, and it is the part we do with you rather than for you.

1

### Your sketch, our questions

You send sketches, a reference piece, or a description. We come back with honest feedback on whether it can be built the way you have drawn it, what it will take, and which cloth suits it.

2

### Pattern and toile

We cut a pattern from scratch and make it up in calico first, so the shape can be corrected before it is cut in your cloth. Changes at this stage cost you nothing but time.

3

### The finished piece

Your design is cut and sewn by hand in our atelier, inspected, and shipped with export documentation handled. One piece or fifty, the process is the same.

#### Frequently Asked Questions

##### Do I need minimum order quantities to work with Baron Boutique?

No. One of our core principles is supporting emerging designers without financial barriers. You can start with a single prototype piece or order just a few units. There are no mandatory minimums or penalties for small orders. This allows you to test your designs affordably before committing to larger production runs.

##### How much does it cost to create a prototype?

Prototype pricing is designed to be accessible for emerging designers. While costs vary based on design complexity, materials, and specifications, prototype development is significantly more affordable than traditional manufacturing. We encourage you to reach out with your design details for a transparent quote. Many designers start with a single prototype to validate their concept before scaling.

##### What if my design doesn’t sell well? Will I be stuck with inventory?

No. Since you order only what you need without minimums, there’s no inventory risk. Order 10 pieces to test the market. Get customer feedback. Refine your design. Order another small batch with improvements. This iterative approach lets you reduce waste and invest progressively as demand grows.

##### How long does it take from design concept to finished product?

Timelines depend on design complexity, material availability, and order volume. Prototype development typically takes 3-4 weeks. Small batch production (10-50 pieces) usually takes 4-6 weeks. Larger orders may take 8-12 weeks. We’ll provide realistic timelines during your initial consultation so you can plan accordingly.

##### Can you help me if I’m not sure how to construct my design?

Absolutely. Our experienced tailoring team provides design consultation and technical feedback. If you have sketches, ideas, or reference pieces, we’ll evaluate feasibility, suggest construction methods, recommend materials, and provide professional guidance on how to execute your vision. This technical expertise is invaluable for designers new to production.

##### What materials and fabrics are available?

We work with premium materials including superfine wool, wool-cashmere blends, silk blends, 100% cashmere, and other luxury fabrics. You can select from our existing material collection or specify your own. Our team can also source specialty materials if needed for your unique design concept.

##### What design categories can you produce?

We support menswear (tailored suits, dress shirts, separates), womenswear (dresses, tailored pieces, custom designs), luxury cashmere (scarves, shawls, sweaters, accessories), and specialty items (screen-inspired garments, theatrical pieces, themed collections). If you have a concept outside these areas, contact us. We may be able to help or source the right partner.

##### Can I scale from prototypes to larger production runs?

Yes. This is exactly how our program is designed. Start with 1-5 prototypes to test your concept. Move to 50-100 piece batches as demand grows. Eventually scale to thousands of units when you’re ready. We support you at every scale without forcing you to commit to large volumes upfront.

##### Do you provide quality control on every piece?

Yes. Every single piece goes through our quality control process. Hand-cutting and tailoring by master craftspeople means attention to detail on each garment. We inspect for stitching quality, fit, material consistency, and finishing. Your reputation depends on quality, and we take that seriously.

##### How do I get started? What’s the process?

Contact us with your design concept, sketches, or ideas. Share your vision, goals, and any specific requirements. We’ll schedule a consultation to discuss your project in detail, provide honest feedback on feasibility and timeline, and give you transparent pricing. If you move forward, we’ll guide you through sampling, revisions, and production. There’s no pressure and no commitment, just a conversation to explore how we can support your creative journey.

##### What if I want to keep my designs confidential?

We understand confidentiality is important. We’re happy to discuss non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) if needed. Your designs and concepts are treated professionally and with respect. Many designers work with us on confidential projects, and we maintain strict confidentiality standards.

##### Can I work with Baron Boutique while keeping my day job?

Yes. Our flexible approach makes this ideal for designers building their brand on the side. Start with prototypes on your timeline. Test designs with small orders. Grow gradually without needing to quit your job immediately. Many successful designers started exactly this way, testing their concept while maintaining financial stability.

##### What makes Baron Boutique different from other manufacturers?

Several factors: no minimum orders (you can start with one piece), in-house production with quality control, master craftsmanship on every piece, transparent communication, design consultation included, affordable prototyping, and genuine commitment to emerging designers. We’ve been doing this for over 20 years. We believe great design shouldn’t require great wealth, and we back that belief with flexible terms and fair pricing.
