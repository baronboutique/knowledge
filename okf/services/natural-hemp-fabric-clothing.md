# Natural Hemp Fabric & Clothing

Baron Boutique produces 100% natural hemp fabric and hemp clothing from hemp grown between 1,500m and 3,000m in the Nepal Himalayas without pesticides, herbicides or synthetic fertilizers. Fabric is sold by the meter from a minimum of 10 meters in weights from 150gsm to 400gsm, in plain, twill and herringbone weaves plus custom patterns and hemp-linen blends. Hemp clothing is made to the customer's own measurements with no minimum, covering shirts, tunics, pants, jackets, outerwear, scarves, shawls and custom pieces such as suits, dresses and hemp T-shirts, for men and women. Private label garment production starts at 10 pieces per style with the brand's own labels, tags and packaging. Over 350 hand-dyed azo-free colors are available with Pantone matching. Hemp is a natural plant fiber, not a synthetic: it is roughly three times the tensile strength of cotton, biodegradable, naturally antimicrobial and hypoallergenic, sheds no microplastics, softens with every wash, and a well-made hemp garment lasts 20 years or more. Everything is handwoven and made in Nepal by artisan communities, many of them women-led cooperatives, and shipped worldwide with tracking.

## Questions & Answers

**Q:** Is hemp fabric natural or synthetic?  
**A:** Natural. Hemp is a plant fiber, in the same family of textiles as linen and cotton. Nothing petroleum-based goes into it, it sheds no microplastics when washed, and it is fully compostable in about six months. Polyester, by contrast, is plastic and takes 200 years or more to break down.

**Q:** What does hemp fabric actually feel like?  
**A:** Close to linen. Handwoven Himalayan hemp has a soft, breathable texture with a slight irregularity in the surface that comes from being made on a loom rather than a machine. The thing people do not expect is that it improves: hemp softens with every wash, where synthetics deteriorate. A hemp shirt is at its best after a year, not on the day you buy it.

**Q:** Do you sell hemp clothing, or only the fabric?  
**A:** Both, and they are the same cloth. You can buy fabric by the meter from 10 meters and make something yourself, or we can make the garment for you. Finished clothing is made to your measurements with no minimum, so a single shirt is a valid order.

**Q:** What hemp garments do you make?  
**A:** Shirts and tunics, pants and bottoms, jackets and outerwear, and scarves and shawls, plus anything custom. Suits, dresses, a hemp T-shirt, a jumpsuit: if you can specify it, our tailors can make it. Custom garments are made to your measurements rather than picked from a size run.

**Q:** Do you make hemp T-shirts?  
**A:** Not as a stock line, but yes as a custom garment. Tell us the fit, the weight you want and the neckline, and it is made to your measurements. For a brand ordering a run, hemp T-shirts fall under private label at 10 pieces per style.

**Q:** What makes Himalayan hemp different from other hemp fabric?  
**A:** Altitude. Ours grows between 1,500m and 3,000m, where intense UV-B makes the plant produce more lignin, which armors the fiber, and where mountain wind and cold build denser crystalline cellulose in the stem. The result is measurably stronger and more UV-resistant than low-altitude industrial hemp. It is then hand-processed and handwoven rather than mechanically stripped, which preserves the softness that mass processing removes.

**Q:** Is hemp really better than cotton or linen?  
**A:** On several measures, yes. It uses 50% less water than cotton, needs no pesticides, and produces fabric about three times stronger. Against linen it is more durable, softer over time, and offers better UV protection. Where cotton wins is familiarity and price. We would rather tell you that than pretend hemp is better at everything.

**Q:** How long does hemp last compared to synthetic fabric?  
**A:** A well-made hemp garment can last 20 years or more with proper care, while synthetics typically deteriorate within 2 to 5. Hemp's strength actually increases when wet, and the fabric improves with age rather than thinning. That makes it an investment piece rather than a disposable one.

**Q:** What fabric weights and weaves do you offer?  
**A:** Weights from 150gsm for summer shirting up to 400gsm for outerwear and upholstery. Weaves include plain, twill and herringbone, plus custom patterns. Each combination behaves differently for drape, durability and texture, so tell us what the fabric is for and we will tell you which to use.

**Q:** What is the minimum order?  
**A:** Hemp fabric by the meter starts at 10 meters. Private label hemp garments have a minimum of 10 pieces per style. For an individual custom garment there is no minimum at all: you can order a single bespoke hemp piece.

**Q:** Can I get hemp fabric in custom colors?  
**A:** Yes. We offer over 350 hand-dyed colors and can Pantone-match to a specific brand color. Our artisans dye by hand using azo-free formulations, so whether you need an exact brand shade for a line or a single custom color for one garment, it can be matched.

**Q:** Is hemp clothing suitable for sensitive skin?  
**A:** Yes. Hemp is naturally hypoallergenic and antimicrobial, and our chemical-free cultivation and processing means no pesticides, synthetic dyes or chemical finishes touch the fiber. People who react to synthetic fabrics often find hemp unusually comfortable.

**Q:** How do I care for hemp clothing and fabric?  
**A:** Machine wash cold or hand wash with a gentle detergent. Air dry or tumble dry low. Avoid bleach and harsh chemicals. Hemp softens and improves with washing, and its natural antimicrobial properties mean it needs washing less often than most fabrics.

**Q:** Can you produce custom hemp garments like suits or dresses?  
**A:** Yes. We offer full bespoke tailoring and garments made to your measurements, using 100% natural hemp fabric. From tailored suits and dresses to scarves and specialty pieces, our tailors can create any design. We also offer CMT, cut, make and trim, if you already have your own hemp designs.

**Q:** Do you ship internationally?  
**A:** Yes, worldwide with tracked delivery included. International orders typically arrive within 1 to 2 weeks after production. We handle export documentation and pack for safe transit. Import duties and taxes are payable by you in the receiving country.

**Q:** Is your hemp sustainably and ethically sourced?  
**A:** Yes. It is grown without pesticides, herbicides or synthetic fertilizers in Nepal's high-altitude regions. We work directly with artisan farming communities, many of them women-led cooperatives, ensuring fair compensation and preserving traditional cultivation methods. Hemp also enriches the soil it grows in rather than stripping it.

**Q:** What is the difference between your hemp and industrial hemp fabric?  
**A:** Industrial hemp is typically mass-produced with mechanical processing that compromises fiber quality and softness. Ours is grown at altitude, hand-processed using traditional methods, and handwoven by skilled artisans. The result is a better texture and hand feel, and the artisanal route also means fair wages and preserved cultural knowledge.

**Q:** Can I visit your production in Nepal?  
**A:** We welcome visits from serious buyers, designers and brand partners. Contact us in advance to arrange it, and you can meet the artisans, watch traditional hemp weaving, and judge the quality in person.

**Q:** How does your pricing compare to other hemp suppliers?  
**A:** Our direct relationship with Nepalese artisan communities and our own in-house production mean no middleman markup. Quotes are transparent and based on your specific requirements: fabric type, quantity, customization and destination. Send the specification and you will get a real number rather than a range.
