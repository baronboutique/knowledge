The problem

## Why Ready-Made Silk Sleepwear Rarely Fits

Silk charmeuse has almost no stretch. A cotton pajama can hide a size that is slightly wrong; silk shows it at once. The complaints we hear, and that fill the reviews of ready-made silk, come back to the same four places.

### Sleeves that stop short

A sleeve labeled “long” still lands above the wrist when the shoulder seam sits too narrow. We cut the sleeve to your arm and set the shoulder to your frame.

### Trousers the wrong length

Standard women’s silk pajama inseams sit around 30.5 inches, too short for tall wearers and too long for petite ones. Your inseam and rise come from your measurements, not a size chart.

### Button fronts that gape

The bust is where a button front has to bridge the most curve. We space the buttons to your measurements so the front lies flat when you turn on your side.

### Robes that fall open

A robe can fit at the bust and still be short on a tall frame, or slip open at night. Length, wrap overlap and belt position are drafted for you, with an inside tie to hold the wrap.

## The Silk: 100% Mulberry Silk, 22 Momme Charmeuse

A great deal of what is sold as silk sleepwear is polyester satin. Satin is a weave, not a fiber, so the word alone tells you very little. Here is exactly what we use.

### How to tell real silk from polyester satin

- Real silk warms quickly in your hand; polyester stays cool and slippery.
- Silk has a soft, shifting sheen; polyester has a flat, even shine that looks the same from every angle.
- A burn test on a loose thread smells like burnt hair and leaves a crumbly ash; polyester melts into a hard bead.
- The label must state the fiber. “Silky”, “satin” or “silk feel” is not a fiber.

We send swatches before you commit, so you can judge the weight and color yourself.

## Measuring at Home, Without the Worry

You do not need to be a tailor to measure yourself, and you do not need to be perfect. A tape measure, a mirror and ten minutes are enough, and a second pair of hands helps. The free test garment is there precisely so that a small error at home never reaches the silk.

Here are the ten measurements we ask for, the same for everyone. Before you start: measure over light clothing, keep the tape level and snug but never tight, and write each number down as you go. Inches or centimeters are both fine; just tell us which you used.

### 1Height

Stand barefoot with your back against a wall and your heels together. Ask someone to mark the top of your head, then measure from the floor to the mark.

### 2Chest or bust

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest or bust, under the arms and level all the way round. Keep it snug, with room for one finger.

### 3Waist

Measure around your body where you like your pajama waistband to sit, usually at or just below the navel. Keep the tape level and comfortable.

### 4Hips

Stand with your feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat, keeping the tape level.

### 5Shoulder width

Measure across your back from the edge of one shoulder to the edge of the other, following the natural curve. A second pair of hands makes this one easy.

### 6Sleeve length

With your arm relaxed at your side, measure from the edge of your shoulder, down the outside of the arm, to where you want the sleeve to end.

### 7Back length

Tilt your head forward to find the bone at the base of your neck. Measure from there straight down your spine to your natural waist, which is usually a little above your pajama waist.

### 8Inseam

Measure from the top of your inner thigh straight down the inside of your leg to where you want the trousers to end.

### 9Rise

Start at your waistband height at the front, pass the tape down between your legs and bring it up to the same height at the back. Keep it close but not tight.

### 10Robe length

From the highest point of your shoulder, next to your neck, measure straight down to where you want the robe to end: the knee, mid-calf or ankle.

Keeping it well

## Caring for Silk Sleepwear

1. Hand wash in cool water, or machine wash on a delicate cycle inside a mesh bag.
2. Use a pH-neutral or silk detergent. No bleach, no fabric softener.
3. Roll in a towel to remove water. Never wring.
4. Dry flat or on a padded hanger, in the shade. Direct sun fades silk.
5. Press on the reverse at a low silk setting. Never tumble dry.

Lotions, deodorant and perfume mark silk more than sleep does, so let them dry before you dress.

### The Silk

#### What silk do you use?

We use 100% mulberry silk charmeuse, grade 6A, at 22 momme. Charmeuse has a satin face and a matte reverse. The silk is woven and every garment is cut and sewn by our own tailors.

#### What is momme, and why 22 momme?

Momme is the weight of silk fabric. Higher momme means more silk in each square meter, which usually means more opacity and more durability. Much ready-made silk sleepwear is 16 to 19 momme. We chose 22 momme because it is dense and opaque, drapes with weight, and stands up better to regular washing.

#### How can I tell real silk from polyester satin?

Satin is a weave, not a fiber, so check the label for the fiber itself. Real silk warms quickly in your hand and has a soft, shifting sheen, while polyester stays cool and has a flat, uniform shine. A burned thread of silk smells like burnt hair and crumbles to ash; polyester melts into a hard bead. We send swatches so you can judge before you order.

### Care and Comfort

#### How do I wash silk pajamas and robes?

Hand wash in cool water, or use a delicate machine cycle with the garment inside a mesh bag. Use a pH-neutral or silk detergent without bleach or fabric softener. Roll the garment in a towel instead of wringing it, dry it flat or on a padded hanger in the shade, and press on the reverse at a low silk setting. Never tumble dry.

#### Are silk pajamas good for warm climates or hot sleepers?

Silk is a natural protein fiber. It absorbs about 11% moisture from the air, compared with less than 1% for polyester satin, and it feels cool on first touch. Comfort in heat also depends on fit and ease: a garment that clings or pulls feels warmer than one that sits correctly, which a bespoke pattern controls. We make no medical claims about silk.

#### Is silk sleepwear slippery in bed?

Charmeuse has a smooth face, so it moves easily against sheets. A correct fit helps: trousers of the right length and a top with the right ease do not twist or ride up. If you are a restless sleeper, choose a slightly closer ease when you order, and we will cut for it.
