## Why Cashmere Works as a Corporate Gift

Most corporate gifts are used once and forgotten. A branded cashmere scarf or wrap is kept, worn, and associated with the company that gave it. It reads as considered rather than transactional, which is the point of a gift meant to strengthen a relationship with a client, a partner, or a team member.  
 Cashmere also carries your brand quietly. Your logo can be a discreet embroidered mark or a woven label inside the piece, so the recipient wears something they genuinely like rather than an advertisement. That is the difference between a gift that gets used and one that gets re-gifted.

## What You Can Customize

Every corporate order is made to your brief. The common customizations are:

- **Brand color matching.** We hand-dye to your Pantone reference, drawing on a palette of over 350 colors.
- **Your logo, your way.** An embroidered logo, a custom woven label, or a custom pattern or motif woven into the cloth itself. Placement and orientation are yours to choose.
- **Monograms and names.** Individual initials or names for personalized recipient gifting.
- **Complete gift sets.** Pair a scarf or wrap with matching pieces such as a notebook or a cedarwood candle, boxed together.
- **Branded packaging.** Gift boxes, silk ribbons, scent sachets, and gift tags in your colors.
- **Scent infusions.** An optional signature scent on the piece or in the packaging.

## Corporate Gifting FAQ

### What is the minimum order for corporate gifts?

Corporate orders start from 6 pieces. The same made-to-order service works for a small executive gift and for a large client or employee program.

### How much do corporate cashmere gifts cost?

Starting prices per piece are $45 for knitted socks, $65 for beanies, $70 for gloves, and $209 for scarves, shawls, wraps, pashminas, and throws. These apply to both 100% cashmere and cashmere-silk blends. Gift sets are priced to their contents. Your final quote depends on the customization, the quantity, and the finish, and shipping is quoted separately. Send your brief and we will return a firm figure within 24 hours.

### How long does a corporate order take?

The design proof and sample take about 3 to 4 weeks depending on the design. Once approved, production takes about 3 to 4 weeks for an order of 6 pieces, and 45 to 50 days for larger orders up to 200 pieces. Programs above 200 pieces are agreed with you directly. Add 4 to 6 days for tracked courier delivery after dispatch. If you have a fixed date, tell us early and we will say honestly whether it is achievable.

### What sizes and weights are the pieces?

Scarves are 28 x 80 inches. Shawls, wraps, and pashminas are 40 x 80 inches. Throws are approximately 50 x 60 inches and can be adjusted. A 28 x 80 inch scarf weighs about 75 grams in a lighter 200/2 yarn or about 130 grams in a medium weight 60/2 yarn. Sizes for knitted beanies, gloves, and socks are confirmed with your quote.

### Is shipping included in the price?

No. Shipping is quoted separately and depends on the quantity and the destination. Everything ships worldwide with tracking, gift-ready, to one address or many. Import duties and taxes are set by the destination country and are the importer’s responsibility.

### Can we return a corporate order?

Personalized pieces are made to your brief in your colors with your branding, so they cannot be returned or refunded once production has begun. They cannot be sold to anyone else. What you do have is a proper check on arrival: report visible problems such as transit damage, short counts, or work that does not match the approved proof within 14 days of delivery, and manufacturing faults that only appear later within 30 days. Where the fault is ours we remake the affected pieces at no charge and cover shipping both ways.

### Can you match our exact brand color?

Yes. We hand-dye to your Pantone reference, with a palette of over 350 colors. Send your brand color code with your quote request and we will match it.

### How do you add our logo?

Three ways: an embroidered logo, a custom woven label, or a custom pattern or motif woven into the cloth. Share your logo artwork and we will advise which suits the piece, including placement and orientation.

### Can you weave a custom pattern, not just a logo?

Yes. Beyond an embroidered logo or woven label, we can weave a custom pattern or motif into the cashmere itself, which is how companies create a piece that is unmistakably theirs.

### Can each gift be personalized to the individual recipient?

Yes. We can add individual names or monograms per piece, so each recipient receives something addressed to them, alongside your company branding.

### Do we see a design before we commit?

Yes. We send a concept illustration and a mockup with your logo and colors, and revise it until your team approves. Nothing is produced until you sign off on the proof.

### Can we get a sample to feel the cashmere first?

Yes. We can send a reference cashmere piece so your team can assess the quality in hand before a bulk order. Reference samples are provided at cost, since each piece is handwoven from 100% cashmere.

### Do you provide branded gift packaging?

Yes. Pieces arrive gift-ready in branded gift boxes with silk ribbon, a gift tag, and an optional scent sachet. Gift sets can pair a scarf or wrap with matching items such as a notebook or a candle.

### Can you ship to multiple addresses?

Yes. We ship worldwide with tracking, to a single office or directly to individual recipients. Share your address list with your order.

### Can we reorder the same gift later?

Yes. We keep your specification on file so repeat programs match your first order in color, branding, and finish.

### Do you offer private label or white label?

Yes. We can produce with your woven label in place of ours for a fully branded, private-label corporate gift.

### Do you work with promotional agencies and resellers?

Yes. We are glad to work behind the scenes on behalf of your clients, providing proofs and quotations you can present as your own.
