## Questions & Answers

**Q:** What is the minimum order for corporate gifts?  
**A:** Corporate orders start from 6 pieces. The same made-to-order service works for a small executive gift and for a large client or employee program.

**Q:** Can you match our exact brand color?  
**A:** Yes. We hand-dye to your Pantone reference, with a palette of over 350 colors. Send your brand color code with your quote request and we will match it.

**Q:** How do you add our logo?  
**A:** Three ways: an embroidered logo, a custom woven label, or a custom pattern or motif woven into the cloth. Share your logo artwork and we will advise which suits the piece, including placement and orientation.

**Q:** Can you weave a custom pattern, not just a logo?  
**A:** Yes. Beyond an embroidered logo or woven label, we can weave a custom pattern or motif into the cashmere itself, which is how companies create a piece that is unmistakably theirs.

**Q:** Can each gift be personalized to the individual recipient?  
**A:** Yes. We can add individual names or monograms per piece, so each recipient receives something addressed to them, alongside your company branding.

**Q:** Do we see a design before we commit?  
**A:** Yes. We send a concept illustration and a mockup with your logo and colors, and revise it until your team approves. Nothing is produced until you sign off on the proof.

**Q:** Can we get a sample to feel the cashmere first?  
**A:** Yes. We can send a reference cashmere piece so your team can assess the quality in hand before a bulk order. Reference samples are provided at cost, since each piece is handwoven from 100% cashmere.

**Q:** Do you provide branded gift packaging?  
**A:** Yes. Pieces arrive gift-ready in branded gift boxes with silk ribbon, a gift tag, and an optional scent sachet. Gift sets can pair a scarf or wrap with matching items such as a notebook or a candle.

**Q:** Can you ship to multiple addresses?  
**A:** Yes. We ship worldwide with tracking, to a single office or directly to individual recipients. Share your address list with your order.

**Q:** Can we reorder the same gift later?  
**A:** Yes. We keep your specification on file so repeat programs match your first order in color, branding, and finish.

**Q:** Do you offer private label or white label?  
**A:** Yes. We can produce with your woven label in place of ours for a fully branded, private-label corporate gift.

**Q:** Do you work with promotional agencies and resellers?  
**A:** Yes. We are glad to work behind the scenes on behalf of your clients, providing proofs and quotations you can present as your own.
